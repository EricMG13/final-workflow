### Debt structure

- Instruments are mostly secured. Secured debt is 25,575 of 29,321 in Note 5 and is made up of term loans under the 2013, 2014 and 2023 credit facilities, 7.25% and 5.75% senior secured notes, the AAdvantage Financing, EETCs, equipment loans and special facility revenue bonds (Q3 page:14 and page:15). Secured financings are collateralized by aircraft, engines, simulators, airport gate leasehold rights, route authorities, airport slots, certain receivables, certain intellectual property and certain loyalty program assets (Q3 page:16).
- The only unsecured debt in Note 5 is the three PSP promissory notes, 3,746 in total. In the contractual obligations table they sit under AAG Parent and Other AAG Subsidiaries and fall due in 2030 and 2031 (Q3 page:66), so they are a parent-level claim that is outside the American maturity table. American's own table totals 25,575 of principal, equal to the secured subtotal.
- Two loan families are tied to the AAdvantage loyalty program: the AAdvantage Financing is borrowed by American and AAdvantage Loyalty IP Ltd. and carries a peak debt service coverage ratio (Q3 page:17 and page:61).
- Rates: the named term loans and PSP notes are floating (5.94% to 6.76% at September 30, 2026) and total 10,869, 37% of Note 5 debt before counting variable-rate equipment loans, which are inside the 4,926 line.
- Bonds versus loans: the senior secured notes outstanding are the 7.25% notes (750) and the 5.75% notes (2,750); the 8.50% notes (1,000) were prepaid in full and the 5.50% notes (583 at year end) are nil at September 30, 2026. Most of the remaining debt is bank term loans, EETC pass-through certificates (9,332, up from 6,912) and equipment loans, not capital markets bonds.
- 2026 financing activity (Q3 page:16 to page:20, page:45 and page:46): revolving commitments of the 2013, 2014 and 2023 facilities were reset to mature in March 2031; the 2014 term loans due January 2027 were refinanced to May 2033 with 703 of incremental term loans; the 2025 AAdvantage term loan margin was reduced; 8.50% secured notes of 1.0 billion and the 629 senior short-term term loan were repaid; 310 of EETC equipment notes were prepaid; and about 4.1 billion of EETC, equipment loan and other note proceeds were raised, including 2026-1, 2026-2 and engine EETCs. Of the 2026-2 EETC proceeds, about 633 remain in escrow and are not reported as American debt (Q3 page:19).
- Maturity profile (American principal): 631 in the remainder of 2026, 3,550 in 2027, 7,861 in 2028, 3,884 in 2029, 972 in 2030 and 8,677 from 2031 (Q3 page:65). The 2028 column is the largest single year and is more than twice the 2027 figure. The sources do not break the 2028 amount down by instrument; named instruments due in 2028 in Note 5 are the 2013 term loan (960, February), the 7.25% notes (750, February) and the 2021 AAdvantage term loan (2,247, April), which do not explain the whole column because installment and equipment debt also amortizes into 2028. Remainder of 2026 through 2028 principal totals 12,042 against available liquidity of 11,797.

### Credit read-through

- Earnings carry little cushion against debt service. LTM EBITDA of 2,891 on the income statement lines covers interest expense, net only 1.74 times, and operating income does not cover it. Fuel price, not volume, drove the Q3 decline; the fourth-quarter outlook assumes a higher fuel price than the third quarter (D3 page:6), and the full-year adjusted loss per share outlook was widened lower from the July outlook (D2 page:6; D3 page:6).
- Leverage on any basis in the sources is well above management's 3.0 times target: management's own net debt of 28,079 is 6.80 times the LTM EBITDAR built from income statement lines (CALC C13). Management's adjusted EBITDAR includes regional depreciation and rent and is not supplied for any period after Q1 2026, so this ratio is conservative in the sense of being lower on EBITDAR and should be read as indicative; the gap to the target is large under any plausible definition.
- Liquidity is the offset: 11.8 billion of liquidity, no revolver draws and more than 26 billion of unencumbered assets and first-lien borrowing capacity per management (D3 page:6). The management claim on unencumbered assets is unverified in the filings supplied.
- Refinancing risk is concentrated in 2028. The 2028 principal of 7,861 for American is the peak, and the secured nature of nearly all debt means asset-backed refinancing capacity (EETCs, equipment loans) is the main route, as 2026 activity shows.
- Structural points for the issuer: American holds 10,430 of related-party receivables from affiliates, which makes up a large part of its current assets (Q3 page:26), and the PSP notes of 3,746 sit at AAG, not American. American's own total debt and finance leases are 25,876 against 29,635 at AAG.
