## Findings

### Overall readiness answer

The seven issuer filings support the four selected consumers, with bounded limitations. No source has blocked preparation. Every source has extracted text (see Extraction status). The set covers the audited FY2025 base, three interim periods in 2026 and three matching earnings releases. The set contains no executed legal financing documents and no presentation decks. Debt terms therefore come only from the notes to the filed statements. The weakest areas are covenant definitions, guarantor detail and cash-paid interest and tax for interim periods. Those are gaps for CP-1 and CP-2 and do not stop either one. Source sufficiency is a separate matter from completion of downstream analysis. Nothing here says that CP-1, CP-1A, CP-2 or CP-5 has been done.

### Source-by-source assessment

#### Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025

Identity and period are established from content. The cover reads "For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025" and gives "Commission File Number 001-36794" for "The Chemours Company" (page:1). Original authority is the filed, audited annual report. Its debt note is "Note 20 – Debt" (cross-referenced from page:102) and holds facility-level terms, guarantee wording, redemption terms and the securitization and factoring facilities (page:211 to page:218). The scheduled-maturity discussion in Liquidity and Capital Resources is at page:106. The 10-K shows the Revolving Credit Facility terms: "$780 in revolving commitments that mature on May 2, 2030 and $220 in revolving commitments that mature on October 7, 2026" (page:211). It also states a springing-maturity feature and a revolver-only maintenance test, "a maximum senior secured net leverage ratio of 2.00 to 1.00" (page:211). Cash paid for interest and income taxes appears in the supplemental cash-flow information at page:163 ("Interest, net of amounts capitalized" and "Income taxes, net of refunds"). The converted text is a text rendering of HTML and carries no images or speaker notes.

#### Forms 10-Q for Q1, Q2 and Q3 2026

Each cover states its quarterly period (March 31, 2026; June 30, 2026; September 30, 2026; page:1 of each file). Each is unaudited and consolidated. The Q3 10-Q is the latest financial report and the primary authority for the September 30, 2026 balance sheet, debt table (Note 15, page:24), the liquidity discussion (page:148 to page:150) and segment note (Note 23, page:130 to page:134). The three 10-Qs are separate active sources and are not collapsed. The Q3 10-Q supersedes the earlier ones only for September 30, 2026 balances. The earlier 10-Qs remain the only filed support for the March and June 2026 balances and for discrete-quarter derivation.

#### Earnings releases (Q1, Q2, Q3 2026)

These are issuer-authored, unaudited and sit below the filings in authority. They provide reported and adjusted figures, a non-GAAP reconciliation, free cash flow, guidance and attributed management narrative. The Q3 release has a Net Leverage Ratio reconciliation and outlook (page:13, page:5 and page:6). Their non-GAAP measures must be carried as management-defined and must not override the filings. The Q3 release also states that "Certain prior period amounts have been revised to correct for certain immaterial errors as further described in our Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025" (Q3 release, page:2). Prior-period comparatives therefore carry a revision flag that CP-1A must resolve against the 10-K.

### Version relationships and conflicts

- Credit Agreement amendments are described, not supplied. They are Amendment No. 1 (November 29, 2024), No. 2 (December 13, 2024), No. 3 (May 2, 2025) and No. 4 (October 15, 2025). Each is a separate active instrument, and none of the documents is available to verify the notes.
- Revolver maturity timing: the Q3 10-Q states the "$220 in revolving commitments that matured on October 7, 2026" (page:25) and still lists "$953 million" revolver availability "as of September 30, 2026" (page:149). The commitment matured after the balance-sheet date and before the as-of date. This is a subsequent event relative to September 30, 2026. The filings do not say whether the $220 million tranche was extended or replaced. Availability after October 7, 2026 is therefore not established. Downstream use must keep the balance-sheet-date figure and the post-balance-sheet maturity apart. Severity high for CP-1 and CP-2.
- Total debt principal: the Q3 10-Q table shows "Total debt principal" of 3,872 at September 30, 2026 and 4,182 at December 31, 2025 (page:24). The Q3 release Net Leverage reconciliation shows 3,872 and, in the comparative column, 4,185 (Q3 release page:13). The comparative column is not December 31, 2025, so the two do not conflict, but the release and the 10-Q use different comparison dates. Period labels need care. Severity low.
- Depreciation and amortization: the Q3 release cash flow statement shows 259 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 (page:10), while the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation shows 236 for the same nine months (page:13). The release footnote on the twelve months ended September 30, 2025 attributes accelerated depreciation to the restructuring caption (page:16). This is a labelled presentation difference and is not resolved here. It is flagged for CP-1A and CP-2.
- Liquidity: the Q3 release gives "Total liquidity was $1.7 billion" built from unrestricted cash and revolver capacity (page:4). The 10-Q gives its components at page:149. The release rounds, so the 10-Q controls the components.
- Prior-period revision: see the earnings release finding above. The scale of the revision is not stated in the Q3 release text. Locate it in the 10-K before using any 2025 comparative.
- Management claims such as "Net Leverage ratio of approximately 4.3x" (Q3 release, page:4) are management-defined non-GAAP and attributed. They do not override the filings. The leverage definition uses unrestricted cash only and Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes litigation and environmental items.

### Read-through

Evidence: the Q3 10-Q debt note and liquidity discussion give facility-level balances, covenant-compliance language and a revolver whose $220 million tranche "matured on October 7, 2026". Source risk mechanism: filing notes summarise rather than reproduce the executed agreements, and the filing's own balance-sheet-date availability may overstate availability on the as-of date. Downstream consequence: CP-1 and CP-2 can describe and measure the capital structure from the filings but must state that revolver availability after October 7, 2026, covenant headroom and guarantor identity are not established. The next evidence action is to obtain the Credit Agreement with Amendments No. 1 to No. 4 and the indentures, and to look for any filing after September 30, 2026 that addresses the matured revolver tranche.
