### Interpretation of what the evidence supports

- Leverage: management net leverage fell during 2026 as debt was repaid with proceeds of the Kuan Yin land sales, but it remains high and the year-end target was moved up in successive releases. The full-year Adjusted EBITDA range was cut twice and the leverage target moved from below 3.8x to below 4.2x (Q1 ER, Q2 ER and Q3 ER outlook pages above). The calculation bridge recomputes the ratio from filed debt and the stated LTM Adjusted EBITDA.
- Earnings quality: Q3 2026 reported net income of 196 was helped by a 150 receipt under the PFAS Insurance MOU booked as a reduction to SG&A (Q3 10-Q page:2) and by a 50 gain on land sales (Q3 10-Q page:133). Management excludes both from Adjusted EBITDA through the litigation-related income and gain lines (Q3 ER page:13). Adjusted EBITDA of 200 therefore rests on operating pricing in Titanium Technologies and Advanced Performance Materials, offset by a decline in Thermal & Specialized Solutions Adjusted EBITDA of 58 year over year (Q3 ER page:11). The 150 receipt is restricted cash held for New Jersey settlement payments (Q3 10-Q page:155), so it is not free liquidity.
- Cash flow: nine-month cash from operations of 263 includes the 150 receipt and about 100 of related settlement payments (Q3 ER page:17), and land-sale tax payments of about 25 in Q3 (Q3 ER page:5). Free cash flow of 119 for nine months is management-defined. Cash taxes and cash interest for 2026 interim periods are not supplied.
- Liquidity: unrestricted cash and revolver availability reported at September 30, 2026 are 698 and 953 (Q3 10-Q page:149). A 220 commitment tranche matured on October 7, 2026, after the balance-sheet date and before the as-of date, and the supplied text does not say what availability is after it. Management states sources of liquidity are sufficient "through at least the end of November 2027" (Q3 10-Q page:156).
- Maturity profile: no maturity falls before 2028 in the Q3 debt table other than small amortization and finance items. The euro term loan (153) and 2028 notes (595) mature in 2028, the 2029 notes (620) in 2029. The 10-K disclosed the revolver springing-maturity feature that keys on those dates (Q3 10-Q page:25). The 10-K scheduled maturities at December 31, 2025 were 11, 505, 1,282, 631 and 10 for 2026 to 2030 (10-K page:106); the 505 and part of the 2028 amount have since been redeemed or repaid.
- Contingent and environmental obligations outside debt: accrued litigation of 553 (191 current) and environmental remediation of 805 (116 current) at September 30, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:155 and page:153). The North Carolina settlement totals 455 over 15 years, with a first payment of 51 in October 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:116). The remaining New Jersey payments are 176 on a net present value basis after the first 100 payment, and 150 of restricted cash is held to fund them (page:155). These are outside management's net debt measure.
- Ratings: Moody's downgraded to B1 from Ba3 on July 17, 2026 with a stable outlook; the S&P statement in the filing is a BB- affirmation with negative outlook dated April 16, 2025 (Q3 10-Q page:149). A newer S&P action is not in the sources.
- Capital returns: a quarterly dividend of 0.0875 per share was declared on October 27, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:156). Dividends paid were 39 in nine months 2026 against 63 in nine months 2025 (Q3 10-Q page:5).
