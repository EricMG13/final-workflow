## Findings

### Credit view

Chemours is a stretched but liquid credit: near-term debt service looks manageable, while leverage and legacy obligations are the weak points. Its strongest support is USD 698 million of unrestricted cash, no revolver borrowings, no bond or term loan maturity of size before 2028 and a TiO2 price recovery that lifted third-quarter 2026 Adjusted EBITDA to USD 200 million. Its weakest point is management net leverage of about 4.3x, which leaves out USD 1,358 million of litigation and environmental reserves, with LTM interest cover of about 2.7x. The outcome turns on whether TiO2 pricing holds, whether the cooling aftermarket stabilises, and how the 2028 and 2029 refinancing meets settlement payments. Confidence is Medium.

### Key findings

The refinancing in March 2026 changed the shape of the debt. The company issued USD 700 million of 7.875% senior notes due 2034 and used the proceeds with cash to retire the notes due 2027 and part of the notes due 2028, so the next material maturities are the 2028 euro term loan and notes and the 2029 notes, together about USD 1,368 million, or about 35% of USD 3,872 million of debt principal [CP-1 debt table; Calculations]. This removes a near-term wall that the 10-K had flagged as unfunded absent refinancing, at the price of a higher stated coupon on the replacement paper than on the notes retired.

Leverage fell during 2026 but remains high, and management's own targets slipped. Management net leverage was about 4.9x at March 31, about 4.4x at June 30 and about 4.3x at September 30, 2026, driven by debt repaid from Kuan Yin land-sale proceeds rather than by earnings growth [CP-1 quarterly trajectory]. Over the same releases the full-year Adjusted EBITDA range was cut from USD 800 to 900 million to USD 775 to 800 million and the year-end leverage target moved from below 3.8x to below 4.2x. A creditor should treat the improvement as asset-sale deleveraging, which cannot repeat once the land is sold.

Earnings quality is weaker than the headline. Third-quarter 2026 net income of USD 196 million includes a USD 150 million insurance-related receipt booked against SG&A and a USD 50 million land gain, and full-year GAAP results include a USD 307 million nine-month tax provision after a full valuation allowance [CP-1 financials table]. Adjusted EBITDA of USD 200 million excludes these items, but it also excludes litigation and environmental charges that consume cash over 15 to 25 years. Segment mix has shifted: Titanium Technologies Adjusted EBITDA rose to USD 85 million from USD 25 million on price, while Thermal and Specialized Solutions fell to USD 136 million from USD 194 million as the stationary aftermarket normalised after prior-year channel fill [CP-1 segment table].

Legacy obligations sit outside the leverage ratio and compete with debt service. Accrued litigation of USD 553 million and environmental remediation of USD 805 million at September 30, 2026 are not debt, yet the New Jersey order (USD 875 million over 25 years) and the North Carolina settlement (USD 455 million over 15 years, first payment of USD 51 million in October 2026) require annual cash [CP-1A transaction summary]. Part is funded by a USD 150 million insurance escrow and shared with DuPont and Corteva under the 2021 MOU, up to a cap. Including the reserves in debt on an illustrative basis raises leverage to about 6.1x (Calculations); that is a sensitivity, not a measure management or any covenant uses.

Liquidity is adequate on the balance-sheet date but its second leg is uncertain. Unrestricted cash of USD 698 million plus revolver availability of USD 953 million gave USD 1,651 million at September 30, 2026. A USD 220 million revolving tranche matured on October 7, 2026, and the filings do not say whether it was extended. If it was not, arithmetic availability would be USD 733 million. The revolver also carries a springing maturity linked to the 2028 and 2029 debt, so access to the revolver depends on that refinancing being completed in advance.

Cash generation covers interest but not by a wide margin. LTM free cash flow is about USD 211 million (Calculations) against USD 274 million of LTM net interest expense, with FCF flattered by the net USD 50 million of insurance escrow receipt over related settlement payments inside operating cash flow. Management's own full-year free cash flow guide is USD 120 to 160 million, and after dividends of roughly USD 50 million per year the margin for settlement payments and voluntary debt reduction is narrow.

Ratings and market signals confirm the pressure. Moody's downgraded to B1 from Ba3 on July 17, 2026 with a stable outlook; the only S&P statement in the sources is a stale April 16, 2025 affirmation of BB- with negative outlook. The sources contain no market quotes or spreads, so no market-implied view is offered.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Liquidity is solid at the reporting date: USD 698 million of unrestricted cash, no revolver borrowings and USD 953 million of availability, with management stating that sources are sufficient through at least the end of November 2027 [Management Language, CP-1 liquidity bridge]. The maturity profile has been extended: the senior secured term loan in dollars is due in October 2032 and no bond matures before November 2028 [Documentary Fact]. Customer concentration is low, with no customer above 10% of net sales and about 2,400 customers across about 110 countries [Documentary Fact, CP-1A]. Demand drivers are partly regulatory (AIM Act and F-Gas transitions to Opteon) and partly price-led recovery in TiO2, with growth in Performance Solutions serving data center and semiconductor markets [Documentary Fact for the regulation, Management Language for growth claims]. The company has acted creditor-supportively on capital allocation, cutting the dividend and repaying debt from asset sales [Documentary Fact, CP-1A].

Weaknesses. Leverage is high on any basis shown and the improvement came from asset-sale proceeds [Analyst Interpretation, CP-1 bridges]. Legacy PFAS and environmental liabilities are large, multi-decade and uncertain in scale, and only part is cost-shared [Documentary Fact]. Earnings are concentrated in a cyclical product (TiO2) and a regulation-timed product (Opteon aftermarket) whose year-over-year trend turned negative in the third quarter [Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation]. Reported GAAP results are volatile because of litigation, valuation allowance and gain items, which complicates covenant and add-back reading [Documentary Fact]. The financing documents are not in the pack, so guarantor coverage, restricted-payment capacity and covenant headroom cannot be verified [Insufficient Information]. A history of restated prior periods and a past Audit Committee review raise the weight a creditor should place on reconciling adjusted measures to filings [Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation].
