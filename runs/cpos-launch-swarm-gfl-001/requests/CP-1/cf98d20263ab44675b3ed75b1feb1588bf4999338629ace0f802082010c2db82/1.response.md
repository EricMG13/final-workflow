- Revolving Credit Agreement financial maintenance covenants: "Total Net Funded Debt to Adjusted EBITDA" at most 5.00 to 1.00 "for a period of four complete fiscal quarters following completion of a Material Acquisition" and otherwise at most 4.50 to 1.00; "Interest Coverage Ratio must be equal to or greater than 3.00 to 1.00". Company states it "was in compliance with these covenants" at September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025 (Q3 statements page:12, page:13). No ratio values are printed.
- Management Net Leverage (Q3 release page:14): 3.8x at September 30, 2026 versus 3.4x at December 31, 2025, using Run-Rate EBITDA of 2,988.0, which is trailing twelve months Adjusted EBITDA of 2,257.1 plus Run-Rate EBITDA Adjustments of 730.9 (management estimates). ### Interpretation (evidence-based, no conclusion on creditworthiness)

- Leverage rose with the SECURE closing and the new financing. Reported debt before adjustments grew from 7,426.7 to 11,696.4 while the management leverage ratio moved from 3.4x to 3.8x, because the ratio's denominator includes 730.9 of management run-rate adjustments. On trailing reported Adjusted EBITDA alone, net debt to Adjusted EBITDA is about 5.05x (calculation below), which sits above the 4.50x general covenant level but is not a covenant calculation (covenant definitions are not supplied; the 5.00x step-up applies for four quarters after a Material Acquisition, and whether SECURE qualifies is not stated).
- Net loss from continuing operations in the nine months is driven by items below Adjusted EBITDA: foreign exchange loss 196.7, interest and other finance costs 482.9, and an equity-method loss, against Adjusted EBITDA of 1,748.4 (Q3 statements page:1).
- Cash generation: operating cash flow less purchase of property and equipment is thin for nine months 2026 (calculation below), and management's Adjusted Free Cash Flow of 484.9 depends on add-backs and a separate "incremental growth investments" addition of 182.4 (Q3 release page:18).
- Reported Q3 revenue growth of 27.2% includes the one month of SECURE and other acquisitions; the release states organic growth of 7.4%.
