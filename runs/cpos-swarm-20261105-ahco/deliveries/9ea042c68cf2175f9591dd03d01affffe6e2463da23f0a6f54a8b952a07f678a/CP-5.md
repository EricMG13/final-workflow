### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Each material claim in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis handoffs was matched to a located source span or to a recomputation from located figures, and the three handoffs agree on every shared figure, period and perimeter label. The credit view of the synthesis, cautious with Medium confidence, follows from the cited basis and is not overstated. Two claims are only partly supported, both as wording rather than figures: that acquisitions and tax receivable agreement payments were funded by borrowing, which the sources support arithmetically but do not state, and operating cash flow and free cash flow quoted in the synthesis without the label that they include discontinued operations. No claim is unsupported and no contradiction between modules was found. The integrity verdict for the evaluated set is nonetheless Restricted, not Committee Ready, because the conclusions that matter most for a creditor, namely covenant headroom, structural priority and trailing leverage on the current business, rest on documents and a recast annual base that are not in the sources. The review itself is limited: it was done in the same session as the analysis, from the same extraction text, and the page locators are extraction chunks rather than printed pages.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious view is that operating cash flow remains large at 395,613 for nine months 2026, the quarterly Adjusted EBITDA margin rose each quarter of 2026 to 19.0%, management says the capitated contract is at run-rate, and the pending sale for 235.0 million equals about 12.4% of principal debt. If equipment spending is growth spending that falls after the ramp, free cash flow would recover quickly. What would make that case win: a fourth quarter free cash flow inside management's guidance (implying 47,368 to 72,368), a Diabetes Health closing in the first quarter of 2027 with proceeds applied to debt, and a margin at or above Q3 2026 in the following quarters. Downside chain with observable triggers: higher utilisation or labour cost in the capitated contract (first break: contract margin) leads to lower Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow (transmission), then revolver drawings above the 137.0 million outstanding in October 2026 and less liquidity than 349,387, then a harder refinancing of the 2029 notes before the springing date of about 2029-05-02. Triggers to watch: quarterly margin, revolver balance, sale closing, covenant certificates when obtainable.
