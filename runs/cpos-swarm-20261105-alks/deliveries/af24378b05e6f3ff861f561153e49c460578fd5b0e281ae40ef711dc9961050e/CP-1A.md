### Business and transaction view

Alkermes plc is an Irish-incorporated, Nasdaq-listed neuroscience company with U.S. commercial operations. Before 2026 its earnings came from three proprietary U.S. products (VIVITROL, ARISTADA and ARISTADA INITIO, LYBALVI) and from royalty and manufacturing revenue on long-acting antipsychotics sold by Janssen and on VUMERITY sold by Biogen. The Avadel Pharmaceuticals plc acquisition, completed on February 12, 2026, added LUMRYZ and its commercial organisation, was financed by senior secured term loans and balance sheet cash, and created a contingent value right that can add a further cash payment. The transaction is the dominant credit event in the pack: it moved the company from no debt at December 31, 2025 to senior secured term loans, it brought acquired intangibles and goodwill that make up most of the purchase price, and it changed the revenue mix toward a product distributed through a closed pharmacy network. The creditor-relevant business risks are concentration in a few products and three wholesalers, expected VIVITROL generic entry in 2027, patent challenges against LYBALVI and LUMRYZ, government rebate exposure, and reliance on the pipeline (alixorexton, ALKS 7290) for growth that the debt service does not depend on today. Mitigants documented in the pack are that ARISTADA patents run to 2039 and LYBALVI patents to 2041, that LUMRYZ has orphan drug exclusivity through May 1, 2030 for adults, that no dividends have been paid, and that the company reports cash and investments of 787.6 US$m at September 30, 2026 (from the CP-1 handoff). Ownership and board composition are Not supplied, so sponsor and control analysis is limited to documented corporate actions.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The Avadel Acquisition is the transaction for this run. In October 2025 the company and Avadel "entered into a definitive transaction agreement, subsequently amended in November 2025" (10-K_2025-12-31_alks-20251231.htm.jsonl page:10). The consideration is $21.00 per Avadel share in cash at closing plus a non-transferable contingent value right of $1.50 per share payable on a specified milestone (page:10). The acquisition closed on February 12, 2026 (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl page:15). Preliminary purchase consideration was 2,306.9 US$m, being 2,064.3 US$m for Avadel shares, 134.9 US$m cash settlement of equity awards and 107.7 US$m fair value of the CVR, so cash paid at closing was 2,199.2 US$m (page:16). The potential CVR payment totals $165.7 million (page:53), and management expects the milestone to be determined by the end of 2027 (page:53); the probability of success was raised from 75% to 90% after positive phase 3 results for LUMRYZ in idiopathic hypersomnia (page:24). The type is a strategic acquisition by a listed strategic buyer; there is no sponsor.

Financing and liquidity effect (CP-1 handoff figures). Before closing the company arranged a senior secured bridge facility of up to approximately $1.5 billion in November 2025 and placed approximately $731.2 million of its cash in escrow (10-K page:193). At closing it entered the credit agreement and borrowed the full $1.525 billion available under two term loans, which replaced the bridge. After the August 12, 2026 amendment the facilities carried 1,497.7 US$m (carrying) at September 30, 2026, Term Loan A due February 12, 2031 and Term Loan B due August 12, 2031, with margins of 1.75% to 2.25% and 2.25% over Term SOFR. No bond or note issuance appears in the debt note; the new debt is bank term debt only. Liquidity at September 30, 2026 was cash and investments of 787.6 US$m with no revolving facility found in the sources. The deal moved leverage from nil at December 31, 2025 to 3.40 times LTM Adjusted EBITDA (management measure with a derived FY2025 component), per the CP-1 handoff.

Use of proceeds and status. The term loan proceeds and the escrowed cash funded the cash consideration; the cash consideration of 2,199.2 US$m less net debt proceeds of 1,511.6 US$m leaves 687.7 US$m from cash, below the escrow, and the sources do not state what the remaining escrow was used for (this is arithmetic, not a sources and uses table). The purchase price allocation is preliminary and may be adjusted for up to one year from closing (page:16). Goodwill of 509.5 US$m and acquired intangible assets of 1,794.9 US$m were recognised at closing, so identifiable intangibles are most of the consideration, and if LUMRYZ underperforms the carrying values are exposed to impairment (Analyst Interpretation; no impairment is reported). The company states it cannot present Avadel's separate net income because the businesses "do not operate as discrete entities within the Company" after integration (page:19).

Executed documents are listed as exhibits but not supplied: the Transaction Agreement and its amendment, the Credit Agreement and Amendment No. 1 (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl page:58; 10-Q_2026-03-31_alks-20260331.htm.jsonl page:50). Terms such as the CVR milestone definition, covenant levels and guarantor names are therefore Not supplied. Subsequent to the balance sheet date and before the cutoff, in October 2026 the company entered into two interest rate collars with a total notional of 750.0 US$m (CP-1 handoff; 10-Q page:30).
