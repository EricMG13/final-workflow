### Financial base view

The financial base is adequate for a credit snapshot of the post-acquisition Alkermes, with the limits stated here. Before February 12, 2026 the company was unlevered: no debt at December 31, 2025, 388.6 US$m of cash and equivalents, 199.8 US$m of investments and 731.2 US$m of restricted cash held in escrow for the Avadel acquisition. The acquisition cost 2,199.2 US$m in cash plus a contingent value right, funded by 1,525.0 US$m of term loans (net proceeds 1,511.6 US$m) and balance sheet cash. At September 30, 2026 the balance sheet carries 1,497.7 US$m of term loan debt (net of 14.0 US$m of discount and fees), 787.6 US$m of cash and investments and no restricted cash. LTM revenue is 1,753.6 US$m against 1,475.9 US$m in FY2025, mostly from the addition of LUMRYZ. GAAP profit is near zero over the LTM because of inventory step-up amortization, acquisition costs, intangible amortization, a contingent consideration fair value charge and 86.6 US$m of interest expense, and the management measures are the main bridge to leverage. On the management Adjusted EBITDA basis, total debt is 3.40 times LTM and net debt 1.61 times; on the company-definition reported EBITDA the same ratios are 10.25 and 4.86 times. The covenant package, the margin grid and any revolver are not readable from the pack, so headroom, undrawn liquidity and cash coverage are Not supplied. Debt service is modest through 2030 (scheduled amortization of 6.6 to 45.3 US$m a year) with nearly all principal due in 2031, which places the refinancing test in 2031.

EBITDA definitions. Reported EBITDA is the company measure: "EBITDA represents earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization." (EX-99.1_2026-11-03_alks-ex99_1.htm.jsonl page:3), reconciled from net income by deducting interest income and adding interest expense, tax, depreciation and amortization of acquired intangibles (page:6). Adjusted EBITDA is management's measure: "Adjusted EBITDA excludes share-based compensation expense and non-recurring gains or losses in addition to the components of EBITDA from earnings." (page:3). Total debt in this handoff is the balance sheet carrying amount of the term loans (current portion plus long-term debt); net debt deducts cash and equivalents plus short-term and long-term investments, which are not restricted. Operating leases, the contingent consideration and the litigation matters are outside the debt figure.

### Debt and capital structure

At September 30, 2026 the debt consists of two senior secured term loans under the February 12, 2026 credit agreement, as amended by Amendment No. 1 on August 12, 2026: Term Loan A Facility due February 12, 2031 at a carrying amount of 735.0 US$m and Term Loan B Facility due August 12, 2031 at 762.7 US$m, total 1,497.7 US$m, of which 26.5 US$m is current (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl pages:6 and 27). Scheduled principal is 1,511.75 US$m, and the company states "At September 30, 2026, our borrowings consisted of $1.511 billion outstanding under the Facilities." (page:55). Unamortized discount and fees are "$3.6 million and $10.4 million, respectively" (page:30). The facilities were drawn in full at closing at 1,525.0 US$m: "the Company borrowed the full $1.525 billion available under the Facilities" (10-K_2025-12-31_alks-20251231.htm.jsonl page:193). Pricing after the amendment is Term SOFR plus 1.75% to 2.25% on Term Loan A, set by a Secured Net Leverage Ratio grid that is not supplied, and Term SOFR plus 2.25% on Term Loan B; the amendment cut the spread by 0.75% on Term Loan A and 0.50% on Term Loan B (page:28). The original margin was 2.75% on both (10-Q_2026-03-31_alks-20260331.htm.jsonl page:23). Three-month SOFR was 4.07% at September 30, 2026, and the company estimates that a 10% rise in the rate would raise interest from October 1 to December 31, 2026 by $1.6 million (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl page:55).

The Facilities are "guaranteed by subsidiary guarantors and secured by a lien on substantially all of the assets of the borrowers and the subsidiary guarantors" (page:28). All debt therefore ranks as senior secured with no unsecured or subordinated tranche; no other debt instrument appears in the debt note. The amendment was accounted for lender by lender, with three exiting Term Loan B lenders treated as extinguished and one new lender as new issuance (page:29), with a 0.5 US$m write-off of deferred costs and 2.6 US$m of arranger fees in Q3 interest expense.

Maturity wall (scheduled principal, US$m, from the table at page:29): remaining 2026 6.625; 2027 26.500; 2028 40.563; 2029 45.250; 2030 45.250; thereafter 1,347.562, total 1,511.750. Both facilities mature in 2031, so the thereafter amount falls in 2031 (inferred from the stated maturity dates). The wall is back-ended: the 2031 maturities represent most of the principal, and Term Loan B matures on August 12, 2031, six months after Term Loan A.

Hedging. In October 2026, after the balance sheet date and before the cutoff, "the Company entered into two zero-cost interest rate collar agreements, each with a notional amount of $375.0 million" with a Term SOFR cap of 5.25% and floors of 4.25% and 4.30%, maturing February 2029 and February 2030 (page:30). The notional total of 750.0 US$m covers about half of the principal.

Claims outside the debt figure. (1) Operating leases: lease liabilities of 7.6 US$m current and 65.8 US$m long-term at September 30, 2026 (page:6); all leases are operating leases (10-K_2025-12-31_alks-20251231.htm.jsonl page:192) and the 10-Q reports "Cash paid for lease liabilities was $2.9 million and $8.6 million during the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026" (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl page:27). (2) Contingent consideration: the CVR entitles former Avadel holders to "a potential additional aggregate cash payment of $165.7 million, or $1.50 per Avadel Share, upon achievement of the CVR Milestone" (page:53), carried at 136.9 US$m on the balance sheet (page:6) against 107.7 US$m at the acquisition date (page:16); management expects the milestone to be determined by the end of 2027 (page:53) and raised the probability of success from 75% to 90% after positive LUMRYZ phase 3 results in idiopathic hypersomnia (page:24). (3) Capital commitments: open purchase orders were $17.7 million at December 31, 2025 (10-K_2025-12-31_alks-20251231.htm.jsonl page:212); the 10-Q does not update this. (4) Litigation: the company states no probable material losses at September 30, 2026 and that no range can be estimated for reasonably possible claims (10-Q_2026-09-30_alks-20260930.htm.jsonl page:34); the Acorda matter, a petition seeking about $66.0 million of additional damages, was decided in the company's favour by the Second Circuit on July 15, 2026 and Acorda filed a certiorari petition on October 13, 2026 (page:36). (5) Pensions, factoring and supply chain finance: no pension, factoring or supply chain finance arrangement was found in the 10-K or the September 10-Q text (searches returned no match), so these are Not supplied rather than nil.

### Leverage and coverage

Leverage. On total debt of 1,497.7 US$m (carrying) and LTM Adjusted EBITDA of 440.2 US$m (derived, see below), total debt to EBITDA is 3.40 times; net debt of 710.2 US$m gives 1.61 times. On principal of 1,511.75 US$m the ratios are 3.43 and 1.65 times. On LTM reported EBITDA of 146.2 US$m the ratios are 10.25 and 4.86 times. The gap between the bases is explained by add-backs of acquisition-related costs, share-based compensation and the inventory step-up that flows through cost of goods sold; the step-up is non-cash and, as an analyst inference, should roll off as the acquired inventory is sold, whereas share-based compensation is a recurring cost. At December 31, 2025 there was no debt; net debt was negative 588.4 US$m (cash 388.6 plus investments 199.8), and 731.2 US$m of restricted cash is excluded from that figure.

Coverage. Cash paid for interest is not presented for any 2026 period (the supplemental cash flow disclosure shows taxes only), and the 10-K shows cash paid for interest of nil in FY2025, so EBITDA to cash interest is Not supplied. On an expense basis, Q3 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of 120.1 US$m covers interest expense of 27.6 US$m 4.36 times, nine months 4.57 times, and LTM 5.08 times; reported EBITDA covers interest expense 2.29 times in Q3 2026 and 1.69 times over the LTM. Interest expense includes non-cash and one-off items: 7.4 US$m of bridge facility financing costs in the nine months, 2.6 US$m of arranger fees and 0.5 US$m of deferred cost write-off in Q3, and amortization of discount and fees of 0.6 US$m in Q3. An analyst estimate that removes the Q3 items gives about 23.9 US$m for the quarter, an annualised 95.4 US$m, but this is an estimate, not a reported figure.
