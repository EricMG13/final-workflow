### Credit view

Allison Transmission Holdings is a solid mid-leverage industrial credit that is still working off acquisition debt. Its strongest support is a high-margin transmission franchise with long-term customer agreements, liquidity of USD 1,493 million with the revolver undrawn, and a first lien leverage ratio of 0.84x against a 5.50x limit. Its weakest point is leverage: total debt of USD 4,037 million is 2.82x trailing Adjusted EBITDA, a figure that mixes periods with and without the lower-margin off-highway business and cannot be restated on a combined basis. The outcome will be decided by how quickly operating cash flow retires debt while dividends and buybacks continue, by how the acquired business performs through a downturn, and by what the unseen credit agreement permits.
