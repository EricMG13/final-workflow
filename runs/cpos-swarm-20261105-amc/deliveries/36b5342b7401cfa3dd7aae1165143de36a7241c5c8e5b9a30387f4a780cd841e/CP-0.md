### Version and basis relationships

1. Adjusted EBITDA definition. S4 page:27 states "During the first quarter of 2026, the Company changed its definition of Adjusted EBITDA to adjust for net periodic pension cost." and "All comparative period information for Adjusted EBITDA has been re-cast to conform with the current definition." The recast covers the 2025 interim periods shown in S4. S1 page:77 reports FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA under the earlier definition and no recast FY2025 figure was located in the supplied set. S7 page:11 repeats the change. Consequence: a trailing twelve-month figure that adds FY2025, nine months of 2026 and nine months of 2025 spans two definitions; the size of the effect on FY2025 is not supplied.
2. Debt table versions. The December 31, 2025 table (S1 page:142), the March 31 and June 30, 2026 tables (S2 page:11-12, S3 page:11-12) and the September 30, 2026 table (S4 page:12) are successive dated views, not duplicates; each controls only its own date. S3 page:12 notes in footnote 1 that the 2026 line "Includes $125.5 million for the 6.125% Senior Subordinated Notes due 2027", which S4 page:45 reports as redeemed on July 24, 2026.
3. Post-quarter structure. S4 page:38 prints a second maturity table "after giving effect to the 2026 Refinancing Transactions". It is a pro forma view beside the September 30, 2026 table, not a replacement for it.
4. Instrument aliases inside the debt note. The September 30, 2026 table lists "Credit Agreement-Term Loans due 2029", while S4 page:84 refers to "we repaid all of the outstanding Muvico Term Loans". S3 page:68 defines the Muvico Term Loans as the new term loans maturing in 2029 under the Muvico Credit Agreement, so the two names refer to one facility on the filings' own wording. The same filings call the notes issued by Muvico "New 2029 Notes" in text and "Senior Secured Notes due 2029" in the table, and the "Existing 7.5% Notes" appear in the table as "7.5% First Lien Notes due 2029". Downstream modules must keep one name per instrument and cite both.
5. Entity roles. Holdings is the registrant and, in the 2026 refinancing, the borrower of the new first and second lien term loans and issuer of the new notes (S4 page:33-34). Muvico, LLC and Centertainment Development, LLC form the Muvico Group, described in S3 page:67 as "are unrestricted subsidiaries under the indenture governing Holdings". Odeon Finco is an indirect subsidiary that borrowed the Odeon term loans and was guaranteed by Holdings "on a standalone and unsecured basis" (S2 page:27). After October 5, 2026 the collateral groups are described as the AMC Collateral, the Centertainment Collateral and the Odeon group (S4 page:37).
6. Authority order. Filed statements and notes (S1 to S4) control facts; releases (S5 to S7) are attributed management claims. No conflict between a release and a filing was found in the locators checked. The one claim with no filing counterpart is the "approximately 97%" refinancing share in S7 page:4.

### Debt, liquidity and covenant evidence located

- Debt detail: the Q3 2026 table lists each instrument with carrying and principal amounts (S4 page:12: "Total principal amount of corporate borrowings"), and the maturity schedule by year as of September 30, 2026 sits beside it on the same chunk. The refinancing terms in Note 11 give issuer, rate, maturity, security and guarantor language: for the new first lien notes S4 page:33 "The Company issued $2,000.0 million aggregate principal amount of 8.875% First Lien Notes due 2031"; for the new first lien term loans S4 page:36 "initial aggregate principal amount of $850.0 million, which mature on October 5, 2031"; for the new second lien term loans S4 page:34 "borrowed $1,120.0 million of new second lien term loans".
- Subsequent-event financial effect: S4 page:38 "The Company estimates that it will record a loss on extinguishment of approximately $182.7 million".
- Liquidity statement: S4 page:6 "The Company believes its existing cash and cash equivalents, together with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to fund its operations and satisfy its obligations currently and through the next twelve months." Cash and restricted cash are separate balance-sheet lines (S4 page:3) and restricted cash is described as cash held as landlord guarantees and cash-collateralized letters of credit (S4 page:7).
- Facilities: a keyword search of all seven sources for "revolv", "undrawn", "credit facility", "borrowing base", "availability under" and "letters of credit" found only the 2013 revolving facility, described in the past tense with a stated maturity of April 22, 2024 (S1 page:161: "maturing April 22, 2024") and the restricted-cash letters of credit. No current revolving or other committed undrawn facility is described.
- Covenants: each reporting date carries a compliance statement, for example S4 page:17 "the Company believes that it was in full compliance with all agreements, including related covenants, governing its outstanding debt." and S1 page:165 for December 31, 2025. The covenant summaries in the notes describe restrictions on debt, liens, restricted payments, investments, asset sales, mergers and affiliate transactions (S4 page:35-36). A keyword search of all seven sources for "financial covenant", "coverage ratio", "springing" and "minimum liquidity" found nothing; "fixed charge" appears only as English-law security over Odeon assets (S2 page:28). The only leverage test found is the Total Leverage Ratio that set the interest rate on the New 2029 Notes (S1 page:146 and S4 page:84), which were redeemed on October 5, 2026 (S4 page:38).
