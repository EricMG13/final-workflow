### Financial base view

AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS, INC. enters the cutoff with sharply better reported earnings, a refinanced and longer-dated but larger debt stack, and cash as its only stated source of liquidity. Adjusted EBITDA for the twelve months to September 30, 2026 is 840.4, built from FY2025 (387.5), nine months of 2026 (707.2) less nine months of 2025 (254.3); against principal debt of 3,782.6 that is 4.50x, and 3.61x net of unrestricted cash of 751.9, down from 10.39x and 9.28x at FY2025. Cash interest paid over the same twelve months was 392.3, so Adjusted EBITDA covered it 2.14x, against 0.95x in FY2025. Free cash flow turned positive on the trailing basis at 168.0 after a deficit of 365.9 in FY2025. Most of the improvement comes from the earnings side: principal fell from 4,024.2 to 3,782.6 while Adjusted EBITDA rose from 387.5 to 840.4. These ratios are the clearest evidence of recovery and rest on filed statements, but they carry three qualifications. First, the trailing figure mixes the pre-2026 and the 2026 Adjusted EBITDA definitions (the effect on FY2025 is not supplied). Second, the 2026 operating cash flow is seasonal: the first quarter used 128.5, the second quarter provided 235.4 and the third 176.6. Third, no revolving or other committed undrawn facility is described anywhere in the sources, so liquidity is cash of 751.9 and no more; the working capital deficit excluding restricted cash was still 792.8. On October 5, 2026, after the quarter end, the company issued 2,000.0 of 8.875% first lien notes due 2031, borrowed 850.0 of first lien term loans due 2031 and 1,120.0 of 11.25% second lien term loans due 2033, and repaid or discharged the Muvico and Odeon term loans, the New 2029 Notes and the Existing 7.5% Notes. Pro forma principal is 4,086.1 (4.86x on trailing Adjusted EBITDA); before 2030 only 8.5 a year of first lien term loan amortization falls due, and 79.2% of principal is fixed rate. The stated coupons on the two new fixed-rate instruments alone come to 303.5 a year, and the floating margin on the first lien term loan is not printed, so pro forma interest cover cannot be computed from the sources. Operating lease liabilities of 3,761.9 and the 454.3 exhibitor services agreement liability sit outside the debt figure and are fixed claims on the same cash flow. Covenant headroom cannot be assessed: the filings give compliance statements but no covenant levels.
