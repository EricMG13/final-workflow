### Credit view

AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS, INC. is a recovering but thinly cushioned credit: earnings have rebounded sharply, yet its fixed charges leave little margin if film attendance weakens. The strongest support is trailing Adjusted EBITDA of USD 840.4 million, which brings total debt to 4.50x, cash interest cover to 2.14x and free cash flow to USD 168.0 million, while cash of USD 751.9 million and the October 2026 refinancing push the main maturities to 2031 and 2033. The weakest point is that liquidity is cash alone, with no committed facility described, and fixed-charge cover including rent is only about 1.35x. What decides the outcome is whether attendance and the studio film slate hold near 2026 levels.

### Key findings

Deleveraging is real but earnings-led. AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS, INC. reports principal debt of USD 3,782.6 million at September 30, 2026 against USD 4,024.2 million at December 31, 2025, while trailing Adjusted EBITDA is USD 840.4 million against USD 387.5 million for FY2025. Total debt to Adjusted EBITDA is therefore 4.50x, down from 10.39x, and 3.61x net of USD 751.9 million of cash. Most of the improvement comes from the earnings side, which means leverage will move back up quickly if earnings fall (Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation). The trailing figure combines FY2025 on the earlier Adjusted EBITDA definition with 2026 on the revised one, a difference worth about USD 0.9 million on the nine-month comparison and unquantified for FY2025.

Cash interest cover is adequate on trailing earnings but was below 1.0x a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA covered cash interest paid 2.14x over the twelve months to September 30, 2026, against 0.95x in FY2025 and 0.86x in FY2024. A cover that moved from below to above 1.0x between FY2025 and the twelve months to September 30, 2026 shows how exposed debt service is to the earnings level.

Fixed charges beyond debt are the main cushion issue. Operating lease liabilities of USD 3,761.9 million sit outside reported debt, and cash paid for operating leases was USD 704.7 million in nine months 2026. Adjusted EBITDA plus rent covered cash interest plus rent about 1.35x on trailing figures against 0.99x in FY2025 (Analyst Interpretation from the financial base). Rent does not flex with attendance, so the cushion falls quickly in a weak year.

Liquidity is cash, and cash is the only stated backstop. Cash was USD 751.9 million at September 30, 2026, up from USD 428.5 million at December 31, 2025, and no committed undrawn facility is described. Operating cash flow is seasonal: USD 128.5 million was used in the first quarter of 2026, then USD 235.4 million and USD 176.6 million were provided in the second and third. Free cash flow is USD 168.0 million over the trailing twelve months after USD 365.9 million of negative free cash flow in FY2025.

The October 2026 refinancing removes near-term maturities but adds debt. Pro forma principal after October 5, 2026 is USD 4,086.1 million, with USD 8.5 million a year of amortization before 2030, USD 2,816.0 million due in 2031 and USD 1,120.0 million of second lien term loans due in 2033; 79.2% is fixed rate. Pro forma total debt to trailing Adjusted EBITDA is 4.86x. The fixed coupons on the new notes and second lien term loans alone are USD 303.5 million a year, and the floating margin on USD 850.0 million of first lien term loans is not printed, so pro forma interest cover cannot be computed from the sources.

Business risk is concentrated in supply and operating leverage. Seven distributors supplied about 83% of U.S. box office revenue in 2025, release windows have shortened, and attendance in the third quarter of 2026 rose from 58.4 million to 74.9 million on film popularity (Documentary Fact). The Adjusted EBITDA margin rose from 9.4% to 20.3% on 31.6% higher revenue, the operating leverage that also works against creditors in a weak slate.

The record of dealing with lenders raises the monitoring bar. AMC moved 175 theatres and intellectual property into unrestricted subsidiaries in July 2024, financed there, exchanged USD 590.0 million of USD 950.0 million of the Existing 7.5% Notes into Muvico notes in 2025 with certain holders, and has repeatedly paid consent fees and exchanged debt in shares, lifting shares outstanding from 512,943,561 to 892,604,638 during 2026 (Documentary Fact; the creditor-adverse reading is Analyst Interpretation). It also repaid the Muvico and Odeon term loans in full in October 2026, which removes those structures from the debt stack.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Scale and footprint: AMC ENTERTAINMENT HOLDINGS, INC. operated 840 theatres and 9,472 screens at September 30, 2026 across the United States and Europe, with about 42.0 million loyalty member households, which gives it purchasing weight with studios and a recurring customer base (Documentary Fact; the market-leader claim is Management Language and is not adopted). Operating recovery: trailing Adjusted EBITDA of USD 840.4 million is more than double FY2025, and free cash flow turned positive at USD 168.0 million (Documentary Fact). Maturity profile: the October 2026 refinancing leaves only USD 8.5 million a year of principal before 2030 and pushes USD 2,816.0 million to 2031 (Documentary Fact). Access to capital: AMC raised USD 333.6 million of net equity proceeds in nine months 2026 and raised USD 3,970.0 million of new debt on one day, showing market access for now (Documentary Fact; the durability of that access is Analyst Interpretation). Cash: USD 751.9 million is held, USD 145.6 million of it by the International markets operations, whose availability to the group was not assessed (Documentary Fact).

Weaknesses. Film supply dependence: seven distributors supplied about 83% of U.S. box office revenue and windows have shortened, so revenue visibility is short (Documentary Fact). Thin fixed-charge cover: cover including rent is about 1.35x on trailing figures and was 0.99x in FY2025, and USD 3,761.9 million of operating lease liabilities sit outside debt (Analyst Interpretation from the financial base). No described backstop liquidity: no committed undrawn facility is described, so a weak slate would draw directly on cash (Insufficient Information on whether one exists; the missing datapoint is the executed credit agreements). Higher debt: pro forma principal rises to USD 4,086.1 million, with USD 1,120.0 million of second lien debt behind USD 2,850.0 million of new first lien debt, so junior claims depend on collateral values the sources do not quantify (Documentary Fact; recovery is not assessed here). Creditor-behaviour record and dilution: the unrestricted subsidiary transfer, partial exchanges and share-paid fees show a willingness to restructure around existing lenders, and about 143,258,701 authorized shares remain unissued and unreserved, limiting further equity-funded exchanges (Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation). Information limits: ownership, covenant levels and executed financing documents are not supplied (Insufficient Information).

## Calculations

All inputs are figures in the accepted financial base, in USD millions.

C1. Fixed-charge cover, an analyst measure the issuer does not define: (Adjusted EBITDA + rent expense) / (cash interest paid + rent expense), where rent is the statement-of-operations rent line.
- Trailing twelve months: (840.4 + 891.6) / (392.3 + 891.6) = 1,732.0 / 1,283.9 = 1.35x.
- FY2025: (387.5 + 887.3) / (406.9 + 887.3) = 1,274.8 / 1,294.2 = 0.99x.
- FY2024: (343.9 + 873.6) / (401.6 + 873.6) = 1,217.5 / 1,275.2 = 0.95x.

C2. Free cash flow buffer: trailing free cash flow of 168.0 as a share of trailing Adjusted EBITDA of 840.4 is 20.0%. A fall in Adjusted EBITDA of 168.0, with every other item unchanged including working capital, would take trailing free cash flow to zero. This is arithmetic, not a forecast.

C3. Cash interest break-even: trailing Adjusted EBITDA could fall by 840.4 - 392.3 = 448.1, or 53.3%, before cash interest cover reaches 1.0x; this ignores capital expenditure of 242.2, which free cash flow in C2 does include.

C4. Pro forma fixed obligations from the financial base: stated coupons of 303.5 plus first lien term loan amortization of 8.5 give 312.0 a year of the debt service that can be computed; the floating margin on 850.0 and the toggle on the Existing Exchangeable Notes are not printed.

C5. Pro forma first lien debt: 2,000.0 of 2031 Notes + 850.0 of first lien term loans = 2,850.0; second lien 1,120.0.
