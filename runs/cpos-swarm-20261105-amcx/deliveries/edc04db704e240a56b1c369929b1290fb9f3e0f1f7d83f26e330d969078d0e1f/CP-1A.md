### Business and transaction view

AMC Global Media is a holding company that owns and operates linear cable networks (AMC, We TV, BBC America, IFC, SundanceTV), a portfolio of subscription streaming services (AMC+, Acorn TV, Shudder, Sundance Now, ALLBLK, HIDIVE, All Reality), a studio that owns much of its scripted content and a film distribution arm, plus an international channels business. Revenue comes from per-subscriber affiliate fees and streaming subscriptions, advertising sold on ratings guarantees, and content licensing that is lumpy by delivery timing. The business is controlled by the Dolan family group through a dual-class structure, not by a financial sponsor, so the sponsor-behaviour lens is applied to documented issuer actions only. The credit-relevant history is a sequence of liability-management steps: repurchases and a tender of the 4.25% senior notes and a new 10.50% secured issue in 2025, purchase of the remaining minority interests in BBC America and RLJ Entertainment in 2024 and 2025, an exchange of the 10.25% secured notes into the 10.50% notes in March 2026, an indenture consent that widened the buyback covenant, and in May 2026 the repayment of the Term Loan A and termination of the revolver. The creditor view is that the maturity profile has been pushed out while earnings and liquidity buffers have narrowed (CP-1 C3), that the remaining protections sit in indentures this source set does not contain, and that a pending damages claim and a streaming licence with Netflix are the main identified swing factors after June 30, 2026. The evidence is strong for facts stated in the filings and weak where it depends on management expectations or on documents not supplied.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The 2026 transaction set is a refinancing and simplification, not a cash raise. On February 23, 2026 the company launched an exchange offer for any and all of its $875 million of 10.25% Senior Secured Notes due January 2029 into additional 10.50% Senior Secured Notes due July 2032, which form part of the same series as the $400.0 million of 2032 notes issued in July 2025 (10-Q Q2-26 page:17-18). Holders tendering by March 6, 2026 received $1,065 of 2032 notes per $1,000 and later tenders $1,015; early settlement on March 13, 2026 issued about $884 million of notes against about $830.6 million tendered, and final settlement on March 25, 2026 issued about $31.1 million against about $30.7 million (page:18). The remaining 2029 notes of about $13.7 million were redeemed on April 6, 2026 at 105.125% of principal, with a $0.7 million charge (page:19). The 2032 notes outstanding are 1,315,098 (page:16). The incremental principal of $53.8 million was accounted for as a debt modification and recorded as a debt discount, and third-party exchange fees of $16.7 million were expensed in Miscellaneous, net (page:18). These are Documentary Facts. Status: completed.

In parallel, on February 23, 2026 the company received requisite consents to amend the indenture for the 2032 notes: to permit buybacks of equity interests up to an aggregate of $50.0 million, to limit transfers of certain trademarks to unrestricted subsidiaries to non-exclusive licences, and to restrict investments in unrestricted subsidiaries under the Permitted Investments definition to specified clauses; a $2.0 million consent fee was paid and capitalized (page:17). Status: effective. The legal terms themselves (definitions, baskets, ratio tests) are not supplied.

On May 12, 2026 the company repaid the $80.0 million remaining Term Loan A under the Credit Agreement and terminated the revolving credit facility, recording a $3.1 million charge (page:17; Rel Jul page:4). Status: completed. This ended the maintenance covenants of the Credit Agreement (CP-1 Gaps); no replacement committed facility is described.

The prior-year steps explain today's structure. In July 2025 the company issued $400.0 million of 10.50% secured notes due 2032 for net proceeds of $394.5 million, which with cash funded a tender of $600.0 million of 4.25% senior notes at a discount of $111.0 million and the prepayment of the remaining $70.0 million of the non-extended Term Loan A; during 2025 it also repurchased $108.3 million of senior notes in the open market at discounts of $28.2 million (10-K page:110). On October 29, 2025 Amendment No. 5 to the Credit Agreement extended $111.8 million of revolver commitments to the earlier of October 29, 2030 or 90 days before certain capital markets maturities, lowered the minimum interest coverage ratio to 1.50:1.00 and retired $165.7 million of term loans held by consenting lenders (10-K page:110-111, 169). On November 26, 2025 the company bought the remaining 17% of RLJ Entertainment for $75.0 million cash, and on November 1, 2024 the remaining 50.1% of BBC America for $42.0 million, together with a new $40.0 million program license agreement with BBC Studios (10-K page:190-191).

Use of proceeds and liquidity effect: none of the 2026 steps raised cash. Cash outflows were $82.9 million on the Term Loan A and $14.4 million on the 2029 notes redemption in six months 2026 (10-Q Q2-26 page:5), with debt principal falling to 1,735,554 and committed liquidity to nil at June 30, 2026 (CP-1 chart rows). Sponsor: none. Dividend recapitalisation: none documented; the cash flow statements show no dividend payments to stockholders in FY2025 or six months 2026 (10-K page:136; 10-Q Q2-26 page:5-6).

Analyst Interpretation: the exchange and the April 2026 redemption retired the whole 875,000 January 2029 secured maturity (about 861.3 million exchanged and about 13.7 million redeemed), extended secured debt to July 2032, and left the 4.25% unsecured senior and convertible notes of 420,456 as the next maturity in February 2029, ahead of the secured 2032 notes (CP-1 C11). The coupon on the exchanged debt rose from 10.25% to 10.50% and the principal exchanged rose by the early-tender and exchange premium. Whether the 2032 indenture restricts the company's ability to refinance the 2029 unsecured notes ahead of the secured notes is Insufficient Information, because the indenture is not supplied.

Subsequent event after June 30, 2026 and before the cutoff: the Netflix licence of July 30, 2026 (co-exclusive global streaming rights to The Walking Dead Universe; $500 million fee; expected present-value revenue about $445 million; expected recognition of $200 million to $225 million in each of 2026 and 2027) and an accelerated share repurchase of $30 million with Citibank entered on May 8, 2026 with final settlement expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 (Rel Jul page:4). ### Credit translations

1. **Revenue durability.** Evidence: Domestic subscription revenue is declining on basic subscriber losses, advertising is lower on ratings and pricing, and content licensing fell by 32.4% in Q2 2026 on delivery timing (10-Q Q2-26 page:42-44); management expects linear subscriber and advertising declines to continue. Risk mechanic: technical and operating and selling, general and administrative costs rose while revenue fell (10-Q Q2-26 page:3), so the revenue loss fell through to operating income (page:47). Credit implication: debt service depends on streaming growth, price increases and licensing, which are less predictable; the Netflix licence adds expected cash of about $25 million in 2026 and about $100 million a year in 2027 to 2030 but is Management Language and the contract is not supplied. Countering item: none logged as a conflict.
2. **Margin and cash-flow risk.** Evidence: Q2 2026 operating income fell from 64,469 to 15,854 on a revenue decrease of 52.5 million (10-Q Q2-26 page:47); twelve-month AOI of 313,045 against cash interest paid of 151,153 gives 2.07x cover (CP-1 C4). Mechanic: fixed programming and marketing cost base with falling revenue compresses cover; the run-rate contractual coupon on notes outstanding is 155,955 (CP-1 C8). Implication: limited tolerance for further earnings decline before leverage and cover weaken; the net debt to AOI ratio was 4.06x at June 30, 2026 (CP-1 C3).
3. **Concentration.** Evidence: few distributors hold a concentration of subscribers; two customers were 13% and 10% of receivables at December 31, 2025 (10-K page:146-147); a customer filed for Chapter 11 on June 30, 2026 and sought authority to pay prepetition ordinary-course claims for which the company is named as a vendor (10-Q Q2-26 page:57). Mechanic: renewal repricing and counterparty failure hit revenue and receivables together. Implication: receivables of 547,187 at June 30, 2026 (10-Q Q2-26 page:2) are exposed to counterparty events; exposure to the filing customer is Insufficient Information.
4. **Sponsor and control alignment.** Evidence: family control and ownership of all Class B stock (10-K page:72-73); the 2032 indenture was amended to allow buybacks up to $50.0 million, and 30,000 of treasury stock was bought in six months 2026 (10-Q Q2-26 page:6, 17), with $87 million of authorization remaining (Rel Jul page:4). Mechanic: controlled equity can direct cash to buybacks within indenture capacity at the expense of debt reduction or liquidity. Implication: monitoring item for restricted payment capacity; the gap between the $87 million board authorization and the $50.0 million indenture basket as summarised is not resolved by the sources because other baskets may apply.
5. **Refinancing.** Evidence: no debt due before February 2029 when 420,456 of unsecured 4.25% notes mature, and 1,315,098 of secured notes in July 2032; management states cash flow and cash will not repay all maturities and the company depends on capital markets access (10-Q Q2-26 page:55-56). Mechanic: the unsecured notes mature ahead of the secured notes; refinancing the earlier maturity depends on earnings stabilising. Implication: refinancing risk is deferred, not removed; the revolving facility's absence removes a bridge.
6. **Key uncertainty: litigation.** Evidence: plaintiffs claim damages in excess of $200 million in the MFN Litigation; summary judgment motion was filed; trial rescheduled to October 27, 2026; the company believes the claims are without merit and no determination of outcome can be made (10-Q Q2-26 page:24-25). Mechanic: an adverse result would be a cash claim against liquidity of cash 463,996 with no revolver. Implication: unquantified event risk dated before the as-of date with no outcome supplied; no loss estimate is recorded in the pages read.
