### Financial base view

The financial base is usable and the group is moderately levered but liquid. At July 3, 2026 principal debt was 3,875 against cash of 459 and undrawn revolver capacity of 917, and trailing Adjusted EBITDA of 1,128 gives gross leverage of 3.44x and net leverage of 3.03x, matching management's 3.0x. Leverage has fallen through FY2026 from 3.23x net at October 3, 2025, driven by a debt reduction of 133 (a voluntary 125 term loan B payment on June 30, 2026 and scheduled amortization) and a 24 rise in trailing Adjusted EBITDA. Trailing cash interest cover is 3.93x on interest paid of 287, an improvement from 3.61x for FY2025, alongside lower interest paid (175 against 194 in the nine months) and the April 2026 repricing (term loan B margin now 1.75% over Term SOFR, from 2.25%).

Three points qualify the picture. First, cash conversion is lower than margins suggest: nine-month operating cash flow of 235 was down on 273, free cash flow of 213 includes a net 120 inflow from selling receivables, and the group is guiding to an implied fourth-quarter free cash flow of 312 to 362 to reach its 525 to 575 range. Second, the earnings base includes equity earnings of 88 on a trailing basis (7.8% of trailing Adjusted EBITDA) and management add-backs of 76 in the nine months (Adjusted EBITDA 828 against company-defined EBITDA 752), so the headline measure is more generous than a plain operating measure; the financial maintenance covenant definition is not supplied. Third, the capital structure is mostly floating and secured: 2,866 of the 3,875 is term loan debt, hedged only by swaps with notional 1.3 billion that mature through January 31, 2027, and the senior notes (1,000 at 7.250%, due August 1, 2032) sit behind the secured facilities. Tangible equity is negative (equity of 4,789 against goodwill and intangibles of 7,378), so creditor protection rests on cash flows rather than hard assets. Revenue is shrinking (nine months 2026 down 2.5%) though margins and backlog (48.2 billion) are rising. Comparability limits: FY2025 had 53 weeks, and the FY2024 base excludes the merged business until September 27, 2024, so FY2024 ratios are not shown.

### Earnings quality and cash flow

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income attributable to common shareholders adjusted for interest expense and other, net, income taxes, depreciation and amortization and discrete items: acquisition, transaction and integration costs, non-cash gains and losses, loss on extinguishment of debt, utilization of fair market value adjustments from purchase accounting, and stock-based compensation (10-K page:156; Q3 release page:9). Add-backs between company-defined EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA were 110 in FY2025 (85 of acquisition and integration costs, 12 of debt extinguishment, 21 of stock compensation, less 8 of fair market value utilization), 71 in 9M 2025 and 76 in 9M 2026 (36 of integration costs, 16 of debt extinguishment, 1 of fair market value utilization, 23 of stock compensation). Equity earnings of non-consolidated subsidiaries (73 in 9M 2026) are inside both EBITDA measures; distributions received were 87 in 9M 2026 against 57 in 9M 2025, so the cash returned exceeded the earnings recognized in the latest period.

Cash flow. 9M 2026 operating cash flow of 235 was 38 lower than 9M 2025; management attributes this to a 129 increase in cash earnings offset by 167 of working capital outflows (Q3 10-Q page:30). The nine-month outflows include a 160 reduction in accrued compensation and a 198 reduction in payables, contract liabilities and other current liabilities (Q3 10-Q page:4). Net cash from receivable sales was 120 in 9M 2026 against 42 in 9M 2025. Cash taxes were 26 against 67. Interest paid was 175 against 194. Investing cash flow was an outflow of 48 against an inflow of 236 in 9M 2025, which held the 358 of proceeds from the Rapid Solutions sale (Q3 release page:8). Financing outflow was 165 against 231.

Segments. Digital Solutions (DS) and Global Engineering Solutions (GES).
