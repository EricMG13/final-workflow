### Financial base view

The financial base is sound on reported earnings and weaker on balance sheet capacity. Revenue and adjusted EBITDA grew through FY2025 and the first nine months of 2026, and free cash flow after capex remained positive, but 2026 acquisitions of 1,356 million (cash, net) and share repurchases of 252 million were funded by a 500 million senior note issue, revolver borrowing and a 539 million fall in cash, moving net debt up by 1,364 million in nine months. On the issuer's adjusted EBITDA, last-twelve-months total leverage is 3.02x and net leverage 2.71x, against 2.67x and 1.79x at FY2025; liquidity of 1,032 million at September 30, 2026 compares with 1,657 million at FY2025. Two further points shape the view: the last-twelve-months EBITDA includes acquired businesses only from their closing dates while their purchase price is fully in debt, so leverage is overstated against a pro forma run-rate, and interest cost is understated by cash paid, because the 500 million 5.750% notes issued on May 14, 2026 and the revolver draw have only partly reached the cash interest line. Debt matures in 2029 (two note series), 2031 (revolver), 2033 (term loan) and 2034, so there is no near-term wall, but the 335 million revolver balance appears to be classified as short-term debt and the credit agreement's covenant definitions are not supplied. All adjusted measures are management-defined; reported (unadjusted) EBITDA gives 3.61x total and 3.23x net leverage on the same last-twelve-months basis, and the add-backs are material (systems and business enablement alone is 82 million for nine months 2026).

### Covenant, liquidity and refinancing position

The only financial maintenance covenant described is a springing first lien net leverage test on the revolver, with a limit of 3.75 to 1.00 for each fiscal quarter. The Q3 report states the limit and the trigger and reports the ratio ((10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:60: "Our first lien net leverage ratio as of September 30, 2026 was 1.6:1.0")); the ratio was (10-Q_2026-06-30_apg-20260630.htm.jsonl page:59: "Our first lien net leverage ratio as of June 30, 2026 was 1.3:1.0") at June 30, 2026 and (10-K_2025-12-31_apg-20251231.htm.jsonl page:73: "Our first lien net leverage ratio as of December 31, 2025 was 1.1:1.0") at year-end. The trigger is (10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:60: "is greater than 30% of the total revolving credit commitments thereunder subject to a right of cure") measured on revolving loans and letter of credit obligations excluding undrawn letters of credit up to 60. On the filing wording, usage at September 30, 2026 was 335 drawn of 1,000 commitments, which is 33.5% and above the trigger; at June 30, 2026 it was 280 of 1,000 (28.0%), below it; and at October 29, 2026 it was 435 of 1,000 (43.5%). The test therefore applied at the latest quarter-end on the filing's own description, with reported headroom of 2.15x on rounded figures (3.75 less 1.6). This relies on summarised wording; the credit agreement and its definitions are not supplied, so whether the trigger and ratio are computed exactly this way is a stated limitation. An indicative reading of the covenant EBITDA: first lien debt (term loan 2,147 plus revolver 335, less cash 373, giving 2,109) over the reported 1.6x implies an EBITDA of about 1,318 (range 1,278 to 1,361 for ratio rounding), against derived LTM adjusted EBITDA of 1,191; the gap of about 127 is consistent with the pro forma and cost-savings adjustments the release mentions ((EX-99.1_2026-10-29_apg-20260930xexx991.htm.jsonl page:7: "includes pro forma adjustments for acquisitions and cost savings not reflected in adjusted EBITDA")) but is an analyst inference, not a reported figure, and assumes the term loan and revolver are the first lien debt and cash is fully netted.

Liquidity is cash plus undrawn revolver: 373 + 659 = 1,032 at September 30, 2026 ((10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:56: "$1,032 million of total liquidity")), down from 912 + 745 = 1,657 at December 31, 2025 and 499 + 494 = 993 at December 31, 2024. No committed facility expires within twelve months: the revolver matures in 2031 and the term loan in 2033 after the Ninth Amendment; the 2029 notes are the first bond maturities. Hedging: (10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:24: "the 2026 Interest Rate Swap with $720 of notional value, exchanging one-month SOFR for a fixed rate of 3.59% per annum") and a second swap of 400 notional; (10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:25: "The remaining $1,027 of the term loan balance will bear interest based on one-month SOFR plus 175 basis points"); the 720 forward-starting swap matures in January 2029 ((10-Q_2026-09-30_apg-20260930.htm.jsonl page:25: "commencing in October 2026 and maturing in January 2029")), before the term loan maturity in 2033, so floating exposure on the term loan returns thereafter. Fixed coupons on the three note series total about 56 a year (Calculations). The issuer-reported fair values at September 30, 2026 were 318 for the 4.125% notes, 263 for the 4.750% notes and 473 for the 5.750% notes (carrying values 337, 277 and 500), i.e. all three below carrying value; fair values are issuer estimates from discounting at available rates (Level 2), not traded prices.
