### Financial base view

ATS Corporation entered Q2 FY2027 with a moderately levered balance sheet that is drifting the wrong way. The company's own measure, net debt to pro forma adjusted EBITDA, rose from 2.8x at March 31, 2026 to 3.1x at September 27, 2026, because trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA fell while net debt rose, helped along by a share repurchase of 61.3 under the normal course issuer bid. Management now expects to operate above its 2.0x to 3.0x target range for the rest of FY2027. Earnings pressure is volume driven: Q2 FY2027 revenues fell year over year while Order Bookings rose, so backlog of 2,009 supports near-term revenue but at a lower margin. Debt service looks comfortable on cash interest cover of 4.18x for the trailing twelve months, and liquidity is large relative to the debt wall: the senior secured revolver runs to December 2029, and the first large principal maturity on the disclosed instruments is the U.S. dollar senior notes in December 2028. The quality of the numbers deserves care. Cash flow in FY2026 was flattered by a large working capital release, adjusted EBITDA adds back restructuring charges that recur every year, the covenant tests are described but not quantified, the interim statements arrive without notes, and management reports a material weakness in internal control. The view is therefore moderate leverage, strong liquidity, deteriorating earnings and incomplete covenant evidence.
