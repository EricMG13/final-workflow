### Evidence trace view

Belden's analyses hold up where they can be checked: the figures for debt, cash, revolver availability, Adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, the Ruckus purchase and the customer concentration were re-found in the 10-K, the three 10-Qs and the releases at the cited locators, and the same figure carries the same value, unit, currency and period wherever it recurs. The weak points are two interpretive claims, not figures. First, the financial base and the credit synthesis call the 5.57x trailing total leverage an overstated or upper-bound figure because Ruckus is in the earnings for one quarter only, but the direction depends on Ruckus having earned a positive Adjusted EBITDA before July 1, 2026, which no source supplies. Second, the statement that the net leverage goal was dropped rests on absence from two 10-Q goal lists while the releases still refer to a net leverage outlook. Neither changes a figure, but both qualify the credit view, and committee use is Restricted because the debt documents and Ruckus history are not supplied, not because the traced figures failed.
