### Financial base view

Belden is a profitable, cash-generative manufacturer whose capital structure was transformed on July 1, 2026. Before the Ruckus acquisition, debt was three euro senior subordinated notes totalling USD 1,292.0 million at FY2025 against Adjusted EBITDA of USD 458.7 million, and a revolver that had been drawn and repaid within 2025. At September 27, 2026, debt is USD 3,103.8 million before unamortised costs: USD 1,253.8 million of euro notes (2028, 2031 and the new 2033 series) and USD 1,850.0 million of Term Loan B due 2033, which the company priced at term SOFR plus 2.25%. Cash is USD 380.7 million and the USD 400.0 million revolver is undrawn, with USD 373.5 million available. On trailing Adjusted EBITDA of USD 557.2 million, which includes Ruckus for one quarter only, total debt is 5.57x and net debt 4.89x. Those ratios are conservative against a full-year combined base but cannot be restated, because Ruckus earnings before July 1, 2026 are not supplied. GAAP results for Q3 2026 were a net loss of USD 11.4 million because of USD 100 million of purchase-accounting charges that the company excludes from Adjusted EBITDA; the Ruckus loss before tax in the quarter was USD 63.1 million. The company's earlier long-term net leverage goal of about 1.5x appears in the 10-K and the Q1 2026 10-Q, and is absent from the Q2 and Q3 2026 10-Qs. Net interest expense was USD 41.8 million in Q3 2026 against USD 11.6 million in Q3 2025, and nine months 2026 operating cash flow less capital expenditure and disposal proceeds (the company's free cash flow) was USD 107.4 million against USD 97.6 million. The term loan amortises USD 18.5 million a year (0.25% a quarter of USD 1,850.0 million). Legal terms of the new term loan (covenants, security, guarantors) are not in the supplied sources.

### Quality of earnings and definitions

Adjusted EBITDA is the company's non-GAAP measure, built from net income plus depreciation, amortisation of intangibles and of software development intangibles, tax, net interest, restructuring and integration costs, debt extinguishment and acquisition adjustments (10-Q Q3 page 32). For nine months 2026 it adds back USD 59.4 million (59,363 thousand) of acquisition and divestiture adjustments, primarily the fair value step-up of acquired inventory, and USD 36,696 thousand of severance, restructuring and integration costs. The MD&A attributes part of the increase in gross profit and Adjusted EBITDA to tariff refunds: USD 17.9 million on 2025 transactions in nine months 2026 (USD 4.3 million in Q3), plus USD 8.1 million net on 2026 transactions that offsets 2026 tariff cost (10-Q Q3 pages 25 and 28). Analyst interpretation: refunds of past-year tariffs are unlikely to recur at that level, and they are inside Adjusted EBITDA. No covenant EBITDA definition is supplied, so the company measure should not be read as covenant EBITDA.

Ruckus contributed USD 167.8 million of revenue and a USD 63.1 million loss before taxes in Q3 2026, after USD 56.3 million of inventory fair value charges and USD 43.7 million of intangible amortisation (10-Q Q3 page 13). The purchase price allocation is preliminary (page 12). The 10-Q prints unaudited pro forma revenue, net income and EPS as if Ruckus had been acquired on January 1, 2025 (nine months 2026 revenue USD 2,763.5 million), but no pro forma EBITDA.

## Calculations

All inputs are as printed (USD thousands unless stated). Ratios use debt before unamortised issuance costs, which is the sum of the debt register above.

1. LTM Adjusted EBITDA at Q3 2026 = FY2025 458,734 + nine months 2026 435,104 - nine months 2025 336,672 = 557,166. Inputs: 10-K page 41; 10-Q Q3 page 32.
2. Total debt, Q3 2026 = notes 1,253,780 + Term Loan B 1,850,000 = 3,103,780; this equals the USD 3,103.8 million carrying value stated on 10-Q Q3 page 20. Less unamortised costs 53,370 gives 3,050,410, which equals the balance sheet (18,500 + 3,031,910).
3. Net debt, Q3 2026 = 3,103,780 - cash 380,706 = 2,723,074. FY2025 = 1,291,950 - 389,887 = 902,063.
4. Total debt / LTM Adjusted EBITDA: Q3 2026 3,103,780 / 557,166 = 5.57x; FY2025 1,291,950 / 458,734 = 2.82x.
5. Net debt / LTM Adjusted EBITDA: Q3 2026 2,723,074 / 557,166 = 4.89x; FY2025 902,063 / 458,734 = 1.97x.
6. EBITDA / cash interest, FY2025 = 458,734 / 43,157 = 10.63x. Q3 2026: Not calculable, cash interest paid not printed.
7. EBITDA / net interest expense (not cash), Q3 2026 LTM: net interest expense = FY2025 46,355 + nine months 2026 68,860 - nine months 2025 33,866 = 81,349; 557,166 / 81,349 = 6.85x. FY2025 458,734 / 46,355 = 9.90x. This uses expense, not cash, and one quarter of Term Loan B interest.
8. Liquidity, Q3 2026 = cash 380,706 + revolver availability 373,500 = 754,206 (USD 754.2 million). FY2025 = 389,887 + 383,900 = 773,787. Net of letters of credit by facility: Not calculable, because the filings print the letters of credit as a total without saying how much is issued under the Revolver. The commitment less availability is 400,000 - 373,500 = 26,500 at Q3 2026; the sources do not attribute that gap.
9. Free cash flow, FY2025 (company definition: operating cash flow less capital expenditures plus disposal proceeds) = 354,864 - 136,172 + 191 = 218,883. Nine months 2026 as printed 107,444 = 240,200 - 132,863 + 107.
10. Funding cross-check: borrowings under credit arrangements 2,432,630 compare with Term Loan B 1,850,000 + 2033 Notes 537,300 at issuance + revolver draw 50,000 - discount 4,600 = 2,432,700 (difference 70, rounding of the stated USD 537.3 million and USD 4.6 million). Payments under borrowing arrangements 585,860 compare with 2027 Notes repurchase 535,900 + revolver repayment 50,000 = 585,900 (difference 40, rounding).
11. Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation re-added from the printed rows: FY2025 458,734, nine months 2026 435,104, nine months 2025 336,672 and Q3 2026 171,137 each equal the printed totals.
12. Nominal growth, Q3 2026 revenue over Q3 2025: 965,954 / 698,221 - 1 = 38.3%, which agrees with the 38% in the release; the release attributes 11% to organic growth, 24% to acquisitions and 3% to copper pass-through (release page 6).
