### Business and transaction view

Belden is a US-listed manufacturer of copper and fiber cable, connectivity, industrial networking and security products sold mainly through distributors, which moved in 2026 from a low-leverage, buyback-oriented company to one carrying USD 3,103.8 million of debt after a debt-funded acquisition. The Ruckus purchase (wireless access points, switches, cloud management and software, bought from Vistance Networks) closed July 1, 2026 for USD 1,869.6 million net of cash acquired, funded by a USD 1,850.0 million seven-year Term Loan B. For creditors the mechanics are: Ruckus is consolidated for one quarter only, so trailing leverage of 5.57x is on a partial earnings base; the purchase price allocation is preliminary; and goodwill and intangibles of USD 2,040.8 million (the sum of the two printed allocation lines) now sit against equity of USD 1,392.0 million. Management describes record demand in 2026, but that is attributed management language. Belden is NYSE-listed and no sponsor or controlling holder appears in the supplied sources, so sponsor-extraction behaviour is not documented, and neither is any sponsor equity support. What is missing is as important as what is present: the Term Loan B, revolver and indenture documents are not in the pack, so legal capacity for distributions, additional debt and collateral release cannot be judged. Governance conclusions here are limited to documented issuer actions.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Ruckus. On April 29, 2026 Belden signed a definitive agreement to acquire certain entities comprising Ruckus Networks, for approximately USD 1.846 billion to be funded through additional debt (10-Q Q1 pages 16 and 19); the April 30, 2026 release names the seller as Vistance Networks (Nasdaq: VISN) and gives approximately USD 1.85 billion (release page 1). The acquisition closed July 1, 2026 for USD 1,869.6 million net of cash acquired and is accounted for as a business combination (10-Q Q3 page 11). The three descriptions of price differ in rounding and basis (signed price, rounded price, closing price net of cash); the closing figure in the 10-Q controls. The preliminary allocation records receivables USD 146.457 million, inventory USD 153.555 million, intangible assets USD 1,040.0 million (developed technologies USD 800.0 million over 5.0 years, customer relationships USD 160.0 million over 15.0 years, trademarks USD 80.0 million over 20.0 years), goodwill USD 1,000.819 million, and liabilities assumed USD 535.212 million including deferred income taxes USD 175.882 million (10-Q Q3 pages 11-12). In-process R&D has no fair value yet and the allocation may change (page 12). Ruckus contributed USD 167.8 million of revenue and a USD 63.1 million loss before taxes in Q3 2026, after USD 56.3 million of inventory fair value charges and USD 43.7 million of intangible amortisation (page 13). Pro forma revenue and net income as if acquired on January 1, 2025 are printed, but not EBITDA (page 13). Status: completed; integration is management's stated focus (release October 29, 2026, page 2, Management Language).

New debt and use of proceeds. (1) Term Loan B: USD 1,850.0 million, entered July 1, 2026 to fund Ruckus, term SOFR plus 2.25% or base rate plus 1.25%, amortising 0.25% a quarter, maturing July 1, 2033, issued at a discount of USD 4.6 million with USD 38.8 million of fees; fair value approximates carrying value (10-Q Q3 page 20). Guarantors, security and covenants are not described. (2) EUR 450.0 million 4.250% senior subordinated notes due 2033, issued January 28, 2026 (10-K page 107, Note 25), guaranteed on a senior subordinated basis by current and future domestic subsidiaries, ranking equal to the 2028 and 2031 notes, with fees of USD 8.6 million (10-Q Q3 page 20); proceeds with cash on hand funded the full repurchase of the EUR 450.0 million 3.375% notes due 2027 on February 11, 2026 for USD 535.9 million, with a USD 1.3 million loss on extinguishment (10-Q Q3 page 18; the 10-K quotes USD 537.3 million, a translation difference carried from the readiness review). (3) Revolver: a USD 50.0 million draw on June 29, 2026 was repaid in full on September 25, 2026 (page 18). Liquidity effect: per the financial base, liquidity at September 27, 2026 is USD 754.2 million (cash USD 380.7 million plus revolver availability USD 373.5 million). ### Credit translations

1. Evidence: Ruckus contributed USD 167.8 million of revenue and a USD 63.1 million loss before taxes in Q3 2026 and is funded by a USD 1,850.0 million term loan (10-Q Q3 pages 13 and 20). Mechanic: the earnings that service the new debt are unproven and include non-cash charges that will fade, while cash interest begins in full. Implication: debt service and leverage tolerance depend on Ruckus delivering earnings; the 5.57x trailing ratio (CP-1) is on a partial base and is not a stable measure. Countering item: none logged except the price-basis discrepancy.
2. Evidence: ten largest customers are about 47% of revenue, eight of them distributors, with no minimum purchase obligations (10-K pages 5-6 and 106). Mechanic: channel destocking or a distributor change moves volume quickly, and short backlog limits visibility. Implication: revenue durability depends on distributor behaviour; backlog was USD 567.7 million at December 31, 2025 with almost all shipping in 2026.
3. Evidence: copper pricing is passed through with a lag (10-K pages 9-10) and tariff refunds of USD 17.9 million were recorded (10-Q Q3 page 25). Mechanic: margins have a timing exposure to copper and tariff policy; refunds boost reported results without recurring. Implication: margin resilience should be judged excluding the refunds on 2025 transactions; the amounts are disclosed for Q3 2026 and nine months 2026, while the refunds on 2026 transactions net to zero against tariff cost (10-Q Q3 page 25).
4. Evidence: no sponsor or controlling holder appears in the supplied sources. Mechanic: no sponsor extraction behaviour is documented, but there is also no sponsor equity support. Implication: sponsor alignment is Insufficient Information rather than adverse.
