### Credit view

Belden is a cash-generative manufacturer whose credit profile weakened in 2026 because it borrowed to buy Ruckus, so the credit now turns on whether Ruckus earns what the debt needs. The strongest support is liquidity of USD 754.2 million, an undrawn revolver due in 2030 and no maturity of the euro notes before 2028. The weakest point is leverage: total debt of USD 3,103.8 million is 5.57x trailing Adjusted EBITDA, which holds Ruckus for one quarter only, against 2.82x at FY2025. What decides the outcome is Ruckus earnings after a USD 63.1 million pre-tax loss in Q3 2026, and the unsupplied term loan terms. Category: Analyst Interpretation, built on the cited financial base and business context.

### Key findings

First, the capital structure changed in July 2026. Belden's debt rose from USD 1,291.950 million at FY2025 to USD 3,103.780 million at Q3 2026 before unamortised costs, because a USD 1,850.0 million Term Loan B funded Ruckus (Documentary Fact; financial base debt register and business context transaction summary). The mechanic is that the debt is carried in full while Ruckus earnings are in the trailing base for one quarter only; the implication is that the 5.57x total and 4.89x net leverage ratios are upper bounds on the true level, but the true level cannot be calculated because Ruckus pre-acquisition earnings are not supplied (Analyst Interpretation).

Second, liquidity is sound. Cash is USD 380.7 million and revolver availability is USD 373.5 million, giving USD 754.2 million, with the USD 400.0 million revolver due in 2030 and undrawn at September 27, 2026 (Documentary Fact; financial base liquidity). The only near-term scheduled debt payment is Term Loan B amortisation of USD 18.5 million a year; the first euro note maturity is the 2028 series (Documentary Fact). A springing fixed charge test applies to the revolver only if borrowings exceed 90% of the combined borrowing base or availability falls below USD 27.0 million (Documentary Fact); the test level is not supplied.

Third, earnings quality needs care. Adjusted EBITDA is a company measure that for nine months 2026 adds back USD 59.4 million of acquisition adjustments and includes tariff refunds of USD 17.9 million on 2025 transactions (Documentary Fact). Refunds of past-year tariffs are unlikely to recur at that level, so reported margin of 18.0% for nine months 2026 overstates the underlying run rate by an amount that cannot be measured from the sources (Analyst Interpretation).

Fourth, cash flow is positive but the post-deal interest burden is not yet visible in it. Free cash flow was USD 107.4 million for nine months 2026 against USD 97.6 million a year earlier, but carries one quarter of Term Loan B interest. Net interest expense was USD 41.8 million in Q3 2026 against USD 11.6 million in Q3 2025 (Documentary Fact). Cash interest for 2026 is not supplied, so interest cover on a cash basis is Not calculable; the net-expense cover of 6.85x on a trailing basis is a substitute that mixes one quarter of the new interest with a partial earnings base (Analyst Interpretation).

Fifth, the business depends on its distributors and on Ruckus integration. The largest distributor is about 14% of FY2025 revenue and the ten largest customers about 47%, with no minimum purchase commitments (Documentary Fact). Ruckus contributed USD 167.8 million of revenue and a USD 63.1 million pre-tax loss in Q3 2026 after non-cash purchase-accounting charges, and the allocation of the USD 1,869.6 million net purchase price is preliminary (Documentary Fact). Management says demand is robust and has announced a USD 100 million data center supply agreement for 2027 (Management Language, not adopted).

Sixth, capital allocation tilted toward shareholders before the deal and is undocumented after it. Share repurchases were USD 195.6 million in FY2025 and USD 31.8 million in nine months 2026, and the long-term net leverage goal of about 1.5x no longer appears in the Q2 and Q3 2026 filings (Documentary Fact). No replacement leverage target or deleveraging path is documented; this is a gap, not an adverse conclusion (Insufficient Information).

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. (1) Liquidity of USD 754.2 million with an undrawn revolver due in 2030 and no euro note maturity before 2028 (Documentary Fact). (2) Operating cash generation: free cash flow of USD 107.4 million for nine months 2026 and USD 218.883 million for FY2025 on the company definition (Documentary Fact and Calculated figure from the financial base). (3) A diversified product and geographic base, with revenue in the Americas, EMEA and APAC and about 42% of FY2025 sales outside the U.S. (Documentary Fact). (4) A pre-deal balance sheet with trailing net leverage of 1.97x at FY2025 and interest cover on cash interest of 10.63x, showing capacity before the acquisition (Documentary Fact and Calculated figures). (5) Reporting transparency, including non-GAAP reconciliations and disclosure that purchase accounting is preliminary (Documentary Fact).

Weaknesses. (1) Leverage of 5.57x total and 4.89x net on trailing Adjusted EBITDA that holds Ruckus for one quarter only (Calculated, Analyst Interpretation on its direction). (2) Unproven Ruckus earnings: a USD 63.1 million pre-tax loss in Q3 2026 and goodwill and acquired intangibles of USD 2,040.8 million against stockholders' equity of USD 1,392.0 million (Documentary Fact). (3) Customer concentration through distributors and short-cycle orders (Documentary Fact). (4) Exposure to copper and tariff policy with only a lagged pass-through; earnings include tariff refunds (Documentary Fact; durability an Analyst Interpretation). (5) The term loan and revolver terms, ownership and ratings are not supplied, so legal capacity for distributions, additional debt and collateral release cannot be judged (Insufficient Information).
