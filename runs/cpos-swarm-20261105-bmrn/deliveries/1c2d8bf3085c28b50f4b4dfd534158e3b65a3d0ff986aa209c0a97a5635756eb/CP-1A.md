### Business and transaction view

BioMarin is a global rare-disease biotechnology company that discovers, manufactures and sells enzyme therapies and peptide medicines, reports one operating segment, and has no sponsor. Its credit story changed in 2026: it bought Amicus on 2026-04-27 for 5,323,561 thousand, adding GALAFOLD for Fabry disease and POMBILITI + OPFOLDA for Pompe disease, and financed it with debt and cash instead of equity, taking principal debt from 600,000 thousand to 4,235,000 thousand. Revenue is spread across nine marketed products, with VOXZOGO and VIMIZIM the largest in Q3 2026, but three customers account for about a third of net product revenue and ALDURAZYME depends on a Sanofi arrangement with change-of-control termination rights. Ownership is public with 28 holders of record at 2026-02-19, no controlling holder was found in the pack and no dividends have ever been paid, so there is no documented extraction. Documented capital allocation is mixed: since 2025 the company has made three pipeline-led deals, Inozyme, Alesta and the BMN 450 license, alongside the large Amicus transaction, while management states a target of gross leverage below 2.5 times by mid-year 2027 on its own projected adjusted EBITDA definition. That target is management language and the pack gives no projected adjusted EBITDA, so whether it is achievable cannot be tested here. Transaction-specific legal terms, guarantors and ownership percentages are not supplied. The view for the committee is a diversified but concentrated-customer rare-disease franchise that has moved to a levered, acquisition-driven profile with unproven deleveraging.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transaction is the acquisition of Amicus Therapeutics, Inc., a publicly traded biotechnology company, completed on 2026-04-27 under a merger agreement dated 2025-12-19. Amicus became a wholly owned subsidiary and brought GALAFOLD, POMBILITI + OPFOLDA and the BMN 820 candidate for focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, with U.S. rights held under an assumed Dimerix license of up to 510.0 million in milestones (Q3 10-Q page:14, page:32; exhibit list Q3 10-Q page:123). Total consideration was 5,323,561 thousand: 4,563,463 paid to shareholders, 142,636 for restricted and performance stock units, 62,946 for the pre-combination part of stock options, 121,596 of seller transaction costs and 432,920 to repay Amicus debt including a prepayment penalty and accrued interest (page:14-15). The purchase price allocation is preliminary and includes intangible assets of 4,750,000, goodwill of 469,786 and a deferred tax liability of 577,589 (page:15-16). The intangibles are GALAFOLD at 3,000,000 over 11 years, POMBILITI + OPFOLDA at 1,450,000 over 12 years and the BMN 820 research asset at 300,000, which is not amortized (page:16). Acquisition-related costs of 32.8 million were expensed in selling, general and administrative expense in the nine months (page:14).

The financing was cash on hand and non-convertible debt, with no equity issued (page:14). First, in December 2025 the company signed a commitment letter for a 364-day senior secured bridge of up to approximately 3.7 billion; it was reduced to 2.8 billion when the 2034 Notes were issued and to zero when the term loans were signed (page:25). Second, in February 2026 (indenture dated 2026-02-12) the company issued 850,000 thousand of 5.5% senior unsecured notes due 2034-02-15, with interest payable on February 15 and August 15 from 2026-08-15, net proceeds of 832.3 million after 17.7 million of issuance costs, held in escrow until the Amicus close (Q3 10-Q page:25; Q1 10-Q page:13, page:17). The notes were not registered under the Securities Act (page:25), and a change of control repurchase feature exists for them (page:103). Third, on 2026-04-27 the company entered a senior secured credit agreement with Citibank, N.A. as administrative and collateral agent: an 800.0 million Term Loan A due 2031-04-27, a 2.0 billion Term Loan B due 2033-04-27 and a 600.0 million revolver due 2031-04-27, with issuance costs of 46.2 million (Q3 10-Q page:23-25; Q2 10-Q page:118). Obligations are guaranteed by certain subsidiaries and secured by first-priority liens on substantially all assets of the Company and the guarantors (Q3 10-Q page:50). The guarantors are not named. The 2024 unsecured 600.0 million revolver was terminated (Q2 10-Q page:43). The 1.25% convertible notes of 600,000 thousand due May 2027 stay outstanding. The company says it used the notes proceeds, the term loans and approximately 1.7 billion of cash on hand to pay the consideration (page:47). A bridge commitment fee of 22.8 million was expensed (page:23). The cash flow statement ties to this: proceeds from borrowings were 3,650,000, the equal of 850,000 plus 2,800,000, and cash paid for the business combination net of cash acquired was 5,067,630, equal to total consideration less the 255,931 of Amicus cash acquired (page:10, page:15).

Liquidity effect: cash and investments fell from 2,052,851 thousand at 2025-12-31 to 736,007 at 2026-09-30, and principal debt rose from 600,000 to 4,235,000 (Q3 10-Q page:8, page:22, page:45). Management states a target of gross leverage below 2.5 times by mid-year 2027, a year earlier than the timeline given at announcement, supported by profitability growth, defined as undiscounted debt divided by projected four-quarter non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, which is income from operations excluding depreciation, amortization and stock-based compensation (Q2 release page:3, page:10). That statement is Management Language, was made on 2026-08-06 and is not repeated in the November release. Management also states approximately 280 million of GAAP cost synergies, about 220 million on a non-GAAP basis, fully realized in 2028 (Q3 release page:3). The company announced restructuring tied to Amicus: severance of 29,478 in Q3 2026 and 82,618 in the nine months (Q3 10-Q page:30). Since the Amicus close the company has also completed the Alesta acquisition on 2026-08-31 for 303.4 million with 282.3 million expensed as acquired research and up to 215 million of milestones, and agreed in October 2026 the BMN 450 license with a 70.0 million upfront payment and up to 405.0 million of milestones (Q3 10-Q page:18, page:34). The scheduled maturity of the 2027 Notes, 600.0 million, is the first refinancing event; the notes are convertible at 137.47 per share and otherwise payable in cash (10-K page:206; Q3 10-Q page:48). No transaction source other than the filings is supplied: the merger agreement, credit agreement, indentures and supplemental indenture are exhibits incorporated by reference and their text is not in the pack, so legal terms, baskets and guarantor lists are Not supplied.
