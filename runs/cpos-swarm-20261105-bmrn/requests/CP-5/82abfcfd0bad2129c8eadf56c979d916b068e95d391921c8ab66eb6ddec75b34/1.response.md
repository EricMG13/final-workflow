### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious view is the operating cash generation: nine-month operating cash flow of 576,974 thousand after 244,514 of depreciation and amortization, an 80.0% gross margin, liquidity above the next twelve months of maturities, and Amicus products already contributing 200,416 thousand of Q3 revenue. If integration synergies of about 280 million (220 million non-GAAP) are realized and acquired research charges fall, EBITDA on the management definition could approach the level implied by the target. What would make that case win: a fourth quarter and first half of 2027 with adjusted EBITDA run-rate near 1.4 to 1.7 billion, the May 2027 notes repaid from cash, and a compliance certificate showing covenant headroom. What would make the cautious case win: further acquired research charges, order-timing misses like the November guidance cut, or a leverage ratio above 3.50x on the covenant definition.
