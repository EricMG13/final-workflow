### Credit view

Brightstar Lottery PLC has an adequate fundamental credit profile with real near-term support but limited margin for error. The strongest support is contracted, wager-linked revenue, about 92% of FY2025 revenue, and liquidity of USD 1,742 million with no debt maturing before 2027, after the final USD 1,675 million Italian Lotto payment was absorbed. The weakest point is the quality of 3.24x net leverage: it uses management's Adjusted EBITDA, which adds back USD 323 million of license amortization and would read 4.48x without it, while partners and a controlling shareholder take part of the cash flow. The outcome depends on free cash flow after the investment cycle, the Scratch and Win renewal in September 2028 beside USD 798 million of 2028 maturities, and covenant terms that are not supplied.

### Key findings

Net leverage is acceptable on management's measure but weaker on a cash-oriented one. Net debt was USD 3,796 million at June 30, 2026 against last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,170 million, which is 3.24x, and total debt over the same EBITDA is 3.72x [financial base: leverage]. Adjusted EBITDA adds back USD 323 million of license-fee amortization for the same period, and without that add-back net leverage is 4.48x [financial base: definition review]. The add-back relates to fees already paid in cash, which is why the reported figure is not unreasonable, but it means the leverage ratio flatters the cash burden of renewals in a business whose Italian licenses run for about nine years. The headline rise from 2.44x at December 31, 2025 to 3.24x mostly reflects the settlement of a USD 1,680 million regulator payable that sat outside debt; counting that payable, leverage at December 31, 2025 would have been 3.94x, so the like-for-like movement over six months was a decrease, helped by USD 650 million of partner capital contributions [financial base: claims outside debt; business context: Italian Lotto license].

Liquidity and maturities are comfortable for two years and heavier after that. Liquidity is USD 1,742 million, made of USD 558 million of cash and USD 1,185 million of undrawn revolver, against 2027 and 2028 principal maturities of USD 1,026 million combined, which is 1.70x cover [financial base: liquidity and maturities]. Total principal is USD 4,383 million, of which USD 2,883 million is in euros and all of it is senior secured, and the 2028 to 2030 maturities total USD 2,801 million, about 64% of principal. The new revolver runs to March 2031 with USD 604 million drawn. The risk is a concentrated refinancing window in 2028 to 2030 that coincides with the Scratch and Win renewal.

Cash generation is positive before license payments but is shared. Operating cash flow excluding Lotto payments was USD 761 million over the last twelve months and free cash flow excluding them USD 387 million; reported free cash flow was negative USD 2,214 million because of the USD 2,601 million of license payments in the period [financial base: financials]. Out of the USD 387 million, partners took USD 163 million of distributions, leaving USD 224 million for debt reduction and shareholders, and shareholder returns of USD 1,090 million over the same twelve months (including the USD 600 million special dividend funded from the sale of the gaming business) therefore came from sale proceeds and not from recurring cash flow [business context: ownership and governance]. In the first half of 2026 free cash flow excluding Lotto payments of USD 269 million less partner distributions of USD 163 million was USD 106 million, below the USD 130 million returned to shareholders. Capital expenditure of USD 190 million is expected in the second half of 2026 for new contracts.

The business has strong contractual visibility, concentrated in Italy and exposed to renewals. About 92% of revenue comes from long-term contracts, but the two Italian licenses supplied about 37% of FY2025 revenue and many U.S. contracts can be ended at will with limited notice [business context: operating model]. The Italian Lotto license runs to November 2034, while the Scratch and Win license expires in September 2028 and is not renewable unless its terms say so; the new Lotto license required an upfront fee of EUR 2,230 million. If a renewal needs a comparable upfront payment, it would arrive when USD 798 million of debt matures, a mechanic and not a forecast, since the renewal terms are not in the evidence.

Partners and the controlling shareholder claim part of the cash flow. The Italian Lotto manager is 61.5% owned by the company's Italian subsidiary, partners contributed USD 650 million of capital in the first half of 2026, and in FY2025 income attributable to non-controlling interests of USD 136 million exceeded total income from continuing operations of USD 135 million [business context: operating model]. De Agostini holds 46.29% of the shares and 63.28% of the votes, so distribution and leverage policy is its to set; the term loan agreements limited distributions at December 31, 2025, but the March 2026 amendment is not supplied.

What is not known matters. Covenant levels are not supplied, so headroom under the maximum net leverage and minimum interest coverage tests cannot be calculated even though the company reports compliance; nothing after August 4, 2026 is available; and ratings are known only at December 31, 2025 (BBB- from Fitch, Ba1 from Moody's, BB+ from S&P) [readiness; financial base: covenants].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strength: contracted, wager-linked revenue. About 92% of FY2025 revenue comes from long-term operating, facilities management and lottery management contracts with a revenue-weighted remaining term of 5.1 years for facilities management contracts and 5.6 years for the Italian licenses, so revenue is visible, and the company owns the technology and equipment it places, including more than 350,000 point-of-sale devices [business context: operating model]. Category: Documentary Fact.

Strength: liquidity and a staggered maturity profile. Liquidity of USD 1,742 million and no maturity before 2027 give two years of room, and the 2027 maturity of USD 228 million is small against that liquidity [financial base: liquidity and maturities]. Category: Documentary Fact.

Strength: deleveraging since the sale of the gaming business. Carrying debt fell from USD 5,361 million at December 31, 2024 to USD 4,178 million at December 31, 2025 and net leverage on management's measure from 4.08x to 2.44x before the Lotto payment, with refinancing already completed for 2026 [financial base: leverage; business context: transactions]. Category: Documentary Fact.

Strength: interest cover. Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest 6.09x over the last twelve months, against 5.26x in FY2025 [financial base: interest cover]. Category: Analyst Interpretation.

Weakness: leverage quality. Net leverage of 3.24x depends on management's measure that adds back USD 323 million of license amortization; without it leverage is 4.48x and cash interest cover is 4.41x [financial base: definition review]. Category: Analyst Interpretation.

Weakness: Italian concentration and the 2028 renewal. The two Italian licenses are about 37% of revenue, the Scratch and Win license expires in September 2028, and the license assets pass to the State free of charge at expiry [business context: operating model]. Category: Documentary Fact.

Weakness: partner leakage and consolidated figures. Consolidated debt and EBITDA include 100% of the Italian joint venture while partners hold 38.5% of the Lotto manager and non-controlling interests took USD 163 million of distributions in the first half of 2026, so consolidated leverage does not isolate the claims of the parent's creditors on partner-owned cash [business context: operating model]. Category: Analyst Interpretation.

Weakness: customer and jackpot exposure. The top ten customers outside Italy supplied about 35% of FY2025 revenue, a customer that contributed USD 37 million of FY2025 revenue left in August 2025, and lottery management incentives fell by USD 51 million in FY2025 on weak jackpot activity [business context: operating model]. Category: Documentary Fact.

Weakness: control and distributions. One holder controls the vote, shareholder returns have exceeded recurring free cash flow after partners, and creditor protection depends on distribution caps whose current text is not supplied [business context: ownership and governance]. Category: Analyst Interpretation.

### Thesis

Brightstar's contracted lottery revenue and USD 1,742 million of liquidity support an adequate credit profile, but leverage quality, Italian renewal risk and partner and shareholder claims on cash flow limit headroom until the 2028 to 2030 refinancing window is addressed. Category: Analyst Interpretation.
