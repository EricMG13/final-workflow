The latest quarterly report records later events through October 21, 2026, including "an Order reversing and vacating the Judgment" (page:13), and notes the plaintiffs may seek further review. The latest filing controls; the earlier one remains the record as of its date.
- Debt table versions: the December 2025, March 2026 and September 2026 quarterly debt tables and the annual table are each retained as separate dated sources. They are not collapsed; later tranches (a second term loan tranche and an add-on of senior notes) appear only from the March 2026 report.
- Period basis: the first-quarter release states fiscal year 2027 guidance with no reference to the calendar-year change that the quarterly report discloses ("approved changing the Company’s fiscal year to a calendar year", page:13). A search of that release for calendar year and transition found nothing. Whether the stated guidance still covers a year ending June 2027 or the transition period is unresolved. This is a conflict of basis, not of figures.
- Release versus filing: release non-GAAP measures (EBITDA, adjusted net income, free cash flow excluding the receivables sale program) are management claims. They do not override the statements.

### Analytical read-through

Usable coverage. Evidence: a full set of filed statements for the year ended June 30, 2026 and three quarters, plus the debt note listing each facility ("Revolving Facility November 2030", annual report page:86). Mechanism: facility-level principal, rate, maturity and amortisation can be read directly, so maturity and liquidity work does not depend on estimation. Consequence: financial, liquidity and transaction modules can proceed from filed evidence.

Strongest contrary evidence. The only text on covenant tests is generic: "including a maximum total net leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio" (10-Q ended December 31, 2025, page:12), and later filings state only compliance ("in compliance with all of its financial covenants related to all long-term debt", 10-Q ended September 30, 2026, page:9). Mechanism: with no executed credit agreement, no level, definition or step-down can be located, and management compliance statements are attributed assertions. Consequence: any covenant headroom, definition difference or cushion analysis cannot proceed; downstream modules must write the gap, not an estimate.

Structure. Evidence: "A majority of our assets serve as collateral under the Credit Facility." (annual report page:88) and senior unsecured notes described as "subordinated to existing senior secured indebtedness" (page:89). Mechanism: ranking and collateral are described in prose without guarantor names or collateral schedule; the wording on the notes is quoted as printed and not reinterpreted. Consequence: recovery and structural-ranking work is limited to the prose descriptions.

Period change. Evidence: the transition-period note (10-Q ended September 30, 2026, page:13). Mechanism: the next report will cover six months, not a quarter, so a year-over-year quarter comparison will not be available from the next filing. Consequence: later modules must label every figure by period and must not mix a three-month, six-month or twelve-month amount.

Next evidence action: obtain the credit agreement and its amendments, the indentures, the subsidiary list and the guarantor schedule, and, if discrete quarterly history is wanted, the second-quarter FY2026 release and the balance sheet at the end of the first quarter of FY2026.
