### Financial base view

The financial base is adequate for a leverage, liquidity and maturity review to 2026-09-30, on the stated bases and with named restrictions. Leverage is calculated on Adjusted EBITDA built from the filed segment reconciliation (segment Adjusted EBITDA plus the other non-reportable segment less corporate expenses), because no filing prints a consolidated Adjusted EBITDA; management's own trailing figure is 744 and its net leverage is approximately 4.3x, consistent with the filing-basis 4.25x. The reading is that the issuer has reduced principal by 310 since December 2025 and moved net leverage down by about 0.4x, from a high starting point, with thin book equity and a large claims layer outside debt (environmental remediation, accrued litigation, leases, pensions, supplier and receivable financing). ### Interpretation: evidence, mechanism, implication

- **Leverage is high and falling slowly, helped by asset sales.** Evidence: debt principal fell from 4,182 to 3,872 while nine-month 2026 proceeds from sales of assets were 342 (10-Q Q3 page:5). Mechanism: the reduction comes mainly from euro term loan repayments of EUR 280 million (about 324) in the nine months, partly funded from Kuan Yin land sale proceeds (release Q1 page:4); the March 2026 issue of 700 replaced redemptions of 683 of 2027 and 2028 notes (page:27), so it did not reduce debt. Implication: further deleveraging relies on EBITDA growth (management guides full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of 775 to 800, release page:6) and on the cash cost of PFAS settlements, so leverage near 4x is the likely floor without further disposals.
- **Adjusted EBITDA overstates cash for debt service.** Evidence: FY2025 litigation-related charges of 320 and environmental charges of 93 are excluded (10-K page:317), and nine-month 2026 environmental charges of 168 and net litigation charges of 106 are excluded (10-Q Q3 page:133). Mechanism: these are accrued liabilities paid over years, including 100 to New Jersey in September 2026 and 51 to North Carolina in October 2026. Implication: coverage and leverage on Adjusted EBITDA should be read next to reported operating cash flow, which was negative 633 in FY2024 and 264 in FY2025.
- **Interest burden is steady.** Evidence: interest expense, net was 269 for FY2025 and 206 for nine months 2026; the new 7.875% notes replaced 5.375% and 5.750% notes while the term loan margin rose to 3.50% (page:26-27). Mechanism: refinancing at higher coupons offset debt reduction; management expects annual interest expense to fall by only about 3 (page:151). Implication: little interest relief from the deleveraging to date.
- **Segment mix.** Evidence: Q3 2026 segment Adjusted EBITDA was 136 in Thermal and Specialized Solutions, 85 in Titanium Technologies and 24 in Advanced Performance Materials against 194, 25 and 14 a year earlier (10-Q Q3 page:133). Mechanism: titanium pricing recovery offsets lower aftermarket refrigerant sales. Implication: the earnings base is more cyclical than the refrigerant franchise alone suggests.
- **Thin equity.** Evidence: total equity of 156 against total liabilities of 7,137 (10-Q Q3 page:3-4). Mechanism: litigation and environmental charges have consumed retained earnings. Implication: book equity gives no cushion and debt capacity rests on cash flow and asset values.
