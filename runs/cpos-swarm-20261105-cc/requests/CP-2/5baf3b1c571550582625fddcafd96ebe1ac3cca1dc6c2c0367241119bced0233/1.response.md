### Credit view

Chemours is a leveraged, cyclical chemicals credit with adequate near-term liquidity, whose cash flow is increasingly claimed by legacy PFAS settlements. The strongest support is deleveraging: total debt principal fell to USD 3,872 million at 2026-09-30, net leverage eased to 4.25x, unrestricted cash was USD 698 million, no revolver amounts were drawn, and DuPont and Corteva share half of future qualifying legacy PFAS cost. The weakest point is that Adjusted EBITDA excludes settlement and environmental cash while book equity is only USD 156 million. What decides the outcome is whether titanium dioxide pricing holds as refrigerant sales normalise, and whether the 2028 and 2029 debt is refinanced before the revolver springing maturity applies.
