### Business and transaction view

View: the business facts are well documented and the transaction facts are documented only at summary level. CCC is a US software-as-a-service and AI platform for the auto insurance and collision repair economy; 96% of revenue is software subscriptions on multi-year contracts, the customer base is broad but includes a few very large insurers, and there is a documented history of financing acquisitions and shareholder returns with debt. The transaction is a pending merger signed on 2026-10-14, a subsequent event to the Q3 2026 period: each share converts to 7.75 US dollars in cash and the company becomes a privately held wholly owned subsidiary of Mozart Parent, LLC, while the release separately attributes the acquisition to a consortium led by GTCR and including Elliott Investment Management; the filings do not state how that consortium relates to Parent. The strongest contrary evidence for a stable creditor view is that everything that governs the creditor after closing is outside the pack: the executed merger agreement, the debt and preferred equity financing it contemplates, and any change-of-control terms in the 2021 Credit Agreement. The ownership picture before the deal is also thin: the filings name a "principal equity owner" without naming it or giving a holding. The mechanism that matters is a change of control that is documented as to price and timing but not as to leverage: the equity purchase price at the offer price is far larger than the existing net debt, and the split between new debt, preferred equity and equity is Not supplied. What can proceed: business risk assessment, revenue durability, concentration and cash conversion analysis, and a documented capital-allocation history. What is restricted: any statement about sponsor behaviour after closing, willingness to support, post-deal leverage, or the fate of the existing term loan.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transaction is a pending acquisition of the whole company. On 2026-10-14 the company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Mozart Parent, LLC and Mozart Merger Sub (DE), Inc.; the merger sub merges into the company, which survives as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (10-Q Q3 page:29). Each outstanding share other than certain excluded shares converts into the right to receive 7.75 US dollars in cash; the board approved it with all directors present voting in favour and resolved to recommend it (10-Q Q3 page:29). The company's release, dated 2026-10-29, says the buyer is a consortium of private investment firms led by GTCR and including Elliott Investment Management, that the all-cash deal represents an enterprise value of approximately 6.1 billion US dollars, that guidance is suspended and that no earnings call will be held (release page:2); these are management statements, and neither GTCR nor Elliott is named in the sections of the 10-Q that were read (searched for both names in the 10-Q Q3 text). Status: signed, not closed. Closing is subject to customary conditions including adoption by holders of a majority of the outstanding shares, expiry or termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period and the absence of a prohibiting law or order; the merger is not subject to a financing condition and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, after which the shares are delisted (10-Q Q3 page:30). Either side may terminate if the merger is not consummated on or before 2027-04-14, among other rights (10-Q Q3 page:51). The company must pay Parent a fee of 149.52 million if the agreement ends in specified cases such as a superior proposal, and Parent would owe the company approximately 313.99 million if it fails to fund the equity financing or close the debt or preferred equity financing the agreement contemplates (10-Q Q3 page:51). The agreement restricts, without Parent's consent, incurring certain debt, paying dividends, repurchasing stock, settling certain legal proceedings and some capital expenditure (10-Q Q3 pages:22, 45, 52), and stockholder complaints about the merger are a disclosed risk (10-Q Q3 page:53).

New debt issues: no new debt was raised in 2026 to date (10-Q Q3 page:45, "We did not incur any new borrowings in 2026"). The merger agreement contemplates "debt or preferred equity financing" for the buyer (10-Q Q3 page:51), but the amounts, instruments, ranking, security and the issuer of that financing are Not supplied, as is the treatment of the existing 2021 Credit Agreement term loan and revolver at closing (repaid, assumed or amended). Use of proceeds is not stated in the pack; the merger consideration is payable to stockholders (10-Q Q3 page:29). The liquidity effects disclosed are the fee exposures above and the restrictions on the company's use of cash. Arithmetic on stated figures (calculation T1): 591,041,172 shares at 7.75 US dollars is about 4,581 million US dollars on the basic share count, to which net debt of 1,078 million at 2026-09-30 adds about 5,659 million; the release's approximately 6.1 billion US dollars is about 441 million higher, and the pack does not bridge the difference (dilutive securities, transaction costs and other items are not itemised). The enterprise value is about 12.6 times LTM Adjusted EBITDA of 484,946 thousand, an arithmetic ratio on management's approximate figure and not a valuation conclusion. The filings also describe 8,625,000 Sponsor Vesting Shares and 15.0 million Company Earnout Shares whose release depends on a share price threshold or a change in control "as defined in the Business Combination Agreement" (10-K page:165); that agreement is not in the pack, so whether the merger is such a change in control is Not supplied.
