### Credit view

Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. is a highly leveraged advertising credit whose earnings are improving while its debt is unchanged, and a pending take-private merger leaves its capital structure unresolved. The strongest support is trailing Adjusted EBITDA of USD 544.3 million, which puts total debt at 9.46x and cash interest cover at 1.40x, with USD 192.1 million of cash, USD 205.5 million of undrawn facilities and no maturities of size before 2028. The weakest point is thin cover: USD 5,148.8 million of debt and fixed airport rent leave little room if advertising demand softens. What decides the outcome is whether earnings growth continues and whether the merger closes, which no source after August 5, 2026 answers.

### Key findings

Leverage has eased, but only through earnings. Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. carries debt principal of USD 5,148.8 million at June 30, 2026 against USD 5,148.9 million at December 31, 2025, while trailing Adjusted EBITDA is USD 544.3 million against USD 504.8 million for FY2025. Total debt to Adjusted EBITDA is therefore 9.46x, down from 10.20x, and 9.11x net of USD 192.1 million of cash. Because principal has not moved, leverage will rise as quickly as it fell if earnings decline: a 10% fall in trailing Adjusted EBITDA would put the ratio at about 10.51x (Documentary Fact for the inputs, Analyst Interpretation for the sensitivity). The measure is the issuer's own non-GAAP figure, stated after site lease expense, and the covenant definition is not supplied.

Cash interest cover is above 1.0x but not by much. Adjusted EBITDA covered cash interest paid of USD 390.1 million 1.40x on the trailing basis, against 1.28x for FY2025 and 1.09x for FY2024, and covered net interest expense 1.37x. After capital expenditure of USD 76.1 million, Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest about 1.20x, and trailing free cash flow is USD 84.3 million. Trailing Adjusted EBITDA could fall by about 28.3% before cash interest cover reaches 1.0x, and the issuer's own estimate of cash interest of about USD 394 million in 2027 would give cover of about 1.38x on trailing earnings (Analyst Interpretation from the financial base). Cash flow and cash interest include discontinued operations, so the cover is indicative.

Secured debt is most of the stack. First-priority secured debt is USD 3,340.0 million, or 6.14x trailing Adjusted EBITDA and about 64.9% of principal, made up of a USD 425.0 million term loan and USD 2,915.0 million of senior secured notes due 2030, 2031 and 2033. The USD 1,805.3 million of senior unsecured notes due in 2028 and 2029 are effectively subordinated to that debt and structurally subordinated to non-guarantors, and the guarantor list is not supplied. The ratio is an indicator of secured leverage, not the covenant measure.

Liquidity is adequate for now, with the main maturities two years away. Cash is USD 192.1 million and excess availability under the revolver and receivables-based facility is USD 205.5 million, together USD 397.6 million, with no borrowings drawn and both facilities maturing June 12, 2030. That sum is about one year of the issuer's expected 2027 cash interest. Availability under the receivables-based facility depends on a borrowing base, and the revolver's springing first lien net leverage covenant of 7.10x was not in effect at June 30, 2026, so headroom cannot be measured. The next significant maturities are USD 1,324.6 million in 2028 (the 7.750% notes and the term loan) and USD 906.3 million in 2029, which together are about 43.3% of principal.

The pending merger dominates the capital structure outlook. The take-private merger at USD 2.43 per share was approved by stockholders on May 12, 2026 and was expected to close by the end of the third quarter of 2026, but its first outside date of November 9, 2026 falls four days after the cutoff and the supplied sources do not say whether it closed. Debt amendments make the merger not a change of control, but they become operative only on closing, and conditional redemption notices cover the 2028 and 2029 unsecured notes. The funding source and price of those redemptions and the buyer's financing are not supplied. Equity consideration on basic shares is about USD 1,237.1 million, about 24.0% of debt principal, so the offer implies a thin equity layer beneath the debt (Analyst Interpretation; no valuation or recovery view is stated).

Business risk is short-cycle revenue against fixed rent. Roadside advertising is mostly sold in four-week periods while site leases and municipal and airport concessions run for years, so a demand dip reaches margin before contracts roll. Airports Segment Adjusted EBITDA margin was 25.3% in H1 2026 against 41.0% for America because airport site lease expense was 59.2% of revenue, and airport minimum guarantees rise with prior-period performance. Revenue grew 8.7% in the second quarter of 2026 and Adjusted EBITDA grew 11.6%, partly on event-driven demand from the FIFA World Cup (Documentary Fact; the event effect is Analyst Interpretation as to repeatability).

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Earnings momentum: trailing Adjusted EBITDA of USD 544.3 million is above the FY2025 level of USD 504.8 million, and H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA grew about 19.0% over H1 2025 (Documentary Fact). Permit-protected, diversified inventory: more than 64,500 displays and operations in 81 U.S. markets at December 31, 2025, and no single advertiser is a material portion of revenue (Documentary Fact; the leading-position claims are Management Language and are not adopted). Liquidity and runway: USD 192.1 million of cash, USD 205.5 million of undrawn facilities maturing in June 2030, and no significant maturities before 2028 (Documentary Fact). Simplified, domestic business: substantially all international operations were sold, with the Spain sale completed on August 4, 2026, which simplifies the perimeter (Documentary Fact). No distributions: no dividends are intended and none are permitted while the merger agreement stands (Documentary Fact). Positive free cash flow: USD 84.3 million on the trailing basis against USD 32.0 million in FY2025 (Documentary Fact for inputs, Analyst Interpretation for the measure).

Weaknesses. High leverage with flat debt: 9.46x total debt and 9.11x net, with principal essentially unchanged, so deleveraging relies on earnings and asset sale proceeds (Analyst Interpretation from the financial base). Thin cover: cash interest cover of 1.40x and about 1.20x after capital expenditure leave limited room for an advertising downturn, and the stockholders' deficit is USD 3,457.1 million (Documentary Fact). Fixed airport rent: airport site lease expense at 59.2% of revenue makes the fastest-growing segment the lowest-margin one, and USD 1,361.9 million of operating lease liabilities sit outside the debt figure (Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation). Refinancing concentration: about 43.3% of principal falls due in 2028 and 2029, and the unsecured notes rank behind USD 3,340.0 million of secured debt (Documentary Fact). Metro and sector concentration: America is about three quarters of revenue, Los Angeles is a leading market, and 2026 growth drew on technology advertisers in the San Francisco area (Documentary Fact). Unresolved ownership and merger outcome: control, merger financing, the post-closing capital plan and the consent terms are not supplied, and willingness of the prospective owner cannot be inferred from its identity (Insufficient Information). Information limits: covenant headroom, guarantor financials and executed documents are not supplied (Insufficient Information).

## Calculations

All inputs are figures in the accepted financial base and business context, in USD millions unless stated; trailing means the twelve months to June 30, 2026 (LTM) as built in the financial base.

C1. EBITDA after capital expenditure over cash interest: (544.3 - 76.1) / 390.1 = 468.2 / 390.1 = 1.20x. Exact inputs in thousands: (544,254 - 76,122) / 390,057 = 1.2002.

C2. Break-even for cash interest cover of 1.0x: 544.3 - 390.1 = 154.2; 154.2 / 544.3 = 28.3%. This holds interest constant and is arithmetic, not a forecast.

C3. Free cash flow of 84.3 as a share of trailing Adjusted EBITDA of 544.3 is 15.5%. A fall in Adjusted EBITDA of 84.3 with everything else unchanged would take trailing free cash flow to zero.

C4. Forward cover on the issuer's estimate: 544.3 / 394 = 1.38x, where about 394 is the issuer's estimate of 2027 cash interest on June 30, 2026 debt (financial base, liquidity section). It assumes no refinancing, repayment or merger effect.

C5. Liquidity: 192.1 + 205.5 = 397.6; 397.6 / 394 = 1.01, about one year of the estimated 2027 cash interest. Availability under the receivables-based facility is subject to a borrowing base and the revolver is net of letters of credit.

C6. Debt mix. Secured principal 3,340.0 / 5,148.8 = 64.9%; unsecured notes 1,805.3 / 5,148.8 = 35.1%. Maturity concentration: (1,324.6 + 906.3) / 5,148.9 = 2,230.9 / 5,148.9 = 43.3% (the schedule is as printed at December 31, 2025, which equals the printed total of 5,148.9).

C7. Sensitivity of leverage and cover to Adjusted EBITDA. A 10% decline gives 544.3 x 0.9 = 489.8; 5,148.8 / 489.8 = 10.51x and 489.8 / 390.1 = 1.26x. A 20% decline gives 435.4; 5,148.8 / 435.4 = 11.83x and 435.4 / 390.1 = 1.12x. Interest and debt are held constant.

C8. Equity consideration against debt: 1,237.1 / 5,148.8 = 24.0%, where 1,237.1 is 509,093,845 basic shares x USD 2.43 (business context), before equity awards. It is an indicator of the equity layer at the offer price, not a valuation or recovery measure.

C9. Growth: Q2 2026 revenue 438.0 / 402.8 - 1 = 8.7%; Q2 2026 Adjusted EBITDA 143.4 / 128.6 - 1 = 11.6%; H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA 247.3 / 207.8 - 1 = 19.0% (financial base, segment and reconciliation tables).

C10. Directions used in the text: leverage fell from 10.20x (FY2025) to 9.46x (LTM) and cash interest cover rose from 1.28x to 1.40x (financial base, calculated); principal moved from 5,148.9 to 5,148.8, essentially flat.
