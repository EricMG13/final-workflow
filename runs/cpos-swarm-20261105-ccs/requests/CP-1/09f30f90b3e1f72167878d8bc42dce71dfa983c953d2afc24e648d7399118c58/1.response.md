### Financial base view

The reported financial base is complete enough for a credit view and shows clear deterioration. For the last twelve months to September 30, 2026, company-defined adjusted EBITDA was $294.366 million against $583.926 million in FY2024, and total debt at carrying value was $1,812.587 million against $1,476.213 million, so total debt to adjusted EBITDA is 6.16x and net debt to adjusted EBITDA is 5.87x, up from 2.53x and 2.27x. Both a lower earnings base and a higher revolver balance contribute: holding adjusted EBITDA at its FY2025 level, September 2026 debt alone gives 5.18x. Nine-month operating cash flow was negative $267.475 million, so inventory growth, share repurchases and dividends were financed by revolver borrowings. Liquidity of $744.0 million of undrawn revolver plus $86.086 million of cash and cash equivalents remains sizeable, the first bond maturity is August 2029, and the filings state covenant compliance, but covenant levels and headroom are not available in the sources, and the October 7, 2026 acquisition adds a cash call that the balance sheet does not yet reflect. The view is therefore a supported but restricted base: leverage and liquidity figures are firm, covenant headroom and post-acquisition leverage are not calculable.

### Definition review

- EBITDA is net income before income tax expense, interest in cost of home sales revenues, other interest expense (income) and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA further adds back inventory impairment, abandonment of lot option contracts, stock-based compensation, restructuring costs, loss on debt extinguishment, impairment on other investment and purchase price accounting for acquired work in process inventory (10-K page:91; Q3 10-Q page:65).
- Interest in cost of home sales revenues (60.738 in FY2025) is added back, because most interest is capitalized to inventory and expensed through cost of sales. EBITDA is therefore before most interest cost, and cash interest paid (81.3 in FY2025) is the better measure of the debt service burden. Interest incurred is close to cash interest in FY2025 (81.980 against 81.3).
- Adjusted EBITDA adds back inventory impairment of 19.605 in the nine months of 2026, 21.816 in FY2025 and 8.778 in FY2024 (10-K page:91; Q3 10-Q page:65); impairment appears in each period shown, so the unadjusted EBITDA ratios are shown alongside.
- Adjusted EBITDA definitions changed: lot option abandonment was added from Q3 2025 and stock-based compensation from Q4 2025, with prior periods recast (10-K page:91, page:92; Q3 10-Q page:66). - Evidence: nine-month operating cash flow of negative 267.475 against negative 57.654 a year earlier, while dividends of 27.519 and repurchases of 71.198 continued. Mechanism: shareholder returns are discretionary but were paid while cash flow was negative. Implication: the flexibility to cut returns is a mitigant that the sources show has not yet been used.
- Evidence: unsecured notes of 1,000.0 principal sit behind at least 320.716 of identified secured debt and share the guarantor group. Mechanism: effective subordination to secured claims at the guarantor level. Implication: bondholders rank behind secured construction and warehouse lenders, though these are small beside 4,790.025 of total assets.

- Limitation: covenant headroom, post-acquisition leverage and cash interest for 2026 cannot be assessed from the sources, so the credit view is bounded by those gaps.
