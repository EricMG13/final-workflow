### Financial base view

The financial base is usable for CP-1A, CP-2 and CP-5 on the consolidated Churchill Downs Incorporated perimeter. Facility-level debt, the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation for FY2025 and nine months 2026, cash flow and cash-paid interest and tax are printed in filed statements. On those figures, total debt at September 30, 2026 was 4,748 million and unrestricted cash 167 million. Last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA computed here is 1,245.3 million, giving 3.81x gross and 3.68x net of unrestricted cash. That compares with 4.28x gross and 4.11x net at year-end 2025 on the same method, so leverage fell materially over nine months. The reduction is real in principal terms but involves three pieces that a reader must keep apart: repaid revolver and term loan B-1 borrowings, a new term loan B, and a subsequent-event shift of 600 million of note debt onto the revolver. The view is restricted in three respects: covenant levels are available only at December 31, 2025; the governing legal documents are not in the pack; and the last-twelve-months build mixes sources printed to one decimal (FY2025) with whole-million sources. No restriction blocks the downstream modules.

### Evidence-based interpretation by dimension

#### Leverage

On the method used here, gross leverage fell from 4.28x at December 31, 2025 to 3.81x at September 30, 2026 and net leverage from 4.11x to 3.68x. Secured debt (revolver, Term Loan A and Term Loan B) is 1,648, which is 1.32x last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA gross, and about 34.7% of total debt. At December 31, 2025 the company reported a covenant secured net leverage ratio of 1.4x against a limit of 4.0x. The same secured-debt arithmetic on year-end figures gives 1.54x net of unrestricted cash, so covenant definitions (EBITDA, cash netting, letters of credit) differ from the metric computed here by an unexplained margin. Evidence to risk to implication: Adjusted EBITDA includes affiliate EBITDA that is not cash to the issuer, so leverage on a cash basis is higher than the ratios above; the subsequent-event shift of 600 of notes to the revolver does not change total debt but raises the secured share.

#### Coverage

Last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA covers net interest expense 4.34x and cash interest 4.54x. For comparison, the company's reported covenant interest coverage at December 31, 2025 was 3.9x against a 2.5x minimum, and the simple FY2025 ratio of Adjusted EBITDA to interest expense is 4.05x. The covenant figure is lower than the simple ratio; possible causes are a narrower covenant EBITDA or a broader interest definition, but neither is verified because the definitions are not supplied. Headroom at September 30, 2026 is Not calculable because neither the covenant figure nor its definition is printed for any 2026 date.

#### Cash flow quality

Last-twelve-months operating cash flow of 755.8 less capital expenditures of 225.9 gives free cash flow of 529.9 before dividends and buybacks. Capital project spending fell from 172 to 119 across nine months, and FY2025 project spending of 204.7 itself followed 463.4 in FY2024 (10-K page:79); falling project capital lifts free cash flow relative to those years. Equity-method income in Adjusted EBITDA (143 in nine months 2026, Q3 10-Q page:26) exceeded distributions received (106, page:4), a 37 difference that is earnings, not cash, in the period. Cash taxes rose from 17 to 65 in nine months, reducing operating cash flow.

#### Liquidity

Unrestricted cash 167 plus computed revolver availability 1,111 gives 1,278 at September 30, 2026. After the October 19, 2026 redemption, illustrative availability is 511 and liquidity 678 if no other revolver or cash movement occurred, which cannot be confirmed from the sources. Cash at October 19, 2026 is not supplied. Near-term maturities after the redemption: next note maturity January 15, 2028.

#### Data quality

The sums tested above tie: the Q3 balance sheet, both Adjusted EBITDA reconciliations, the segment and quarterly totals and the capital expenditure table. Units differ (one decimal versus whole millions). Derived last-twelve-months figures inherit rounding of up to about 0.5 per whole-million input. The net debt line in the MD&A (4,063) is total debt net of current maturities and issuance costs, not net of cash.

#### Definition risks

(1) Adjusted EBITDA includes proportionate equity-affiliate EBITDA and noncontrolling interest EBITDA and excludes stock-based compensation, pre-opening expense and "other charges"; (2) "net bank leverage" is undefined; (3) covenant ratios use agreement definitions not supplied; (4) "Net debt" in the MD&A excludes cash netting; (5) restricted cash of 92 is excluded from liquidity because the sources do not state what it is restricted for; the 65 account wagering deposits liability (page:20) is a possible but unconfirmed link.
