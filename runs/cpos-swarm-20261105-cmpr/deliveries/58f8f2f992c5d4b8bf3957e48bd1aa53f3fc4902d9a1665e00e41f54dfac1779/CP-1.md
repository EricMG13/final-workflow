### Financial base view

The financial base is adequate for downstream credit work, with stated limits. Reported statements for FY2024, FY2025, FY2026, Q1 FY2026 and Q1 FY2027 are complete and their expense blocks reconcile to operating income. On the issuer's own adjusted EBITDA, total debt to EBITDA was 3.70x at FY2025, 3.57x at FY2026 and 3.71x on the trailing twelve months to September 30, 2026; net debt to EBITDA was 3.16x, 3.03x and 3.44x. EBITDA to cash interest was 3.93x, 4.18x and 4.11x. The movement in the latest period is explained by the July 2026 Saxoprint acquisition, which was funded from cash and revolver borrowings, while trailing EBITDA contains only the post-acquisition months and Q1 FY2027 adjusted EBITDA fell year on year. In US$m: total debt principal 1,667.6, cash 120.1, liquidity 317.8 (June 30, 2026: 481.6), share-based compensation added back in FY2026 61.4, and adjusted free cash flow 122.4 in FY2026 (FY2025: 148.0). Three limits bind every metric: adjusted EBITDA is the issuer's measure and adds back share-based compensation, the covenant definition and reported ratio are not supplied, and debt excludes finance leases, supply chain finance and purchase commitments, which are shown separately.

### Credit interpretation

- Leverage and acquisition effect. Between June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2026 debt principal rose by 31,227 thousand while cash fell by 128,724 thousand, because Saxoprint cost 131,557 thousand net of cash acquired and was funded from cash and 35,000 thousand of revolver borrowings (10-Q Sep 2026 pages:4, 21). Net leverage therefore rose to 3.44x from 3.03x, while LTM EBITDA holds only the post-acquisition part of the new business and its pre-acquisition EBITDA is not supplied. The first-quarter adjusted EBITDA decline (89,820 against 98,715) came mainly from higher start-up costs for the North American network, tariff costs and depreciation, partly offset by a 7.5 million tariff refund benefit (10-Q Sep 2026 page:33). Run-rate leverage after acquired EBITDA and any sale-leaseback proceeds cannot be derived from the sources.
- Quality of EBITDA. Issuer adjusted EBITDA adds back share-based compensation (61,379 in FY2026, 13.4% of FY2026 adjusted EBITDA) and deducts realised losses on currency hedges (14,169). Treating share-based compensation as a cost gives FY2026 EBITDA of 397,119 and total leverage of 4.12x; on LTM figures 387,106 and 4.31x. The hedge programme targets adjusted EBITDA to maintain stability on debt covenants (10-Q Sep 2026 page:50), so adjusted EBITDA is plausibly close to the covenant definition, but that cannot be confirmed without the credit agreement.
- Cash generation. Operating cash flow fell from 350,722 (FY2024) to 283,706 (FY2026) while capital spending, including capitalised software, rose to 167,290 in FY2026. Issuer adjusted free cash flow, which is after cash interest because operating cash flow includes interest paid (10-Q Sep 2026 page:34), fell from 261,053 to 122,432. In FY2026 cash was also used for acquisitions of 32,390, share repurchases of 50,077, withholding taxes on equity awards of 19,313 and finance lease payments of 11,740 (10-K pages:89-90). A buyback authorisation of 200 million remains (10-Q Sep 2026 page:52); none was used in Q1 FY2027, and share repurchases are restricted payments under the secured facility (10-Q Sep 2026 page:21).
- Liquidity. Liquidity at September 30, 2026 was 317.8 million against 481.6 million at June 30, 2026. Contractual obligations falling due within one year total 278,887 thousand including interest, leases and purchase commitments (10-Q Sep 2026 page:45). The second fiscal quarter is the peak season; the first-quarter working capital outflow was 23.7 million (10-Q Sep 2026 page:44).
- Maturities and refinancing. Scheduled amortisation is small (11.5 to 18.5 million a year), with 1,570.6 million due after FY2031. The Term Loan B springs to June 16, 2032 unless the 2032 Notes are repaid, refinanced or extended, so the practical wall is the 525.0 million note refinancing in 2032.
- Debt-like claims outside the debt figure. Finance leases of 56,003, operating lease payments of 157,858 (undiscounted fixed), supply chain finance of 58,781 inside accounts payable, purchase commitments of 310,257 (cloud services 192,636), redeemable noncontrolling interests of 78,361 (a put exercised by minority holders of one small business will require 9,602 in the second quarter of FY2027), and pension liabilities of 9,060. Including finance leases, total leverage is 3.83x on LTM figures. Book equity is a deficit of 479,409 after cumulative share repurchases (treasury shares at cost 1,363,550).
- Covenant. A maintenance test of Consolidated Leverage Ratio not above 4.50x applies only if revolving loans, swingline loans and unreimbursed letter of credit disbursements exceed 20% of commitments at a fiscal quarter end (10-Q Sep 2026 page:21). On the printed 35,000 of loans against 250,000 of commitments, usage was 14.0%, so the test was not triggered at September 30, 2026; 15,000 of further drawings would reach the 20% threshold. Management states compliance with all covenants (10-Q Sep 2026 page:21). The covenant ratio itself is not printed.
- Data checks. Operating income reconciles to gross profit less the expense block of technology, marketing, general and administrative, intangible amortisation and restructuring for all five periods. The adjusted EBITDA bridges recompute to the printed figures. Debt principal by instrument sums to the printed total at each date.
