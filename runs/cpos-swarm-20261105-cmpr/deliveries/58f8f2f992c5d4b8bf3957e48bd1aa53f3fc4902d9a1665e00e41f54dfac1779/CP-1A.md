### Business and transaction view

Cimpress is a decentralised group of web-to-print and promotional-product businesses run by a small central team, financed with a covenant-light structure of a secured term loan and revolver plus unsecured notes. The creditor-relevant business facts are that one brand, VistaPrint, supplies most segment EBITDA with high gross margin and low working capital; that the Upload and Print segments earn lower margins but are growing faster and are the acquisition platform; that profits are concentrated in the December quarter; and that U.S. tariffs, energy and North American capacity start-up costs are compressing margin. The transaction facts are a June 2026 amendment that extended maturities, and a July 2026 acquisition of Saxoprint and viaprinto that was funded from cash and the revolver and moved leverage higher. Governance is equity-oriented rather than sponsor-driven: management states that maximising intrinsic value per share is its uppermost objective and has repurchased shares every year in the periods supplied, but the filings document no priming, uptiering, dividend or debt-funded distribution. On the evidence in the pack the behaviour category is Neutral or Mixed: transparent reporting and an extension of maturities beside steady repurchases and an unresolved acquisition funding plan. Ownership percentages, control rights and sponsor history are not supplied; the filing says about 70% of shares are held by the top 10 shareholders without naming them.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Saxoprint and viaprinto. On July 2, 2026 Cimpress acquired the Saxoprint and viaprinto businesses of CEWE Stiftung & Co. KGaA, a subsequent event to the FY2026 10-K period and part of Q1 FY2027. The September 2026 10-Q states total purchase consideration of $141,081 (US$141.1m; €123,996), funded in cash from the available cash balance and the senior secured revolving credit facility (10-Q Sep 2026 page:18). Business acquisitions net of cash acquired were 131,557 (10-Q Sep 2026 page:4) and revolver borrowings were 35,000 (page:21). The preliminary allocation records goodwill of 36,480, intangible assets of 22,471 in total, and assets held for sale of 46,449 (page:19); final values are expected in the second quarter of FY2027. The business is managed within PrintBrothers, and its revenue and earnings in the quarter are described as not material. The 10-K, written after closing, stated the price as €120,000 subject to a post-closing adjustment and said no definitive sale-leaseback agreement had been executed (10-K pages:70, 142); the 10-Q says a plan to market and execute a sale-leaseback of the manufacturing facility was approved in Q1 FY2027 and the asset is held for sale (10-Q Sep 2026 page:19). Status: closed; sale-leaseback planned, not evidenced as signed. Liquidity effect: cash fell from 248,851 to 120,127 over the quarter (CP-1).

Credit agreement amendment and restatement, June 4, 2026. The Restated Credit Agreement increased the Term Loan B by 35,289 to cover fees and increase liquidity, issued at 99.75% of par, extended the term loan to June 2033 and the revolver to June 2031, with no other material changes (10-K page:118). Costs: debt issuance costs of 7,789 and a discount of 2,750 capitalised, and a loss on early extinguishment of 3,722 for FY2026 (10-K page:119). Status: executed and effective. The agreement itself is not supplied. At September 30, 2026 the Term Loan B was 1,094,500 and the revolver commitment 250,000 with 35,000 drawn (10-Q Sep 2026 page:21).

7.375% Senior Notes due 2032. Issued under an indenture dated September 26, 2024 with certain subsidiaries as guarantors (10-K page:147); FY2025 proceeds of 525,000 and payments for early redemption or purchase of 7.0% Senior Notes due 2026 of 522,135 (10-K page:89; FY2025 column). Debt issuance costs of 11,658 were capitalised in FY2025 (10-K page:119). The notes are unsecured, pay semi-annual coupons on March 15 and September 15, and are redeemable at the indenture's prices (10-Q Sep 2026 pages:22, 47). Use of proceeds: refinancing of the 2026 notes, per the cash flow lines; the offering document is not supplied.

PrintBrothers minority sale, FY2026. A minority 8.75% interest in each PrintBrothers business was sold to members of management for cash of 24,814 (€21,000), with a 10-year lock-up and a put right from 2036; the redeemable interest was recorded at fair value 61,498 (€53,000), with a share-based compensation asset of 36,684 (10-K page:131). This adds a long-dated equity-like claim that can become a cash call from 2036; it also shows funding of the business from management, not from lenders.

Subsequent put exercise. Minority holders of one smaller business exercised a put; the purchase of their shares will cost 9,602 in Q2 FY2027 (10-Q Sep 2026 page:24). Status: committed, not yet paid at September 30, 2026.

Prior debt in the period: Q1 FY2027 borrowings of 100,848 and repayments of 69,529 (10-Q Sep 2026 page:5), consistent with the revolver draw and term loan amortisation. No new bond issue, rating action, or exchange offer is reported after June 30, 2026 within the supplied filings; the earnings releases that might describe later events are unreadable. Nothing after 2026-11-05 is used.
