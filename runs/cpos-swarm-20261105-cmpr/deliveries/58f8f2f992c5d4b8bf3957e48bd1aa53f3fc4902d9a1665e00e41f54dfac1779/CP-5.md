### Evidence trace view

The analysis is traceable enough for a fundamental credit snapshot, but not for covenant, priority or ownership conclusions. The debt, cash, adjusted EBITDA, liquidity, maturity and segment-profit figures that the four upstream handoffs rely on tie to filed statements and recompute without difference, so the gaps are source gaps, not unsupported claims. The credit conclusion is unchanged.
