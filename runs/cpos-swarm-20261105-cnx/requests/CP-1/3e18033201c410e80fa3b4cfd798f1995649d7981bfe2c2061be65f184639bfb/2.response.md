### Financial base view

CNX Resources Corp has a clear financial base for the current period: audited FY2025 statements, three unaudited 2026 quarters and a nine-month income and cash flow statement are all supplied, and the debt, facility, cash-paid and commitment notes allow facility-level work. On the management measure of trailing adjusted EBITDAX, gross and net leverage both sit below two times at September 30, 2026, cash interest is covered about eight times, and the funding position is almost entirely revolver capacity rather than cash. The maturity wall is empty until the revolvers and the CNXM notes fall due in 2029 and 2030. Three conditions qualify the view. First, the earnings measure is management's non-GAAP adjusted EBITDAX, which removes unrealized derivative gains while leaving realized settlements in; it is not the credit agreement's consolidated EBITDA, which is not supplied. Second, the parent and CNXM groups carry separate debt, covenants and guarantors, so consolidated ratios blur which group bears which claim. Third, figures from the thousand-dollar filings and the million-dollar supplements are combined only where stated, and the trailing cash-interest figures mix a full-year amount net of capitalized interest with interim amounts that carry no such label.
