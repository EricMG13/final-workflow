### Business and transaction view

CNX Resources Corp is a publicly listed, non-sponsored producer whose business is selling Appalachian natural gas, mostly from Shale and a smaller Coalbed Methane position, through short-term market-priced contracts to a few large marketers, cushioned by a hedge book and supported by owned gathering systems. For a creditor the operating model is stable in volume but exposed to price, basis and counterparty concentration, and its reported revenue includes derivative gains that are largely non-cash. The 2026 transactions are mainly liability management and shareholder returns: new 2034 notes against the repurchase of the 2029 notes, a convertible that matured into shares rather than cash, and buybacks that exceeded free cash generation in the nine months. The pack shows no sponsor, no dividend recapitalisation and no documented priming, uptier or drop-down; that absence is limited to seven filings and is not a clearance, because indentures, credit agreements and the proxy statement are not supplied. The midstream subsidiary sits in a separate credit group with its own notes and revolver, a structural feature rather than an observed behaviour.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No acquisition, sponsor transaction or merger was announced in the pack for the cutoff window. The new debt and related capital actions of the nine months ended September 30, 2026 are as follows. CNX issued 500,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.875% Senior Notes due March 2034 at par under an indenture dated February 26, 2026 with UMB Bank, N.A. as trustee, guaranteed by the restricted subsidiaries that guarantee the CNX Credit Facility; interest is payable on March 1 and September 1 beginning September 1, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:22). In the same period CNX repurchased all 500,000 of its 6.00% Senior Notes due January 2029, recording a 12,009 loss on debt extinguishment (page:22), and the supplement places that loss in Q1 2026 (Q1 supplement page:12). The 2.25% Convertible Senior Notes matured on May 1, 2026; the remaining 208,553 principal was settled through the issuance of 12,636,743 net shares and no cash was exchanged, and the related capped calls expired with 3,605,684 shares delivered to CNX and retired (page:23). The use of proceeds of the 2034 notes is Insufficient Information: the filings state both events but do not link them, so the link is an analyst reading only. The liquidity effect is visible in the balance sheet: the 2026 convertible notes and the January 2029 notes are gone, leaving the May 2029 revolvers as the nearest maturity (accepted financial handoff). The revolver borrowing base and elected commitments were unchanged at 2,400,000 and 1,400,000 on May 13, 2026 (page:19). Earlier, in the nine months ended September 30, 2025, CNX issued 200,000 of additional 7.25% Senior Notes due March 2032 at 100.5% of par (page:23) and on January 27, 2025 acquired Apex Energy II, LLC's upstream and associated midstream business for cash consideration of about 517,599 (page:17). Other 2026 capital actions: share repurchases of 432,781 in the nine months at an average price of 35.08 per share (page:35), and a July 1, 2026 sale of federal tax credits for about 30,000 net of costs, which approximated carrying value (page:18). Subsequent to September 30, 2026 and inside the pack: share count and hedge positions as of October 2026, and updated 2026 guidance in the Q3 supplement (adjusted EBITDAX 1,290 to 1,310 and capital expenditures 571 to 586, US$m, page:7). Draft or pending transactions: none in the pack.
