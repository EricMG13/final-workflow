### Financial base view

The financial base is usable for the credit view, with restrictions. The audited FY2026 statements, the Q1 FY2027 interim statements and the debt note allow every debt instrument, the maturity wall, liquidity and the claims outside debt to be set out from the filings. On an analyst basis, total debt principal is 2.58x LTM EBITDA (4.43x at FY2025) and net debt, after unrestricted cash and short-term investments, is 1.46x. Interest cover is 8.80x on cash interest. Liquidity at 2026-09-30 is 1,752.9 million of unrestricted cash and short-term investments plus 669 million of stated revolver availability.

The restrictions matter. First, no source reports EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or a covenant ratio, so every EBITDA-based ratio here is an analyst calculation and the covenant definitions are not supplied. Second, the business is in a steep ramp: operating cash flow was negative and free cash flow deeply negative in Q1 FY2027, funded by 803 million of new unsecured borrowing at foreign subsidiaries that sits outside the guarantor group. Third, claims outside reported debt are large: purchase commitments, customer prepayments and a multi-year capacity agreement that the company says may require material further cash. Fourth, the maturity schedule is only as of 2026-06-30. The NVIDIA purchase of shares for aggregate gross proceeds of 2 billion in March 2026 is the main reason FY2026 debt fell before the Q1 FY2027 rise (10-Q Q1 FY2027 page:18). Fifth, restricted cash of 583 million, almost all held by Silicon Carbide LLC for its own use, is excluded from liquidity. Sixth, the three conflicts in the upstream readiness handoff are carried: the Munich gain follows the audited annual report, and period labels in the Q1 FY2027 narrative follow the statements.
