### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Each material claim in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis handoffs was matched to a located source span or to a recomputation from located figures, and the three handoffs agree on every shared figure, period and perimeter label. The cautious credit view of the synthesis, with Medium confidence, follows from its cited basis and is not overstated. Three claims are only partly supported, all as wording or attribution rather than figures: the description of 803 million as new borrowing in the quarter, the reading that the equity raise funded the free cash flow gap or the fall in debt, and the statement that both groups of foreign facilities repay intercompany loans. One locator and one label point were found in the readiness handoff. No claim is unsupported and no module contradicts another. The integrity verdict for the evaluated set is nonetheless Restricted, not Committee Ready, because the conclusions that matter most to a creditor, namely covenant headroom, concentration in two customers, the size of the NVIDIA capacity agreement and the legal capacity to add debt, rest on documents that are not in the sources. ### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious reading is that the earnings base is growing fast: analyst EBITDA rose from 843,476 in FY2025 to 1,563,235 on an LTM basis, Q1 FY2027 EBITDA to cash interest was 17.86x, liquidity of 2,421.9 million is above the springing tests, and management states fiscal 2027 growth well above fiscal 2026. If capital additions of 775,547 in Q1 FY2027 are mainly the capacity build that peaks and then converts to EBITDA, free cash flow would recover quickly. What would make that case win: Q2 FY2027 revenue inside the guided 2.55 billion to 2.75 billion range, a lower Q2 capital additions figure than in Q1, and falling inventory growth. Downside chain with observable triggers: a pause in orders from one of the two large customers (first break: revenue and gross margin) lowers EBITDA while capital commitments of 3.4 billion for fiscal 2027 and lease and debt service stay fixed (transmission), then liquidity falls below 2,421.9 million and revolver availability below 669 million (consequence), then the springing test of 250 million plus the Term B or notes principal becomes relevant before 2029 and refinancing of the 2029 to 2031 wall is harder. Triggers to watch: customer order trends, capital additions, inventory and contract liabilities each quarter, revolver availability, and the covenant certificate when obtainable.
