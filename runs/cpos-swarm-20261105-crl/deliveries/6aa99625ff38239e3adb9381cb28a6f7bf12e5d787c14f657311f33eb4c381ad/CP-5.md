### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Each material claim in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis handoffs was matched to a located source span or to a recomputation from located figures, and the three handoffs agree on every shared figure, period and perimeter label. The credit view of the synthesis, sound but weakening on leverage with Medium confidence, follows from the cited basis and is not overstated. Two items need correction in wording, neither of which changes a figure: the synthesis states flatly that the last annual maturity table places the 2031 notes in FY2030 where the financial base says only that the amount appears to include them, and the debt register in the financial base cites the note pages that follow the debt table rather than the page holding the balances. One further point is a correction the business context handoff already makes to the financial base: the cash flow D&A line includes inventory step-up and biological asset amortization, which partly explains the gap the financial base described as unexplained. Committee use is restricted by missing evidence, not by tracing defects.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious view is that cash interest cover is 7.97x, the fixed-rate notes carry coupons of 3.75% to 4.25%, liquidity is about USD 1,257.2 million before letters of credit, DSA backlog rose to USD 2.0 billion, and only USD 2.9 million falls due before FY2028. The strongest evidence for a harsher view is that leverage is 5.53x on unadjusted EBITDA, while the 3.00x figure adds back USD 376.0 million of Q4 2025 impairments, and the covenant definition is unknown. What would make the favourable case win: a 2026 compliance statement showing headroom on the lender definition, free cash flow applied to debt rather than buybacks (USD 700.0 million remains on the authorization), and EBITDA holding after the May 2026 sales. What would make the harsh case win: a covenant definition without impairment add-backs, further revolver-funded acquisitions, or a biotech funding downturn that lowers DSA volumes before the cost programme delivers.

### Committee use

Committee use is restricted. The research may support a screening view of liquidity, near-term maturities, interest burden, business risk and the direction of leverage, with the stated labels. It should not support covenant headroom, structural priority, guarantor or recovery conclusions until the credit agreement, indentures and a 2026 compliance statement are obtained.
