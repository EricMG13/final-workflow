### Financial base view

The financial base is usable for the downstream credit work, with stated limits. Reported statements for FY2024, FY2025, Q3 2026 and nine months 2026 are extracted from the filings and agree internally where checked. Because the issuer reports no EBITDA, this handoff calculates EBITDA as operating income plus depreciation and amortization from the cash flow statement, and also shows it before the two separately printed impairment lines (goodwill and intangible asset). The second form is used in the chart tables because the impairments are non-cash and sit entirely in Q4 2025; the unadjusted ratios are shown alongside and are materially higher.

Leverage on the chosen basis is moderate and has risen since year-end, because debt grew faster than the LTM earnings base. Cash interest cover is high on both bases. The main limits are that LTM mixes pre-divestiture and post-divestiture periods without pro forma adjustment, that covenant EBITDA and indebtedness are defined in a credit agreement that is not in the pack, and that the revolver's availability net of letters of credit is not stated. Nothing in this handoff is a covenant calculation or a rating view.

### Covenants

The 10-K states two maintenance covenants under the revolving credit facility, tested over four consecutive fiscal quarters: consolidated EBITDA less capital expenditures to consolidated cash interest expense of no less than 3.50 to 1.0, and consolidated indebtedness plus principal outstanding under permitted receivables financing to consolidated EBITDA of no more than 4.25 to 1.0. The Company stated it was compliant with all financial covenants at December 27, 2025 (10-K page:152). No 2026 compliance statement or covenant figure was found (CP-0 gap, confirmed).

Indicative comparison only, on this handoff's definitions (C8): EBITDA before impairment lines less capex over cash interest is 5.75x for FY2025 and 5.81x for LTM Q3 2026 against the 3.50x floor, and total debt over EBITDA before impairment lines is 2.68x and 3.00x against the 4.25x ceiling. The covenant definitions of EBITDA and indebtedness differ from these calculations and are not in the pack. On unadjusted EBITDA, FY2025 leverage of 5.02x would exceed 4.25x while the Company reported compliance, so the covenant EBITDA must add back impairments or other items (inference from the two printed facts, not verified).

### Interpretation

Leverage. Total debt rose by 304,279 thousand between FY2025 and September 26, 2026 (C9). The 10-Q cites net proceeds of 339.0 million, primarily from the Credit Facility, and nine-month cash uses of 467,254 thousand for acquisitions, 324,105 thousand for treasury stock and 111,394 thousand for contingent and deferred consideration, partly offset by 176,563 thousand of sale proceeds (Q3 10-Q pages 7, 48). The mechanism is borrowing under the Credit Facility while acquisition, buyback and deferred-consideration outflows exceeded free cash flow, which lifts leverage on a flat earnings base. EBITDA before impairment lines rose only from 804,448 to 819,935 thousand over the same comparison.

Coverage and cash flow. Cash interest cover is high, because the three note series carry coupons of 3.75% to 4.25% and the weighted average rate on all debt is 4.11%. Free cash flow for the nine months was 341,064 thousand (C4) against 324,105 thousand of treasury stock purchases, so buybacks absorbed nearly all of it. Operating cash flow fell from 590,126 to 474,887 thousand between the two nine-month periods; the 10-Q attributes this to higher variable compensation payments and professional fees (Q3 10-Q page:46).

Liquidity. Undrawn revolver capacity fell from 1,383.5 to 1,056.8 in US$m as the draw grew. The facility matures in December 2029; letters of credit of 21.7 million may use some of it.

Maturity wall. Little matures before the 2028 notes. The 10-K table shows 2,120.297 in US$m across FY2028 to FY2030 (C10), roughly the three note series plus the revolver, so the refinancing need is concentrated in one three-year window. The revolver's higher balance moves more of that into FY2029.

Earnings quality and definitions. EBITDA is not issuer-reported. Nine-month 2026 operating income includes a 38.5 million pre-tax gain on the Wilmington site sale (Q3 10-Q page:23) and divestiture-period results; the cash flow D&A line is 40,826 thousand higher than depreciation expense plus intangible amortization (C11) and its composition is not given. Q3 2026 net income includes a large tax benefit and is not a measure of operating performance.

Perimeter. LTM figures include the divested CDMO, Cell Solutions and European Discovery businesses up to their May 2026 sales and the Cambodian NHP Supplier and PathoQuest only from January 14 and April 17, 2026. The Q3 release reports organic revenue growth of 4.7% and revenue of 982.7 million (Q3 release page:1); organic growth is a management measure.
