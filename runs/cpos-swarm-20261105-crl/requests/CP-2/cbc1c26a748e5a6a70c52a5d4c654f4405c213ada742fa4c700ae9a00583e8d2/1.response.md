### Credit view

Charles River Laboratories is a moderately leveraged but cash-generative research and testing company whose credit is sound yet weakening on leverage. Its strongest support is diversified, backlog-backed demand: no client above 4% of FY2025 revenue, a DSA backlog of USD 2.0 billion, and EBITDA before impairment lines covering cash interest 7.97x. Its weakest point is capital allocation: total debt rose to USD 2,456.5 million and leverage to 3.00x as borrowing under the revolver accompanied acquisitions and buybacks on a broadly flat earnings base. The outcome depends on whether free cash flow and divestiture proceeds go to debt reduction rather than further buybacks, and on whether covenant EBITDA, which is not defined in the pack, leaves room under the 4.25x ceiling.

### Key findings

Leverage has risen and its level depends on the EBITDA basis. Total debt of USD 2,456.5 million at September 26, 2026 compares with USD 2,152.2 million at FY2025, so total debt to EBITDA before impairment lines moved from 2.68x to 3.00x and net debt to the same EBITDA from 2.41x to 2.75x [CP-1]. Unadjusted for the Q4 2025 goodwill and intangible impairments of USD 376.0 million, the ratios are 5.02x and 5.53x. The impairments are non-cash and fall entirely in Q4 2025, which is why the adjusted basis is the primary view, but the lender definition is unknown. Covenant-compliant status at FY2025 with an unadjusted 5.02x against a 4.25x ceiling implies that covenant EBITDA adds back impairments or other items; this is an inference, not a verified fact.

Interest burden is light. Cash paid for interest was USD 102.8 million over the LTM against EBITDA before impairment lines of USD 819.9 million (7.97x; 4.32x unadjusted) [CP-1]. Three note series carry fixed coupons of 3.75% to 4.25%, and the weighted average rate on all debt was 4.11%. The floating-rate revolver of USD 943.2 million is 38.4% of total debt (C5), and the 10-K reports no swap after the fiscal 2024 swap matured, so the revolver is an unhedged rate exposure; an illustrative 100 bp move would change annual interest by about USD 9.4 million (C5).

Liquidity is adequate and the maturity profile is back-ended. Cash of USD 200.4 million plus undrawn revolver capacity of USD 1,056.8 million gives about USD 1,257.2 million before letters of credit of USD 21.7 million (C1). The revolver matures in December 2029; the last annual table shows USD 1.3 million and USD 1.5 million maturing in FY2026 and FY2027 and USD 501.3 million in FY2028 [CP-1]. The USD 2,120.3 million across FY2028 to FY2030 is concentrated in one window, and the larger revolver draw moves more of it into FY2029.

Cash conversion funds operations but not the 2026 programme. LTM free cash flow of USD 399.6 million is below FY2025's USD 518.5 million [CP-1]. In nine months 2026 acquisitions of USD 467.3 million and treasury stock of USD 324.1 million, together USD 791.4 million, exceeded free cash flow of USD 341.1 million by USD 450.3 million (C2); sale proceeds of USD 176.6 million and USD 58.1 million from surrendered life insurance policies offset part, and the revolver rose USD 326.7 million. The mechanism is shareholder returns and M&A outrunning internal cash generation, so leverage tolerance is a management choice, and the pages read set no leverage target [CP-1A].

Covenant headroom cannot be confirmed. The revolver carries a minimum 3.50x test of EBITDA less capital expenditures to cash interest and a maximum 4.25x test of indebtedness to EBITDA, and the issuer stated compliance only at December 27, 2025 [CP-1]. On this handoff's definitions the cover measure is 5.81x and leverage 3.00x for the LTM, which look comfortable, but the covenant definitions differ and are not in the pack, so no 2026 headroom can be calculated.

Business risk is moderate and backlog-supported. No client exceeded 4% of FY2025 revenue, DSA backlog was USD 2.0 billion at September 26, 2026 against USD 1.9 billion at FY2025, and DSA revenue is recognized over time as work is performed [CP-1A]. The risks are client funding and study cancellations, which carry no termination fee in many contracts, and the animal-sourcing exposure that led to government investigations now closed [CP-1A]. A securities class action is pending with no loss estimate.

Claims outside the debt figure add to the burden. Operating lease liabilities were USD 491.7 million at FY2025, unconditional purchase obligations about USD 340 million, contingent consideration USD 45.0 million, unfunded venture capital commitments about USD 49.4 million (C3), and the issuer may fund up to USD 45.0 million of the divested CDMO businesses' losses and capital spending [CP-1, CP-1A]. None is in the debt total.

Earnings quality needs care. LTM EBITDA is calculated from operating income and cash flow D&A, includes the divested businesses up to May 2026, and includes a USD 38.5 million gain on the Wilmington site sale in nine months 2026 [CP-1]. Management savings targets are claims, not results [CP-1A]. Q3 2026 net income includes a large tax benefit and is not an operating measure.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. (1) Diversified customer base with no client above 4% of FY2025 revenue and DSA backlog of USD 2.0 billion [Documentary Fact, CP-1A]. (2) High interest cover of 7.97x on EBITDA before impairment lines and a majority of debt in low-coupon fixed-rate notes [Documentary Fact for the inputs, Analyst Interpretation for the ratio basis, CP-1]. (3) Large undrawn revolver capacity of USD 1,056.8 million with maturity in December 2029 and little principal due before FY2028 [Documentary Fact, CP-1]. (4) Positive free cash flow of USD 399.6 million over the LTM [Documentary Fact inputs, calculated, CP-1]. (5) Resolution of the DOJ and SEC investigations in 2025 removes a legal overhang [Documentary Fact, CP-1A].

Weaknesses. (1) Leverage rose to 3.00x on the primary basis and is 5.53x unadjusted, with covenant EBITDA unverified [Analyst Interpretation, CP-1]. (2) Capital allocation favors acquisitions and buybacks funded partly by the revolver, and debt repayment is listed among several uses without a target [Documentary Fact, CP-1A]. (3) The revolver is floating-rate and unhedged on the 10-K evidence, and the FY2028 to FY2030 maturities are concentrated [Documentary Fact, CP-1]. (4) Earnings carry restructuring charges of USD 73.8 million in nine months 2026, large impairments in Q4 2025, and pending savings claims [Documentary Fact and Management Language, CP-1A]. (5) The legal structure is unknown: guarantors, security of the notes and covenant definitions are Insufficient Information because the agreements are not supplied [CP-0].
