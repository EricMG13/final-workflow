### Document conflicts and anomalies found

1. Unit conflict: the Q3 2025 file states "(in thousands, except per share data)" while the 10-K and the 2026 filings state millions. Effect: any series that joins the Q3 2025 figures to later periods must rescale before use; CP-0 did not rescale.
2. Within the Q2 2026 release, depreciation and amortization for the three months ended June 30, 2025 appears as "Depreciation and amortization 1,393 560" in the cash flow statement on page:6 and as "Depreciation and amortization 1,393 559" in the adjusted EBITDA reconciliation on page:7. The one-unit difference sits in the prior-year column. Effect: low, but the downstream modules should cite one location and name the other.
3. The 2032 convertible notes show a stated rate of 1.75% in the issuance table (10-Q 2026-06-30, page:28) and an effective rate of 2% in the debt table (page:26). These are different measures (stated versus effective), not a conflict, but both must be labelled.
4. The Q2 2026 release states backlog as approximately $104 billion and the filing states $103.7 billion of unsatisfied remaining performance obligations. The release defines backlog as including "other amounts we estimate will be recognized as revenue in future periods", so the two are different measures; the filing controls for remaining performance obligations.
