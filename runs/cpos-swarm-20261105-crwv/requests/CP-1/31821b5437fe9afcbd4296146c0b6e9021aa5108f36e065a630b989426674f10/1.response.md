### Financial base view

The financial base is sufficient to describe the June 30, 2026 position and the path from FY2025, with stated limits. The audited FY2025 statements, three quarterly filings and three releases tie to each other: six months 2026 revenue of $4,653 million equals Q1 2026 plus Q2 2026, and the debt table sums to the printed total. The picture is of fast, debt-financed growth. Revenue in Q2 2026 was $2,575 million against $1,212 million a year earlier, adjusted EBITDA was $1,510 million, but the company still reported an operating loss of $(49) million and net interest expense of $(640) million in the quarter. Principal debt of $35,551 million (about 89.5 percent recourse by principal, calculated below) is mostly secured, project-level delayed-draw debt plus unsecured notes and convertibles. Leverage on LTM adjusted EBITDA is 8.08x gross and 6.82x net of unrestricted cash and marketable securities, and cash interest cover is 3.35x; both are flattered by the EBITDA definition (it adds back stock compensation and acquisition costs, and is struck after operating lease cost, so lease cost is not in the add-backs) and by cash interest being net of capitalized interest. Debt at period end also funds assets not yet earning revenue, so period-end debt over trailing earnings overstates run-rate leverage, and the contracted backlog (remaining performance obligations of $103.7 billion) is the offsetting evidence. The main restrictions are that financial covenant levels are not printed, so headroom is Not calculable, that guarantors are not named, and that nothing after the Q2 2026 filing except the August 2026 events in the filing note is in the pack.
