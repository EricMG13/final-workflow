### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Each material claim in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis handoffs was matched to a located source span or to a recomputation from located figures, and the three handoffs agree on every shared figure, period and perimeter label. The credit view of the synthesis, a strong standalone profile that is conditional on a pending acquisition, with Medium confidence, follows from its cited basis and is not overstated. Three points need wording care rather than correction. The headline liquidity figure includes cash and a facility that belong to the consolidated CVR Partners entity, and the synthesis mentions the cash but not the facility. The chief executive change is inferred from exhibits dated June 18, 2026 and from who is quoted in the April and July releases, not from a stated effective date. The unsecured status of the 2031 and 2034 notes rests on the guarantee wording, with the title of the 2028 notes stating it directly. No claim is unsupported and no contradiction between modules was found. The integrity verdict for the evaluated set is Restricted, not Committee Ready, because the conclusions that matter most to a creditor after the transaction, namely covenant headroom, ranking after closing and pro forma leverage, rest on documents and earnings that are not in the sources. The review itself is limited: it was done in the same session as the analysis, from the same extraction text, and page locators are extraction chunks rather than printed pages.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a cautious reading is that nine-month 2026 free cash flow of 872 and trailing Adjusted EBITDA of 919 are real cash and earnings, that debt is little changed, that no note principal is due before 2028, and that the controlling holder has committed equity. The evidence against a comfortable reading is that the improvement comes from the numerator. What would make the supportive case win: a permanent financing and equity package that holds post-closing leverage near the standalone level, clearance of the antitrust review, and margins staying above mid-cycle through the first quarters after closing. Downside chain with observable triggers: the initiating condition is a normalization of crack spreads or an end to Middle East supply disruption, which cuts refining margin per barrel first, then Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow; the financial transmission is a return of leverage toward the FY2025 level (4.53x on current debt in the CP-2 sensitivity), with RFS purchases, derivative settlements and tax payments drawing on the 1,853 of liquidity; the refinancing consequence is the 364-day bridge and the 733 maturing in 2028. Triggers to watch: Group 3 crack spreads, RIN prices and the D.C. Circuit outcomes, antitrust clearance, launch and pricing of the permanent financing, and the covenant certificates once obtainable.
