### Financial base view

On the company's own measure, leverage is moderate and falling. Total debt including finance leases is 5,261 at September 30, 2026 against LTM Adjusted EBITDA of 2,752, a ratio of 1.91x, and net of unrestricted cash of 3,049 the ratio is 0.80x. The ratio has fallen at each of the four balance sheet dates read (FY2025 2.29x, Q1 2026 2.14x, Q2 2026 2.03x, Q3 2026 1.91x), driven by LTM EBITDA growth from 2,237 to 2,752 on debt that moved little. The headline understates three features. First, 1,125 of the debt is secured asset-based financing (floor plan, financing of retained securitization interests, sale-leaseback real estate financing and fleet financing), which is not corporate funded debt; the senior notes, term loan and unsecured notes total 4,036 (1.47x). Second, Adjusted EBITDA is a company-defined measure that includes loan sale gains (1,084 in nine months 2026, 48.4% of the nine-month Adjusted EBITDA of 2,241) and adds back share-based compensation, while operating cash flow for those nine months was 900. Third, claims outside the debt figure add to fixed obligations: the tax receivable agreement liability (2,230), operating leases (469), and trade floor plan payables (300) that sit in accounts payable.

Interest cover on cash interest is above 5x at every date but is lowest at Q3 2026 (5.88x), because cash interest paid in the nine months rose to 443 from 311 as the former payment-in-kind coupons turned to cash, even though interest expense, net fell to 298 from 407. The August 2026 refinancing (a 1,660 term loan at Term SOFR plus 2.25%, replacing 1,660 of 9% notes) lowers the stated coupon, but the interest rate swap hedges only to July 31, 2028 and the term loan matures in 2033.

Liquidity is strong on cash (3,049) but the borrowing capacity is collateral-limited: revolving commitments are 5,600 and outstanding 71, yet availability under the borrowing base is 2,071. Most commitments mature within twelve months of the balance sheet date. No financial maintenance covenant applies to the term loan, and compliance with all debt covenants is stated; covenant levels and headroom for the other facilities are Not supplied. The view is a base for the credit snapshot, not a credit conclusion; the four limits above and the gaps in `## Gaps` apply to every use of it.

### Credit analysis

- Leverage. Evidence: total debt including finance leases over LTM Adjusted EBITDA fell from 2.29x to 1.91x across four dates, while net debt fell from 2,806 to 2,212 as cash rose. Mechanism: EBITDA growth outpaced a flat debt stock, and the refinancing was debt neutral (1,660 in, 1,660 out) with a premium of 50 paid from cash. Implication: leverage is moderate on the company measure; of the debt, 1,125 is asset-based, so the measure mixes funding of inventory, receivables and property with corporate debt. Excluding asset-based debt, corporate funded debt is 4,036 (1.47x). Including trade floor plan payables (300) the ratio is 2.02x.
- Coverage. Evidence: cash interest rose to 443 in nine months from 311 while interest expense, net fell to 298 from 407. Mechanism: the secured notes moved from payment-in-kind to cash coupons during 2025, so reported expense fell while cash paid rose; the refinancing then reduced the stated coupon on 1,660. Implication: cash cover of 5.88x is the right measure; it is the lowest of the four dates, so cover did not improve with leverage. Comparisons across the refinancing are not like for like.
- Cash flow quality. Evidence: nine months operating cash flow 900 against Adjusted EBITDA 2,241; vehicle inventory used 1,025 and accounts payable and accrued liabilities provided 475. Mechanism: growth consumes working capital, and Adjusted EBITDA includes non-cash gain on loan sales (1,084) whose cash arrives through receivable sales in operating cash flow. Implication: free cash flow after purchases of property and equipment was 734 in nine months 2026 (900 less 166), well below EBITDA; the credit depends on continued access to receivable sale and financing markets.
- Liquidity. Evidence: cash 3,049 and availability 2,071; maturities cluster within twelve months. Mechanism: revolvers are borrowing-base limited and renewed routinely, and the receivable sale arrangements are committed with terms into 2027 and early 2028. Implication: day-to-day liquidity is adequate; a market disruption that cuts renewals or sale capacity would hit the 5,600 revolving lines and the sale capacity together.
- Data quality and definition risks. Evidence: the redemption description differs between two sections of the Q3 report (see conflict below); the maturity table is stale; LTM figures are derived. Implication: figures that depend on these are labelled and carry the limitations in `## Gaps`.
