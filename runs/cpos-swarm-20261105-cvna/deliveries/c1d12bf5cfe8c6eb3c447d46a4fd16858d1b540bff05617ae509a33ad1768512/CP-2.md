### Credit view

Carvana has a sound but market-dependent fundamental credit profile: its debt is moderate against earnings, but those earnings depend on consumers, loan buyers and funding lines that can tighten together. The strongest support is financial. Total debt including finance leases of USD 5,261 million is 1.91x LTM Adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,752 million, and unrestricted cash of USD 3,049 million brings net leverage to 0.80x. The weakest point is quality and structure. Adjusted EBITDA includes loan sale gains equal to 48.4% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA, operating cash flow trails EBITDA, and the control and related-party dealings of the Garcia Parties cannot be tested because the governing documents are not supplied. Continued access to loan sales and renewal of revolvers decide the outcome.

### Key findings

1. Moderate and improving leverage on a company-defined measure (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 leverage chart table and Calculations). Carvana's total debt including finance leases was USD 5,261 million at Q3 2026 against LTM Adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,752 million, a ratio of 1.91x, down from 2.29x at FY2025; net of USD 3,049 million of unrestricted cash the ratio is 0.80x. The mechanism is EBITDA growth on a flat debt stock, so the improvement reflects earnings rather than repayment. Of the debt, USD 1,125 million is asset-based financing; corporate funded debt of USD 4,036 million is 1.47x. Including trade floor plan payables of USD 300 million the ratio is 2.02x.
2. Earnings quality is the central financial weakness (Analyst Interpretation; basis: CP-1 Credit analysis on cash flow quality and CP-1A Calculations). Loan sale gains of USD 1,084 million were 48.4% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,241 million, while operating cash flow was USD 900 million, 40.2% of that EBITDA, and free cash flow after purchases of property and equipment was USD 734 million. The mechanism is that gains are recognised when loans are sold and the cash arrives through sale proceeds, with growth absorbing working capital. A pull-back by loan buyers would cut gain on sale and cash conversion at the same time.
3. Cash interest cover is adequate but did not improve with leverage (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 interest cover chart table). Cover was 5.88x at Q3 2026, the lowest of four dates, because cash interest paid rose as former payment-in-kind coupons turned to cash, even though interest expense, net fell. The August 2026 refinancing lowers the stated coupon, but the interest hedge ends on July 31, 2028 while the term loan runs to 2033.
4. Liquidity is strong on cash and conditional on collateral (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 liquidity chart table and liquidity by facility). Carvana held USD 3,049 million of cash and USD 2,071 million of borrowing-base availability against USD 5,600 million of revolving commitments, of which USD 71 million was drawn; commitments of USD 3,900 million mature within twelve months after the October 2026 extension. The mechanism is renewal risk on lines that fund inventory and receivables; a market shock that cuts renewals and loan-sale capacity together would hit both sources.
5. Control and related-party dependence cannot be tested (Documentary Fact for the facts, Insufficient Information for capacity; basis: CP-1A ownership, governance and operating model rows). The Garcia Parties hold about 83% of the voting power, and DriveTime, which they own, administers service contracts and warranties; its commissions are 21.2% of other sales and revenues and 8.4% of gross profit for the nine months. Ally, which lends on the floor plan, bought about 41.0% of the principal of loans sold. The documented record since 2023 is supportive (equity purchase, equity-funded note redemption, no dividends), but the agreements that would limit extraction or priming are not in the pack.
6. Claims outside the debt figure add to fixed obligations (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 Claims outside the debt figure). The tax receivable agreement liability is USD 2,230 million, operating lease liabilities USD 469 million, and trade floor plan payables USD 300 million, equal to the size of that line. None is in the leverage ratio.
7. The term loan refinancing was debt neutral (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 Latest quarter and CP-1A Transaction summary). A USD 1,660 million term loan priced at Term SOFR plus 2.25% replaced USD 1,660 million of 9% secured notes, moving the maturity to 2033 and ranking pari passu with the 2031 secured notes. No financial maintenance covenant applies to the term loan, so headroom is not measurable.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Carvana has scale and momentum: retail units rose 39.4% in Q3 2026, and Adjusted EBITDA reached USD 800 million in the quarter (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1A operating model row on volume and CP-1 financial table). Management describes the platform as the leading one in its market, which is Management Language and is not adopted. Carvana has liquidity depth, with USD 3,049 million of cash and a broad set of revolving and loan-sale lines (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 liquidity tables and CP-1A loan sale model row). Its leverage has fallen at each of four balance sheet dates and its term-out of the 2030 secured notes removed the largest near-dated bullet shown in the printed schedule (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 leverage and maturities chart tables). The documented conduct of the controlling holders since 2023 is supportive: equity support, equity-funded redemption and no dividends (Analyst Interpretation; basis: CP-1A governance and sponsor behaviour table).

Weaknesses. Carvana's earnings depend on gain on sale: loan sale gains are 48.4% of nine-month Adjusted EBITDA, and other revenue carries no cost of sales, so margin follows funding markets (Analyst Interpretation; basis: CP-1 Credit analysis and CP-1A operating model rows). Unit economics are thinner: total gross profit per unit fell to USD 6,997 in Q3 2026 from USD 7,362, and Adjusted EBITDA margin was 10.0% on an LTM basis against 11.0% in FY2025 (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1A unit economics row and CP-1 financial table). Management attributes the decline to giving back lower loan rates, which is Management Language. Carvana's funding is short-dated and collateral-limited, with counterparty concentration in Ally and DriveTime (Documentary Fact; basis: CP-1 liquidity by facility and CP-1A Calculations). Control by one group, related-party dealings and a tax receivable liability to related parties are not constrained by any document in the pack (Insufficient Information on capacity; basis: CP-1A ownership and governance rows). Covenant levels, guarantor schedules and a post-refinancing maturity schedule are missing, so protection and refinancing risk cannot be measured (Insufficient Information; basis: CP-0 and CP-1 gaps).
