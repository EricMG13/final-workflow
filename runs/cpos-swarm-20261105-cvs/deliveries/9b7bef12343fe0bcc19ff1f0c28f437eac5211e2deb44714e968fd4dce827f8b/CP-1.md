### Financial base view

The financial base is usable and shows improving credit metrics, with limits that the committee should hold alongside the ratios. On adjusted EBITDA built as adjusted operating income plus depreciation and amortization less amortization of intangibles, total debt to EBITDA is 3.78x at FY2025 and 3.06x on LTM Q3 2026, net debt to EBITDA 3.29x and 2.47x, and EBITDA to cash interest 5.71x and 6.51x. Both parts of the leverage move contributed: total debt carrying value fell from 64,570 to 60,680 while LTM adjusted EBITDA rose from 17,073 to 19,837. The LTM window now excludes the large 2025 add-backs, so reported and adjusted EBITDA are close on LTM Q3 2026 (19,581 against 19,837), whereas at FY2025 they differ by 7,807, mainly a non-cash goodwill impairment of 5,725. The company reports no EBITDA and the filings disclose no covenant definition or level, so every ratio here is an analyst construct and headroom is Not calculable. Cash is flattered as a deleveraging input: consolidated cash of 11,738 includes cash held outside the parent, and net debt on the parent and nonrestricted cash of about 5,900 gives 2.76x rather than 2.47x. Cash flow from operations for nine months 2026 was 15,318 against 7,249 a year earlier, with the Q3 10-Q attributing the increase to timing of receipts and payments and improved Health Care Benefits performance, so the LTM free cash flow of 15,567 is not a steady-state run rate. Claims outside the debt figure include an opioid settlement accrual of about 3,400, operating lease liabilities of 14,617, the Omnicare guarantee charge of 193 and an announced separation whose financing terms are not supplied.

### EBITDA definitions and add-back review

- No source reports EBITDA or a covenant EBITDA; a search of all seven sources for EBITDA found no hit. Both EBITDA measures here are analyst constructs. The company's own measure is adjusted operating income, which is defined as GAAP operating income excluding amortization of intangible assets, net realized capital gains or losses and other items that neither relate to the ordinary course of business nor reflect underlying performance (Q3 10-Q page:35).
- Reported EBITDA is GAAP operating income plus depreciation and amortization from the cash flow statement. It carries every one-off through the period. In FY2025 it is depressed by the goodwill impairment of 5,725 recorded in Q3 2025.
- Adjusted EBITDA is adjusted operating income plus depreciation and amortization less amortization of intangible assets, because adjusted operating income already adds back that amortization and adding the whole of D&A would count it twice. The check is that adjusted EBITDA less reported EBITDA equals the add-backs other than amortization of intangibles: 7,807 for FY2025 and 256 for LTM Q3 2026.
- FY2025 add-backs other than amortization (10-K page:138): net realized capital losses 44, acquisition-related integration costs 117, goodwill impairment 5,725, Health Care Delivery clinic closure 83, opioid litigation charge 320, office real estate 10, legacy litigation charges 1,220 and loss on Accountable Care assets 288. The litigation items are cash claims, so adjusted EBITDA overstates cash earnings available for debt service in the periods they are paid.
- LTM Q3 2026 add-backs other than amortization of 256 comprise the Omnicare guarantee of 193 and 63 of realized capital losses and integration costs; the 2025 items have rolled out of the window.
- Net investment income of 2,456 on an LTM basis (FY2025 2,233 plus nine months 2026 1,862 less nine months 2025 1,639) is part of revenue and operating income, so EBITDA includes insurance investment income.
- Departures from a covenant definition cannot be assessed because none is disclosed. The only covenant statements are that the facilities and unsecured senior notes contain customary restrictive financial and operating covenants, that they do not accelerate on a downgrade, and that the Company was in compliance at September 30, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:64).

### Cash flow quality

- Nine months 2026 operating cash flow was 15,318 against 7,249. The Q3 10-Q (page:63) says the increase was primarily due to the timing of receipts and payments and improved operating performance in Health Care Benefits. Accounts receivable used 2,254 against 7,537 a year earlier and other liabilities provided 2,810 (page:4), so working capital swung. Full-year 2026 guidance is cash flow from operations of at least 15.5 billion (Q3 release page:1).
- LTM operating cash flow of 18,708 is below LTM adjusted EBITDA of 19,837; LTM free cash flow of 15,567 less dividends of 3,423 leaves 12,144. In nine months 2026 the Company repaid 4,057 of long-term debt, issued none, and cash rose from 8,453 to 11,738. No share repurchases appear in the nine months 2026 or 2025 cash flow statements.
- LTM interest paid has stayed between 2,991 and 3,070 across the four dates, so the cover improvement comes from the numerator, not from lower interest.
