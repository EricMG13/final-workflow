### Business and transaction view

The credit story is a large, lease-heavy casino operator with a diversified but physically concentrated revenue base, entering a change of control on terms the sources describe only in part. Regional properties and Las Vegas supply about 85% of net revenues, Caesars Digital is the growing third leg, and the VICI and GLPI real estate leases make the company a tenant at 25 of the 47 casinos it owns or leases. Ownership today is public, with about 273 holders of record, no sponsor named in the context and no dividend history. The pending merger converts each share into cash of $31.00 plus a ticking fee after June 26, 2027, was approved by stockholders on September 22, 2026, and remains subject to antitrust and gaming approvals with an outside date that can run to November 27, 2027. For creditors, the open questions are who finances the purchase, whether the existing notes and loans stay in place, and whether leverage rises; the sources state that financing risk exists but give no terms. Until the Merger Agreement, proxy statement and financing papers are supplied, the transaction can be described but not assessed for debt effects.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transaction is a pending cash acquisition of Caesars Entertainment, Inc. On May 27, 2026 the company, Fertitta Gaming Holdco, LLC, Empire Merger Sub, Inc., Landry's Fertitta, LLC (for Section 9.14 of the agreement only) and Hospitality Headquarters, Inc. (for Section 9.14(j) only) signed an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which Merger Sub merges into the company and the company survives as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Fertitta Gaming (Q3 10-Q page:6). The earnings releases state that the agreement is with `Fertitta Entertainment, Inc.` and that it was announced on May 28, 2026 (Q3 release page:4). The two names are logged below as an unresolved conflict. Each share other than excluded, parent rollover and dissenting shares converts into $31.00 in cash plus, if closing has not occurred by June 26, 2027, a ticking fee of $0.007150 per day from the first day of the following month; parent rollover shares convert into shares of the surviving corporation, and the sources do not disclose a rollover percentage (Q3 10-Q page:7). Restricted stock units are cancelled for cash at the same consideration (Q3 10-Q page:7).

The status is stockholder-approved and awaiting regulatory conditions. The company filed its proxy statement on August 26, 2026 and the proposals were approved at a special meeting on September 22, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:7). Closing remains conditioned on expiry or termination of the HSR Act waiting period and receipt of certain gaming regulatory approvals; the filings do not say which approvals have been received (Q3 10-Q page:8 and page:62). The initial end date is May 27, 2027, extended automatically to August 27, 2027 and then November 27, 2027 if only those regulatory conditions remain open (Q3 10-Q page:8). A termination fee of $200 million is payable by the company in specified circumstances and a reverse termination fee of $450 million by Fertitta Gaming in others, including antitrust or gaming law prohibitions or the end date occurring with only regulatory conditions open (Q3 10-Q page:9). A no-shop has applied since July 11, 2026 (Q3 10-Q page:8). On completion the common stock is delisted from NASDAQ and deregistered, which ends the public filings that this credit view relies on (Q3 10-Q page:10).

For debt, the sources give no use of proceeds, sources and uses, new debt raised, financing commitments, equity contribution or liquidity effect. The filings list `risks associated with increased leverage from the proposed transaction` and `the risk that the financing required to fund the proposed transaction is not obtained on the terms anticipated or at all` among forward-looking risks (Q3 10-Q page:57), and the company represented its `present debt financing and covenants` in the agreement (Q3 10-Q page:8). They do not state whether the existing CEI Credit Agreement, the CEI notes or the CVA facilities are repaid, assumed, amended or subject to a change-of-control offer at closing. That absence is Insufficient Information, not an adverse conclusion. The company incurred transaction costs related to the merger of 10 for Q2 2026 and 12 for Q3 2026, 22 for nine months 2026 (Q2 10-Q page:9; Q3 10-Q page:10).

New debt issues: none appear in the nine months to September 30, 2026. The debt table lists no instrument issued in 2026, and proceeds from long-term debt and revolving credit facilities of 1,335 against repayments of 1,581 reflect revolver activity and scheduled repayment (Q3 10-Q page:5). The last term debt issues in the filings are the CEI Senior Notes due 2032 of $1.1 billion at 6.00% on October 17, 2024, the CEI Senior Secured Notes due 2032 of $1.5 billion at 6.50% and the CEI Term Loan B-1 of $2.9 billion on February 6, 2024, and the Caesars Virginia facilities of April 26, 2024 (Q3 10-Q page:18 to page:20). The CEI Senior Notes due 2027 of $546 million were redeemed on July 8, 2025 with revolver borrowings and note proceeds (10-K page:80). Merger-related debt is not described.
