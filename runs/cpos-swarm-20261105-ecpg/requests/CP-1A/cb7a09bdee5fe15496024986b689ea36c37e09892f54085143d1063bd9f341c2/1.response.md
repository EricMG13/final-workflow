### Business and transaction view

For ENCORE CAPITAL GROUP INC the business is a consumer debt purchaser and collector that buys charged-off receivables at deep discounts, collects through call centers, digital channels and legal action, and recognises revenue from portfolio yield plus forecast-driven changes in recoveries. The United States business (MCM) is the dominant revenue and purchasing engine and Europe (Cabot, with the United Kingdom, France and Spain) is smaller and growing more slowly, with competitive pricing limiting European deployment. The company is a widely held listed company with no sponsor or controlling owner named in the sources, which removes sponsor extraction and sponsor support as drivers but leaves shareholder returns, funded in part from the revolver, as the capital allocation question. There is no live acquisition or sponsor transaction. The transaction record is a sequence of refinancings in 2025 and 2026 that extended the senior secured note maturities to 2031 to 2033 for the new issues, replaced a high coupon note, and, in the third quarter of 2026, retired the convertible notes in cash from revolver drawings; the effect on liquidity and leverage is in the financial base handoff. Credit-relevant business risks are forecast and pricing risk in a business where revenue recognition depends on collection forecasts, regulatory and litigation exposure that is continuing but not reserved, supply concentration among a few large sellers whose shares are not disclosed, and a heavy reliance on forward flow agreements that can be repriced or terminated. The view is Medium confidence because ownership, seller concentration and the executed financing terms are not in the sources.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

There is no sponsor transaction, merger, disposal or leveraged recapitalisation in the sources; the transaction record is financing activity only, and each item is completed unless stated. In the fourth quarter of 2025 the company issued 500.0 US$m of 6.625% Senior Secured Notes due April 2031 at an issue price of 100.000% and used a portion of proceeds to pay down revolver drawings (10-K page:69 "we issued $500.0 million 6.625% in senior secured notes that mature in 2031"); settled its 100.0 US$m 2025 convertible notes in cash for 106.2 US$m using revolver borrowings (10-K page:109 "In October 2025, the Company settled its $100.0 million 2025 Convertible Notes"); and repaid 100.0 EUR m of the 2028 Floating Rate Notes with revolver borrowings in November 2025 (10-K page:70). In 2026, on 2026-05-22 the company issued 750.0 US$m of 6.625% Senior Secured Notes due June 2032 at 100.000% and used proceeds to redeem in full the 500.0 US$m 9.250% notes due 2029, with a loss on extinguishment of 28.0 US$m including a 23.1 US$m make-whole premium (Q3 10-Q page:22 "Encore 2032 Notes"). On 2026-05-28 it issued 325.0 EUR m of floating rate notes due July 2033 at three-month EURIBOR plus 3.250% and, with revolver drawings, redeemed the 415.0 EUR m 2028 Floating Rate Notes, with a loss on extinguishment of 2.5 US$m (Q3 10-Q page:22). The nine month loss on extinguishment of 30,533 thousand is the sum of these two items and was booked in Q2 2026 (Q2 10-Q page:3). Management said the Q2 result absorbed 1.00 US$ per share of refinancing costs (Q2 release page:1 "includes $1.00 per share of refinancing costs"), which is management language. On 2026-07-22 the company called its 230.0 US$m 4.000% convertible notes due 2029 and on 2026-09-24 settled them in cash for 367.9 US$m funded by revolver borrowings, unwinding the capped call hedges for 31.8 US$m of proceeds; the 137.9 US$m excess over principal was charged to equity (Q3 10-Q page:24). On 2026-08-17 the U.S. Facility was extended from October 2028 to October 2029 and its margin reduced from 3.50% to 3.00% (Q3 10-Q page:25). In October 2026, after the balance sheet date and before the cutoff, new interest rate swaps with 150.0 US$m notional replaced swaps that matured on 2026-10-27 (Q3 10-Q page:14). The use of proceeds was refinancing in every case; the effect on liquidity and leverage is that debt rose over 2026 and revolver availability fell to 510.2 US$m at 2026-09-30 (financial base handoff). In the equity-side actions, the company repurchased 73.4 US$m of stock in the nine months to 2026-09-30 (Q3 10-Q page:58), expected to be funded from cash and revolver drawings. The company has not announced a pending transaction in the sources read, and the only acquisition-type event in the sources is the December 2024 step-up acquisition of EARC, with 11.3 US$m of goodwill recognised (10-K page:128).

### Credit translations

Risk mechanic: legal-channel collections carry court and fee costs and conduct risk. Implication: margin gains depend on the legal channel remaining productive; operating cash flow is small relative to Adjusted EBITDA because purchases are in investing cash flow (financial base handoff).
- Concentration risk: Evidence: purchases may be concentrated with a few large sellers (10-K page:98), with no shares disclosed. Risk mechanic: a seller exit would cut supply and pricing leverage. Implication: Insufficient Information; seller shares are a priority monitoring datapoint.
- Sponsor alignment: Evidence: no sponsor or controlling holder is named (10-K page:45). Risk mechanic: no sponsor support to cure a liquidity shortfall and no sponsor extraction. Implication: creditors rely on internal cash generation and market access; the repeated 2025 and 2026 refinancings show access but are not a commitment.
- Key uncertainties: executed covenant terms, seller concentration, large holders, target leverage and ratings, all not supplied.
