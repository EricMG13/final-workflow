### Credit view

Encore Capital Group's credit profile is improving but still highly levered: earnings and collections have grown faster than debt through 2026, yet the company remains a refinancing-dependent debt purchaser. The strongest support is last twelve months leverage of 5.13x total debt to Adjusted EBITDA at Q3 2026, down from 5.96x at FY2025, with cash interest cover of 3.07x, no scheduled maturity before 2028 in the notes tables and a long-dated senior secured note stack. The weakest point is earnings quality and liquidity: Adjusted EBITDA includes forecast-driven revenue, portfolio purchases sit outside it, and revolver availability fell to USD 510.2 million after the convertible notes were settled in cash. What decides the outcome is whether collections keep beating forecasts and whether the revolver is rebuilt rather than drawn further. [CP-1] [CP-1A: Revenue recognition and forecast risk]

### Key findings

1. Leverage fell on the company's own measure while debt rose. Evidence: total debt to Adjusted EBITDA moved from 5.96x at FY2025 to 5.13x at Q3 2026 on a last twelve months basis, with principal debt rising from USD 4,033,394 thousand to USD 4,267,981 thousand and last twelve months Adjusted EBITDA rising from USD 676,958 thousand to USD 831,829 thousand [CP-1]. Risk mechanic: the gain comes from earnings growth, not from debt reduction. Credit implication: leverage is falling because collections are strong; it would rise again if earnings fell, since debt is not being repaid. Category: Documentary Fact for the figures, Analyst Interpretation for the reading.

2. Cash interest cover improved and is adequate on a last twelve months basis. Evidence: Adjusted EBITDA over cash paid for interest was 3.07x at Q3 2026 against 2.61x at FY2025 [CP-1]. Risk mechanic: cash interest is paid on semi-annual coupon dates, so single quarters mislead and the last twelve months basis is the right one. Credit implication: debt service is covered more than three times on the company measure, but the measure is before the portfolio purchases that sustain collections. Category: Documentary Fact.

3. Revolver headroom narrowed after the convertible settlement. Evidence: issuer-stated availability under the Global Senior Facility moved from USD 814.3 million at FY2025 to USD 510.2 million at Q3 2026, the revolver draw rose from USD 691,648 thousand at Q2 2026 to USD 974,763 thousand at Q3 2026, and utilisation was 65.6% against 42.6% at FY2025; the other facilities are drawn to their commitments [CP-1]. Risk mechanic: purchases, refinancing and share repurchases are all funded from the revolver, and the loan to value covenant applies once utilisation exceeds 20%, so it is live. Credit implication: liquidity is thinner than a year ago and covenant headroom cannot be measured. Category: Documentary Fact; the covenant gap is Insufficient Information.
