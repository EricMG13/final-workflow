### Business and transaction view

Eldorado's business is a producing gold group with polymetallic by-products, now adding two copper-bearing mines through commissioning at the same time. Revenue in the first nine months of 2026 came mainly from gold, in three geographic segments, with Canada the largest after the Foran acquisition. The operating mines carry the credit today: Lamaque and Olympias improved, while Kisladag and Efemcukuru weakened on lower grades, and cost guidance was raised. The transaction that matters to a creditor is the all-share purchase of Foran, which closed on April 14, 2026: it added McIlvenna Bay and debt assumed of 355,321 (US$ thousand, recorded at fair value), mainly the Sprott facility, which is secured on the acquired assets with a company guarantee, plus an equipment facility. No new notes were issued in the period; new funding was revolver draws, the final overrun draw on the Skouries project loan and the acquired debt. Management describes Skouries as fully funded; that is a management claim, and the pack does not include a funding model. Skouries first concentrate was achieved on September 8, 2026 and McIlvenna Bay first copper concentrate in June 2026, but both commercial production dates are late in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the capital estimates and 2026 production guidance have moved adversely. Ownership, control rights and any shareholder agreements are not disclosed in the pack. Behaviour is assessed on documented actions only: a dividend programme was started and buybacks continued during the build, leadership changed on September 30, 2026, and no priming, drop-down or other creditor-adverse action is documented. Source evidence is primary and dated, so evidence quality is High for filed statements and Medium for management releases.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The one transaction in the period is the acquisition of Foran Mining Corporation, completed on April 14, 2026 by court-approved plan of arrangement. The Q3 statements say the Company acquired 100% of the issued and outstanding common shares of Foran for 0.1128 Eldorado common shares and C$0.01 in cash for each Foran share (Q3 statements page:8: "0.1128 Eldorado common shares and C$0.01 in cash for each Foran common share"). Total consideration transferred was 2,394,995 (US$ thousand), of which share consideration was 2,385,625, cash 4,154, replacement option value 3,096 and the previously held equity interest 2,120; 64,668,321 shares were issued (Q3 statements pages:8, 17). The principal asset is the McIlvenna Bay project in Saskatchewan. The acquisition was therefore financed with equity, not debt, and the statements show net cash received on acquisition of 159,110 (Q3 statements page:3). Net assets acquired include property, plant and equipment of 2,696,191, goodwill of 446,181, debt assumed of 355,321 and a deferred tax liability of 501,024; the measurement is preliminary and may be adjusted for up to one year (Q3 statements pages:9, 10). Acquisition-related costs expensed in the nine months were 19.3 million (US$) (Q3 statements page:10). The Q1 statements disclose the completion as an event after the reporting date (Q1 statements page:22).

The debt assumed comprises the Sprott Credit Facility (a fully drawn 250 million non-revolving facility plus 44.9 million capitalised interest, maturing September 30, 2031, floating at 6.95% over the greater of three-month SOFR and 2.00%, secured by the realisable value of the acquired Foran assets and subject to a Company guarantee), the Sandvik Equipment Finance Facility (C$50.1 million outstanding, 60-month repayment) and repayable government contributions (Q3 statements pages:14, 15). Principal repayments on the Sprott facility start on June 30, 2027 at 2.65% of outstanding principal per quarter. Its terms mean the lender has a claim over the acquired assets ahead of unsecured creditors of the group on those assets, and the Company guarantee ties the facility to the parent; the pack does not include the credit agreement, so the full security and guarantee scope is Insufficient Information.

No new bond or note issue is reported in the period. New borrowing was: draws on the revolving senior secured credit facility totalling 325.0 million in the nine months (225.0 million of it in Q3 2026); the final 60.0 million euro draw on the Skouries Contingent Overrun Facility in Q2 2026; and net proceeds of 2,469 (US$ thousand) from other debt. The revolver was increased from 350 million to 450 million on May 26, 2026 through the accordion, and on August 12, 2026 amended to a 600 million limit with maturity extended to March 1, 2029 and, in the Company's words, improved pricing and covenant terms (Q3 statements page:14: "enhancing financial flexibility through improved pricing and covenant terms"). The amended covenant terms are not stated beyond the interest cover and net leverage levels in the note, so what changed is Insufficient Information. The equity commitment letter of credit under the revolver for Skouries was reduced to nil as the equity funding was completed in Q3 2026 (Q3 statements page:14), which releases revolver capacity; at September 30, 2026 the revolver availability was 274.7 million (Q3 statements page:14).

Distributions during the build: the Company initiated a quarterly dividend programme with the first payment on March 13, 2026 (Q1 release page:2), paid 54.1 million of dividends in the nine months, bought back and cancelled 2,419,992 shares for 83.9 million and bought a further 240,000 shares for 8.7 million held in trust (Q3 statements page:18), and declared a fourth quarter dividend of 0.075 per share on October 28, 2026 (Q3 statements page:18). The senior notes restrict distributions in certain circumstances and the Company states compliance (Q3 statements page:11). Mechanically, these distributions total about 138.0 million for dividends and cancelled buybacks in nine months 2026 while company-defined free cash flow was negative 654.3 million (CP-1), so they were funded from cash and borrowings. This is an Analyst Interpretation of capital allocation, not a finding of extraction: the covenant tests are met per the Company, and no related-party leakage or collateral release is documented.

Subsequent events to the cutoff: the fourth quarter dividend declaration of October 28, 2026 is the only post-quarter event in the pack. Nothing dated after October 28, 2026 is supplied, so developments between that date and November 5, 2026 are Insufficient Information.

### Credit translations

1. Revenue durability and margin. Evidence: realised price up from 3,527 to 4,326 per ounce (US$) while ounces sold fell and AISC rose (Q3 release pages:14, 15). Mechanic: revenue is carrying lower volumes at higher cost, so margin per ounce is more sensitive to the gold price than a year ago. Implication: debt service capacity rests on gold staying near current levels until the new mines add volume; the Skouries and McIlvenna Bay start-ups are the offset, and they are not yet in commercial production. Countering evidence: Lamaque output rose and Olympias costs per ounce fell (Q3 release pages:19, 21).
2. Cash conversion and refinancing. Evidence: acquired debt, revolver draws and a larger project capital estimate (Q3 statements page:14; Q3 release page:7). Mechanic: cash needs continue until commercial production, funded by the revolver and the cash balance; the Term Facility begins amortising on December 31, 2026 (Q3 statements page:13). Implication: refinancing and liquidity are the pressure points; the senior notes and the revolver both mature in the third year bucket (CP-1). Counter: equity paid for Foran and the revolver was extended to March 1, 2029.
3. Concentration. Evidence: Canada, Turkiye and Greece segments; Greece carries the Skouries project and the Term Facility secured on Skouries and Hellas Gold assets (Q3 statements pages:12, 16). Mechanic: the non-recourse project loan keeps Skouries debt away from the parent but ring-fences the cash flows of the Greek assets for that lender; the revolver and notes sit with the rest of the group. Implication: creditor claims on Greek cash flows differ by instrument; the pack does not contain the intercreditor terms, so recovery or ranking is not assessed.
4. Sponsor alignment and distributions. Evidence: dividends and buybacks during heavy spending (Q3 statements page:18). Mechanic: shareholder returns draw on the same cash as project funding. Implication: a monitoring point; no creditor-adverse action is documented. Counter: the Foran purchase was equity-funded.
5. Key uncertainties: commercial production timing and cost at both start-ups; covenant headroom (not supplied); ownership; Foran fair values (preliminary).
