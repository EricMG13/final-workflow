### Readiness view

The effective source set is sufficient for a limited-scope credit snapshot of Encompass Health Corporation on a consolidated basis. The filed statements and notes in the audited annual report and the three quarterly reports control the financial and debt facts. The slide decks add attributed management claims such as guidance, a debt schedule and an Adjusted EBITDA bridge, and none of them overrides a filing. The view for each requested consumer is READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS, because the owner can perform its method with bounded gaps. No consumer is CONDITIONAL or BLOCKED. No identity boundary is unresolved: every source is the consolidated filing of one registrant. The assertion is SOURCE_SUFFICIENCY_ONLY; CP_MODEL_INPUT_CONTRACT_NOT_EVALUATED. This module does not assert normalized quarters, reconciliation or downstream input acceptance.

### Identity and perimeter

- The registrant is the same in every filing. The Q3 10-Q cover reads "Encompass Health Corporation (Exact name of Registrant as specified in its Charter)" (10-Q_2026-09-30, page:1), with Commission File Number 001-10315. The 10-K carries the same file number and registrant name (10-K_2025-12-31, page:1).
- Perimeter: the statements are consolidated and include noncontrolling interests. The Q3 10-Q states "We are the sole owner of 110 of these hospitals. We retain 50.0% to 97.5% ownership in the remaining 67 jointly owned hospitals." (10-Q_2026-09-30, page:12). Net income attributable to noncontrolling interests is shown separately as "Net income attributable to noncontrolling interests (53.7) (48.1) (160.9) (135.9)" (10-Q_2026-09-30, page:7), so leverage and coverage metrics on the consolidated perimeter include debt and earnings of partly owned hospitals.
- Borrower, guarantor and issuer roles: the Q3 10-Q says the credit agreement and the senior notes "are guaranteed by certain consolidated subsidiaries" and that the guarantees "are full and unconditional and joint and several" (10-Q_2026-09-30, page:44). The list of guarantor subsidiaries (Exhibit 22) is listed in the exhibit index (10-Q_2026-09-30, page:51) but the exhibit text is not in the pack.
