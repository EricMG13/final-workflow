### Financial base view

The financial base is adequate for a consolidated credit snapshot. Reported figures reconcile: operating expense lines sum to the printed totals, the Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation returns to pre-tax income, and the debt table ties to the balance sheet and to the maturity table. Encompass Health is a low-leverage, cash-generative issuer on the evidence supplied: total debt to trailing four quarter Adjusted EBITDA is 1.88x at Q3 2026, net debt to Adjusted EBITDA is 1.83x, EBITDA to interest excluding amortization is 11.7x, and revolver availability is about $871m. The 2026 refinancing moved the revolver maturity from October 2027 to March 2031 and replaced $400m of the 2028 notes with 2034 notes, so no bond matures before the remaining $400m of 2028 notes. Three points temper the view. First, the company deducts net income attributable to noncontrolling interests inside Adjusted EBITDA while reporting debt on a fully consolidated basis, and only part of the group guarantees the notes. Second, free cash flow after capex, noncontrolling distributions, dividends and buybacks was negative for nine months 2026, so debt rose while revenue and EBITDA grew. Third, the covenants are stated as compliance only, and the agreements that define them are not in the pack. The leverage and coverage figures below are therefore analyst calculations on carrying values and reported Adjusted EBITDA, and are not the covenant ratios.

### Perimeter, definitions and carry-forward from the readiness handoff

- Reporting entity: Encompass Health Corporation, consolidated, with 177 hospitals of which 110 are wholly owned and 67 are jointly owned at 50.0% to 97.5% (10-Q Q3 page:12). Figures below are for that consolidated perimeter, and the parent is not separately reported except in the guarantor summary (10-Q Q3 page:45).
- Adjusted EBITDA is company-defined, "Adjusted EBITDA as defined in our credit agreement" (10-Q Q3 page:45). It is reached from income before tax by adding back D&A, interest, stock compensation and other items, and by adding back net income attributable to noncontrolling interests, which means the measure is after the noncontrolling interest share of earnings (10-Q Q3 page:25 and page:48). The company reconciles it to operating cash flow and net income (deck Q3 page:13).
- Leverage in this handoff: total debt means long-term debt including current portion and finance lease obligations at carrying value, as in Note 4 ("2,599.1" at 30 September 2026, 10-Q Q3 page:17). The covenant definition, "consolidated total debt (less cash on hand) to Adjusted EBITDA for the trailing four quarters" with permitted pro forma adjustments (10-Q Q3 page:40), may differ in debt included, pro forma EBITDA and cash netting, and the agreement is not supplied.
- Carried forward from the readiness handoff: no credit agreement, indentures, guarantor list or ratings; covenant values not printed; deck charts not inspected.

### Statement reconciliation

The relevant expense block was reconciled to the printed totals. For Q3 2026 the operating expense lines (salaries and benefits 840.7, other operating expenses 245.2, occupancy 15.0, supplies 65.0, G&A 59.9, D&A 95.7) sum to the printed "Total operating expenses 1,321.5" (10-Q Q3 page:7). Revenue less operating expenses, less interest, plus other income and equity income, equals the printed pre-tax income in all three periods (see Calculations). The segment note presents other operating expenses and G&A on a different basis from the income statement (FY2025 other operating expenses 886.0 in the segment note against 888.7 in the income statement, and G&A 173.8 against 236.2; 10-K page:216 and page:162); no bridge between the two presentations is printed in the pages read, so the income statement is used here and the difference is left open (see Gaps).

The Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation in the Q3 10-Q returns to the printed pre-tax figure: 346.6 less stock compensation 15.0, D&A 95.7, loss on disposal 0.8, interest 32.2, plus noncontrolling interests 53.7, less fair value change 0.3, plus 1.5 for the state regulatory change impact, equals 257.8 (10-Q Q3 page:25).

### Liquidity

Liquidity at 30 September 2026 is cash and cash equivalents of $76.2 million plus about $871 million available under the $1 billion revolver, "approximately $871 million available to us under our revolving credit facility" (10-Q Q3 page:40). The availability reconciles to the commitment: 1,000 less 75.0 drawn less 53.6 letters of credit (10-Q Q3 page:20) equals 871.4. The deck shows the same split, "$871 Available $75 Drawn + $54 reserved for LCs" (deck Q3 page:11). Restricted cash of 24.0 and restricted marketable securities of 158.5 are excluded (10-Q Q3 page:40). Total liquidity on this basis is 947.2. The revolver matures 9 March 2031 (10-Q Q3 page:18), so no facility expires within twelve months of the cutoff. The only maturities within twelve months are scheduled amortization of 9.2 in the remainder of 2026 and 47.0 in 2027 on the face basis (10-Q Q3 page:17); the 12 months from 30 September 2026 therefore include 9.2 plus part of the 47.0.

Availability across the year: about $824 million at 31 Dec 2025 (10-K page:132), $726 million at 31 Mar 2026 (10-Q Q1 page:37), $746 million at 30 Jun 2026 (10-Q Q2 page:40) and $871 million at 30 Sep 2026. The rise from June to September follows the August 2026 add-on issue of 5.875% notes, whose net proceeds of about $97 million repaid revolver borrowings (10-Q Q3 page:40), and the revolver draw fell from 200.0 to 75.0 over the quarter (deck Q2 page:11 and deck Q3 page:11).

### Cash flow quality

For nine months 2026, net cash from operating activities was 957.7 against 829.6 a year earlier (10-Q Q3 page:10). Capex was 617.2 (10-Q Q3 page:10), leaving 340.5 after capex. Distributions to noncontrolling interests of 141.8, dividends of 57.9 and buybacks of 203.7 followed (10-Q Q3 page:10), so cash flow after these uses was negative 62.9. Total debt rose 108.3 over the same period (deck Q3 page:8; 10-Q Q3 page:17). For FY2025 operating cash flow of 1,175.6 less capex of 736.4, noncontrolling distributions of 152.1, dividends of 71.1 and buybacks of 158.0 left 58.0 (10-K page:165). Management's adjusted free cash flow, which deducts only maintenance capex and noncontrolling distributions, was 634.2 for nine months 2026 (deck Q3 page:14); it excludes growth capex of 441.0 on capacity expansions (deck Q3 page:8). Capex guidance for 2026 is 695 to 755 for growth (deck Q3 page:8) and 225 to 240 for maintenance, with total capex of approximately 920 to 995 (10-Q Q3 page:43). Cash taxes rose to 176.5 in nine months 2026 from 124.0 for all of 2025, partly from a 2025 underpayment (deck Q3 page:8).

Cash interest has two definitions in the sources. The 10-K reports cash paid for interest of 133.1 in FY2025 (10-K page:166) while management's "cash interest payments (net of amortization of debt discounts and fees)" are 113.6 for 2025 and 90.2 for nine months 2026 (deck Q3 page:8). The 113.6 equals the 123.2 interest expense less 9.6 of amortization (10-K page:165), so the deck measure is an accrual-based figure rather than cash paid. Interest capitalized was 17.6 in FY2025 (10-K page:196), which explains part of the gap between cash paid and expense. The Q3 10-Q omits cash paid for interest from its supplemental cash flow disclosure (10-Q Q3 page:11), so a cash-paid basis cannot be extended to Q3 2026.
