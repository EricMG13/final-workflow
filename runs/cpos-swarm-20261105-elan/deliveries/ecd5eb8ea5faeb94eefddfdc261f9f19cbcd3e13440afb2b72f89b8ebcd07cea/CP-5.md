### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious view is that Elanco generated operating cash flow of 290 in six months 2026 against 233, cut net capital spending to 84 from 122, took total debt including the lease from 4,045 to 3,944, holds 1,445 of liquidity, and has no facility maturing before June 2028. If trailing Adjusted EBITDA converts to cash as the first half suggests, deleveraging toward the management target of below 3x could come faster than the cautious view assumes. What would make that case win: third-quarter results with Adjusted EBITDA inside the 200 to 215 guided range, restructuring cash costs within the reserve of 86, and a refinancing of the 2028 maturities ahead of the August 2028 notes date. Downside chain with observable triggers: a distributor destocking or loss of Zenrelia share (first break: Pet Health volume, 718 in Q2) lowers Adjusted EBITDA; restructuring and acquisition payments continue regardless (transmission), drawing on cash of 530 and the undrawn revolver of about 750; unhedged floating-rate exposure of 1,489 raises interest; leverage drifts above 3.5x on the management measure ahead of the 1,236 due in 2028. Triggers to watch: quarterly Adjusted EBITDA against guidance, net leverage on the management measure, the revolver balance, any covenant certificate when obtainable, and hedge disclosures after August 2026.
