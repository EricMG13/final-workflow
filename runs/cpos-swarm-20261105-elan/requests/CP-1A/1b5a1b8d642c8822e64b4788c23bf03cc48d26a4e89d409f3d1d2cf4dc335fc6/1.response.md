### Business and transaction view

Elanco Animal Health Incorporated is a global animal health company selling pet health and farm animal products in more than 90 countries, with about 200 brands, as one reporting segment (10-K page:7-8; Q2 10-Q page:24, page:26). It was a Lilly business unit, became an independent company on 2018-09-18, completed separation from Lilly in March 2019 and bought Bayer Animal Health in August 2020 (10-K page:7). No sponsor, parent or controlling shareholder is evidenced in the pack, and the company says it does not anticipate paying dividends (10-K page:70). The creditor-relevant facts fall into three groups. First, the operating model is diversified by species and region but concentrated by channel and product: the largest customer was about 12 percent of FY2025 revenue and the top five product families about 38 percent, with seasonality in pet parasiticides that places most of Seresto and Advantage Family sales in the first half (10-K page:9, page:12-13). Second, the 2025 and 2026 transactions changed the capital structure: the October 2025 refinancing, the Blackstone sale of future royalties, the headquarters finance lease with a purchase option, and the AHV acquisition with deferred and contingent consideration. These are described in the Transaction summary and are priced in CP-1. Third, management is pursuing an innovation-led growth plan (Zenrelia, Credelio Quattro, Befrena) with a restructuring and a stated deleveraging path; those are management statements, and CP-1 shows that part of the improvement in leverage to date came from higher trailing Adjusted EBITDA rather than from repayment. Documented issuer behaviour is mainly supportive of creditors (debt repaid, no dividends, the AHV acquisition funded from cash on hand and a short-lived securitization draw), with two items that need monitoring: the use of a royalty monetization and a headquarters lease with a purchase option rather than debt, and management's statement that falling below 3x leverage will unlock capital allocation flexibility.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

No new debt issue, sponsor transaction or change of control appears in the evidence after 2025-12-31. The relevant transactions are as follows. On 2025-10-31 Elanco refinanced the Term Loan B due 2027, paying off a balance of 2,102, with three new facilities of aggregate gross proceeds of 2,106 (the Term Loan B due 2032 of 1,100, the Euro Term Loan due 2029 of 400 million euros and the Incremental Term Facility due 2032 of 540) plus cash on hand; issuance costs and discounts were about 32 and 20 was charged to interest expense for refinancing costs and deferred cost write-offs (10-K page:75, page:109). The documented purpose was to extend the debt maturity profile and lower cash interest, which is a supportive action for creditors. In May 2025 Elanco sold to Blackstone the rights to U.S. royalties and sales milestones on XDEMVY owed by Tarsus for 295, with net proceeds of 290 used to repay debt; the rights run for U.S. net sales from 2025-04-01 to 2033-08-24, Elanco keeps ex-U.S. royalties and later U.S. royalties, and the liability is carried at 315 at 2026-06-30 with an effective interest rate of 18.3 percent (10-K page:75-76; Q2 10-Q page:17). That cost of capital is high against the term loans priced at Term SOFR plus 1.75 to 2.25 percent, and the pack does not say whether the royalty rights were part of the lenders' collateral. In June 2025 Elanco started a five-year finance lease on its new Indianapolis headquarters, with a purchase option and a landlord put at 250 at the end of the term, which management expects to exercise (10-K page:76, page:121); a City of Indianapolis tax increment incentive totalling 64 funds part of the project (page:121). In June 2025 the securitization facility was amended to extend its maturity to June 2028 (page:111). On 2026-02-19 Elanco signed to buy AHV International B.V., a Netherlands farm animal health company (10-K page:138, a subsequent event to that report), and closed on 2026-04-30 for total estimated purchase consideration of 243: 76 cash paid at closing, 100 of deferred cash due through 2030 and a contingent consideration fair value of 67 for up to 140 of milestone payments through 2032 (Q2 10-Q page:10). The acquisition was funded from cash on hand and a 50 draw on the securitization facility, drawn on 2026-04-22 and repaid in the quarter (Q1 10-Q page:11; Q2 10-Q page:30). Management says the deal does not materially affect the deleveraging timeline (Q2 10-Q page:30), a management statement. In 2024 Elanco sold its aqua business to Merck Animal Health for 1,294 in cash, used mostly to repay term loan debt (10-K page:105). Financing documents for these transactions are not in the pack and are known only through the descriptions cited. The Q4 2025 rating upgrade is described only through the senior notes rate reset from 6.650 to 6.40 percent (10-K page:111; Q2 10-Q page:12).

### Credit translations

Revenue durability. Evidence: Pet Health volume growth in Q2 2026 was driven by new products Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro, and the largest customer is a distributor about 12 percent of FY2025 revenue (Q2 10-Q page:32; 10-K page:13). Risk mechanic: new-product volume supports revenue growth now, but a small number of distributors and five product families carry the base, so a destocking or competitive event transmits quickly to revenue and receivables. Credit implication: revenue visibility is good in the near term and concentrated in the medium term; monitor distributor inventory and new product share.

Cash conversion and add-backs. Evidence: CP-1 shows trailing net add-backs of 236 to reported EBITDA, partly cash restructuring, and a restructuring reserve of 86 (Q2 10-Q page:11-12). Risk mechanic: cash costs paid after the add-back reduce cash available for debt service. Credit implication: leverage on Adjusted EBITDA overstates cash capacity until the reserve is paid; test free cash flow alongside the measure. The conflict logged below on AHV payments does not counter this.

Claims outside the debt table. Evidence: the royalty sale liability of 315 at an 18.3 percent effective rate, the 250 headquarters option, the AHV deferred and contingent payments (Q2 10-Q page:10, page:17; 10-K page:121). Risk mechanic: these claim cash flow through 2033 without appearing in the leverage ratio. Credit implication: debt service coverage should include them; the pack does not show their ranking against lenders.

Governance and sponsor alignment. Evidence: no sponsor, no dividends and an explicit statement that capital allocation flexibility follows leverage below 3x (10-K page:70; Aug release page:4). Risk mechanic: absence of a sponsor removes sponsor-extraction risk, but the stated flexibility could be used for distributions or acquisitions funded by new debt. Credit implication: monitoring posture should watch restricted payment and acquisition activity once leverage approaches the target.

Key uncertainties. Evidence: no executed financing documents, no ratings, no covenant achieved ratios, latest evidence dated 2026-08-05 (CP-0). Risk mechanic: documents control priority, guarantees and baskets. Credit implication: the credit view remains provisional until the documents are added.
