### Credit view

Energy Transfer's debt looks serviceable on current earnings and liquidity, but it depends on subsidiary earnings growth and continued market access. The strongest support is scale and diversity: USD 19,216 million of LTM Adjusted EBITDA across eight segments, no customer above 10 percent of revenue, and USD 8,692 million of cash and undrawn facilities. The weakest point is that free cash flow does not cover distributions and growth capital, so leverage of 3.58x (4.28x for FY2025) rests on earnings growth, much of it from Sunoco LP, and on refinancing about USD 4,350 million due by September 30, 2027. Covenant terms, the 2026 change of state and the Vaquero funding plan decide whether the view holds.

### Key findings

Leverage has eased through earnings, not repayment. Total debt was USD 68,850 million at September 30, 2026 against USD 68,333 million at December 31, 2025, while LTM Adjusted EBITDA rose to USD 19,216 million from USD 15,984 million, which moves total debt over Adjusted EBITDA from 4.28x to 3.58x and net debt from 4.20x to 3.53x [financial base: leverage and coverage]. Nine months of 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of USD 15,034 million compares with USD 11,802 million, and the Sunoco LP segment supplied USD 560 million of the USD 1,193 million Q3 increase [financial base: reported results; business context: operating model]. The issuer's covenant leverage ratio under the Five-Year Credit Facility was 2.83x against a maximum of 5.00x (5.50x in a Specified Acquisition Period), so on the issuer's own basis headroom is 2.17 turns, which would allow Adjusted EBITDA to fall by about 43.4 percent at constant debt (Calculations C1) [financial base: leverage and coverage]. The covenant definition is not supplied and gives a lower ratio than the simple one, so the headroom is indicative only.

Earnings quality needs care. Adjusted EBITDA is an issuer non-GAAP measure that for nine months 2026 adds back USD 519 million of unrealized commodity losses, removes USD 445 million of inventory valuation gains and includes USD 588 million of Adjusted EBITDA of unconsolidated affiliates [financial base: reconciliation]. On operating income plus depreciation, depletion and amortization, total debt is 3.90x LTM against 4.65x for FY2025, so the improving direction survives a GAAP cross-check, but the level is higher than the covenant figure. Revenue of USD 124,528 million LTM is mostly pass-through product sales (fees were 14.5 percent of FY2025 revenue), so margin analysis must use Adjusted EBITDA [business context: operating model].

Cash flow covers interest but not distributions plus growth capital. Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest paid 5.43x LTM and interest expense 5.14x; interest expense takes 19.4 percent of LTM Adjusted EBITDA (Calculations C2) [financial base: leverage and coverage]. LTM operating cash flow of USD 13,569 million less capital expenditure of USD 7,487 million leaves free cash flow of USD 6,082 million, below distributions of USD 6,955 million to partners and noncontrolling interests, a shortfall of USD 873 million that arose in the fourth quarter of 2025 [financial base: cash flow quality]. On the issuer's own Distributable Cash Flow basis, which excludes growth capital, the partnership is close to self-funding: for nine months 2026, Distributable Cash Flow attributable to partners, as adjusted, of USD 7,813 million covered distributions of USD 3,515 million 2.22x and left USD 4,298 million against accrual-basis growth capital of USD 4,432 million, a gap of USD 134 million (Calculations C3; Q3 release page:8, Q3 10-Q page:86). Growth capital is guided at USD 5.4 billion to USD 5.6 billion for 2026 and about USD 7 billion for 2027 [business context: operating model], so the partnership will keep relying on debt and subsidiary-level capital.

Liquidity is ample in aggregate and thinner at the parent. Cash of USD 926 million plus undrawn facilities of USD 7,766 million gives USD 8,692 million, but only USD 4,406 million is the parent's Five-Year Credit Facility; USD 2,300 million belongs to Sunoco LP and USD 1,060 million to USAC, and cash is consolidated [financial base: liquidity]. The parent commitment steps down by USD 160 million on April 11, 2027.

The near-term maturity wall is smaller at the parent than CP-1 implied. The issuer states that about USD 4,350 million of principal due on or before September 30, 2027 is classed as long-term on refinancing intent and ability (Q3 10-Q page:88). Adding the instruments in the December 31, 2025 table that mature by that date and were not redeemed in 2026 gives USD 3,300 million at the parent (6.05% notes of USD 1,000 million due December 1, 2026; 4.40% notes of USD 700 million; 4.20% notes of USD 600 million; and two 5.50% notes due June 1, 2027 of USD 44 million and USD 956 million) and USD 1,050 million at Sunoco LP (5.625% and 5.875% notes due 2027, 550, 499 and 1), which sums to USD 4,350 million (Calculations C4; 10-K page:322). This is an Analyst Interpretation: the footnote does not split the amount and the 2027 months for the Sunoco LP notes are not printed. It corrects the CP-1 sentence that compared the whole USD 4,350 million with the parent's USD 4,406 million of availability: that comparison overstates the parent's need, though the parent still has USD 3,300 million to refinance within about eleven months, including USD 1,000 million on December 1, 2026, which is within a month of the as-of date.

Structure matters for the parent's creditors. The parent's notes and debentures plus Five-Year Credit Facility are USD 52,035 million of principal and its senior notes are not guaranteed by subsidiaries; Sunoco LP and USAC together carry USD 16,276 million with separate facilities [financial base: debt and capital structure; business context: ownership and capital allocation]. As an indicative proxy, parent principal over LTM Adjusted EBITDA excluding the Sunoco LP and USAC segments (USD 14,946 million) is 3.48x (Calculations C5). That proxy ignores the cash distributions the parent receives from them (Sunoco LP USD 306 million and USAC USD 73 million for nine months 2026 on the Distributable Cash Flow basis, Q3 release page:7) and counts non-wholly-owned joint ventures at 100 percent, so it shows only that excluding the subsidiaries does not make parent leverage lower than the consolidated figure of 3.58x by much. Whether parent-level coverage is adequate cannot be tested without parent-only statements, which are not supplied.

Hybrids and preferred equity shifted toward debt. The parent's junior subordinated notes are USD 5,550 million of principal after the July 2026 issues of USD 1,750 million (on CP-1's calculation), the Series H preferred units of USD 900 million were redeemed, and preferred equity outstanding is USD 2,480 million [financial base: debt and capital structure; business context: transaction summary]. Equity credit for these instruments is Not supplied.

Acquisitions and control are the main forward risks. Vaquero (total consideration about USD 2.63 billion, USD 1.95 billion in cash) is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 and Offen (about USD 600 million) closed on October 27, 2026 at Sunoco LP; the cash consideration for Vaquero equals 44.3 percent of the parent's revolver availability, and the funding plan is Not supplied [business context: transaction summary]. One individual holds 81.2 percent of the general partner and all Class A units, so creditors have no governance rights and depend on covenants; derivative suits are pending [business context: ownership and governance]. The issuer's state of organisation and exchange changed in 2026 and the filing explaining it is not supplied [source readiness; business context: conflicts]. Contingent matters are moderate against EBITDA: accruals of USD 536 million, additional reasonably possible losses up to approximately USD 76 million, and unrecorded favourable verdicts of approximately USD 795 million in aggregate, of which USD 393 million was recorded in October 2026 [business context: operating model and timeline].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. (1) Scale and diversity: eight segments, no customer above 10 percent of revenue and a largest segment of 24.3 percent of Q3 2026 Adjusted EBITDA, which limits single-asset or single-customer risk (Documentary Fact; business context). (2) Earnings momentum and conversion: LTM Adjusted EBITDA of USD 19,216 million, raised 2026 guidance of USD 19.7 billion to USD 19.9 billion, and Distributable Cash Flow covering distributions 2.22x for nine months (Documentary Fact for the figures; guidance is Management Language). (3) Liquidity: USD 8,692 million including subsidiary facilities, USD 4,406 million at the parent, and revolver extended to 2029 (Documentary Fact; financial base). (4) Demonstrated market access: USD 3,000 million of senior notes in January 2026 and USD 1,750 million of junior subordinated notes in July 2026, with USD 4,350 million of near-term maturities classed as refinanceable (Documentary Fact). (5) Covenant headroom on the issuer's reported basis of 2.17 turns (Documentary Fact for the figures; the headroom is an Analyst Interpretation).

Weaknesses. (1) Free cash flow does not cover distributions: USD 873 million LTM shortfall, with growth capital of about USD 7 billion guided for 2027 (Documentary Fact; guidance is Management Language; the funding implication is an Analyst Interpretation). (2) Leverage fell through earnings growth concentrated in the Sunoco LP segment, whose creditors rank ahead of the parent in subsidiary assets (Documentary Fact; the priority statement is an Analyst Interpretation). (3) Debt-funded acquisitions continue with Vaquero and Offen, funding not stated, so pro forma leverage is Not calculable (Insufficient Information). (4) Control by an individual through the general partner, limited unitholder rights and pending derivative litigation (Documentary Fact). (5) Unresolved identity change and missing executed documents, so obligor, covenant and guarantee analysis rests on filing summaries (Insufficient Information). (6) Adjusted EBITDA dependence: add-backs and affiliates' EBITDA, and a covenant ratio on a definition not supplied (Documentary Fact; dependence is Analyst Interpretation).

After the 2026 redemptions, the 2026 remainder is about USD 1,000 million (the December 1, 2026 notes) and the 2027 bucket falls from USD 5,314 million to about USD 4,100 million (C4); later buckets also change for redeemed Sunoco LP notes and new issues. These are derived and approximate; a current instrument-level table is not supplied.

## Calculations

C1. Covenant sensitivity on the issuer's stated basis. Headroom: 5.00 - 2.83 = 2.17 turns. EBITDA decline to reach the limit at constant debt: 1 - 2.83 / 5.00 = 43.4 percent. Additional debt capacity at constant EBITDA: (5.00 - 2.83) / 2.83 = 76.7 percent of current covenant debt. These use the reported ratio and the 5.00x limit (CP-1; 10-K page:330) and are indicative only because the definition is not supplied.

C2. Interest burden. Interest expense, net of interest capitalized, LTM 3,735 over LTM Adjusted EBITDA 19,216 = 19.44 percent (CP-1).

C3. Distributable Cash Flow check, nine months 2026. Distributable Cash Flow attributable to partners, as adjusted, 7,813 over distributions to partners 3,515 = 2.22x (Q3 release page:8). Retained: 7,813 - 3,515 = 4,298. Growth capital, accrual basis, 4,432 (Q3 10-Q page:86); 4,298 - 4,432 = -134. The growth capital figure includes only the proportionate share of joint ventures and is net of contributions in aid of construction, so the comparison is approximate. Q3 2026: 2,522 over 1,181 = 2.14x (Q3 release page:8).

C4. Maturities by September 30, 2027 (instrument amounts from 10-K page:322; redemptions from Q3 10-Q page:17 and page:88). Parent: 6.05% notes due December 1, 2026, 1,000; 4.40% notes due March 15, 2027, 700; 4.20% notes due April 15, 2027, 600; 5.50% notes due June 1, 2027, 44 and 956. The 5.625% notes due May 1, 2027 of 600 were redeemed in February 2026, and the 4.00% notes due October 1, 2027 of 750 fall after September 30, 2027. Parent sum: 1,000 + 700 + 600 + 44 + 956 = 3,300. Sunoco LP: 5.625% notes 550; 5.875% notes 499 and Parkland 5.875% notes 1 = 1,050 (the 6.000% notes due 2027 of 600 were redeemed in March 2026). Total 3,300 + 1,050 = 4,350, equal to the issuer's stated approximately 4,350. 2027 bucket after redemptions: December 31, 2025 bucket 5,314 less 600 (parent) less 600 (Sunoco LP) = 4,114, against 4,100 from the instrument sum (700 + 600 + 44 + 956 + 750 + 550 + 499 + 1 = 4,100); the difference of 14 is other debt in the bucket. No redemption of the December 1, 2026 notes is described in the 2026 filings read. Parent 2026 remainder: 1,000 plus 12 of current maturities in the balance sheet is a lower bound only.

C5. Parent proxy. LTM Adjusted EBITDA excluding the Sunoco LP and USAC segments: FY2025 15,984 - 2,047 - 614 = 13,323 (10-K page:187); nine months 2026 15,034 - 2,889 - 580 = 11,565; nine months 2025 11,802 - 1,401 - 459 = 9,942 (Q3 10-Q page:55). LTM = 13,323 + 11,565 - 9,942 = 14,946. Parent principal 51,470 + 565 = 52,035 (CP-1). 52,035 / 14,946 = 3.48x. Consolidated principal 69,251 over 19,216 = 3.60x for comparison (CP-1).

C6. Liquidity composition (CP-1): 926 + 4,406 + 2,300 + 1,060 = 8,692; undrawn 7,766; parent share of undrawn 4,406 / 7,766 = 56.7 percent.

C7. Acquisition scale. Vaquero cash 1,950 over parent availability 4,406 = 44.26 percent (CP-1A). Cash for Vaquero plus Offen at different borrowers: 1,950 + 600 = 2,550 (not additive for one borrower).

C8. Hybrid share. Junior subordinated notes 5,550 over parent principal 52,035 = 10.67 percent; over consolidated principal 69,251 = 8.01 percent (CP-1, CP-1A).
