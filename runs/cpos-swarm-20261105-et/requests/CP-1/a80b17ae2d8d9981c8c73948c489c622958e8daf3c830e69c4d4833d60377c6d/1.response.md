### Financial base view

The financial base is adequate for a credit snapshot, with the limits stated here. On the printed statements, consolidated debt was 68,850 US$m at September 30, 2026 against 68,333 US$m at December 31, 2025, cash was 926 US$m, and Adjusted EBITDA (issuer non-GAAP, consolidated) for the twelve months to September 30, 2026 is 19,216 US$m, built as FY2025 plus nine months 2026 less nine months 2025. Total debt over that EBITDA is 3.58 times, against 4.28 times for FY2025 and 4.12 times on the March 31, 2026 LTM. The improvement is driven by earnings: Adjusted EBITDA for nine months 2026 is 15,034 US$m against 11,802 US$m, while debt rose. The issuer's own covenant leverage ratio, calculated under a definition not supplied, was 2.83 times at September 30, 2026 and 3.21 times at December 31, 2025, so the covenant basis is lower than the simple ratio at both dates (by 1.07 turns at year-end and 0.75 turns at September 30, 2026, C9). Interest cover on Adjusted EBITDA over cash interest paid is 5.43 times LTM, with cash interest approximated from nine-month figures printed in billions.

Three features limit how far the headline can be read. First, the perimeter mixes the parent's debt (notes and debentures, the Five-Year Credit Facility) with that of Sunoco LP (13,131 US$m of senior notes, bonds and lease-related obligations plus a 111 US$m facility) and USAC (2,350 US$m of senior notes plus a 684 US$m facility), each with separate facilities and creditors; Adjusted EBITDA is consolidated at 100 percent, with 15,209 US$m of noncontrolling interests outside partners' capital. Second, earnings quality: the nine-month 2026 reconciliation adds back 519 US$m of unrealized commodity losses and removes 445 US$m of inventory valuation gains, and Q3 revenue was 37,103 US$m against 19,954 US$m, concentrated in fuel sales of the Sunoco LP segment, so margins are not comparable year on year. Third, claims outside debt (operating leases of 1,862 US$m, preferred units of 2,480 US$m, redeemable noncontrolling interests of 266 US$m, contingent accruals of 536 US$m) and the pending Vaquero acquisition (1.95 billion US$ cash plus about 33.3 million common units) are outside the leverage figures.
