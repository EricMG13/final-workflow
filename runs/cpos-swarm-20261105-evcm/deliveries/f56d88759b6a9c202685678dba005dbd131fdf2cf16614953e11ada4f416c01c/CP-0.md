- Financial reports. The 10-K and three 10-Qs are filed, SEC-form reports for the consolidated entity EverCommerce Inc. and subsidiaries, US GAAP, USD in thousands. Continuing operations exclude the marketing technology business sold on October 31, 2025, which is shown as discontinued operations (page:9 and page:10 of the latest 10-Q). Prior-period quarterly comparatives are therefore recast, and the cash flow statements include both continuing and discontinued operations through the sale date.
