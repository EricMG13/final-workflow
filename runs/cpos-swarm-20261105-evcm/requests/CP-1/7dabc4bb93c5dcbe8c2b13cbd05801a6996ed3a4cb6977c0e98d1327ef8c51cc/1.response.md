### Financial base view

The financial base for EverCommerce Inc. is a software group with one funded debt instrument, a secured term loan of 523.9 US$m at June 30, 2026 maturing in July 2031, and an undrawn revolver. On company Adjusted EBITDA the group is at 2.98x gross and 2.22x net leverage on an LTM basis, down from 3.24x and 2.41x at FY2024, with LTM cash interest cover of 5.31x. The evidence behind this is the filed statements and the company's own Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation, and the credit mechanics are favourable: floating-rate exposure is largely fixed by interest rate swaps to October 2027, scheduled amortization is 5.500 US$m per year, and the revolver covenant only springs if revolver borrowings exceed 35% of commitments. The strongest contrary evidence is earnings quality and direction: H1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA fell against H1 2025, add-backs are large and recurring, free cash flow conversion has weakened from 57.3% to 40.8% of Adjusted EBITDA, net revenue retention fell, buybacks consume more than free cash flow, and management says full-year results will trend to the low end of guidance. Limitations carried from the readiness review remain: no credit agreement, no letters of credit amount and no covenant-basis EBITDA. These restrict covenant headroom and net revolver availability, not the reported-fact summary.

### Debt structure and claims outside debt

- Instrument. One term loan: principal 523,875 thousand at June 30, 2026, Term SOFR plus 2.25% with a 0.50% floor, 5.89392% at June 30, 2026, quarterly amortization of 0.25% of original principal, balloon July 2031; revolver capacity 155,000 thousand until July 6, 2026, then 125,000 thousand to July 29, 2030, undrawn (10-Q June 2026 page:18 to page:20). Borrower, co-issuers: not stated in the located text; EverCommerce Intermediate Inc. and various subsidiaries guarantee, and the facilities are secured by liens on substantially all assets including equity of EverCommerce Solutions Inc. (page:51). The revolver covenant is expressed as a first lien leverage ratio (page:52), but the sources do not state the lien ranking of the term loan in those words. No bonds, no indenture, no second-lien debt appear in the sources.
- Hedging. Three swaps with 425,000 thousand notional fixing the floating component through October 31, 2027 at 4.212%, 3.951% and 3.395% (page:21). Net swap fair value at June 30, 2026 is an asset of 970 thousand and a liability of 623 thousand (page:25). Fair value of the term loan is about 502.3 US$m against 523.875 US$m principal (page:20), a Level 2 measure from trading prices.
- Outside the debt figure. Operating lease liabilities 12,007 thousand (page:18); non-cancelable purchase obligations 31,968 thousand (page:26); contingent consideration for ZyraTalk of up to 6.5 US$m over three years tied to post-combination employment services, current accrual not separately printed (page:12, page:8); sales and use tax provision 7.8 US$m (page:28); customer deposits of 13,051 thousand that customers may reclaim pro rata (page:6, page:15). There are no finance leases (10-K page:169), and a search of the 10-K and the June 2026 10-Q found no pension, retiree medical, factoring or supply-chain finance disclosure.
- Litigation. A putative class action in Delaware Chancery (Case No. 2024-0077) challenges the sponsor stockholders' veto over hiring or firing the chief executive; no loss is accrued as not probable and not estimable, oral argument scheduled for October 19, 2026, a date before the cutoff whose outcome is not in the sources (page:27).
- Covenant. Maintenance-type test applies only to the revolver: if revolving loans excluding letters of credit exceed 35% of commitments at a quarter end, first lien leverage must be 7.50x or less (page:51, page:52). The company reports compliance with all covenants at June 30, 2026 (page:54). No reported covenant ratio is printed.

### Dimensions reviewed

- Leverage. Gross 2.98x and net 2.22x LTM, below FY2024 on both measures; a small rise from 2.92x gross at FY2025 because LTM EBITDA is below FY2025 while debt fell only by 2.75 US$m of scheduled repayment.
- Coverage. Cash interest cover rose from 3.61x in FY2024 to 5.31x LTM because cash interest fell from 45.548 to 33.109 US$m on lower base rates and the margin reductions in 2024 and 2025 (10-K page:142; 10-Q June 2026 page:48). Interest expense before interest income was 16,369 thousand in H1 2026 against 17,713 thousand in H1 2025 (page:29).
- Cash flow quality. Free cash flow after capitalized software was 79,605 thousand in FY2025 and 71,687 thousand LTM, below FY2024's 94,256 thousand, as capitalized software rose; buybacks of 82,105 thousand LTM exceeded LTM free cash flow of 71,687 thousand, and in FY2025 disposal proceeds of 37,051 thousand were largely offset by the ZyraTalk acquisition outflow of 35,773 thousand (EX-99.1 March 2026 page:10).
- Liquidity. See the chart data; the next scheduled use of cash is amortization of 2.75 US$m in the rest of 2026, buybacks (19.2 US$m authorization remaining, program through December 31, 2026, page:54) and purchase obligations of 7,805 thousand in the rest of 2026 (page:26).
- Data quality. A material weakness in order-to-cash revenue accounting identified at December 31, 2023 was not remediated at December 31, 2025 (10-K page:67) and disclosure controls were not effective at June 30, 2026 (10-Q June 2026 page:56).
- Definition risks. Adjusted EBITDA adds back stock compensation, other amortization of contract acquisition costs and a broad class of transaction-related and non-recurring costs including system implementation, severance and integration (page:39). Add-backs were 52,275 thousand LTM, 29.7% of Adjusted EBITDA.
