### Credit view

Flutter's debt is adequately supported but stretched: the group is large, diversified and still cash generative, yet total debt to Adjusted EBITDA has risen to 5.28x and net debt to 4.55x, well above management's 2.0x to 2.5x target. The strongest support is liquidity of USD 2,477 million at September 30, 2026 and no sizeable maturity before 2029 once the October 2026 amendment is counted. The weakest point is earnings: higher UK gaming duty, the Brazil ban and weak US profit cut last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA to USD 2,331 million. What decides the outcome is whether cost actions and the buyback pause bring leverage down before further tax rises and legal settlements, and the covenant headroom for that cannot be measured from these sources.

### Key findings

Flutter's total debt of USD 12,313 million at September 30, 2026 is almost unchanged from USD 12,266 million at December 31, 2025, but last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA fell to USD 2,331 million from USD 2,845 million, so total debt to Adjusted EBITDA rose to 5.28x from 4.31x and net debt to 4.55x from 3.67x [CP-1]. The reason is earnings, not borrowing: nine-month 2026 Adjusted EBITDA was USD 1,499 million against USD 2,013 million, with the US segment down to USD 241 million from USD 612 million and the UK remote gaming duty rising from 21% to 40% in April 2026 [CP-1, CP-1A]. The Group's own leverage ratio is 4.6x against 3.7x at December 31, 2025 and a 2.0x to 2.5x target, and management has paused share repurchases to prioritise deleveraging.

Liquidity is the main support. Flutter held USD 1,708 million of unrestricted cash and USD 782 million of undrawn revolver commitments at September 30, 2026, USD 2,490 million in all, or USD 2,477 million if the USD 13 million guarantee reservation is also deducted. No debt matures within twelve months beyond USD 52 million of scheduled repayments, and the October 30, 2026 amendment extends the term A loans and the revolver to May 3, 2032, so the 2028 repayment of USD 2,681 million largely moves out, leaving about USD 51 million of 2028 amortisation on an indicative basis. The next large maturity is USD 1,143 million in 2029, then USD 3,692 million in 2030. The sterling revolver headroom did shrink during 2026, from USD 1,185 million at March 31 to USD 782 million at September 30.

Interest is the pressure point. Cash interest paid was USD 636 million over the last twelve months, and the Group expects USD 696 million of interest in the next twelve months net of hedging, which is 3.35x covered by last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA (calculated). About USD 6,629 million of debt principal at December 31, 2025 was floating and unhedged, and the Group states that 100 basis points on rates added USD 61 million of annualised interest on its December 31, 2025 debt. Free cash flow after capital expenditure and interest was USD 515 million over the last twelve months, but only USD 319 million once player deposit movements are removed [CP-1]. That is thin against the cash costs now coming: an announced UK restructuring of USD 80 million to USD 90 million and potential legal settlements.

Claims outside debt are material but mostly uncrystallised. The Group discloses a Fast Code class action provision of USD 109 million awaiting judgment, an Indian GST demand of USD 527 million (provision USD 54 million), US tax notices proposing USD 181 million (no provision), Austrian claims of USD 52 million not accrued, and a Belgian claim of USD 27 million; the gross amounts without provision total USD 733 million (calculated), which is not an estimate of loss. Operating leases of USD 559 million and purchase commitments of USD 4,998 million sit outside debt. The Fox option over 18.6% of FanDuel is a contingent sale rather than a debt claim.

Debt ranking and market signals complete the picture. Every funded instrument, the term loans, the revolver and the five senior secured note series, is secured first priority on the same collateral and guaranteed by the same group of guarantors, so creditors share one pool and there is no junior or unsecured debt to absorb losses first. The senior secured notes had a quoted fair value of about 94% of carrying amount at September 30, 2026, against about 100% at December 31, 2025 (calculated), which shows the market has repriced the credit even though the Group reports compliance with all covenants. The leverage covenant moved from a net total leverage level of 5.20:1 to a net first lien leverage level of 5.70:1.00, but no covenant ratio value is printed, so the Group's own ratio of 4.6x cannot be read as headroom.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Scale and diversification (Documentary Fact): Flutter operates two segments, the US at 43% and International at 57% of FY2025 revenue, with 88% of revenue online [CP-1A, company and operating model], which reduces dependence on any one market. Liquidity and maturity profile (Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation): USD 2,477 million of liquidity, USD 52 million of scheduled repayments within twelve months and an extension of term A and the revolver to May 2032 [CP-1, CP-1A transaction summary]. Cash generation (Documentary Fact): free cash flow remained positive at USD 515 million over the last twelve months after capital expenditure and cash interest [CP-1]. Deleveraging intent (Management Language): the Group has paused share repurchases and committed to returning to 2.0x to 2.5x over the medium term, which management states and has not yet achieved [CP-1A, governance table]. Structure (Documentary Fact): there is no sponsor, no dividend since May 2020 and no documented priming, uptiering or collateral release [CP-1A, company profile and governance table]; the sources cannot test legal capacity for any of these because the credit documents are not supplied.

Weaknesses. High and rising leverage (Documentary Fact): total debt to Adjusted EBITDA of 5.28x and net debt to Adjusted EBITDA of 4.55x against 4.31x and 3.67x at FY2025 [CP-1]. Earnings pressure (Documentary Fact): the UK gaming duty, the Brazil ban, which stopped operations on September 26, 2026 and produced a USD 873 million impairment, and weak US profit, partly from investment in prediction markets and new states [CP-1A, company and operating model]. Weakening cover (Documentary Fact): 3.67x on cash interest against 5.36x at FY2025 [CP-1]. Earnings quality (Analyst Interpretation): Adjusted EBITDA excludes restructuring that is planned to run until 2029, legal loss contingencies of USD 200 million and share-based compensation, so cash costs fall below the reported line [CP-1 definition review]. Single collateral pool (Documentary Fact): all funded debt is first lien, so creditors depend on one claim on the same assets with no cushion below. Contingent claims (Documentary Fact and Insufficient Information): the legal and tax matters above and the Fox option, whose rights on exercise are described as unresolved [CP-1A, credit translations]. Covenant opacity (Insufficient Information): headroom against the 5.20:1 and 5.70:1.00 tests is not calculable because covenant EBITDA and net borrowings definitions are not supplied.

### Consistency with upstream handoffs

- This answer corrects a reading in the financial base handoff. That handoff described the undrawn revolver figures as including the USD 13 million guarantee reservation. The sterling arithmetic points the other way: the commitment of £1.10 billion less drawings of £500 million less the £10 million reservation equals the printed £590 million, and the same holds at the other three dates (C2). The reservation therefore appears already deducted, so liquidity is USD 2,490 million on that reading and USD 2,477 million on the conservative one. This answer uses USD 2,477 million.
- The business handoff logged the facility-size statements (a release increase of about USD 240 million against a revolver of USD 1.86 billion) as unreconciled. A calculation from printed figures closes most of the gap: the revolver commitment rises by about USD 402 million, the term A loans fall by about USD 160 million, and the net of about USD 242 million matches the release's approximately USD 240 million (C3). This is a reading, not a statement in the sources, so the item stays logged as unconfirmed.
- The source readiness and financial base handoffs both said covenant ratio values are not printed; that remains true.
