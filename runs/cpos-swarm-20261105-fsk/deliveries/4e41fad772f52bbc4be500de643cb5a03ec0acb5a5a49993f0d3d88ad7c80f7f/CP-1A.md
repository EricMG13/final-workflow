### Business and transaction view

The business is a closed-end, externally managed business development company that lends mainly to private U.S. middle-market companies and is run by an adviser jointly operated by KKR Credit and an affiliate of Future Standard. Its creditors therefore face an asset manager's fee-and-distribution model rather than an operating company: income is investment income, the adviser is paid on gross assets and on income above a hurdle, and equity holders expect a quarterly distribution that the regulated investment company rules also require in substance. The 2026 transaction set is a package announced on May 11, 2026: a convertible preferred issue of 150 to a KKR affiliate, a tender offer by a KKR subsidiary, a 300 repurchase authorization and a 50% incentive fee waiver for four quarters, alongside a revolver amendment dated May 8, 2026, an issue of 900 of 7.500% unsecured notes on June 8, 2026 and the termination of two secured facilities on June 29, 2026. The documented behaviour is mixed rather than adverse: sponsor capital and a fee waiver are supportive, share repurchases and special distributions draw on the same cash that could reduce debt, and no priming, collateral release or non-pro-rata exchange appears in the sources. Five stockholder suits filed from May 4, 2026 allege misstatements about valuations, restructuring and the distribution strategy, which links governance to the quality of reported net asset value that supports the asset coverage and equity floor tests. The main limits are that ownership of common stock, the executed purchase and advisory agreements and the use of proceeds of the new notes are not in the pack.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On May 11, 2026 the Company announced, with its first quarter results, four actions it called strategic value enhancement actions: a 150 cumulative convertible perpetual preferred investment by a KKR subsidiary, a fixed price tender offer by a KKR subsidiary for up to 150 of common stock at 11.00 per share, a 300 share repurchase program, and a waiver of 50% of the subordinated income incentive fee for four consecutive quarters (Q1 release pages 2 and 3). The preferred closed on June 29, 2026 with 6,000,000 shares sold at 25.00 to KKR Alternative Assets L.P. under a purchase agreement dated May 10, 2026 (10-Q Q3 2026 page:74), paying 5.00% in cash or, at the Company's option, 7.00% in kind, rising by 1.00% a year after 5.5 years, with a conversion price of 18.83, the March 31, 2026 net asset value per share. It ranks senior to common stock and, per the release, "junior to all existing indebtedness"; the Company may redeem it in cash at any time with board approval, holders may convert after six months and may require cash redemption at par after six years on 90 days' notice, and a majority of holders can require redemption on a change of control (10-Q Q3 2026 pages 81 to 83). The Company states it will use the proceeds for general corporate purposes including repurchases or debt repayment (10-Q Q2 2026 page:158). The Q2 release says the tender offer "was successfully completed", the preferred closed and the repurchase program commenced (Q2 release page:2); the number of shares KKR acquired in the tender and its resulting ownership are not supplied. Status of the package at 2026-09-30 is therefore closed for the preferred and tender, ongoing for the repurchase program (6,533,672 shares for 73 through September 30, plus 1,546,162 shares for 17 in October) and running for the fee waiver, which has no recoupment (10-Q Q3 2026 pages 75, 77 and 123).

New and changed debt in the period are as follows. The Senior Secured Revolving Credit Facility was amended on May 8, 2026 (Amendment No. 1, reported as a Q1 subsequent event) to cut total commitments to about 4,051.7 from 4,700.0, to raise the applicable margins for extending lenders by 0.125 percentage points in each pricing tier, and to reset the minimum shareholders' equity floor to 3,750.0 from about 5,048.6 (10-Q Q1 2026 page:107; 10-Q Q2 2026 page:108). On June 8, 2026 the Company issued 900 of 7.500% Notes due August 1, 2031 under a Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture to the 2014 base indenture, with the trustee U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association; the notes are general unsecured obligations that "rank effectively junior to any of the Company’s secured indebtedness" and are structurally junior to subsidiary debt, carry covenants requiring compliance with the statutory asset coverage requirement, and give holders a change of control repurchase offer at 100% of principal (10-Q Q2 2026 pages 108 and 109). A swap of 900 notional to floating was entered at issuance (10-Q Q2 2026 page:95). The coupon is well above the 3.400% of the notes that reached their listed maturity of January 15, 2026; the use of proceeds of the new notes is not stated in the sources read, though the Q2 filing shows lower revolver borrowings in the same quarter. On June 29, 2026 the Meadowbrook Run and CCT Tokyo Funding secured facilities were terminated with realised losses of 2 and 0 respectively (10-Q Q2 2026 page:109). Earlier new debt that remains outstanding is the Callowhill facility (June 2, 2025, up to 400), the CLO-2 financing of 380 (March 28, 2025) and the CLO-3 financing of 389.5 closed December 18, 2025 (10-K pages 266, 277 and 278). Subsequent to the quarter and before the as-of date, the board on October 7, 2026 declared a regular distribution of 0.44 and a special distribution of 0.20 per share, and on October 28, 2026 a preferred dividend of 0.3125 per share (10-Q Q3 2026 page:123); the special distribution is described as a portion of estimated accumulated spillback income (Q3 release page:2). No further debt issue, amendment or refinancing after September 30, 2026 appears in the sources up to the cutoff.
