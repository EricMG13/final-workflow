### Coverage, strongest evidence and perimeter

- Period coverage. Statements and notes exist for Q1 (three months), Q2 (three and six months) and Q3 (three and nine months) of 2026, each with comparative periods of 2025, so discrete quarters and quarter versus year-to-date distinctions are separable. FY2025 as a full period is covered only through management's trailing-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA (`Trailing twelve months Adjusted EBITDA(2) 2,257.1 1,985.0` (Q3PR page:14)).
- Perimeter. All figures are consolidated GFL. Equity-method associates, including the retained stake in the divested environmental services business (`GFL has the option to repurchase the balance of the equity of GFL Environmental Services (the “Call Option”).` (Q3FS page:8)), sit outside Adjusted EBITDA by definition of the add-back. Non-wholly-owned subsidiaries carry stand-alone credit facilities inside long-term debt (`Certain of GFL’s non-wholly owned subsidiaries have stand alone credit facilities included in other in long-term debt.` (Q3FS page:13)). The issuer-versus-restricted-group distinction is not drawn in any source.
- Debt. The Q3 debt note lists the revolving credit facility, the Term Loan B Facility, eight series of USD notes (two of them secured at issue, one since released from collateral), the tax-exempt bonds and other facilities, and totals `Total long-term debt 11,671.4 7,422.6` (Q3FS page:11) in CAD millions for September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025. Maturities appear in the footnotes, not in a year-by-year table.
- Covenants. `equal to or less than 5.00 to 1.00 for a period of four complete fiscal quarters following completion of a Material Acquisition` (Q3FS page:13) applies to Total Net Funded Debt to Adjusted EBITDA, with `GFL was in compliance with these covenants.` (Q3FS page:13). The ratio as defined in the agreement is not printed.
- Off-balance-sheet and other claims. `letters of credit totaling approximately $535.1 million outstanding` (Q3FS page:18), `$187.8 million in outstanding letters of credit under the EDC Guaranteed LC Facility` (Q3FS page:18), `performance bonds totaling $2,324.1 million` (Q3FS page:19), `crude oil transportation commitments totaling $561.4 million` (Q3FS page:19), and lease obligations on the balance sheet (`Lease obligations 605.7 450.6` (Q3FS page:2) non-current). Pension and retiree medical obligations: no mention found in any source (searched "pension", "retirement", "post-employment").
- Management claims. Guidance, run-rate EBITDA, Net Leverage and Adjusted Free Cash Flow are management non-IFRS measures; guidance has no reconciliation (`GFL does not have information available to provide a quantitative reconciliation of such projections` (Q3PR page:3)). They are attributed claims, not controlling facts.
