### Business and transaction view

GFL is a diversified solid waste and industrial waste processing business in Canada and the United States whose 2026 credit story is acquisition-driven. In the third quarter of 2026 acquisitions contributed 18.7% of the 27.2% revenue growth, organic growth was 7.4% of which price was 5.7% and volume was negative, so the operating model currently relies on price realisation and acquired scale rather than volume. The SECURE acquisition (completed September 1, 2026, total consideration approximately 5,414.1, funded with 4,334.8 of shares and 1,079.3 of cash) is the transaction that changes the credit: it raised Canada segment goodwill and indefinite-life intangible assets from 1,942.0 to 6,902.2, added Western Canada industrial waste processing, disposal and recycling, and brought new crude oil transportation and storage commitments that did not exist at year-end 2025. The accounting is provisional, and the debt financing is documented in the CP-1 handoff rather than by transaction-level sources, so use of proceeds is partly inferred. There is no sponsor named in the sources, the controlling shareholder position is not described, and the board has formed a Special Committee to review unsolicited proposals to take the company private; the outcome is after the cutoff and no terms are supplied. Governance evidence is mixed: documented actions include a large 2025 share repurchase and a cash payment to the Founder and CEO in place of share-unit settlement, offset by an independent committee process. Business description, customer concentration, contract tenor and control rights are Insufficient Information because the annual information form is not in the pack.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The principal transaction is the acquisition of SECURE Waste Infrastructure Corp. A definitive agreement was entered on April 12, 2026 (`On April 12, 2026, GFL entered into a definitive agreement pursuant to which it has agreed to acquire all of the issued and outstanding common shares of SECURE Waste Infrastructure Corp.` (Q1FS page:15)), with consideration satisfied by a combination of subordinate voting shares and cash. Management said SECURE shareholders approved it in May (`SECURE shareholders approved the transaction in May` (Q2PR page:1), Management Language), and it closed on September 1, 2026 for total consideration of approximately 5,414.1, of which 4,334.8 was shares and 1,079.3 cash, with 77,708,769 subordinate voting shares issued in the nine months (Q3FS page:6 and page:16). SECURE is described as `an operator of waste processing, industrial waste disposal and recycling located in Western Canada and North Dakota` (Q3FS page:7). The purchase price allocation is provisional: goodwill recognized for SECURE is 4,968.1, property and equipment 1,616.9, other long-term liabilities 1,006.6 and closure obligations 271.8 (Q3FS page:6), and adjustments `may significantly impact the recognized values of intangible assets, goodwill, property and equipment and deferred income tax liabilities` (Q3FS page:7). SECURE net working capital includes cash of 73.4. No source identifies a sponsor. A SECURE nominee joins the board, which grows to nine directors (Q3PR page:2). The status is completed, with provisional accounting.

Other acquisitions: GFL acquired 13 businesses in nine months 2026 including SECURE (the note says each is individually immaterial except SECURE), 3 in Q1 and 10 for the six months, so 3 in Q3 including SECURE. Total net assets acquired in the nine months were 7,012.6 including SECURE, of which others 1,598.5, with 2,569.8 of cash paid net of cash acquired. This corrects the CP-1 handoff, which described the 13 as other businesses besides SECURE; the note's wording and the six-month count of 10 indicate the 13 includes SECURE. Management describes year-to-date completed acquisitions as generating approximately 2 billion of annualized revenue (Q3PR page:1, Management Language). Pro forma nine-month revenue as if all 2026 acquisitions had occurred on January 1, 2026 would have been 6,905.3 against 5,746.9 reported (Q3FS page:8).
