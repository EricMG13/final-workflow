### Evidence trace view

Hilton Grand Vacations' analysis can be traced to its sources for the debt, liquidity, earnings and business facts it uses, but not for the legal protections a creditor would rely on. Every quotation in the three evidence-bearing handoffs sits on the page it cites, every ratio and total reproduces from the cited figures, and the credit synthesis uses only figures present in the financial base. The defects found are minor: wrong or surplus page references on a few rows, one loosely worded debt increase, a comparison of a nine month mix with a full year mix, and one strengths label that states an inference as fact. The weakest point is absence, not error: no covenant text, guarantor terms or ratings exist in the sources, so those conclusions are explicit gaps. The result is a traceable factual base with a bounded legal view.

### Traceability conclusion

The run is traceable enough for a view of the corporate debt, liquidity, earnings quality and business model, and not for the decision that matters most to a secured or unsecured creditor, namely what the documents permit. Strongest support: 207 quotations in the source readiness, financial base and business context handoffs were each found on a cited page, and 56 recomputations matched. Weakest point: covenant levels, definitions and headroom, guarantor identities and collateral are Not supplied, so claims about them rest on a general compliance statement only. What decides the outcome is adding the credit agreement and indentures. Of twelve material claims, four are directly sourced, four calculated, three analyst inference and one without evidence. Four minor findings are open.
