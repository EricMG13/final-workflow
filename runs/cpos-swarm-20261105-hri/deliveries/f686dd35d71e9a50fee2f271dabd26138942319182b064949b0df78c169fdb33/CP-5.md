### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest contrary evidence to a cautious reading is management's own pro forma net leverage of 3.90x against 4.15x calculated here, and the improving trend (5.38x at FY2025 to 4.15x at Q3 2026). The LTM window to Q3 2026 starts after the H&E closing, so the stated pro forma element should add little, and the gap is likely add-backs (Analyst Interpretation, unverified). If the Q4 2025 release confirmed add-backs of that size and the synergy plan were quantified, the leverage view would improve. The opposite case is a downturn with a sequence the filings let us trace: construction or project demand slows and interest rates stay high; dollar utilization and rental rates soften first while fixed overhead stays; EBITDA falls against a debt balance that has barely moved; fleet purchases can be cut but used-fleet disposal proceeds depend on the resale market; free cash flow, already negative in Q3 2026, goes further negative and the ABL borrowing base shrinks with fleet values; liquidity then depends on the revolver while the AR Facility (due August 31, 2027) needs extension. Observable triggers: quarterly free cash flow, dollar utilization, ABL availability against the unquantified fixed charge trigger, and the AR Facility extension. No stress number is asserted.
