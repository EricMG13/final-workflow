### Financial base view

IRON MOUNTAIN INC is a growing, highly levered REIT whose reported leverage on its own Adjusted EBITDA is around six times and whose free cash flow after capital expenditure is negative. For the twelve months to September 30, 2026 (LTM Q3 2026), Adjusted EBITDA is 2,888,192 thousand (FY2025 2,573,950 plus nine months 2026 2,182,915 less nine months 2025 1,868,673), revenue is 7,799.0 US$m and the Adjusted EBITDA margin is 37.0%. Total debt at carrying amount is 17,972.2 US$m, so total debt to LTM Adjusted EBITDA is 6.22x and net debt to LTM Adjusted EBITDA is 6.16x, against 6.38x and 6.32x at FY2025. Adjusted EBITDA to cash interest paid is 3.16x on an LTM basis against 3.12x at FY2025. These ratios improved against FY2025 because Adjusted EBITDA grew faster than debt, but total debt over Adjusted EBITDA edged up from 6.19x at LTM Q2 2026 to 6.22x at LTM Q3 2026.

Cash generation does not cover the investment programme. Operating cash flow less capital expenditure is negative 520.6 US$m for nine months 2026 and negative 536.8 US$m LTM, before 790.0 US$m of nine-month dividends. Financing in the period included 1,500,000 thousand principal of new 6 1/4% notes due 2035 (net proceeds of 1,485,000 thousand in the cash flow statement), data center project loan and revolver borrowings, and 95.2 US$m of noncontrolling interest equity contributions; the filings do not tie each source to the funding gap. The Credit Agreement covenant ratios (4.8x and 2.5x) show wide reported headroom, but they are EBITDAR and EBITDA based with adjustments and the filings state they are not directly comparable to Adjusted EBITDA. Liquidity of 2,406.4 US$m is cash plus revolver availability only; other undrawn lines are small, project specific or tied to eligible receivables. The Adjusted EBITDA definition adds back stock-based compensation and other items, so a stricter operating income plus depreciation and amortization measure gives a higher LTM leverage of 6.81x (calculation below); both are shown and neither is a covenant measure.
