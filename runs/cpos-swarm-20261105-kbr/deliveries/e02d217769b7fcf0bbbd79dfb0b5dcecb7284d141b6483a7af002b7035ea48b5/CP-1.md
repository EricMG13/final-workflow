### Financial base view

KBR enters the cut-off with a moderately levered balance sheet, drifting toward lower reported leverage, and a perimeter about to change. On management's own measure, net debt of 2,118 against trailing twelve month adjusted EBITDA of 1,015 gives 2.1x at October 2, 2026, compared with 2.2x at January 2, 2026, 2.3x at April 3, 2026 and 2.3x at July 3, 2026. Gross debt principal was 2,557 at October 2, 2026, made up of 957 Term Loan A, 975 Term Loan B, 250 of 4.750% Senior Notes due 2028 and 375 drawn on the 1,000 revolver. On the same earnings measure gross leverage is 2.52x and net leverage is 2.09x. Those ratios carry a definition risk that matters for the credit view. Beginning with the first quarter of fiscal 2026, adjusted EBITDA was revised to add back the group's share of joint venture interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization and acquisition costs, prospectively and without recasting history; the add-back is 44 over nine months of fiscal 2026. Removing it gives trailing EBITDA of 971 and net leverage of 2.18x, which is almost level with the 2.19x computed for fiscal 2025, so nearly all of the reported improvement reflects the change of definition. The measure also adds back spin-off, acquisition and restructuring costs of 72 over nine months (18 a year earlier), and starts from operating income that includes equity in earnings of unconsolidated affiliates of 167 against cash distributions of earnings of 145. A plain operating income plus depreciation and amortization proxy for the trailing twelve months is 895, which gives net leverage of 2.37x on this unadjusted basis.

Cover is available on cash interest only for fiscal 2025: adjusted EBITDA of 968 over cash interest paid of 141 gives 6.87x. No 2026 cash interest figure is supplied in the interim sources, so the interim cover cells are Not supplied. As a labelled memo, adjusted EBITDA over interest expense is 7.00x on a trailing basis to October 2, 2026 (interest expense of 145). Liquidity at October 2, 2026 is cash of 439 plus 607 undrawn on the committed revolver after 18 of letters of credit, 1,046 in total. The release states revolver capacity of 625, which is the commitment less borrowings with no deduction for the letters of credit; the 10-Q reports the letters separately. Bilateral and uncommitted lines of 491 can be used for letters of credit only, with 186 unused, and are excluded from liquidity. Of the 439 cash, 67 is joint venture and Aspire Defence project cash and the captive insurer holds part of the domestic balance, so the cash that is freely available is smaller than the headline.

The first scheduled wall is near. Current maturities of long-term debt are 554 at October 2, 2026, against 49 at January 2, 2026, because Term Loan A-2 matures in August 2027; Senior Notes follow in September 2028 and the revolver and Term Loan A-1 and A-3 in February 2029. The refinancing sits alongside the planned spin-off targeted for January 4, 2027, after which management intends to use cash distributed by the MTS business to reduce debt; the amount, the post-spin debt and the post-spin earnings are not supplied. For scale, MTS carried 441 of nine-month adjusted EBITDA out of 777, and the other segment plus corporate carried 336. Operating cash flow of 331 for nine months fiscal 2026 compares with 506 a year earlier. Receivable monetization programs sold 935 in the period and settled 935, leaving 65 outstanding, the same as at the start of the year, so the programs did not add net funding over nine months but remain a recurring working capital tool. Claims outside the debt figure include operating and finance leases, a 41 Kuwait court judgment under appeal that is not accrued, letters of credit and surety support, and a United Kingdom pension surplus rather than a deficit. The view is therefore moderate leverage, adequate liquidity, a near-term amortization step, an EBITDA definition that flatters the trend, and a covenant package that cannot be tested against defined terms.
