### Evidence trace view

The analysis is traceable enough for restricted committee use. Nearly every figure in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis traces to a located page in the pinned filings, and recomputation from separately extracted filing lines reproduced gross debt, net debt, trailing adjusted EBITDA, liquidity and the maturity schedule. The most important provenance gap is covenant compliance and the post-spin structure: the credit agreement, its Amendment No. 14 and the Form 10 are not in the pack, so statements that covenants are met are management assertions and the surviving credit cannot be sized. A second gap is the cash flow attribution, which the cash flow statement does not support as written. The defects found are minor and none changes the credit view. Because the same analyst wrote the modules and this trace, an independent reviewer should repeat the highest-risk checks before the report is used.
