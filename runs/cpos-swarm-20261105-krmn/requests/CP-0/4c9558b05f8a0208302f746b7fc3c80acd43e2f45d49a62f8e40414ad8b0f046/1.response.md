### Conflicts and source-quality flags

1. Term loan principal after the Third Amendment. 10-K FY2025 page:152 states "for a total principal amount of $767.8 million". 10-Q Q1 2026 page:18 and 10-Q Q2 2026 page:18 state "for a total principal amount of $772 million". The 10-Q Q2 2026 page:18 also states "As of June 30, 2026, the principal of the term note outstanding was $764.0 million." Effect: the committed term loan size is stated two ways; the outstanding principal is the figure to use for leverage, with the conflict kept visible. Not reconciled here.
2. Seemann consideration. 10-K FY2025 page:152 gives "$210.0 million in cash" plus shares valued at "$10.0 million", as agreed on December 31, 2025. 10-Q Q2 2026 page:15 gives "approximately (i) $215.9 million in cash" and shares "with an aggregate value equal to $17.0 million", and "Total consideration transferred was $232.9 million." The cash flow statement line is net of cash acquired (10-Q Q2 2026 page:4 "Acquisitions of businesses, net of cash acquired"). Effect: the transaction-summary consumer must state which basis it uses. Not reconciled here.
3. Purchase accounting revised. 10-Q Q2 2026 page:6 states that the Company "identified and recorded certain adjustments associated with the purchase accounting recorded for the acquisition of Seemann Composites, LLC and Materials Sciences LLC in the interim period ended March 31, 2026" and judged them immaterial. Effect: Q1 2026 balances as filed may differ from the Q2 allocation.
4. Internal control. 10-K FY2025 page:153 states "the Company’s disclosure controls and procedures were not effective at a reasonable assurance level" and 10-Q Q2 2026 page:41 states that controls "were not effective as of June 30, 2026 due to the material weaknesses". Effect: reliability risk on reported figures; it does not make any source unusable, but every consumer must carry it.
5. Backlog and remaining performance obligations are different measures. The release defines backlog as "the total value or current estimated value of existing contracts, less amounts previously invoiced" (EX-99.1 Aug 2026 page:2); the filing reports "$703.9 million of remaining performance obligations" (10-Q Q2 2026 page:7). The release says backlog was "$1.3 billion". Effect: the two must not be mixed; the filing figure controls where the two are compared.
6. End-market labels. The 10-K FY2025 page:95 describes "three end markets"; the 10-Q Q2 2026 page:9 table has four, adding "Maritime Defense Systems", with a prior-year value of nil while the release footnote says that revenue "was previously included within other end markets" (EX-99.1 Aug 2026 page:2). Effect: end-market series across FY2025 and 2026 are not like-for-like.
7. Management claims. The release statement that organic growth was "24.4%" (EX-99.1 Aug 2026 page:1) is an attributed claim; the filing does not print an organic growth figure in the pages read. It supports narrative only.
