### Business and transaction view

For Mativ Holdings, Inc. the creditor-relevant facts are these. It is a publicly listed Delaware company (NYSE: MATV) that has been independent since a 1995 spin-off from Kimberly-Clark and took its current form on July 6, 2022 when Schweitzer-Mauduit International merged with Neenah and renamed itself Mativ. Its 2023 sale of the Engineered Papers business for 620.0 in cash ended participation in tobacco-based product markets and was followed by a debt repayment of about 641.2 that the 10-K ties to a covenant trigger. There is no sponsor, and no controlling holder is disclosed in the supplied text (Part III of the 10-K is incorporated from a proxy statement that is not in the sources), so ownership behaviour can only be judged from documented issuer actions.

Operating model: two reportable segments, Filtration & Advanced Materials (FAM, about 39 percent of FY2025 revenue) and Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions (SAS, about 61 percent), serve dozens of end markets through 34 production locations on three continents and sales in more than 100 countries, with roughly 57 percent of Q3 2026 net sales to U.S. customers and 25 percent to Europe. The risk mechanics that matter to a creditor are: pricing power against resin, fiber, pulp and energy inputs (selling prices rose in 2026 while volume and mix fell), a weakening SAS volume position in Q3 2026 (tornado and Healthcare) set against FAM growth, a margin structure in which FAM earns materially higher gross margin than SAS, and a funding model that sells receivables off balance sheet and relies on a revolver that is now 305.0. Credit implication: revenue is diversified and customer-qualified, which supports durability, but gross margin of roughly 19.6 percent for nine months 2026 and reported-basis interest cover of 2.62x leave little room for a volume shock; the July 27, 2026 tornado showed that a single third-party distribution node can interrupt supply for a whole product line.

Transactions: the April 3, 2026 Ninth Amendment refinanced the entire bank group (revolver cut from 600.0 to 305.0, Term Loan A 89.9, Term Loan B 500.0, delayed draw term loan removed, maturities moved from May 2027 to 2031 and 2033 on the stated terms, subject to a springing wording that is unresolved against the maturity table). The 400.0 of 8.000% senior unsecured notes issued in October 2024 are the nearest maturity on the company's own statements (October 1, 2029). The interaction between the term loan B's size and the covenant scope is the main structural point: the maintenance covenants are stated to apply to the revolver and Term Loan A only, so the 500.0 Term Loan B lenders hold no maintenance test of their own. Restrictions on information: the credit agreement, indenture and receivables agreement are not supplied, so baskets, guarantor coverage and restricted payment capacity are Not supplied.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The one financing transaction in the period is the Ninth Amendment of April 3, 2026 to the 2018 multicurrency credit agreement (the ninth amendment, after an Eighth Amendment dated December 17, 2024). Status: executed, effective April 3, 2026, reported in the 10-Q Q1 as a subsequent event and in the 10-Q Q2 and Q3 as completed (10-Q Q1 page:21; 10-Q Q3 page:13). It provided a 305.0 revolving facility with euro and sterling sub-facilities, an 89.9 Term Loan A and a 500.0 Term Loan B, 894.9 of committed facilities in total, and eliminated the delayed draw term loan. Use of proceeds is shown in the cash flow statement: nine-month debt proceeds of 705.7 against repayments of 728.1, 32.7 of issuance costs and an 8.7 loss on debt extinguishment. The revolver and Term Loan A mature on the earlier of five years from April 3, 2026 or 182 days before the notes' maturity, and the Term Loan B on the earlier of seven years or 91 days before it; certain subsidiaries became additional U.S. borrowers and another a guarantor (names not supplied). The Term Loan B pays fixed margins of 3.50 to 4.50 percent depending on the benchmark, against 2.75 percent over Term SOFR and 3.75 percent over EURIBOR in the 10-K description of the prior agreement, which reads as roughly 75 basis points more at the stated ends, although the 10-Q does not say which end applies to which benchmark. This is an Analyst Interpretation. Liquidity effect: revolver availability dropped from 416.2 (old 600.0 facility, March 31, 2026) to 279.2 at June 30, 2026, and total liquidity from 498.5 to 345.5, in exchange for the removal of the May 2027 maturity wall (10-K page:103 shows 514.2 of maturities in 2027).

New debt issues in the supplied sources: none in the twelve months to the cutoff other than the refinancing; the 400.0 of 8.000% senior unsecured notes due October 1, 2029 were issued in a Rule 144A and Regulation S private offering on October 7, 2024 and remain outstanding, redeemable from October 1, 2026 at indenture prices not supplied (10-Q Q3 page:12). The use of proceeds of the 2024 notes is Not supplied in the sources. The 10-K shows 2024 debt proceeds of 531.0 and repayments of 554.7 and a 7.3 loss on debt extinguishment (10-K page:75, page:78).

Other transactions with creditor consequences. The Engineered Papers sale on November 30, 2023 to Evergreen Hill Enterprise Pte. Ltd. for 620.0 in cash was followed by a debt repayment of about 641.2 that the 10-K says was based on the triggering of a financial covenant in the loan agreement, and Mativ retains an indemnity capped at 10 with no material claims pending (10-K page:95; 10-Q Q3 page:17); this made the company smaller and ended tobacco exposure. The Neenah merger of July 6, 2022 created the current company. On October 23, 2026 the Receivables Sales Agreement was amended to extend its term to October 23, 2029 (it previously ran through November 5, 2026 with a maximum funding commitment of 150.0; the post-amendment commitment is Not supplied). On November 4, 2026 a held-for-sale North American facility was sold; proceeds are Not supplied and accumulated restructuring charges on the site were 7.9 (10-Q Q3 page:11, page:23). The July 27, 2026 tornado is an operating event with an open insurance claim, not a financing transaction, and is described in the CP-1 handoff.
