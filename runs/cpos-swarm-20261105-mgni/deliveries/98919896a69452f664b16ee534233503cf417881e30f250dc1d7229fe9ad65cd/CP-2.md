### Credit view

Magnite is a low-leverage, cash-rich credit with one secured term loan, and the supplied evidence supports a strong but cyclical view with Medium confidence. The strongest support is that gross debt of USD 357,730 thousand is 1.27x last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA, cash of USD 392,862 thousand exceeds it, the revolver is undrawn and no debt matures before February 2029 [CP-1]. The weakest point is that earnings follow advertising budgets, depend on a few demand platforms and sit beside large seller payables, so reported cash overstates free liquidity [CP-1, CP-1A]. The outcome turns on whether connected TV volume growth outruns fee compression and whether cash returns to shareholders stay modest, and the credit agreement terms that would limit them are not supplied [CP-0].

### Key findings

Magnite's capital structure is the simplest part of the credit. Since the convertible senior notes were repaid from cash at maturity on March 15, 2026, the only debt is the 2024 term loan, with principal of USD 357,730 thousand at September 30, 2026, scheduled amortization of USD 3,632 thousand a year, and USD 342,294 thousand falling due after 2030, at the February 2031 maturity [CP-1]. The USD 175.0 million revolver matures in February 2029, was undrawn at every 2026 quarter end, and had USD 170.9 million available after USD 4.1 million of letters of credit. The October 7, 2026 repricing, a subsequent event, cut the term loan margin to Term SOFR plus 2.50%; at the September 30, 2026 principal that is roughly USD 1,789 thousand a year of lower interest before changes in Term SOFR [CP-1, calculation 11].

Earnings have improved sharply. Adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve months to September 30, 2026 is USD 280,586 thousand against USD 232,131 thousand for FY2025, and Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest 10.98x [CP-1]. That figure is the issuer's own measure and adds back stock-based compensation; on income from operations plus depreciation and amortization, gross leverage is 1.81x, still low [CP-1]. Reported net income for FY2025 includes a one-off tax benefit of USD 73,986 thousand and should not be read as run-rate.

Cash is the item most open to misreading. The company collects from buyers and pays sellers, and the filing states that "in the event a buyer defaults on payment, we may still be required to pay sellers for the inventory purchased" [Q3 2026 10-Q]. Two buyers made up 40% and 12% of receivables at December 31, 2025 [CP-1A]. Cash fell from USD 553,362 thousand at FY2025 to USD 184,648 thousand at the end of the first quarter, helped by the USD 205,067 thousand note repayment and a negative first-quarter operating cash flow, before recovering to USD 392,862 thousand [CP-1]. Net debt of USD -35,132 thousand is therefore a point-in-time figure, not a permanent surplus.

The leverage covenant is a springing test: First Lien Net Leverage Ratio below 3.25 to 1.00, tested at quarter end only if revolver utilization exceeds 35% of commitments [Q3 2026 10-Q]. Thirty-five percent of USD 175.0 million is USD 61,250 thousand; the USD 60.0 million drawn and repaid within the second quarter of 2026 was just below that level, and whether letters of credit count toward utilization is not stated in the supplied text. Nothing was drawn at quarter end, so the test did not apply. As an indicator only, not the covenant calculation, first-lien debt at the term loan principal would reach 3.25x of LTM Adjusted EBITDA only if that EBITDA fell by about 61%, with cash netting and the covenant EBITDA definition both unknown [CP-0].

Claims outside the debt figure are modest on the evidence: lease liabilities of USD 21,558 thousand current and USD 40,436 thousand non-current, and non-cancelable cloud, software and data center commitments of USD 115,219 thousand, of which USD 78,343 thousand falls in 2027 [CP-1, Q3 2026 10-Q]. No pension, factoring or consent-decree obligation was found in the sections read. The Google lawsuit is a possible recovery, with nothing recorded, and the court's September 2026 remedies are under appeal [CP-1A]; guidance for 2027 excludes any upside from them.

Management's board-approved repurchase plan has USD 144.9 million of authorization left [Q3 2026 10-Q], and USD 55,559 thousand of treasury stock was purchased in the nine months to September 30, 2026 [Q3 2026 10-Q cash flow statement]. This is the main route by which cash could leave the credit; the restricted-payment limits in the credit agreement are not supplied [CP-0, CP-1A].

### Strengths and weaknesses

The main strengths are balance-sheet capacity and growth momentum. Gross leverage of 1.27x on Adjusted EBITDA, net cash after the March 2026 repayment, an undrawn USD 175.0 million revolver, no maturity before 2029 and interest cover of 10.98x leave wide room against the one springing covenant [CP-1]. Operating momentum is strong: Contribution ex-TAC for nine months 2026 was USD 551,127 thousand against USD 474,583 thousand a year earlier, led by connected TV [CP-1]. The absence of a financial sponsor, dividends or debt-funded distributions in the sources read, and the repayment of the convertible notes without new borrowing, are supportive behaviours [CP-1A]. The Google ruling is a potential tailwind to open-web display share, though it is management's expectation, unquantified and under appeal.

The main weaknesses are cyclicality, concentration and unverified terms. Revenue is a percentage of ad spend, with lower fees on connected TV reserve auctions, so growth in the fastest channel can dilute the fee rate [CP-1A]. Demand is concentrated in a few platforms and two buyers hold over half of receivables, so a payment delay or loss would hit revenue and cash together. Cash includes publisher and customer float, seasonality lifts the fourth quarter, and first-quarter cash flow can be negative [CP-1]. Adjusted EBITDA excludes stock compensation of USD 76,648 thousand in FY2025. The credit agreement, covenant definitions, restricted-payment capacity, guarantor list and ratings are not supplied, so legal capacity to add debt or distribute cash cannot be tested [CP-0, CP-1A]. Business is one segment, so no other activity offsets a weak ad market.
