### Readiness view

The effective source set supports a credit snapshot for MURPHY OIL CORP with limitations, and no consumer is blocked. The reasoning runs from evidence to source risk to consequence. Evidence: the audited FY2025 10-K and the Q3 2026 10-Q carry complete balance sheets, operations statements, cash-flow statements, a note-level debt schedule, a lease maturity table, a segment note and a liquidity paragraph, and the Q3 release adds non-GAAP reconciliations. Source risk: all debt terms, guarantee structure and covenant levels are read from summaries inside filings, because the indentures and the credit agreement are only listed as exhibits incorporated by reference and are not in the pack; the filings do not report credit ratings; and the 2042 notes coupon differs between two filings. Consequence: the debt-structure, liquidity and coverage consumers can proceed on filed figures, but each must carry the covenant, coupon and perimeter limitations forward, and none may present covenant headroom or instrument-level seniority as verified. The assessment is source sufficiency only and CP_MODEL_INPUT_CONTRACT_NOT_EVALUATED: it does not assert normalized quarters, reconciliation, or acceptance of any downstream input.

Strongest contrary evidence: the Q3 2026 release and the Q3 10-Q describe different perimeters for the same quarter (production including versus excluding NCI), and the Q3 10-Q segment note shows 2025 comparative revenue that differs slightly from its own statement of operations. Neither blocks use; both require a perimeter label on every figure.

## Calculations

These are consistency checks used to establish whether sources tie. They are not normalized outputs and are not handed on as analytical results. Inputs are thousands of US dollars from the cited pages unless stated.

1. Notes payable tie, September 30, 2026 (10-Q Q3 page:13): 117,582 + 600,000 + 500,000 + 339,761 = 1,557,343, which equals the printed total notes payable of 1,557,343. Less unamortized debt issuance cost and discount of 17,712 gives 1,539,631, equal to the printed 1,539,631.
2. Total debt tie (page:13-14): 1,539,631 + finance lease obligations 94,721 = 1,634,352, equal to the printed total debt including current maturities. Balance sheet (page:2): 11,229 + 1,623,123 = 1,634,352. Ties. The revolver balance is nil at September 30, 2026 and 100,000 at December 31, 2025 (page:14).
3. December 31, 2025 notes tie (10-K page:112): 78,899 + 148,590 + 117,582 + 600,000 + 339,761 = 1,284,832, equal to the printed total. The 10-K principal repayable schedule (page:113: nil in 2026, 78.9 million in 2027, 148.6 million in 2028, 217.6 million in 2029, nil in 2030, 939.8 million thereafter, in dollars of millions) sums to 78.9 + 148.6 + 217.6 + 939.8 = 1,384.9 million dollars, versus 1,284.8 million dollars of notes plus the 100.0 million dollar revolver draw at that date = 1,384.8 million dollars; the 0.1 million dollar difference is rounding. The 10-K contractual obligations table prints 1,384.8 million dollars of debt excluding finance leases and interest (page:73).
4. Weighted-average coupon test for the 2042 notes (principal-weighted, on the four tranches outstanding at September 30, 2026; the release gives 6.3 percent, release 2026-11-04 page:2). Using 6.375%: (117,582 × 7.050 + 600,000 × 6.000 + 500,000 × 6.500 + 339,761 × 6.375) ÷ 1,557,343 = (828,953 + 3,600,000 + 3,250,000 + 2,165,976) ÷ 1,557,343 = 9,844,929 ÷ 1,557,343 = 6.32 percent, which rounds to 6.3. Using 5.875% for the 2042 notes: (828,953 + 3,600,000 + 3,250,000 + 1,996,096) ÷ 1,557,343 = 9,675,049 ÷ 1,557,343 = 6.21 percent, which rounds to 6.2. The release figure therefore matches the 6.375% label. This supports, but does not prove, that 6.375% is the current coupon; the cause of the change is not supplied.
5. Liquidity inputs (10-Q Q3 page:47; page:14): cash and cash equivalents 500.3 million dollars; Amended RCF commitment 2.0 billion dollars; borrowings nil; letters of credit 0.4 million dollars. Undrawn capacity net of letters of credit = 2,000.0 − 0.4 = 1,999.6 million dollars; plus cash 500.3 = 2,499.9 million dollars, consistent with "approximately 2.5 billion" and the release's 2.50 billion dollars. Not a liquidity analysis: no minimum cash, expiring facility or restricted cash review is done here. The cash figure includes NCI (release page:2) and 105.1 million dollars held outside the United States (10-Q Q3 page:47).
