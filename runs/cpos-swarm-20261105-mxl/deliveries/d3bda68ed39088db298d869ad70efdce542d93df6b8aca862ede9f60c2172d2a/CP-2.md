### Credit view

The fundamental credit view is constructive but low-conviction: MaxLinear has moved from losses to operating profit within three quarters on a mix shift toward data center products, carries only secured bank debt of 165,000 (USD 165.0 million) maturing in 2028, and shows ample covenant liquidity on reported inputs, yet the improvement is one quarter old, cash conversion is negative and the contingent claim from the terminated Silicon Motion merger is unsized. Evidence: net revenue of 218,057 and income from operations of 30,227 in Q3 2026 against an operating loss of 17,209 in Q1 2026; trailing EBITDA proxy of 29,865, so total debt to EBITDA of 5.52x, net debt to EBITDA of 2.81x and EBITDA to cash interest of 3.51x; nine-month operating cash flow of negative 17,653 and free cash flow of negative 32,688 (financial base). Risk mechanic: the ratios are carried by Q3 2026 alone (excluding that quarter the trailing EBITDA proxy would be negative 9,215), revenue is order-based with no long-term purchase commitments and the ten largest customers were 56% of nine-month 2026 revenue (business handoff), and growth requires wafer prepayments and committed purchases of 272,967 before cash returns. Credit implication: default risk in the next twelve months looks contained by cash of 81,085, an undrawn revolver of 90,000 at quarter end (pro forma 160,000 after the 2026-10-16 amendment) and a lower liquidity floor, but leverage tolerance, refinancing in 2028 and the unsized legal claim cannot be assessed with confidence until the covenant EBITDA definition, the executed agreement and an indicator of the Silicon Motion exposure are available. Confidence is Medium because the upstream business handoff rates this module READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS, not READY, which keeps it below High. The strongest contrary evidence to the constructive tone is the 2028 concentration of all maturities and the negative cash conversion; the strongest support is the speed and mix of the revenue shift and the covenant relief obtained in October 2026.

### Key findings

First, the earnings inflection is real but unseasoned. Operating income moved from negative 17,209 in Q1 2026 to negative 4,185 in Q2 2026 and 30,227 in Q3 2026 while net revenue rose from 137,188 to 168,847 and 218,057, with gross margin of 58.7% in Q3 2026. The trailing EBITDA proxy was negative until the Q3 2026 window, which means the 5.52x gross and 2.81x net leverage and 3.51x coverage exist for one window only and are carried by one quarter. This is a documentary fact from the financial base; the sustainability judgment is an analyst interpretation.

Second, cash conversion lags profit and growth is financed by the revolver. Net cash from operating activities was negative 17,653 for nine months 2026, free cash flow negative 32,688, and the revolver went from undrawn at 2026-06-30 to 40,000 drawn at 2026-09-30; management attributes the working-capital use to wafer prepayments for data center demand. The mechanic is that supplier prepayments and committed purchases of 272,967 precede cash from the ramp, so each step up in the Q4 2026 revenue outlook (management language: 240 to 260 million) needs further financing before it returns cash. Implication for liquidity: availability, not earnings, is the binding constraint.

Third, the debt structure is simple but concentrated. Debt principal is the Initial Term Loan of 125,000 due 2028-06-23 and the revolver of 40,000 drawn due 2028-03-23, floating rate at about 7.0% effective, secured on substantially all assets, with no amortization and no bonds. The mechanic is a single refinancing window in the first half of 2028 for the whole structure; the implication is no near-term maturity wall but a refinancing exposure that will be priced on the then-trailing earnings and on whether the growth is sustained.

Fourth, covenant headroom is wide on liquidity and unverified on leverage. The liquidity covenant requires 80,000 of cash plus available undrawn commitments at 2026-09-30 and the amendment of 2026-10-16 lowered the floor to 60,000; reported inputs give 171,085 against 80,000, headroom of 91,085 (about USD 91.1 million) before letters of credit, and 241,085 against 60,000 pro forma. The total net leverage ceiling is 3.50 to 1.00; on the strict GAAP proxy net leverage is 2.81x, 0.69x below the ceiling, but the agreement's Consolidated EBITDA is not supplied and may be much higher, and the second quarter 10-Q wording of the liquidity covenant conflicts with the others. The implication is that compliance is stated by the issuer but the true cushion is unknown.

Fifth, business risk is concentrated and short-visibility. Infrastructure was 57% of Q3 2026 revenue against 32% a year earlier; the ten largest customers were 56% of nine-month 2026 revenue and 65% in FY2025; customers order without long-term commitments; one supplier took 52% of Q3 2026 inventory purchases; Hong Kong was 52% of Q3 2026 revenue; export controls have already curtailed some China business. The mechanic is fast revenue transmission from a design win or order pause into cash flow while R&D and people costs are fixed in the near term.

Sixth, the Silicon Motion claim is an unsized tail. The arbitration seeks the termination fee, additional damages, fees and costs; three stockholder suits are pending; no loss is accrued; the company states damages could be significant and could require drawing all lines of credit. On a strict-proxy basis a cash outflow of about 20,613 (USD 20.6 million) would take net leverage to the 3.50x ceiling, and the liquidity covenant would absorb about 91,085 before reaching its quarter-end floor (see Calculations); neither can be tested against a claim size because none is disclosed.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths are, first, the revenue and margin momentum in infrastructure products, where revenue rose from 40,343 to 124,907 year on year in Q3 and gross margin improved to 58.7% from 56.9%; second, liquidity that is ample on the covenant measure, with cash and equivalents of 81,085, undrawn revolver of 90,000, and lender support shown by the 2026-04-22 and 2026-10-16 amendments that extended the maturity, added 30 million then 70 million of commitments and lowered the floor from 80.0 to 60.0 million; third, a simple capital structure of bank debt only, with no amortization before 2028, and a modest historical cash interest bill of 8,508 over the trailing twelve months; fourth, an asset-light fabless model with capital purchases of 15,035 over nine months; and fifth, reporting quality, with effective internal control over financial reporting and prompt disclosure of the 2026 amendments.

Weaknesses are, first, negative cash conversion, with nine-month operating cash flow of negative 17,653 and growth financed by the revolver; second, thin and new earnings, since the trailing EBITDA proxy of 29,865 is carried by Q3 2026, the trailing net loss is 29,372 and stock-based compensation of 86,923 over the trailing twelve months is a large non-cash charge relative to EBITDA; third, concentration in customers (the ten largest at 56% of nine-month 2026 revenue), in one inventory supplier (52% of Q3 2026 purchases), in ship-to geography (Asia 85% of nine-month 2026 revenue) and in an order basis with no long-term commitments; fourth, claims outside debt that are large relative to cash, notably purchase obligations of 272,967 and software and other obligations of 74,589; fifth, the unsized Silicon Motion exposure and related suits, whose count grew from two in the 10-K to three in the third quarter 10-Q; sixth, a single 2028 maturity for all debt at a floating rate of about 7.0%; and seventh, unverified covenant terms because the executed agreement, amendments and compliance certificate are not supplied.
