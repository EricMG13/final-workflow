### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Across the four handoffs, 62 groups of figures and locators were re-opened against the pinned filings and every group matched the page it cites, every derived figure in CP-1 and CP-2 was recomputed from its stated inputs and reproduced, the quotes in the evidence registers of CP-0, CP-1 and CP-1A are exact substrings of the cited pages, and the four modules agree on the same values for net debt, leverage, liquidity, coupons, free cash flow, customer concentration and the conflicts that were logged. The strongest supported contrary evidence to the cautious credit view in CP-2 is the Q3 2026 operating improvement in the filings, with higher rig counts, adjusted free cash flow of 74.5 US$m and reported covenant coverage of 5.20x; the handoffs take it into account, so it would win only if the October rig suspensions proved immaterial and SANAD spending stayed below plan. The weakest-supported element is the Saudi downside, because the five suspended rigs and the guidance are management statements in a furnished release that no filing repeats, and the handoffs correctly tag them as such. Five minor issues were found and are logged below; none is Critical or Material, so the severity engine gives a QA status of Passed, but committee use is Draft Only because the reviewer is not independent of the author, legal documents and ratings were never supplied, and the covenant EBITDA definition cannot be read. The risk mechanism for the unverified parts is direct: guarantor, ranking and covenant claims that rest on summaries could move structural priority and headroom conclusions if the executed documents differ, so those conclusions carry the label of summary evidence.
