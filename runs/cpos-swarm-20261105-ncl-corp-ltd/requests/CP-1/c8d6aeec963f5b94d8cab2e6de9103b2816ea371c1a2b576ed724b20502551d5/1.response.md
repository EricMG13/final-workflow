### Financial base view

The reported base for the issuer, as filed, shows balance-sheet debt of 14,884.5 at Q3 2026 (current and long-term debt plus exchangeable notes at carrying value) against last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA of 2,700.1, which gives 5.51x, and 5.42x after deducting cash of 250.7. At FY2025 the same measures were 5.34x and 5.26x. The deterioration comes from both terms: debt carrying value rose from 14,583.4 while last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA slipped from 2,731.4, with the third quarter of 2026 down 8.1% on the prior-year quarter. Reported net income is not a clean guide because other income of 200.1 in Q3 2026 (largely conversion-option gains) lifted net income to 631.9, above operating income of 624.2. Interest expense, net fell to 564.5 for 9M 2026 from 782.4, but the 9M 2025 figure included an extinguishment-related amount of 202.5, so the underlying fall is small. Cash interest is printed only for FY2025, so interim cash interest cover is Not supplied. Operating cash flow of 1,709.3 for 9M 2026 did not cover additions to property and equipment of 2,292.0, so growth is financed with new debt and export-credit loans; undrawn export-credit facilities of about 10.8 billion are stated against ship contracts of 19,421.2. After the quarter, an 8.750% notes issue, a larger revolver and a covenant-definition amendment changed the picture, but only in summary form. Everything here is on the issuer's own perimeter; the parent's release shows different cash, net income, debt and leverage and is not mixed in.

- Covenant types, from the 10-K: “Certain of our debt facilities include maintenance and financial covenants.” (10-K page:41). Under the Seventh ARCA, a loan to value ratio of less than 0.70 to 1.00, free liquidity of no less than 250 million at all times, an EBITDA to consolidated debt service ratio of at least 1.25 to 1.00 at each quarter-end unless free liquidity is at least 300 million, and a total net funded debt to total capitalization ratio (level not printed) (10-K page:41).
- Reported compliance: “As of September 30, 2026, we were in compliance with all of our debt covenants.” (Q3 10-Q page:13). Reported covenant ratio values and headroom: Not supplied. The issuer's EBITDA, free liquidity and debt service definitions differ from the Adjusted EBITDA and balance-sheet debt computed here, so no headroom is inferred.

### Interpretation: data to risk to credit implication

- Leverage. Evidence: Adjusted EBITDA for Q3 2026 fell 8.1% to 936.7 (Q3 10-Q page:34), while debt rose to 14,884.5. Mechanism: with a higher numerator and a lower denominator, leverage mechanically rises, and the quarter that carries most seasonal profit weakened. Implication: leverage on the issuer's own filings is 5.51x, above the 5.34x at FY2025; the company states net leverage on a parent basis of 5.5x, a consistent but non-identical measure.
- Earnings quality. Evidence: Net Yield per Capacity Day fell to 319.65 from 341.89 in Q3 2026 and occupancy was roughly unchanged; the filing states the booked position “remains below its optimal range” (Q3 10-Q page:32). Mechanism: capacity grew (Capacity Days 6,766,454 against 6,417,724) while yield fell, so volume masks price. Implication: management describes about 125 million, 100 million and 100 million of run-rate savings as benefits to be realized over time (Q3 10-Q page:33); Adjusted Net Cruise Cost Excluding Fuel per Capacity Day was lower at 152.29 against 155.65, but the revenue side has not recovered, so earnings recovery is unproven.
- Cash flow and funding model. Evidence: operating cash flow less additions to property and equipment is (582.7) for 9M 2026. Mechanism: newbuild deliveries are financed by export-credit loans at low fixed rates, such as the 1.91% Luna loan, but total debt grows with each delivery. Implication: deleveraging depends on EBITDA growth, not on free cash flow, until the newbuild cycle slows; committed ship payments of 19,421.2 stay ahead.
- Liquidity and refinancing. Evidence: availability fell to 1.1 billion at Q3 2026, then October 2026 actions raised revolver commitments from about 2.5 billion to about 2.8 billion and repaid 380.0. Mechanism: the remaining 2027 exchangeable notes (216.2 principal in total, 212.5 carrying) are classified as current at September 30, 2026, and the 2030 wall of 4,134.3 follows. Implication: near-term liquidity looks adequate on the filings, but the springing-maturity term and amended covenants need the agreements before comfort.
- Perimeter. The earnings releases describe the parent, whose cash, net income and debt differ from the issuer's. Implication: credit metrics for this issuer must come from the 10-K and 10-Qs.
