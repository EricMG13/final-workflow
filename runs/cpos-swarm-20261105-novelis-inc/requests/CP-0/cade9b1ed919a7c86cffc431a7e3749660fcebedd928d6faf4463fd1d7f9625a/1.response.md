### Evidence, risk mechanism and downstream consequence

- Evidence: debt is described in a table plus per-instrument paragraphs, while legal terms are only summarized and the indentures and credit agreement are only listed as exhibits (annual report pages 206 to 208). Risk mechanism: a summary can omit baskets, definitions and release conditions that decide guarantee coverage and covenant headroom. Consequence: CP-2 must label guarantee and covenant statements as summary-level and may not state headroom beyond the percentages and tests that the filings print.
- Evidence: the parent is Hindalco and the context names none. Risk mechanism: a perimeter built from the context alone would drop parent support, related-party flows and equity contributions. Consequence: CP-1A and CP-2 must take the parent role from the filings and keep parent figures out of the issuer's statements.
- Evidence: short-term borrowings and the ABL are described together with an ABL amendment after the annual report. Risk mechanism: using the annual report terms for current availability or maturity would misstate the facility. Consequence: CP-2 uses the Q2 quarterly report for current terms and the annual report only for March 31, 2026.
- Evidence: the releases are management documents dated within days of the filings. Risk mechanism: a release figure can differ in rounding or definition from the filing. Consequence: CP-1 and CP-5 cite the filing where both state a figure and use the release only for attributed non-GAAP measures.
