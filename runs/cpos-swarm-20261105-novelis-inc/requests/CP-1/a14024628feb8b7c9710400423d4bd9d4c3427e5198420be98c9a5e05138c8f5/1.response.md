### Financial base view

Novelis Inc. enters the latest quarter with higher leverage than a year earlier and thinner liquidity, while earnings are improving. Company-defined net leverage was 2.9x at March 31, 2025, 4.1x at March 31, 2026 and 4.7x at September 30, 2026 (RQ4 page:10; RQ2 page:10). The rise comes from the numerator: total debt principal grew from 6,212 at March 31, 2025 to 7,978 at March 31, 2026 and 9,797 at September 30, 2026 (AR page:145; Q2R page:17), while cash was 1,036, 1,254 and 1,277 at the same dates and trailing Adjusted EBITDA moved from 1,802 to 1,645 to 1,831. Cash used in operations was negative 418 in the six months ended September 30, 2026 against positive 411 a year earlier, and capital expenditure was 1,543 against 913 (Q2R page:4 to page:5), so the Bay Minette plant and the Oswego fires absorbed more cash than operations produced. The funding came from the asset-based revolver, which carried about 2.3 billion of borrowings at September 30, 2026 against 935 at March 31, 2026 (Q2R page:18 to page:19; AR page:146), a 500 term loan taken in July 2026 (Q2R page:21) and 950 of equity contributions from the parent in fiscal 2026 (AR page:111). Liquidity of 2,038 is 718 below the 2,756 at March 31, 2026; availability under committed facilities fell to 761 from 1,502 while cash rose to 1,277 from 1,254, and the filing attributes the decrease primarily to a higher revolver balance (Q2R page:61 to page:62). The latest quarter itself was strong on a reported basis: Adjusted EBITDA of 508 against 422 and net income of 210 against 163 (RQ2 page:9; Q2R page:3), but part of the Adjusted EBITDA gain is a 39 business interruption recovery and favorable metal and scrap effects that management itself cites (RQ2 page:2). Management expects capital spending to fall as Bay Minette moves to start-up and forecasts positive free cash flow and a start of deleveraging toward the end of fiscal 2027 (RQ2 page:3); that is a management expectation and is not used in any figure here.

### Definition review

- Adjusted EBITDA is a management measure. The reconciliation adds back or removes: metal price lag (a deduction of 515 in fiscal 2026 and of 257 in the six months ended September 30, 2026), Oswego fire losses net of recoveries (925 and 236), restructuring and impairment (195 and 24), start-up costs (38 and 44) and a proportional consolidation adjustment for the Logan joint venture (55 in fiscal 2026) (RQ4 page:9; RQ2 page:9; AR page:187). The fire add-back was negative 29 in the quarter, because recoveries exceeded charges.
- Start-up costs are excluded now, and the company states it "will cease excluding such start-up costs from its Adjusted EBITDA once normal production capacity is achieved at the Bay Minette plant" (Q2R page:68). All else equal, Adjusted EBITDA will then be lower than on the present definition; this is an inference from the stated change, not a company figure.
- Business interruption insurance recoveries of 39 are cited by management as a driver of the quarter's Adjusted EBITDA improvement (RQ2 page:2). Cumulative insurance recoveries related to the fires were 519 through the end of the quarter (RQ2 page:1).
- Net debt is a company definition: long-term debt, current portion, short-term borrowings and unamortized adjustments, less cash and cash equivalents (RQ2 page:10). It equals principal debt less cash. Restricted cash of 7 is not netted and operating lease liabilities are not included in debt.
- The trailing twelve month column is the company's own construction: fiscal year, less the prior six months, plus the current six months (RQ2 page:9).
- ABL fixed charge coverage test: the annual report states a minimum of 1.25 to 1.0 that applies "if an event of default has occurred and is continuing and/or excess availability is less than the greater of (1) $150 million and (2) 10% of the lesser of the total ABL Revolver commitment and the borrowing base" (AR page:148). The company reports compliance at September 30, 2026 (Q2R page:18). The June 16, 2026 amendment changed the ABL; its covenant text is not supplied, so headroom is shown in Calculations as an indication only.

### Evidence, mechanism and credit implication

- Evidence: debt rose by 1,819 in six months to 9,797 and short-term borrowings doubled to 2,631 (calculated, see Calculations). Mechanism: capital spending and working capital were funded mainly from the revolver, whose availability is limited by the lesser of the commitment and the borrowing base (AR page:148); the composition of the borrowing base is not printed in the pages read. Implication: remaining availability of 606 is the main committed backstop and is exposed to revolver usage and to any borrowing base movement.
- Evidence: Adjusted EBITDA of 1,831 trailing is built on exclusions of 257 of metal price lag deduction, 236 of fire losses and 44 of start-up costs in the six months. Mechanism: some exclusions lower the measure (metal price lag) and others raise it (fire losses, start-up costs), and the start-up exclusion ends when the plant reaches normal capacity. Implication: the reported 4.7x is definition-sensitive, and the gross ratio of 5.35x should be read alongside it.
- Evidence: the 3.25% notes due November 2026 are 13 and the nearest large note maturities are in 2029 and 2030. Mechanism: refinancing risk in the notes is concentrated beyond the next two years, but the revolver, Brazil and other short-term borrowings of 2,631 are classified as due within one year. Implication: near-term refinancing risk sits in bank lines, not the bonds.
- Evidence: cash interest is printed only annually. Mechanism: interim coverage cannot be computed on a cash basis. Implication: coverage is shown only for fiscal years.
