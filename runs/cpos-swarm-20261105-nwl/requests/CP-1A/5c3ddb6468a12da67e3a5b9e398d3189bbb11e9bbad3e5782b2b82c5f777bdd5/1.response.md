### Business and transaction view

NEWELL BRANDS INC. is a widely held, Nasdaq-listed consumer goods group in the execution phase of a multi-year turnaround, with no sponsor, no controlling holder identified in the pack and a business that depends on large retailers: Amazon and Walmart were about 17% and 13% of FY2025 net sales, and the 10-K says retailer bargaining power may limit its ability to recover cost increases through pricing. Profit is concentrated in Learning and Development, while Home and Commercial Solutions, the largest segment by sales, produced a segment operating loss in FY2025 and a small profit over nine months 2026 that includes a 101 tradename impairment in Q3 2026. For creditors the transaction story is a refinancing, not a sponsor event: in July and August 2026 the issuer put in place a new borrowing-base facility of up to 800, issued 600 of 6.250% senior notes due 2031 and redeemed the 6.375% notes due 2027, which removes the 2027 maturity but moves part of the capital structure to a secured facility and leaves the 2028 notes as the next large maturity. The documented behaviour is a refinancing through a new note issue and a redemption above par, a continued quarterly dividend while free cash flow after capital expenditure was negative over nine months 2026, and a stated intention to reduce debt and factoring; no priming, uptier, drop-down or debt-funded distribution appears in the pack, but the executed documents that would show legal capacity are not supplied.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transactions in the pack are financings by a listed company with no sponsor, not an acquisition or a sponsor transaction, and all are executed and reported as completed except where marked as a subsequent event. On 2026-07-30 the issuer and a subsidiary borrower, Newell Brands Ireland Services DAC, with certain domestic and foreign subsidiary guarantors, entered a five-year asset-based revolving credit facility led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. of up to 800, subject to a borrowing base of qualified cash, receivables, inventory, equipment and intellectual property, with an uncommitted accordion of up to 500; 490 was drawn on closing to repay and replace the 1,000 secured Credit Revolver that was due in August 2027. The facility is secured by a lien on deposit accounts, receivables, inventory, equipment and intellectual property, carries a Consolidated Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio test that applies only when borrowings exceed levels relative to capacity, and matures on 2031-07-30 or, if indebtedness of 125 or more is still outstanding 91 days before that debt's own scheduled maturity, on that 91st day before it. In August 2026 the issuer sold 600 of 6.250% senior unsecured notes due 2031 under an indenture dated 2026-08-19, received proceeds of 592, used them to redeem the 6.375% senior notes due 2027 at 101.530% (total consideration about 508) and to repay part of the ABL borrowings, and recorded a loss on extinguishment of 16 in Q3 2026 including 3 of revolver costs. At 2026-09-30, 215 was drawn on the ABL and letters of credit of 46 were issued under it, leaving net availability of about 480 on borrowing-base availability of 741; gross debt was 4,841 and net debt 4,592. The effect on liquidity is a longer runway and a clear 2027, but drawn secured borrowings now sit ahead of the unsecured notes in the same collateral pool as receivables and inventory, and, on the analyst's reading of the springing maturity (Analyst Interpretation), the facility's 2031 date holds only if the 2028 notes are refinanced or repaid in time; the 2028 notes' exact maturity date is not printed in the pages read.

Two receivables financings changed in the quarter and after it. The Customer Receivables Purchase Agreement limit was cut from 700 to 625 effective 2026-09-14, with a first-priority lien granted to the purchaser over certain receivables not sold and a lower funding rate; 300 was outstanding at 2026-09-30. The separate Receivables Facility, with 135 outstanding at 2026-09-30, was renewed effective 2026-10-02 (a subsequent event) to 2027-10-01 with its limit on factored receivables outstanding cut to 75. Management says it expects to reduce total debt and factoring by a couple of hundred million dollars in 2026 and that lower factoring proceeds reduce expected 2026 operating cash flow, now about 150; this is Management Language and is unproven. Earlier financings in the record: in May 2025 the issuer sold 1.25 billion of 8.500% senior notes due 2028 and used the proceeds to redeem its 4.200% notes due 2026 in June 2025, and in 2025 it repaid its 3.900% notes due 2025. No acquisition, divestiture or ownership change occurs in 2026 in the pack; the 2019 sale of The United States Playing Card Company is the source of a pending indemnification claim by the buyers, whose amount is not quantified. Status of sponsor, ownership percentages and use of any proceeds beyond debt refinancing: Insufficient Information.
