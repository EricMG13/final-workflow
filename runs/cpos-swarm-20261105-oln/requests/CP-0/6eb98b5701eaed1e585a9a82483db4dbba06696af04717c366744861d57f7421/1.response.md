### Readiness view

The pack supports a creditor snapshot of OLIN Corp (the Virginia registrant Olin Corporation and its consolidated subsidiaries, a single reporting perimeter) at Q3 2026, with limitations, and no consumer is blocked. The filed 10-K and three 10-Qs are original authority for statements, notes, debt, liquidity and segment facts, while the three Exhibit 99.1 releases are attributed management claims that carry the non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA and net debt ratio. Evidence runs from covenant relief narrative (10-Q Q3 page:13, page:50) to a source risk: the numeric maintenance levels, the executed credit agreements and the October 27, 2026 amendments are exhibits incorporated by reference and not supplied, so covenant headroom and the combined-company facility terms rest on summary text. The downstream consequence is that leverage and liquidity can be computed from printed inputs but a covenant-compliance conclusion cannot be tested. Pinned sources contain no Huntsman financials, so only Olin-side merger facts are supported. Readiness for each consumer is set out in the tables below.

### Entity and instrument roles evidenced

Olin Corporation is the only issuer and registrant. Guarantors of the Senior Secured Credit Facility are certain domestic subsidiaries, not named, with covenant-relief-period release of guarantees and liens (10-Q Q3 page:13). Blue Cube Operations LLC is a wholly owned subsidiary named as a litigant (10-Q Q3 page:25). Olin Winchester, LLC is a party to the Fifteenth Amendment of the Amended and Restated Credit and Funding Agreement (10-Q Q3 page:60). Huntsman Corporation is the merger counterparty, not an obligor. Which subsidiaries are guarantors and the restricted perimeter of the covenant EBITDA are unresolved and carried as gaps.

### Strongest contrary evidence and limitations

The releases report adjusted EBITDA on a non-GAAP basis and a net debt ratio that differ in definition from the covenant net leverage ratio, which nets only unrestricted cash and excludes receivables financing up to a stated cap (10-Q Q3 page:14); the two cannot be treated as one measure. Quarter versus nine-month distinctions are printed for the statements (10-Q Q3 page:3) and the reconciliation (EX-99.1 Q3 page:10). On the latest quarter, sales, net income and cash agree between the release (EX-99.1 Q3 page:1, page:3) and the 10-Q (page:3, page:2); only these were compared and nothing was reconciled. Management statements about merger timing and synergies are claims, not facts.
