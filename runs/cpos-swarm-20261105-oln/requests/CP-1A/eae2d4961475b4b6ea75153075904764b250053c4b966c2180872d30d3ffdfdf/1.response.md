### Business and transaction view

OLIN Corp is a publicly listed, sponsor-free manufacturer whose creditor case rests on integrated chlor alkali and epoxy assets plus a defence-linked ammunition business, and whose near-term outcome is set by a single pending transaction: an all-stock merger of equals with Huntsman Corporation, expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. The strongest support is diversification and integration, with three segments, no customer above 10 percent of 2025 sales and a captive chlorine outlet through epoxy and chlorinated organics. The weakest point is the Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls segment, where the Freeport vinyl chloride monomer outage cut segment income to USD 80.2 million for the nine months from USD 270.8 million. What decides the credit is whether the merger closes, what the combined company borrows under the new USD 2,500.0 million facility, and whether fourth quarter 2026 earnings meet the USD 80 million to USD 100 million guidance, so execution risk on the merger is the main swing factor.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

On June 15, 2026 Olin entered into a definitive agreement with Huntsman Corporation for an all-stock merger of equals to form OlinHuntsman Corporation; each Huntsman share converts into 0.5476 Olin shares, and Olin holders are expected to own approximately 54.5% and Huntsman holders approximately 45.5% of the combined company (10-Q Q3 page:7, page:8). Shareholders of both companies approved the proposals on August 25, 2026 and the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period expired, but remaining regulatory approvals and customary conditions are outstanding and closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027, having been expected in the first half of 2027 in the July release (10-Q Q3 page:8; EX-99.1 Q2 page:3). The registration statement on Form S-4/A was declared effective on July 13, 2026 (10-Q Q3 page:58). Olin could owe a termination fee of USD 121 million, and the Q2 2026 10-Q adds reimbursement of Huntsman expenses of up to USD 30 million (10-Q Q2 page:56). Olin booked acquisition-related costs of USD 13.7 million in Q3 2026 and USD 24.3 million in the nine months and expects USD 35 million to USD 40 million for 2026 (10-Q Q3 page:45). The merger agreement restricts certain Olin actions without Huntsman's consent before closing (10-Q Q3 page:57).

The new debt is the OlinHuntsman Credit Facility of USD 2,500.0 million under a second amendment dated October 27, 2026 to Olin's credit agreement, effective on the merger Closing Date subject to closing and customary conditions. It has a term loan of USD 750.0 million replacing Olin's existing USD 650.0 million term loan facility, and a revolver of USD 1,750.0 million replacing Olin's USD 1,200.0 million revolver and Huntsman's revolver. The term loan amortises at 1.250% per quarter from the first full fiscal quarter after closing through the fourth anniversary quarter, then 1.875% per quarter, and the facility matures five years after the Closing Date. It is guaranteed by certain Olin subsidiaries and certain Huntsman subsidiaries, secured by first priority liens on certain assets, carries a pricing grid on the net leverage ratio, and has covenants on secured debt to EBITDA and on EBITDA to interest expense, with levels not supplied. The term loan is expected to be fully drawn at closing, with proceeds and any revolver loans used to refinance Olin's senior secured facility, repay and terminate Huntsman's revolver and pay fees (10-Q Q3 page:8, page:9). Olin's senior notes of USD 669.3 million, USD 515.3 million and USD 600.0 million and its USD 465.0 million receivables facility are not stated to be refinanced (CP-1 handoff; 10-Q Q3 page:12). No new notes issuance is described. Huntsman's debt, its balance sheet, its amounts under its revolver and the pro forma capital structure are not in the pack, so combined leverage and the net new debt at closing are not calculable.

### Credit translations

Evidence, mechanic and implication for the main conclusions:

First, earnings concentration. Evidence: Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls segment income of USD 80.2 million for nine months 2026 against USD 270.8 million, with the Freeport VCM outage costing USD 72.2 million (10-Q Q3 page:41). Mechanic: the segment carries the largest share of depreciation and amortization (282.4 of 353.7 in nine months 2026, 10-Q Q3 page:23), so volume loss and price weakness flow directly into trailing adjusted EBITDA, which fell to USD 536.6 million. Implication: net debt to adjusted EBITDA rose to 5.21x from 4.08x at FY2025 and cash interest cover fell to 3.15x from 3.66x (CP-1 handoff), narrowing tolerance for further operating shocks. Counter-evidence: Winchester and Epoxy improved, and management guides fourth quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA of USD 80 million to USD 100 million, which is a management expectation.

Second, merger transition. Evidence: all-stock merger with a new USD 2,500.0 million facility replacing Olin's bank facilities and Huntsman's revolver (10-Q Q3 page:8, page:9). Mechanic: the new term loan of USD 750.0 million is expected to be fully drawn at closing and amortises at 1.250% per quarter, while refinancing resets covenants, collateral and the pricing grid; Huntsman's obligations enter the structure. Implication: refinancing risk for Olin's bank debt is addressed only if the merger closes; the combined leverage and the ranking of existing notes cannot be assessed from this pack, and rating agencies are on watch with different directions (Fitch positive, S&P developing, Moody's negative outlook), which signals uncertainty about the combined credit.

Third, liquidity under relief. Evidence: cash of USD 214.8 million, USD 1,029.6 million undrawn revolver, USD 465.0 million receivables facility maturing November 19, 2027 and covenant relief ending by September 30, 2027 (CP-1 handoff). Mechanic: if the merger does not close, the relief period still ends by September 30, 2027 on current terms and the receivables facility then matures within about two months. Implication: monitoring posture should focus on the merger timetable, fourth quarter earnings and any covenant amendment; the numeric covenant levels are not supplied.
