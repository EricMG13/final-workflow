### Financial base view

For OPEN TEXT CORP the financial base shows a credit that has de-levered and cut interest cost while its top line contracts. Gross debt principal at June 30, 2026 was below June 30, 2025 by the amount of repayments in FY2026, funded by divestiture proceeds and cash on hand, and debt at September 30, 2026 was almost unchanged from June 30, 2026. Against adjusted EBITDA, total debt fell from 3.63x at FY2025 to 3.07x at FY2026 and was 3.10x on a last-twelve-months basis at September 30, 2026; EBITDA to cash interest paid rose from 3.69x in FY2024 to 5.92x in FY2026 and 6.26x on the last-twelve-months basis. The calculated covenant ratio printed in the filings fell from 3.35 at December 31, 2025 to 2.75 at June 30, 2026 against a 4.50 maximum, so maintenance headroom is large on the printed figures. The pressure points are the contracting revenue base (Q1 FY2027 revenue down year on year, with client support and license lines falling while cloud grew), the divestitures that removed earnings together with debt, the heavy shareholder distributions in FY2026 relative to free cash flow, an unquantified Canada Revenue Agency tax dispute, and the October 2026 refinancing that lengthened maturities but swapped 6.90% secured notes due 2027 for 6.70% and 7.15% secured notes due 2031 and 2033, raising the secured debt share. The view is bounded by three missing inputs: covenant EBITDA, letters of credit that may reduce revolver availability, and cash after the October 2026 transactions.

### Covenants and liquidity

The revolver and the term loan both carry a maintenance test [10-Q-Sep26 page:19 — "we must maintain a “consolidated net leverage” ratio of no more than 4.50:1.00 at the end of each financial quarter"]. The ratio is defined with debt reduced by unrestricted cash, including guarantees and letters of credit, over trailing twelve months earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization, restructuring, share-based compensation and other miscellaneous charges. The printed values are: [10-Q-Dec25 page:23 — "as calculated in accordance with the applicable agreement, was 3.35:1:00"], [10-Q-Mar26 page:24 — "as calculated in accordance with the applicable agreement, was 3.02:1:00"] and [10-K page:124 — "as calculated in accordance with the applicable agreement, was 2.75:1.00"]. For September 30, 2026 the 10-Q states [10-Q-Sep26 page:21 — "As of September 30, 2026, we are in compliance with our consolidated net leverage ratio covenant."] and the earnings release gives a management statement [PR-Nov26 page:3 — "Our net leverage ratio is 2.55x as of September 30, 2026."] with no definition or inputs.

Headroom to the 4.50 maximum on the printed ratios (calculated, in turns of leverage): 1.15 at December 31, 2025, 1.48 at March 31, 2026 and 1.75 at June 30, 2026. The covenant ratio is higher than the simple net debt to adjusted EBITDA computed above (2.75 against 2.56 at June 30, 2026). The definition differs from the metric computed here: it adds guarantees and letters of credit to debt, whose amounts are not supplied, and uses covenant EBITDA, which is not printed; the gap cannot be reconciled from the pack. A second leverage test governs incremental term debt capacity [10-Q-Sep26 page:20 — "additional amounts, subject to meeting a “consolidated senior secured net leverage” ratio not exceeding 2.75:1.00"].

Liquidity at September 30, 2026 (calculated): cash and cash equivalents 983,292 plus the undrawn 750,000 revolver is 1,733,292 before any letters of credit drawn on the revolver. Letters of credit and bank guarantees are described only generically [10-Q-Sep26 page:29 — "guarantees relating to taxes and letters of credit on behalf of parties with whom we conduct business"]; a search of the pack for letters of credit, standby, bank guarantee and revolver availability wording found no amount. The same total at June 30, 2026 was 956,024 plus 750,000, or 1,706,024. Near-term maturities: the current portion of term-loan amortization is 35,850 and the first note maturity was December 1, 2027 until the October 2026 redemption. The revolver matured December 19, 2028 at the balance-sheet date and was extended on October 1, 2026, and the first note maturity after the October 2026 redemption is February 15, 2028. Product-category revenue was restated in Q1 FY2027, so category trends before that quarter are not comparable without the bridge (limited to Q1 FY2026). Release headline figures are in millions and rounded; filings are in thousands. Adjusted EBITDA includes add-backs for share-based compensation and special charges (33,290 in Q1 FY2027 and 133,020 in FY2026), so it is higher than reported operating profit plus depreciation and amortization alone; special charges recur each year (135,305 in FY2024, 145,890 in FY2025, 133,020 in FY2026), which weakens the case for treating them as one-off. Cash interest paid is gross of interest received.
