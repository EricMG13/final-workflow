### Business and transaction view

For OPEN TEXT CORP the business is a diversified, global enterprise software and cybersecurity vendor whose revenue is dominated by annual-renewal client support and cloud subscriptions, which gives good revenue visibility but a base that fell in the latest quarter, with the support and license lines declining and growth concentrated in cloud lines. The registrant is a widely held listed company with no sponsor and no holder above about eleven percent, so the creditor risk is not sponsor extraction; it is a management and Board capital allocation choice between debt reduction, a quarterly dividend and share repurchases, taken in a year of leadership change and a strategic enterprise assessment whose conclusions are scheduled for an investor day in February 2027. The credit-relevant history is acquisition-led: the 2023 Micro Focus acquisition created the debt load, the 2024 AMC sale and the 2026 eDOCS and Vertica sales repaid part of it at the cost of earnings, and the October 2026 refinancing replaced the 2027 secured notes and part of the 2028 notes with secured notes due 2031 and 2033, which extends the maturity wall but raises the share of secured debt and the coupon on the replaced paper. Management's FY2027 outlook, a management statement, points to a lower margin and lower free cash flow than the last twelve months, which is the main forward uncertainty. Evidence is primary and dated for facts and management-only for outlook and strategy; no customer-level, rating or executed-document evidence is available.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transaction that matters for the creditor is the October 2026 refinancing, a closed subsequent event after the September 30, 2026 balance sheet and inside the cutoff. Type: a refinancing and partial liability management. Parties: the registrant as issuer, guaranteed on a senior secured basis by certain subsidiaries; the initial purchasers, trustees and holders tendering are not named in the pack. Sponsor: none. On October 1, 2026 the company issued two series of senior secured notes: [10-Q-Sep26 page:43 — "On October 1, 2026, the Company issued $500 million in aggregate principal amount of 6.70% senior secured notes due 2031"] and a second 500 million series at 7.15 percent due 2033, both paying interest semi-annually from April 15, 2027 and maturing on October 15, 2031 and October 15, 2033 respectively. Debt raised is therefore 1,000 million of new secured notes, secured with the same priority as the senior credit facilities [10-Q-Sep26 page:44 — "are secured with the same priority as the Company’s senior credit facilities"] and structurally subordinated to liabilities of subsidiaries that do not guarantee them. Use of proceeds: on October 2, 2026 the proceeds, together with cash on hand, funded the redemption in full of the 1.0 billion 6.90 percent senior secured notes due 2027 including the redemption premium, accrued interest and costs, and the cash purchase and cancellation of 300.0 million of the 3.875 percent senior notes due 2028 [10-Q-Sep26 page:44 — "On October 2, 2026, the Company purchased and cancelled $300.0 million of the aggregate outstanding principal amount of the Senior Notes 2028"]. The revolving credit facility of 750.0 million, undrawn at September 30, 2026, was amended on October 1, 2026 to extend its maturity from December 19, 2028 to October 1, 2031, subject to certain conditions, and its borrowings rank pari passu with the term loan and the new secured notes.

Effect on the credit, as the financial base handoff computes it: principal debt falls from 5,827,563 to 5,527,563 (US$ thousands); the nearest note maturity moves from December 1, 2027 to February 15, 2028 and falls from 1,900,000 across the two near notes to 600,000 for the remaining 2028 notes; new maturities of 500,000 fall in fiscal 2032 and 500,000 in fiscal 2034; secured principal stays at 2,527,563 while its share of the total rises from 43.4 percent to 45.7 percent; and the annual cash coupon on the exchanged notes falls by 11,375 before premium and fees. The cost side is not quantified in the pack: the redemption premium, the tender price and the cash used are not stated, so the liquidity effect is a reduction of cash on hand by an amount that is not supplied. The management characterisation is that the transactions provide greater financial flexibility [PR-Nov26 page:1 — "providing the Company with greater financial flexibility"], which is Management Language and is not adopted; the documented facts are a longer maturity profile, a higher secured share and coupons of 6.70 and 7.15 percent against 6.90 percent on the retired secured notes. Status: closed. New debt issues in the pack: only these two series; there are no pending or committed financings, no acquisition financing and no dividend recapitalisation.

Earlier transactions that shape the structure: the 2023 Micro Focus acquisition for 6.2 billion, for which the term loan and the 2027 secured notes were raised; the 2024 AMC sale for 2.275 billion, which funded a term loan prepayment of 1.31 billion in FY2024 together with cash on hand; and the eDOCS and Vertica sales in FY2026 for 163.0 million and 150.0 million, each applied to prepay the term loan. These sales removed revenue and earnings while reducing debt, which is why the divestiture comparability break is carried in every period comparison. A further dividend of $0.28 per share was declared on November 3, 2026, payable December 18, 2026; it is a future term, not an executed payment.
