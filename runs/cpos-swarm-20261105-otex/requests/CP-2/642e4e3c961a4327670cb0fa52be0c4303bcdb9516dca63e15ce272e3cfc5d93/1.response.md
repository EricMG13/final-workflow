### Credit view

OPEN TEXT CORP is a moderately leveraged, cash-generative software credit whose balance sheet has improved, but whose revenue base is shrinking, so the credit view is stable-to-cautious rather than improving. The strongest support is deleveraging to 3.10x total debt to adjusted EBITDA on a last-twelve-months basis, cash interest cover of 6.26x, USD 844,805 thousand of last-twelve-months free cash flow, an 84.1 percent recurring revenue share and no sponsor. The weakest point is Q1 FY2027 revenue down 4.0 percent with support and license declining, while management guides to a lower margin and lower free cash flow than the last twelve months. What decides the outcome is whether cloud growth and cost actions hold earnings near USD 1,878,183 thousand against the guided USD 1,643.2 to 1,711.1 million, and whether distributions to shareholders are trimmed to keep paying debt down. Category: Analyst Interpretation.

### Key findings

OPEN TEXT CORP has de-levered through divestiture proceeds and cash: debt principal fell from USD 6,485,375 thousand at June 30, 2025 to USD 5,836,525 thousand at June 30, 2026 and USD 5,827,563 thousand at September 30, 2026, taking total debt to adjusted EBITDA from 3.63x to 3.07x and then 3.10x on a last-twelve-months basis, and net debt to 2.58x [financial base]. Documentary Fact. The mechanism matters: part of the repayment came from selling businesses that also carried earnings, so the ratio improvement is real but partly bought with a smaller EBITDA base [business context timeline].

The interest burden has fallen sharply, from cash interest paid of USD 533,866 thousand in FY2024 to USD 300,111 thousand on a last-twelve-months basis, lifting adjusted EBITDA to cash interest from 3.69x to 6.26x [financial base]. Documentary Fact. Lower interest leaves more of each dollar of EBITDA to reach free cash flow, which was USD 844,805 thousand on a last-twelve-months basis, and gives earnings room to fall before debt service is strained. The cover uses issuer-defined adjusted EBITDA, which adds back share-based compensation and special charges that have recurred in each of the last three years (USD 135,305 thousand in FY2024, USD 145,890 thousand in FY2025 and USD 133,020 thousand in FY2026) [financial base], so cover on a stricter definition would be lower. Analyst Interpretation.

The covenant position is wide on the printed values: the calculated net leverage ratio was 3.35, 3.02 and 2.75 at December 2025, March 2026 and June 2026 against a maximum of 4.50 [financial base]. Documentary Fact. The September 30, 2026 value is not printed in the filing; management states 2.55x without a definition, and that statement stays attributed [financial base]. Management Language. Headroom of 1.75 turns at June 2026 means a maintenance breach is unlikely to be the first point of stress; the likelier constraint is cash flow used for distributions.

Recurring revenue gives visibility but the trend is negative: recurring revenue is 84.1 percent of Q1 FY2027 revenue, support revenue fell 9.0 percent and license 8.0 percent, while cloud grew 4.3 percent, and the cloud net renewal rate eased to 95 percent from 96 percent [business context]. Documentary Fact. Support is the largest revenue line and is annual-renewal, so attrition hits the highest-margin contracted base first; cloud growth is replacing it at a lower margin. Total revenue was down 4.0 percent year on year in Q1 FY2027 [financial base], partly from the eDOCS and Vertica sales.

Management's FY2027 outlook is below the last twelve months: revenue USD 5,135 to 5,185 million, adjusted EBITDA margin 32 to 33 percent and free cash flow USD 625 to 725 million, which implies adjusted EBITDA of about USD 1,643.2 to 1,711.1 million against USD 1,878,183 thousand on a last-twelve-months basis [business context]. Management Language, qualified. If met, and with principal unchanged at USD 5,527,563 thousand after the October 2026 events, total debt to adjusted EBITDA would rise to about 3.23x to 3.36x (calculation 1). Analyst Interpretation. The outlook is a management statement and can be revised after the enterprise assessment.

The October 2026 refinancing extended the maturity profile but made the structure more secured. USD 500 million of 6.70 percent secured notes due 2031 and USD 500 million of 7.15 percent secured notes due 2033 replaced the USD 1,000 million 6.90 percent secured notes due 2027, USD 300 million of the 2028 notes were bought back and the USD 750.0 million revolver was extended to October 2031 [financial base; business context]. Documentary Fact. The nearest note maturity moves to February 2028 (USD 600.0 million), but the secured share of principal rises from 43.4 percent to 45.7 percent because unsecured notes were retired, so unsecured creditors rank behind a larger secured pool, while the annual cash coupon on the exchanged notes falls by USD 11,375 thousand. Cash used for the tender, the premium and costs are not supplied, so post-October liquidity is lower than the USD 983.3 million of cash at September 30, 2026 by an amount that cannot be stated.

Shareholder returns absorb most of the free cash flow: dividends and repurchases were USD 737,669 thousand in FY2026, 91.4 percent of USD 807,517 thousand of free cash flow [financial base]. Documentary Fact. The company is widely held, with the largest holder at 11.17 percent and no sponsor, so no sponsor extraction is documented [business context]. Documentary Fact. The distribution policy is a Board choice, and the dividend is subject to credit agreement limits whose terms are not in the pack, so how far the Board can keep distributing if earnings fall cannot be measured. Insufficient Information.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. (1) Deleveraging and cover: total debt to adjusted EBITDA of 3.10x and cash interest cover of 6.26x on a last-twelve-months basis give room for earnings to fall; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact. (2) Recurring revenue and diversification: an 84.1 percent recurring share, no customer above 10 percent of receivables, no country other than the United States above 10 percent of revenue and a single reported segment spread across product categories reduce concentration; basis: business context. Documentary Fact. (3) Liquidity and maturities: USD 983.3 million of cash plus an undrawn USD 750.0 million revolver at September 30, 2026, before any letters of credit, a revolver extended to October 2031 and no note maturing before February 2028; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact. (4) Cash generation: USD 844,805 thousand of last-twelve-months free cash flow, up in Q1 FY2027 to USD 138,517 thousand from USD 101,229 thousand; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact. (5) Ownership: dispersed institutional holders and no sponsor remove the sponsor-extraction mechanic; basis: business context. Documentary Fact. (6) Covenant headroom of 1.75 turns at June 2026 on the printed ratio; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact.

Weaknesses. (1) Shrinking base: revenue down 4.0 percent in Q1 FY2027, support down 9.0 percent, license down 8.0 percent, cloud net renewal rate down to 95 percent; basis: financial base and business context. Documentary Fact. (2) Guided step-down: the FY2027 outlook implies lower adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow than the last twelve months; basis: business context. Management Language, qualified. (3) Earnings quality: adjusted EBITDA relies on add-backs, including special charges that recur every year; basis: financial base. Analyst Interpretation. (4) Distribution intensity: 91.4 percent of FY2026 free cash flow returned to shareholders, leaving debt reduction that in FY2026 was partly funded by divestiture proceeds; basis: financial base. Analyst Interpretation. (5) Structure and cost: a larger secured share and higher coupons on the replacement notes; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact. (6) Contingent and unmeasured claims: the Canada Revenue Agency matter, which the filing sizes at approximately USD 88.8 million for fiscal 2012 to 2016 and up to approximately USD 470 million of deferred tax assets for fiscal 2017 to 2021 with no accrual, sits outside debt; letters of credit, guarantor structure, ratings and post-October cash are not supplied; basis: financial base. Documentary Fact for the amounts; Insufficient Information for the unmeasured items. (7) Leadership and strategy in flux: the chief executive and chief financial officer both changed within FY2026 and an enterprise assessment may change portfolio and reporting; basis: business context. Documentary Fact.

## Calculations

1. Illustrative leverage if the FY2027 outlook is met, with principal unchanged: pro forma principal USD 5,527.563 million divided by outlook-implied adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,711.1 million is 3.23x; divided by USD 1,643.2 million is 3.36x. Inputs from the financial base (principal 5,527,563 thousand) and the business context (outlook-implied EBITDA range 1,643.2 to 1,711.1 million). This ignores scheduled amortization and any further repayment, and uses gross principal because post-October cash is not supplied; it is a scenario, not a forecast.
2. Free cash flow after an annualized dividend at the Q1 FY2027 rate: dividends of USD 66.5 million in Q1 FY2027 times four is USD 266.0 million, which is 42.6 percent of the low end (USD 625 million) and 36.7 percent of the high end (USD 725 million) of guided free cash flow. Inputs: business context outlook range; dividend paid in Q1 FY2027 as stated in the financial base. Illustrative only; repurchases are excluded and discretionary.
3. All other figures (leverage, cover, margins, free cash flow, secured share of 45.7 percent, distributions of 91.4 percent, headroom of 1.75 turns) are taken without recalculation from the financial base and business context handoffs.
