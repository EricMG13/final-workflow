### Business and transaction view

The issuer, PBF Holding Company LLC, is the borrowing and operating holding company for six U.S. refineries with about 1,000,000 barrels per day of capacity, reported as one segment, and it is wholly owned by PBF Energy Company LLC under the listed parent PBF Energy Inc. Its revenue is the sale of unbranded fuels and other refined products at prevailing market prices, almost 89% of it gasoline and distillates, to a diverse base with one customer, Shell, at about 13% of revenue. Because crude and product prices both float, the business has little pricing control: results depend on industry crack spreads, crude differentials, throughput, refinery reliability and the cost of renewable fuel credits. That mechanism explains the swing from a loss year in FY2025, when the Martinez refinery was shut and then restarting, to very large earnings in 2026, when management says geopolitical conflict widened margins and the Martinez refinery returned to full operations in May 2026. The creditor reading is that the operating model is cyclical and event-exposed, with the business risk concentrated in refinery uptime, margin volatility, environmental and emissions obligations and legal investigations, rather than in customer or contract concentration. The transaction picture in 2026 is a refinancing and simplification: the 6.00% notes due 2028 and the 7.875% notes due 2030 were redeemed, 500.0 of 7.25% notes due 2034 and 550.0 of zero-coupon exchangeable notes due 2032 were issued, the asset-based revolver was amended to a 4,000.0 commitment through 2031-09-30, and after the quarter the 9.875% notes due 2030 were redeemed on 2026-10-26 with cash on hand. The new exchangeable notes are exchangeable into shares of the parent, which links the issuer's liabilities and reported earnings to the parent's share price. Ownership is a listed-parent structure with no financial sponsor named in the pack, and documented behaviour toward creditors is mixed: parent cash contributions and full-par or premium note redemptions are supportive, while distributions to the parent chain to fund its dividends, past asset distributions to affiliates and the parent-level secured note on the hydrogen plants are items a creditor should track. This is a fact pack, not a rating or a recommendation.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

All items below are Documentary Facts from the issuer's filings unless marked, with their status. No transaction is a sponsor transaction: the context names no sponsor and the pack describes none.

2034 7.25% senior notes. Type: new senior unsecured notes. Parties: PBF Holding and PBF Finance as issuers with certain subsidiary guarantors, Wilmington Trust as trustee. Status: completed 2026-05-28 (Q3-10Q page:21 "the Issuers issued $500.0 million in aggregate principal amount of 2034 7.25% Senior Notes"). Use of proceeds: net proceeds of 492.1 plus available cash redeemed all of the 2028 6.00% notes on 2026-06-25 (Q3-10Q page:23 "On June 25, 2026, the Company redeemed all $801.6 million aggregate principal amount outstanding of its 2028 6.00% Senior Notes"). Terms: guaranteed on a senior unsecured basis, effectively subordinated to secured debt including the revolver, structurally subordinated to non-guarantor subsidiaries, with a change-of-control repurchase at 101% on a ratings decline (Q3-10Q page:23 "at a purchase price of 101% of the principal amount"). Liquidity effect: cash used was the shortfall between the notes redeemed and net proceeds, and the 2028 maturity was removed. Creditor implication: the nearest maturity moved from 2028 to 2034 for this tranche, at a higher coupon than the notes replaced but with maturity extension; Analyst Interpretation.

2032 0.00% exchangeable senior notes. Type: new senior unsecured exchangeable notes with an embedded exchange option and capped call. Parties: PBF Holding and PBF Finance as issuers, PBF Energy Inc. as a party to the indenture and registration rights agreement, U.S. Bank Trust Company as trustee. Status: completed 2026-09-17. Amount: 550.0; net proceeds about 533.2, of which 27.7 paid for the capped call and the rest, with cash, redeemed the 2030 7.875% notes (Q3-10Q page:18 "The Issuers used $27.7 million of the net proceeds to pay the cost of the capped call transactions"). Terms: no regular interest and no principal accretion (Q3-10Q page:18 "The 2032 Exchangeable Notes do not bear regular interest, and the principal amount of the 2032 Exchangeable Notes will not accrete."), maturity 2032-01-15, initial exchange rate of 10.3306 PBF Energy Class A shares per 1,000 of principal (Q3-10Q page:18 "initial exchange rate is 10.3306 shares of PBF Energy Class A common stock, per $1,000 principal amount"), exchange price about $96.80, capped call cap price about $123.20 (Q3-10Q page:21 "initial cap price of approximately $123.20 per share"), settlement in cash up to principal with the excess in cash or PBF Energy shares at the issuers' election (Q3-10Q page:18 "will be settled in cash up to the aggregate principal amount of the notes being exchanged"). Mechanics: the exchange feature is a bifurcated derivative liability that the issuer marks to fair value, at 197.6 at 2026-09-30 with a 29.9 unrealized loss in Q3 2026, using a PBF Energy share price input of $76.96 (Q3-10Q page:31 "Share price $ 76.96"); the debt host carries a large discount with an effective rate of about 7.0%. Creditor implication: zero cash coupon lowers cash interest, but the issuer carries the principal in cash settlement, the derivative creates earnings volatility linked to the parent's share price, and the instrument depends on parent equity; Analyst Interpretation. A registration rights agreement requires PBF Energy to file a shelf by 2026-12-31 with special interest of 0.25% rising to 0.50% if the issuers fail to comply (Q3-10Q page:20 "on or prior to December 31, 2026").

Redemptions. 2030 7.875% notes: 500.0 redeemed 2026-09-24 at 103.938%, about 519.7 (Q3-10Q page:21 "redeemed at a price equal to 103.938% of the aggregate principal amount thereof"). 2028 6.00% notes: 801.6 redeemed 2026-06-25 at 100%. 2030 9.875% notes: 800.0 redeemed 2026-10-26 at 106.67%, about 853.4, from cash on hand; this is a Subsequent event, with a loss on extinguishment of 70.4 to be booked in Q4 2026 (Q3-10Q page:33 "will be recorded as a Loss on extinguishment of debt in the Consolidated Statements of Operations in the fourth quarter of 2026"). Liquidity effect: cash on hand funds the redemption, reducing cash by about 853.4 plus accrued interest from the 2,510.6 at 2026-09-30. Each redemption was a full redemption of the series, so no holder was treated differently within a series (Analyst Interpretation); no priming, uptier, drop-down or non-pro-rata exchange is documented.

Asset-based revolving credit facility. Type: amendment and restatement of the 2023 agreement with Bank of America as administrative agent. Status: effective 2026-09-30 (Q3-10Q page:24 "extended the Revolving Credit Facility through September 30, 2031, increased the maximum commitment from $3.5 billion to $4.0 billion, and reduced the commitment fees on the unused portion"). It was drawn 100.0 at 2025-12-31 and 750.0 at 2026-03-31, and undrawn at 2026-06-30 and 2026-09-30 (financial base handoff). Hydrogen plants asset purchase. Type: purchase of two hydrogen plants at the Torrance refinery previously under operating leases. Parties: PBF LLC and Air Products; contributed to the issuer. Status: closed 2026-09-09. ### Credit translations

1. Revenue durability and margin risk. Evidence: revenues are priced at spot market (Q3-10Q page:28 "These revenues are largely based on the current spot (market) prices of the products sold") and the Q3 2026 margin rise is attributed by management to crack spreads and differentials (Q3-10Q page:45 "Average industry margins were notably more favorable"). Risk mechanic: no price floor and no contractual margin, so cash flow tracks external spreads and refinery uptime. Credit implication: debt service capacity is high when spreads are wide and thin when they narrow, as FY2025 showed with Adjusted EBITDA of 29.5; the trailing LTM Adjusted EBITDA of 3,505.2 rests on two quarters. Counter-evidence logged: management says refining fundamentals remained exceptionally strong (Q3-PR page:1 "refining fundamentals remained exceptionally strong"), Management Language not adopted as a forecast. Category: Analyst Interpretation.
2. Refinancing and liquidity. Evidence: the 2026 notes sequence, the revolver extension to 2031-09-30 and cash of 2,510.6 at 2026-09-30 with 800.0 redeemed afterwards. Risk mechanic: removal of 2028 and 2030 maturities and the zero-coupon exchangeable lowers refinancing and interest burden; the secured revolver ranks ahead of unsecured notes in collateral. Credit implication: refinancing risk is low in the near term for this perimeter; effective subordination of the unsecured notes to the revolver and to the parent-linked exchange feature should be monitored. Category: Analyst Interpretation.
3. Concentration. Evidence: Shell at about 13% of revenue, six refineries across four regions. Risk mechanic: customer concentration is modest; asset concentration is in the Martinez and Torrance West Coast refineries and regulatory exposure in California. Credit implication: event risk at one refinery (as the Martinez fire showed) matters more than customer loss. Category: Analyst Interpretation.
4. Sponsor alignment and leakage. Evidence: distributions to the parent chain, asset distributions to PBFX and SBR, parent dividend funding language, hydrogen contribution. Risk mechanic: cash can move up to fund parent dividends when liquidity is strong; restricted payment covenants are not supplied. Credit implication: Neutral or Mixed; monitor distributions against liquidity and the hydrogen note security. Category: Analyst Interpretation.
5. Key uncertainties. Evidence: unquantified Martinez investigations, the Chalmette fire, the hydrogen note, labour agreements and the missing documents. Credit implication: open items could add cash costs or lien constraints; they are information gaps, not adverse conclusions.
