### Source-risk mechanisms and downstream consequences

- **Covenant headroom.** Evidence: three maintenance tests are printed, including a total net leverage maximum of "no greater than (a) 4.75 to 1.00 for each of the fiscal quarters in 2026" and compliance ("At September 30, 2026, we were in compliance with these financial covenants.") (Q3 10-Q page:20). Mechanism: each ratio is "as defined in the credit facility agreement", and no reported ratio, EBITDA definition or net debt definition is printed. Consequence: CP-1 and CP-2 cannot compute headroom and must write Not calculable; any ratio they build from adjusted EBITDA is not the covenant ratio.
- **Maturity labels and dates.** Evidence: the Q2 10-Q says the term loan maturity was extended "to March 2031" (page:20) and the revolver was extended to March 2031 (page:20); the Q3 10-Q describes the same Q2 event as an extension "to May 2031" and the revolver "matures in May 2031" (page:20). Mechanism: the sources do not say that a later amendment changed the date; the Q3 10-Q exhibit list shows a Fifth Amendment dated September 29, 2026 (page:39) and the Q2 10-Q lists a Third and a Fourth Amendment (page:40), none of which is supplied. Consequence: the maturity wall must show the Q3 reading as latest filed, flag the Q2 difference, and not state the reason.
- **Revolver capacity.** Evidence: a $450 million facility and "remaining borrowing capacity of $427 million" (Q3 10-Q page:20). Mechanism: the $23 million difference is not itemized; the 10-K says letters of credit reduce borrowing capacity ("Outstanding letters of credit reduce the amount we can borrow under our revolving credit facility", page:45) and gave about $27 million at 2025-12-31, but no 2026 letters-of-credit amount is printed. Consequence: liquidity must use $427 million as printed and label the $23 million as implied, not as letters of credit.
- **Adjusted measure comparability.** Evidence: "Effective January 1, 2026, we are excluding from Adjusted segment EBIT, pension expense related to U.S. and Canada pension plans that we have taken steps to terminate. Prior periods were not recast." (Q3 10-Q page:9). Mechanism: the 2026 adjusted EBIT excludes pension expense of plans to be terminated, which the reconciliation shows as 23,125 for nine months 2026 and nothing for 2025. Consequence: growth in adjusted EBIT and EBITDA between 2025 and 2026 mixes a definition change with performance; CP-1 must disclose the basis and not present it as like for like.
- **Annual report version.** Evidence: later filings cite a Form 10-K/A while the pack holds a Form 10-K. Mechanism: an amendment may restate, add Part III or leave the statements unchanged; the sources do not say which. Consequence: FY2025 figures are taken from the pack 10-K and cross-checked to the December 31, 2025 comparative columns of the 2026 filings (the tie-outs below agree); if the amendment changed them, that is not visible.
- **Perimeter and the Bank.** Evidence: consolidated statements include "Customer deposits at Pitney Bowes Bank" of 576,949 current and 71,000 noncurrent (Q3 10-Q page:3). Mechanism: the Bank is a wholly owned subsidiary (10-K page:6) whose deposits are outside the debt table, and the sources do not give stand-alone issuer, guarantor or Bank financials. Consequence: leverage must state that deposits are excluded from the debt table, and cannot be computed for the restricted group or the borrowers alone.
