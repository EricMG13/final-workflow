### Financial base view

The reported financial base is usable for the current-period credit view at the level of consolidated quarterly statements, but it is built on unaudited interim reports and a single year-end balance sheet, so every annual figure used here is assembled from quarters rather than read from an annual statement. Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA grew in Q3 2026 against Q3 2025, while nine-month Adjusted EBITDA is lower than the prior-year nine months, because of lower upfront capital payments in Canada, higher operating costs in the United States and internationally, and higher share-based compensation (R3 page:5). Debt is a single secured revolving facility drawn in US dollars and one series of US dollar unsecured senior notes, with no current portion of long-term debt on the balance sheet. Because the debt is US dollar denominated and the statements are in Canadian dollars, reported debt moves with the exchange rate independently of repayment. Leverage computed from reported figures is steady and moderate, interest cover on a nine-month basis improved, and covenant headroom is wide. The limits are equally clear: free cash flow weakened as capital spending rose, cash is small, liquidity is revolver-dependent, a contested tax reassessment is a contingent cash call, and the annual comparators, the covenant EBITDA definition and the facility pricing are not in the pack. Every ratio below is a calculation from printed components; the issuer prints only the covenant ratios, the debt-to-capitalization ratio, working capital and the liquidity claim.

### Narrative review by dimension

Each item gives evidence, risk mechanic and credit implication. Figures are in the tables above.

- Leverage. Evidence: the leverage chart table and the debt movement reconciliation. Mechanic: debt in US dollars is revalued in Canadian dollars each period, so carrying value fell by less than the net repayment because the foreign exchange adjustment added back; EBITDA recovered in Q3 2026 while cash fell, so net leverage rose from June 30, 2026 even though debt was flat. Implication: leverage is stable and moderate, but net leverage sits above the issuer's own sustained target and falls only if debt reduction continues.
- Coverage. Evidence: the interest cover chart table. Mechanic: lower debt reduced interest paid, lifting nine-month cover, while EBITDA declined. Implication: coverage is comfortable and improving on the available basis; the LTM cash interest ratio cannot be completed.
- Cash flow quality. Evidence: the cash flow table. Mechanic: capital expenditure rose faster than cash from operations fell, working capital absorbed cash in the first and third quarters in line with seasonality described in R3 page:8, and Q3 2026 free cash flow was negative. Implication: shareholder returns and debt repayment this year exceed internally generated free cash flow; the shortfall was met from cash, which is now low.
- Liquidity. Evidence: the liquidity chart table. Mechanic: available liquidity is mostly undrawn revolver; letters of credit on the revolver rose from US$51 million at March 31, 2026 to US$60 million at September 30, 2026 and consume its availability, while letters of credit on the other two facilities fell; commitments of US$43 million step down in June 2027. Implication: liquidity is adequate but revolver-dependent, and the contested tax reassessment would draw on it.
- Data quality. Evidence: the CP-0 conflicts. Mechanic: all statements are unaudited; the nine-month net earnings percentage change is printed with opposite signs on R3 page:2 and page:9 (the change is a decrease); Q2 2026 diluted loss per share exceeds basic. Implication: use amounts, not printed percentages, and do not rely on Q2 2026 per-share data.
- Definition risks. Evidence: the definitions table. Mechanic: Adjusted EBITDA is after cash-settled share-based compensation and includes upfront customer payments; covenant EBITDA is undisclosed. Implication: covenant headroom is reported rather than verified.
- Key gaps. Evidence: the gap table below. Mechanic: absent annual statements and debt agreements remove the audited base and the legal terms. Implication: committee conclusions on structure, guarantees and recoveries cannot rest on this pack.
