### Credit view

PENN's fundamental credit profile is improving but still burdened by landlord obligations, so the view is constructive on liquidity and refinancing and cautious on fixed charges. The strongest support is liquidity of USD 2,020.9 million and net debt of 1.62x LTM Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,088.2 million, with bank maturities pushed to 2031 and 2033. The weakest point is that USD 8,157.6 million of lease and financing obligations sit outside debt and cross-default into the debt agreements, and that the online segment still loses money. What decides the outcome is whether the retail margin gains and online cost savings hold while rent escalates, and whether the USD 400.0 million of notes due January 15, 2027 are refinanced on acceptable terms. [CP-1; CP-1A]

### Key findings

PENN's reported leverage fell through 2026 on the company's own definition. Total debt over LTM Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA is 2.58x against 3.50x at FY2025, and net debt over EBITDA is 1.62x against 2.67x, as cash rose to USD 1,044.8 million and EBITDA rose to USD 1,088.2 million from USD 830.1 million [CP-1 Chart data: leverage]. The measure is management's non-GAAP view struck after triple net operating lease rent and it leaves out USD 4,359.4 million of financing obligations and finance leases, so an extended perimeter that adds them gives net leverage of 5.63x [CP-1 Calculations]. Creditors should therefore treat the improvement as real but read the level against the landlord claims, not against the headline ratio.

Liquidity is the clearest strength. Cash of USD 1,044.8 million plus an undrawn revolver of USD 976.1 million gives USD 2,020.9 million, against USD 432.3 million of debt maturing in the next twelve months and no committed facility located that expires within that period [CP-1 Chart data: liquidity; CP-1 Calculations]. The 2026 refinancing extended the revolver and Term Loan A to April 2031 and Term Loan B to May 2033 and added USD 600.0 million of 6.75% notes due 2031 [CP-1A Transaction summary and new debt issues]. The near-term maturity that remains is the USD 400.0 million of 5.625% notes due January 15, 2027, which the company classifies as long-term on its intent and ability to refinance.

The earnings recovery has two unequal sources. Retail Segment Adjusted EBITDAR was USD 501.8 million in Q3 2026 against USD 465.8 million, with the margin at 33.7% against 32.8% [CP-1 Financials and credit metrics]. The online segment's loss narrowed to USD 18.3 million from USD 76.6 million, but revenue excluding the gaming tax gross-up was USD 157.0 million against USD 158.2 million, and online gaming revenue fell, so the gain came from lower marketing, general and administrative and labor costs after the ESPN agreement ended [CP-1; CP-1A Company and operating model]. That makes part of the earnings recovery depend on keeping costs low.

Fixed charges remain heavy. LTM cash paid for interest of USD 382.4 million gives cash interest cover of 2.85x on LTM Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA, up from 2.17x at FY2025 [CP-1 Chart data: interest cover]. Leases are the structural issue: the AR PENN and 2023 Master Leases are cross-defaulted and a master lease default could trigger a default under the debt agreements, and the development funding GLPI provides adds annual rent of about 7.9% of the funds received [CP-1A Credit translations]. Covenant headroom is not measurable: the maximum total net leverage of 4.50x and minimum interest coverage of 2.00x are printed but the reported ratios and definitions are not supplied [CP-1 Covenants].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Liquidity and refinancing runway [Documentary Fact]: PENN holds cash of USD 1,044.8 million and an undrawn revolver of USD 976.1 million maturing April 2031, so liquidity of USD 2,020.9 million covers the USD 432.3 million of twelve-month maturities several times over, and bank maturities now sit in 2031 and 2033 [CP-1 Chart data: liquidity; CP-1A Transaction summary and new debt issues]. Deleveraging on management measures [Documentary Fact for the figures, Analyst Interpretation for the reading]: total debt fell to USD 2,806.6 million from USD 2,904.1 million while LTM Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA rose to USD 1,088.2 million, giving 2.58x gross and 1.62x net, which lowers the burden of the floating term loans and eases refinancing [CP-1 Chart data: leverage]. Retail earnings power [Documentary Fact]: Segment Adjusted EBITDAR of USD 501.8 million in Q3 2026 on a 33.7% margin, spread across 28 jurisdictions and 42 gaming and racing properties, supports cash flow from a diversified base [CP-1 Financials and credit metrics; CP-1A Company and operating model]. Governance record [Analyst Interpretation]: no priming, uptier, drop-down or collateral release was found, buybacks of USD 354.4 million in FY2025 stopped in nine months 2026, and the February 2026 activist settlement removed an open contest, which is supportive of creditors though the debt documents that would constrain future behaviour are not supplied [CP-1A Ownership, governance and capital allocation].

Weaknesses. Landlord obligations [Documentary Fact for amounts, Analyst Interpretation for the reading]: lease and financing obligations of USD 8,157.6 million sit outside the debt figure, of which USD 4,359.4 million is not deducted in EBITDA, so fixed-charge leverage is far higher than the headline, and a lease default is also a debt default [CP-1 Claims outside debt; CP-1A Credit translations]. Online segment [Analyst Interpretation]: a loss of USD 18.3 million in Q3 2026 and a loss of USD 78.6 million over the LTM period mean the segment still consumes cash, and the improvement rests on cost cuts with revenue ex gross-up flat, so earnings could slip if marketing returns [CP-1A Company and operating model]. Interest burden [Documentary Fact]: cash interest of USD 382.4 million against LTM Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA of USD 1,088.2 million leaves cover of 2.85x, and LTM net income was a loss of USD 11.0 million [CP-1 Financials and credit metrics]. Earnings quality [Documentary Fact]: Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA is a management measure that adds back items including USD 44.9 million labelled Other for nine months 2026, so its underlying level cannot be tested against the ledger [CP-1 Quality and definition notes on EBITDA]. Information gaps [Insufficient Information]: covenant ratios, guarantor identity, restricted-payment capacity, large-holder data and ratings are not supplied, which prevents a view on headroom and creditor protections and limits confidence to Medium [CP-0; CP-1A Gaps].
