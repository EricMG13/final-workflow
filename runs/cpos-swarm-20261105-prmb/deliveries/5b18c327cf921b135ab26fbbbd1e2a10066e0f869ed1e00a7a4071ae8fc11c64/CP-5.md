### Evidence trace view

The evidence trace holds. Each material claim in the financial base, business context and credit synthesis handoffs was matched to a located source span or to a recomputation from located figures, and the three handoffs agree on every shared figure, period and perimeter label. The credit view of the synthesis, moderately cautious with Medium confidence, follows from the cited basis and does not go beyond it. Four claims are only partly supported, each as wording or inference rather than figures: the remark that first-quarter earnings are structurally lower, the description of the largest customer as a retail customer, the description of the receivables build as seasonal, and the assumption that the revolver is undrawn, which the filing implies but does not print. No claim is unsupported and no contradiction between modules was found. The earlier readiness handoff described a residual of 232.2 as debt the Q3 report does not itemize; the financial base corrected that, and the correction is right. The integrity verdict for the evaluated set is nonetheless Restricted, not Committee Ready, because the conclusions that matter most to a creditor, namely covenant headroom, the springing maturity, guarantor perimeter, sponsor consent rights over distributions and added debt, and the current maturity ladder, rest on documents and a filed schedule that are not in the sources. The review itself is limited: it was done in the same session as the analysis, from the same extraction text, and the page locators are extraction chunks rather than printed pages.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against a moderately cautious view is that liquidity is 1,063.8, free cash flow is 319.6 against 203.4, add-backs have fallen to 124.4 from 262.5, net leverage has fallen from 3.52x in March to 3.32x, the company-reported ratings carry a positive outlook at one agency by column order, and management left Adjusted Free Cash Flow guidance of 790 to 810 unchanged. If integration costs keep falling and freight costs ease, trailing Adjusted EBITDA could rise while shareholder returns stay within free cash flow. What would make that case win: fourth-quarter Adjusted EBITDA of at least 363.0, the amount implied by the low end of guidance, adjusted free cash flow in the implied range of 187.1 to 207.1, repurchases staying below free cash flow, and early refinancing of the 2028 notes.

Downside chain with observable triggers. Initiating condition: a freight, materials or tariff cost shock that pricing does not offset. First operational break: gross margin falling below the 29.9% of nine months 2026. Financial transmission: lower Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow while the dividend of 0.12 a share per quarter and the repurchase program continue. Leverage consequence: with net debt unchanged at 4,772.5, a fall of about 5% in trailing Adjusted EBITDA takes net leverage to 3.5x (Calculations), the level the stockholders agreement summary uses as a test. Liquidity and refinancing consequence: revolver drawings above the assumed nil balance would reduce liquidity below 1,063.8, and lenders would face the 3.875% notes due October 31, 2028 and the April 2029 notes with weaker cover. Triggers to watch: quarterly gross margin, add-backs, the revolver balance, repurchase pace against free cash flow, the sponsor's disclosed holding, and the covenant certificate when obtainable.
