### Readiness view

The effective source set is READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS for the selected minimum module set. Filed statements (audited annual, unaudited interim) from the issuer's own filings control all financial facts; the earnings releases are attributed management material and rank below them. Nothing found blocks preparation or creates an unresolved identity boundary: every source names the same registrant, Qnity Electronics, Inc., and the first-page text of each source states a distinct period. The limits are: (a) the executed credit agreement, its first amendment and the two indentures are exhibits cited by reference and are not in the pack, so debt terms, guarantor mechanics and covenant definitions come only from filing summaries; (b) the annual report and the 2025 comparatives are presented on a carve-out basis for the period before the November 1, 2025 separation, and the release comparatives are pro forma; (c) no agency rating, market quote, valuation input or earnings presentation is supplied; (d) extraction is text only, so charts and images are not inspected. These limits are bounded and do not stop any selected consumer from running.
