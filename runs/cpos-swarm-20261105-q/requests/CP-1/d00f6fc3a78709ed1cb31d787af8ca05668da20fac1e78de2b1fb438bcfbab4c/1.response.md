### Financial base view

The reported-fact base is adequate to underwrite a first view, and it is cleaner than the carve-out history suggests because the standalone capital structure has been in place since November 1, 2025. On filed figures, net sales were 4,300 for nine months 2026 against 3,564 a year earlier, and Adjusted Operating EBITDA was 1,345 against 1,091. Total debt of 4,019 at September 30, 2026 compares with 1,124 of cash and a fully undrawn 1,250 revolver (1,238 after letters of credit). Calculated leverage is 2.37x total and 1.71x net on LTM Adjusted Operating EBITDA, down from 2.79x and 2.16x at FY2025. Three points temper this. First, the EBITDA measure excludes indirect legacy costs (84 in nine months 2026) and transformation, integration and other charges (123), which are real cash outflows. Second, interest cover on LTM interest expense flatters the run rate, because the LTM window includes a quarter in which the term loan was outstanding for only two months. Third, FY2025 and nine months 2025 are carve-out periods, so year-on-year changes in interest, tax and allocated costs mix a basis change with performance. The covenant headroom cannot be read from the sources: the maintenance test and its level are disclosed, the issuer's reported ratio and its covenant EBITDA definition are not.

### Definition review

Adjusted Operating EBITDA is defined as pre-tax earnings before interest, depreciation, amortization, non-operating pension and OPEB items, foreign exchange gains and losses, indirect legacy costs and significant items (Q3 p:39). It is not covenant EBITDA, and it is not a GAAP measure. It adds back items that were cash costs in nine months 2026: indirect legacy costs (84 in the income statement; 114 of cash in the release free-cash-flow bridge, R3 p:11), transformation, integration and other charges (123 in the income statement; 84 of cash, R3 p:11) and a foreign tax cost of the separation (52, R3 p:11). The release prints Adjusted Free Cash Flow of 607 for nine months 2026 against calculated operating cash flow less capex of 357; the difference is these three items (see Calculations). The company's total debt (4,019) is carrying value, net of unamortized discount and issuance costs; the face value of notes and term loan is about 4,100 at December 31, 2025 (K p:120). Net sales and EBITDA comparatives in the releases for Q3 2025 are pro forma (R3 p:2) and are not used.

### Conflicts and consistency

No numeric conflict was found between the 10-Q Q3 2026 statements and the Q3 2026 release (net sales, net income, cash, debt, operating cash flow and capex agree). The release shows two Q3 2025 EBITDA figures (382 unadjusted in the reconciliation, 370 pro forma in the headline); this module uses the unadjusted one because it is on the same carve-out basis as the filed 10-Q comparatives. The 2026 expense caption (Transformation, integration and other charges) differs from the two FY2025 10-K captions; this is a presentation change, and no number is contradicted.
