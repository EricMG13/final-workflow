### Financial base view

The reported base supports a clear picture through June 30, 2026 and a bounded one after it. Through March 31, 2026 the group had 3,100.0 of borrowings (a term loan and secured notes), no drawing on its asset-based revolver, and cash almost equal to borrowings, so net debt was small. During the second quarter it bought Kodiak with preferred equity and shares, and in June it sold 3,000 of new notes whose proceeds sat in escrow until TopBuild closed on July 1, 2026. The June 30 balance sheet therefore shows borrowings of 6,111 but also 3,000 of restricted cash that is not counted in liquidity. After closing there are a further 3,000 incremental term loan and 1,000 of Series C preferred stock, which are reported only as subsequent events. Earnings are measured on company-defined Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes TopBuild, includes Kodiak only from April 1, 2026, and adds back stock compensation, restructuring, transaction, transformation and inventory fair value items. Seasonality is strong: the first quarter earns almost nothing and the half-year cash flow from operations is negative. Every ratio below is therefore labelled with its perimeter and is not a pro forma figure.

## Calculations

All inputs are as printed; US$ millions unless stated. Rounding differs by source: Q2 2026 and half-year figures are whole millions, FY2025 and Q1 2026 have one decimal, so results are accurate to roughly 1.

1. LTM Adjusted EBITDA at June 30, 2026 = FY2025 647.8 + H1 2026 273 - H1 2025 196 = 724.8. LTM at March 31, 2026 = 647.8 + 1.2 - (-9.0) = 658.0. Check on H1 2025: Q1 2025 (-9.0) plus Q2 2025 (204) = 195 against 196 printed, a rounding difference.
2. Borrowings principal at June 30, 2026 = long-term debt principal 6,100 (850 + 1,500 + 2,250 + 1,500) + ABL 11 = 6,111. At December 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026 = 850.0 + 2,250.0 = 3,100.0.
3. Net debt = principal less unrestricted cash: December 2025 3,100.0 - 2,361.6 = 738.4; March 2026 3,100.0 - 3,046.3 = 53.7; June 2026 6,111 - 2,774 = 3,337. If the 3,000 escrow were treated as cash, June net debt would be 337, but that cash was committed to the purchase and released July 1, 2026.
4. Ratios: 3,100.0 / 647.8 = 4.79; 738.4 / 647.8 = 1.14; 3,100.0 / 658.0 = 4.71; 53.7 / 658.0 = 0.08; 6,111 / 724.8 = 8.43; 3,337 / 724.8 = 4.60.
5. Cash interest LTM at June 30, 2026 = 138.0 + 106 - 23 = 221.0; at March 31, 2026 = 138.0 + 15.4 - 0 = 153.4 (Q1 2025 printed as nil). Cover: 647.8 / 138.0 = 4.69; 658.0 / 153.4 = 4.29; 724.8 / 221.0 = 3.28; 273 / 106 = 2.58.
6. Pro forma borrowings at July 1, 2026 = 6,111 + incremental term loan 3,000 = 9,111. The 3,000 of notes released from escrow are already inside 6,111. Contractual coupon run-rate on June 30, 2026 borrowings at printed rates = 850 x 5.64% + 2,250 x 6.75% + 1,500 x 6.500% + 1,500 x 6.875% + 11 x 4.70% = 47.9 + 151.9 + 97.5 + 103.1 + 0.5 = 401.0 (rounded components sum to 400.9), before the incremental term loan whose margin is not supplied. Floating-rate borrowings (term loan 850 and ABL 11) are 14.1% of 6,111 at June 30, 2026; including the 3,000 incremental term loan (assumed floating, as it is Term SOFR based) they are 3,861 of 9,111, or 42.4%, so a 100 basis point rise adds about 38.6 of annual interest.
7. Liquidity = cash + ABL availability: 2,361.6 + 1,970 = 4,331.6; 3,046.3 + 1,980 = 5,026.3; 2,774 + 1,930 = 4,704, agreeing with the filing's 4.70 billion. Netting test for the ABL: commitment 2,000 less drawn 11 less letters of credit 62 = 1,927, which rounds to the stated 1.93 billion; at March 31, 2026, 2,000 less 21.2 = 1,978.8 rounds to 1.98 billion. Both fit capacity stated net of letters of credit. At December 31, 2025, 2,000 less 21.2 = 1,978.8 does not equal the stated 1.97 billion, which implies a borrowing base below commitments at that date. If the stated June 2026 figure were instead before letters of credit, liquidity would be 4,642.
8. Covenant trigger: 10% of the lesser of the borrowing base and commitments of 2,000 is at most 200, so the trigger is the greater of 120 and at most 200, that is at most 200.
