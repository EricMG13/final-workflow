### Credit view

Rithm Capital Corp. is a large, diversified mortgage and real estate finance platform whose credit quality rests on refinancing short-dated, mostly secured funding rather than on stable cash earnings, and the fundamental view is neutral to cautious with medium confidence. The strongest support is a dominant, profitable Origination and Servicing segment, a stated position of compliance with all debt covenants, USD 1,708.750 million of unrestricted cash and repeated senior unsecured note issuance in 2024, 2025 and 2026. The weakest point is the maturity profile: USD 7,546.015 million falls due in Q4 2026 and USD 10,619.584 million in 2027, about half of contractual debt is recourse, and available financing is described as uncommitted. Continued access to secured funding, stable servicing values and the new real estate and asset management businesses reaching profit will decide the outcome.

### Key findings

First, the maturity profile is the central risk. Contractual debt at September 30, 2026 was USD 37,877.221 million on the maturity-table basis, of which USD 19,680.259 million is recourse and USD 18,196.962 million non-recourse [CP-1, chart data and calculations]. Maturities of USD 7,546.015 million in the fourth quarter of 2026 and USD 10,619.584 million in 2027 together are USD 18,165.599 million, a 15-month window holding 47.96% of the total, and USD 12,967.028 million of that window is recourse [CP-1, calculations 4]. Mechanically, most of these are secured warehouse, repurchase and servicing lines that Rithm rolls routinely, so default risk turns on lender willingness and collateral values rather than on cash generation. The implication for creditors is that a pause in secured funding markets or a fall in collateral values would force asset sales or margin posting into a thin cash cushion.

Second, liquidity is thinner than the capacity figure suggests. Unrestricted cash was USD 1,708.750 million at September 30, 2026, against USD 1,847.626 million at December 31, 2025, and 27.43% of the USD 6,230.073 million recourse maturity of the fourth quarter of 2026 [CP-1, liquidity and calculations]. Available financing of USD 10,785.284 million is stated to be uncommitted and collateral-dependent, so none of it is counted as undrawn committed liquidity. Management expects cash, operating cash flow and the ability to extend or refinance secured lines to cover needs for twelve months, and says its ability to extend or refinance short-term borrowings is critical to its outlook [CP-1, liquidity]. The implication is that headroom cannot be measured from the pack, and margin calls on USD 14.2 billion of secured financing agreements would draw on the same cash.

Third, leverage is high on a total basis and lower on a recourse basis. Principal-basis debt to equity in Rithm Capital Corp. was 4.39x at September 30, 2026 against 4.20x at December 31, 2025, and recourse debt to equity was 2.28x against 2.31x [CP-1, calculations 6]. Neither is a covenant measure and recourse debt includes collateralised repurchase and warehouse borrowings. No EBITDA is reported, so conventional leverage and cover ratios are not supplied.

Fourth, earnings quality is weak relative to the funding stack. Pre-tax income plus interest expense over interest expense was 1.54x for nine months 2026, 1.48x for nine months 2025 and 1.49x for FY2025, and includes fair value changes on mortgage servicing rights; the change in fair value of MSRs net of hedges was a gain in Q3 2026 and a loss in nine months 2026 [CP-1, calculations 7; CP-1, base view]. Net income for nine months 2026 was USD 438.665 million against USD 627.514 million, income tax expense was USD 321.554 million against a benefit of USD 27.456 million while cash taxes were USD 11.398 million, so the tax charge appears to be largely non-cash, Analyst Interpretation [CP-1, financials table]. Origination and Servicing produced USD 968.563 million of segment pre-tax income in nine months 2026, against group pre-tax income of USD 760.219 million, so the other segments and corporate costs net to a loss [CP-1A, company and operating model]. Asset Management reported a pre-tax loss of USD 83.484 million and Commercial Real Estate a loss of USD 74.855 million.

Fifth, the notes sit behind other claims. The three senior unsecured notes, USD 775.000 million at 8.000% due 2029, USD 500.000 million at 8.000% due 2030 and USD 500.000 million at 8.500% due 2031, are unsecured, were not guaranteed by any subsidiary at issuance, and require Total Unencumbered Assets of at least 120% of unsecured debt [CP-1, debt instruments; CP-1A, transaction summary]. Collateral of consolidated securitization entities is not available to other creditors of the parent. Total Unencumbered Assets is not printed, so headroom on the 120% test cannot be calculated. As a recovery mechanic only, residual value for noteholders is what remains after secured and subsidiary creditors, and no recovery outcome is concluded here.

Sixth, governance on documented actions is neutral. No sponsor or controlling holder is identified, the REIT distribution rule requires regular payouts, a Series F preferred was issued in January 2026, and no priming, uptiering or collateral release is documented, but legal capacity under the indentures cannot be assessed because they were not supplied [CP-1A, governance table].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Scale and diversification: nine-month 2026 revenue of USD 4,423.413 million came from five segments, with Origination and Servicing the largest by assets, equity and earnings, and the servicing portfolio of about USD 878.9 billion of unpaid principal balance is a recurring-fee base [CP-1A, company profile; Documentary Fact]. Demonstrated market access: the 8.500% notes due 2031 were issued on May 14, 2026 for net proceeds of about USD 493.7 million, a Series F preferred raised about USD 242.1 million in January 2026, and management reports several securitizations in Q3 2026, which is Management Language [CP-1A, transaction summary]. Stated compliance: the filings say Rithm was in compliance with all debt covenants and that about USD 1.1 billion of liquidity at the servicers exceeds regulatory requirements, Documentary Fact but unverifiable without the agreements [CP-1, liquidity and covenants]. Lower recourse leverage than total leverage: recourse debt to equity of 2.28x is about half the total ratio of 4.39x, Analyst Interpretation of the CP-1 ratios. Transparency: quarterly reporting, an unqualified FY2025 audit and effective disclosure controls support monitoring, Documentary Fact [CP-1A, governance table].

Weaknesses. Refinancing concentration: USD 18,165.599 million of debt falls due within the 15 months to December 31, 2027, Documentary Fact and derived [CP-1]. Uncommitted funding: available financing is uncommitted and collateral-dependent, Documentary Fact. Earnings sensitivity and negative operating cash flow: operating cash flow for nine months 2026 was an outflow of USD 1,313.851 million because loans originated for sale are in operating cash flow, offset by financing inflows of USD 3,597.283 million; this is Documentary Fact and the reading that the group depends on continuous external funding is Analyst Interpretation [CP-1, financials table]. Structural subordination of the unsecured notes, Analyst Interpretation of the guarantee and recourse facts. Loss-making new segments after the December 2025 acquisitions of Crestline and Paramount (now Elecor), whose depreciation and amortization of USD 278.619 million for nine months 2026 compares with USD 71.492 million a year earlier, Documentary Fact [CP-1A, company and operating model]. Unmeasured items: ratings, covenant levels and committed liquidity are Insufficient Information, and their absence is not an adverse conclusion.

Thesis. The credit rests on a profitable servicing and origination core and demonstrated access to secured and unsecured funding, set against a large, short-dated and partly uncommitted funding stack that leaves the senior unsecured noteholders exposed to refinancing and structural subordination.
