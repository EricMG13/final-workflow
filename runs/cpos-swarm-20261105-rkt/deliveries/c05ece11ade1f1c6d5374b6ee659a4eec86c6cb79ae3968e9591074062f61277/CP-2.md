### Credit view

Rocket Companies, Inc. is a large but cyclical mortgage credit whose unsecured leverage looks moderate today and could weaken quickly if rates keep rising. The strongest support is a full post-acquisition earnings base: last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,843 million against senior notes of USD 10,743 million gives 3.78x gross and 2.83x net of USD 2,699 million of cash, with no note due before 2027 and an undrawn USD 2,500 million revolver. The weakest point is dependence on mortgage rates, on USD 14,187 million of mostly uncommitted secured funding and on Agency servicing contracts, under a founder whose creditor-protection documents are not available. The outcome will be decided by whether adjusted revenue holds near the guided USD 2.1 billion to USD 2.3 billion without a funding or legal shock.

### Key findings

Rocket's unsecured leverage has fallen to a moderate level, but the improvement is earnings-led and the latest ratio is the first on a clean base. Senior notes of USD 10,743 million (principal) stand at 3.78x last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA of USD 2,843 million and 2.83x net of USD 2,699 million of cash, against 8.16x for FY2025 and 5.65x at March 31, 2026, when earnings held only part of the Mr. Cooper business while debt already held its assumed notes. Principal rose from USD 10,455 million at December 31, 2025, so deleveraging came from earnings, not repayment [CP-1 leverage table; Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation on cause].

The ratio flatters the forward position. Adjusted revenue fell from USD 2,822 million in Q1 2026 to USD 2,761 million in Q2 2026 and USD 2,574 million in Q3 2026, and the company guides Q4 2026 to USD 2.1 billion to USD 2.3 billion, below each quarter in the last-twelve-month window. The third quarter coincided with a 30-year mortgage rate that rose from 6.43% to 7.28% and a smaller market, while GAAP net income of USD 709 million included an MSR valuation gain that the company strips out of adjusted measures. Creditors should anchor on adjusted EBITDA with that caveat and treat the 3.78x as a trailing, not forward, figure [CP-1; CP-1A].

Rocket's funding has two layers that behave differently. The unsecured layer, senior notes and an undrawn USD 2,500 million revolver maturing in 2029, has no maturity before the USD 503 million convertible in April 2027, then USD 1,500 million in 2029, USD 2,076 million in 2030 and USD 2,214 million in 2031. The secured layer of USD 14,187 million sits on lines whose committed amount is USD 7,400 million, with USD 10,295 million outstanding on lines dated 2027 and USD 3,462 million on lines dated 2028. Rocket therefore faces recurring renewal and margin-call risk on warehouse capacity even though its bond maturity profile is long, and most facilities carry cross default or cross acceleration terms [CP-1 debt stack; Documentary Fact].

Liquidity is real but partly unverified. The company states total liquidity of USD 11.4 billion, of which only USD 2,699 million of cash and the USD 2,500 million revolver tie to the filings; the USD 6.2 billion of undrawn available MSR and advance lines cannot be reproduced from the facility table, and cash includes buy-down and margin cash that are not quantified. Stated liquidity is management data and should be attributed as such [CP-1; Management Language].

The servicing franchise is the most durable source of revenue and also a concentration. Servicing fee income was USD 3,181 million in the nine months of 2026 on a portfolio of USD 2,007,176 million of unpaid principal, with 60-plus day delinquency of 1.52%. The Agencies and subservicing clients can terminate servicing with little or no notice, and MSRs of USD 20,257 million at fair value are the largest balance sheet asset and are valued with rate and prepayment assumptions. A change in Agency terms or an MSR valuation reversal would hit income, collateral value for the MSR lines and liquidity at once [CP-1A operating model; Documentary Fact].

Control and sponsor behaviour matter because protections are not visible. Mr. Gilbert holds more than a majority of the voting power, the company relies on controlled company exemptions and renounces certain corporate opportunities in favour of RHI II parties. Documented capital actions are mixed: a USD 120.1 million special dividend in 2025, withdrawal of undrawn related party credit lines, equity-funded acquisitions and no distributions in the 2026 cash flow statements. Nothing in the pack shows the restricted payment, lien or guarantee terms that would limit owner actions [CP-1A governance; Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation on overall category].

Event risk is concentrated in litigation and acquired liabilities. Legal reserves rose to USD 216 million from USD 74 million, acquisition accounting added USD 100 million of litigation and regulatory accruals in Q3 2026, and a USD 175 million HouseCanary verdict against Rocket Close awaits judgment while the plaintiff is in Chapter 11. A judgment above facility thresholds that is not paid, bonded or stayed could default funding facilities, which links a legal outcome to liquidity [CP-1 claims outside funded debt; Documentary Fact].

Earnings carry adjustments and a definition that favours the headline. Adjusted EBITDA is after secured financing interest but before bond interest, adds back USD 291 million of acquisition-related expenses and USD 338 million of acquired intangible amortization in the nine months, and removes an MSR valuation gain of USD 852 million. Accrual cover of last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA over bond interest is 4.94x, and cash interest cover is available only for FY2025 at 2.15x on a mixed basis. Debt service capacity therefore rests on an accrual proxy and on adjusted rather than reported earnings [CP-1 earnings quality; Analyst Interpretation].

Market pricing of the unsecured notes weakened over the period. The aggregate fair value of senior notes of USD 10,258 million at September 30, 2026 was below carrying value of USD 10,716 million, whereas at December 31, 2025 fair value of USD 10,502 million was above carrying value of USD 10,423 million, and the weighted average interest rate on the notes rose to 5.43% from 5.03%. This is an aggregate, not a tranche-level price, and ratings are not supplied [CP-1 debt stack; Documentary Fact, Analyst Interpretation on direction].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths.

Scaled servicing and origination franchise [Documentary Fact]. Rocket Companies, Inc. services USD 2,007,176 million of unpaid principal, earned USD 3,181 million of servicing fee income in the nine months of 2026 and closed USD 43.6 billion of loans in Q3 2026. Recurring servicing fees support revenue visibility through a rate cycle better than origination alone, and the portfolio's 60-plus day delinquency of 1.52% is stable against 1.50% at year end [CP-1A operating model].

Moderate unsecured leverage on a full earnings base [Documentary Fact]. Gross 3.78x and net 2.83x on last-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA, with accrual bond interest cover of 4.94x, give room to absorb some earnings decline before leverage reaches the 5.65x seen at March 31, 2026 [CP-1 leverage table; Analyst Interpretation on headroom].

Long unsecured maturity profile and an undrawn revolver [Documentary Fact]. No note matures before April 2027, the largest annual maturities fall in 2031 and 2033, and the USD 2,500 million revolver is undrawn and runs to 2029, which gives the issuer time and a committed buffer [CP-1 debt maturities; CP-1 liquidity table].

Equity-funded growth [Documentary Fact]. Redfin and Mr. Cooper were bought for stock, with cash of USD 252 million and USD 3,113 million used to settle target debt, so the acquisitions did not require a new debt-funded purchase price. Equity of USD 24,311 million and equity less goodwill and intangibles of USD 11,745 million (a consolidated proxy, not a covenant measure) provide loss-absorbing capital for the debt [CP-1A transactions; CP-1 financials].

Stated compliance [Documentary Fact]. The company states compliance with all funding covenants and with agency net worth, liquidity and capital ratio requirements at September 30, 2026. This is a statement, not a measured headroom [CP-1].

Weaknesses.

Rate-sensitive, cyclical earnings [Documentary Fact]. Gain on sale margin was 2.54% in Q3 2026 against 2.80% a year earlier, adjusted revenue has fallen each quarter of 2026, and fourth-quarter adjusted revenue is guided to USD 2.1 billion to USD 2.3 billion. Rising rates reduce origination volume while raising MSR value, so earnings quality depends on valuation items the company excludes from adjusted measures [CP-1; CP-1A].

Uncommitted secured funding and renewal risk [Documentary Fact]. Secured financing of USD 14,187 million exceeds the USD 7,400 million committed secured lines, almost all of it matures in 2027 and 2028, and most facilities have cross default terms. A margin call or non-renewal would draw on cash first [CP-1 debt stack].

Agency and counterparty dependence [Documentary Fact]. Servicing and loan sales depend on Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and Ginnie Mae programmes and guidelines, and counterparties can terminate servicing. The loss of eligibility or fee cuts would hit income and MSR collateral together [CP-1A operating model].

Founder control with unseen protections [Documentary Fact; Insufficient Information on protective terms]. The controlling holder, controlled company exemptions and renounced corporate opportunities limit board-level checks, and the governance letter, indentures and credit agreements that would limit owner actions are not supplied. This is a monitoring weakness, not evidence of owner extraction [CP-1A ownership and governance].

Legal and acquired-liability exposure [Documentary Fact]. Reserves of USD 216 million, a USD 175 million verdict awaiting judgment, a data breach class action inherited from Mr. Cooper and unquantified investor representation exposure form event risk that could reach liquidity through judgment thresholds [CP-1 claims outside funded debt].

Opaque debt terms and liquidity [Insufficient Information]. Covenant levels, guarantor names, per-instrument issuer roles, ratings, cash paid for interest in 2026 and the basis of USD 6.2 billion of stated secured availability are not supplied, so headroom, ranking and cash cover cannot be sized. Missing evidence is not read as adverse; it limits confidence [CP-0; CP-1; CP-1A].

Comparability and adjustments [Analyst Interpretation]. Perimeter changes, a segment recast, interest caption changes and large add-backs mean year-on-year growth mixes acquired and organic change, and revenue cannot be compared between FY2025 and 2026 captions. Credit judgments should rest on net income, adjusted EBITDA and balance sheet figures [CP-0; CP-1].
