### Business and transaction view

The credit story is a single-asset, pre-revenue construction financing. Skeena states that it is "a mining company in the development stage focusing on the construction and development of the Eskay Creek project" (Q2 FS, page:4), and management says the project is "fully permitted and under construction" with "initial production and cash flow in the second quarter of 2027" (Aug 2026 release, page:1). Those timing and quality statements are management language, not audited facts. The financing was rebuilt in the first half of 2026. The June 2024 package with Orion (private placements, a Gold Stream and a US$350,000,000 Senior Secured Term Loan) was replaced on April 10, 2026 by an offering of US$750,000,000 Senior Secured Notes at 8.50% due April 1, 2031. Part of the proceeds repurchased 66.67% of the Gold Stream for US$184,000,000, part went to an interest reserve account covering three semi-annual interest payments, part to a construction disbursement account, and the remainder to general expenses and issuance costs. Creditors therefore sit alongside a reduced stream, new and existing NSR royalties, a growing closure obligation and unreleased restricted cash. The mechanics that matter are release conditions on the restricted accounts, completion-test timing for the Gold Stream percentage, and cost to complete, none of which can be tested from the retained documents. There is no sponsor and no controlling holder is identified. Documented issuer behaviour is equity-funded and transparent in its disclosure of financing events; no creditor-adverse action is documented. The notes' indenture, the Gold Stream and royalty agreements, the annual information form and the technical report are not in the pack, so legal capacity, security and guarantor structure are Insufficient Information. This view is a statement of business and transaction facts and their credit mechanics, not a view on credit quality or recovery.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The principal new debt issue is the offering of US$750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of Senior Secured Notes, completed on April 10, 2026 and stated in the statements as 1,036,761 (CAD thousands) (Q2 FS page:12). The notes bear interest at 8.50% per annum payable semi-annually, mature on April 1, 2031, are non-callable for the first two years, and are described as senior secured obligations ranking pari passu with the Gold Stream obligations and senior to all present and future subordinated indebtedness. Before April 1, 2028 the company may redeem some or all of the notes at 100% plus the greater of 1% and a make-whole premium of 50 basis points over the treasury yield, up to 10% at 103%, or up to 40% with net cash proceeds of equity offerings at 108.5%; from April 1, 2028 the redemption price is 104.25% until March 31, 2029, 102.125% until March 31, 2030 and 100% thereafter. A change of control triggers an offer at 101%, and excess proceeds from asset sales above a threshold must be used to offer to purchase the notes (Q2 FS page:12, page:13). Parties other than the issuer (initial purchasers, trustee, any guarantors and the security package) are Not supplied. The use of proceeds as stated in the statements was 254,352 (US$184,000,000) to repurchase 66.67% of the Gold Stream, 130,228 (US$94,208,000) to an interest reserve account representing three semi-annual interest payments, 552,940 (US$400,000,000) to support construction through a disbursement account, and 99,241 (US$71,792,000) for general and administrative expenses and issuance costs. By June 30, 2026, 199,176 (US$145,000,000) of the restricted proceeds had been released and restricted cash stood at 500,853, with cash and cash equivalents of 134,808 (CAD thousands). The company says the proceeds are expected to be sufficient to fund capital requirements through commencement of commercial production in 2027 only "As long as the Company meets the conditions precedent to the Senior Secured Notes", and that additional financing would be needed if funds cannot be released or are insufficient (Q2 FS page:4). The conditions precedent and release mechanics are not supplied.

The notes replaced the earlier funding plan. Under the June 24, 2024 agreements with Orion, the Project Financing Package consisted of private placements, a Gold Stream with a US$200,000,000 deposit and a US$350,000,000 Senior Secured Term Loan facility (Q3 2025 FS page:4; Q1 FS page:9, page:10). The deposit was fully received by December 31, 2025 and no draws were made on the term loan, which was terminated on April 10, 2026 (Q2 FS page:14). On March 31, 2026 the company entered into an amending agreement to exercise the buy-down option for US$184,000,000 and cancel the option to draw an additional deposit (Q1 FS page:11). The buy-down reduced the Stream Percentage by 66.67%, from 10.55% to 3.52% of payable gold. The earlier agreement priced the buy-down as the proportional deposit plus an imputed 18% internal rate of return and available for 12 months after the project completion date (Q3 2025 FS page:11); the 2026 early exercise was negotiated, and its pricing basis is not supplied. In the same half-year the company completed an agreement granting third parties a 1% NSR royalty on Eskay Creek and a right to elect an additional NSR royalty or shares, valued at 116,474 and 266,950 at initial recognition respectively (CAD thousands), with a 25,000 prepayment of royalty obligations in the second quarter (Q2 FS page:15, page:16). The equity component of the option, 108,575, was booked to reserves (Q2 FS page:2). Equity funding included a bought deal closed on February 26, 2025 for gross proceeds of 88,347 and another closed on October 8, 2025 for gross proceeds of 143,796 (CAD thousands) (Q3 2025 FS page:12, page:13).

The Q1 FS and the Q2 FS describe the construction account differently: the Q1 FS says the remaining proceeds "will be used to support the remaining construction at Eskay through a reimbursement account", while the Q2 FS says "through a disbursement account" (Q1 FS page:15; Q2 FS page:12). This is logged below as a wording difference and not reconciled. Terms of the Gold Stream covenants (debt service coverage of no less than 1.25:1 after the completion test and a minimum unrestricted cash of 25,000 after the term loan and additional deposit commitments end) were stated before the buy-down (Q1 FS page:10, page:11) and are not repeated in the Q2 FS; their current status is Not supplied.
