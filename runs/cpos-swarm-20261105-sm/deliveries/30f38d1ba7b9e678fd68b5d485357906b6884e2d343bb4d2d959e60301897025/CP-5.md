### Evidence trace view

SM Energy's analysis is traceable: every material claim in the readiness, financial, business and synthesis handoffs rests on a located passage of a pinned source or on arithmetic that reproduces from such passages, and the handoffs tell one consistent story. The review found no critical or material defect and a short list of minor ones, none of which changes a conclusion. The research is cleared for committee use as a screening and monitoring base for balance-sheet facts, maturities, liquidity, the merger and the asset sale. It is restricted for three uses: covenant headroom on current terms, legal capacity to distribute cash or add priority debt, and like-for-like leverage on a pro forma basis. The restriction follows from sources that are not supplied, not from a trace failure, and it lifts only when the credit agreement, indentures and a pro forma earnings figure are added.
