### Financial base view

The financial base is usable for a credit snapshot of the consolidated Partnership, with limitations. Reported statements for FY2025, the first nine months of 2026 and Q3 2026 are complete and internally consistent (the quarterly Adjusted EBITDA figures sum to the nine-month figure; see Calculations). Scale changed sharply because Parkland was acquired on 2025-10-31, so FY2025 contains two months of Parkland and the trailing Q3 2026 window contains eleven. The FY2025 leverage ratios are therefore overstated relative to a pro forma view, and the trailing Q3 2026 ratios are modestly overstated too. The management-reported covenant net leverage (4.03x at FY2025, 3.98x, 3.7x and 3.5x through Q3 2026) tracks the derived net ratio closely only at Q3 2026, which is consistent with the covenant definition being pro forma for acquisitions and netting reserved cash. Debt is dominated by unsecured senior notes with guarantees from subsidiaries; liquidity rests on a single 2,500 US$m revolver maturing 2030-06-17. Earnings quality needs care: Adjusted EBITDA adds back equity-affiliate EBITDA and a large non-cash adjustment, and excludes a favourable inventory valuation adjustment. None of these restricts the base, but each is carried into later modules.

### Debt and claims outside debt

Debt at 2026-09-30 is 13,163 US$m (carrying), made up of senior notes, the revolver, GoZone bonds, lease-related financing and other subsidiary debt, less 80 US$m of issuance costs (Q3 10-Q page:13). Issuers and guarantors, as the filing states: notes of Sunoco LP and Sunoco Finance Corp. (co-issuer) are guaranteed by NuStar Logistics and other subsidiaries; notes of NuStar Logistics are guaranteed by Sunoco LP, Sunoco Finance Corp. and subsidiaries; guarantees rank equally with other unsecured senior debt of each guarantor and are structurally subordinated to debt of non-guarantor subsidiaries (Q3 10-Q page:46 and page:47). The 10-K says the Sunoco senior notes are effectively subordinated to the issuers' and guarantors' secured obligations, including the Credit Facility, to the extent of the collateral value (10-K page:250), and that the Credit Facility is guaranteed by material domestic subsidiaries (10-K page:251); the collateral package is not described in the sources. Energy Transfer guarantees collection of the principal of the 5.875% senior notes due 2028 (10-K page:250). During 2026 the Partnership issued 600 US$m of 5.375% notes due 2031 and 600 US$m of 5.625% notes due 2034 and redeemed the 6.000% notes due 2026 and 2027, the Parkland notes not exchanged, the 3.875% CAD notes due 2026 and the 6.000% CAD notes due 2028 (Q3 10-Q page:14). Notes repaid in the nine months were 1,878 US$m against 1,200 US$m borrowed (Q3 10-Q page:5 and page:6).

Claims outside the debt figure: operating lease liabilities of 185 US$m current and 1,375 US$m non-current (Q3 10-Q page:3); Series A Preferred Units of 1,477 US$m at a 7.875% rate with 39.375 US$ per unit paid semi-annually, which are equity and not debt (Q3 10-Q page:3 and page:20); Class D units held by SunocoCorp with distribution equalisation through 2027-12-31 (Q3 10-Q page:19); environmental liabilities of 213 US$m and asset retirement obligations of 214 US$m (Q3 10-Q page:18 and page:15 to page:16); a New York motor fuel excise tax assessment of about 20 US$m under appeal (Q3 10-Q page:18). The pension plan was terminated in 2024 and the benefit obligation was nil at 2025-12-31 (10-K page:274). Purchase commitments, factoring and supply-chain finance were searched in the 10-K and the Q3 10-Q and no such disclosure was found. Debt-like lease financing obligations are inside the 597 US$m line.

Covenants: the 10-K states a maximum Net Leverage Ratio of 5.50 to 1.00 before an investment grade rating and a minimum Interest Coverage Ratio of 2.25 to 1.00 (10-K page:251). The Q3 10-Q reports net leverage of 3.5 to 1.00 and compliance with all financial covenants (page:15). The reported interest coverage ratio is not printed in any source. The step-down to 5.00 to 1.00 applies only after an investment grade rating, and no rating is supplied.

### Definition review

Adjusted EBITDA is the Partnership's non-GAAP measure and "is used as a performance measure under our Credit Facility" (Q3 10-Q page:32). It starts at net income and adds back interest, tax, depreciation, amortisation and accretion, unit-based compensation, disposal losses and impairments, extinguishment losses, unrealised commodity derivative effects, inventory valuation adjustments, certain foreign exchange effects and other items; it adds Adjusted EBITDA of unconsolidated affiliates and subtracts their equity earnings (Q3 10-Q page:21 and page:22). For the nine months of 2026 the add-back for unconsolidated affiliates is 200 US$m, other non-cash adjustments are 131 US$m, and the inventory valuation adjustment removes 445 US$m of favourable effect (Q3 10-Q page:33). The Q3 release headline of 1.060 billion US$ excludes one-time transaction-related expenses of 11 US$m, whereas the filing and the release body show 1,049 US$m (release Q3 page:2). This handoff uses the filing figure. Differences from the covenant ratio: the covenant net leverage uses the credit-agreement Net Leverage Ratio, which after a 2025 amendment provided for up to 2.00 billion US$ of cash reserved for the Parkland consideration to be netted (10-K page:252) and is not computed from statements here.

### Cash flow and earnings quality

Nine-month 2026 operating cash flow was 2,251 US$m on net income of 1,426 US$m, with working capital contributing a 316 US$m inflow (the cash flow effect of accounts receivable was an outflow of 2,313 US$m and that of accounts payable an inflow of 1,479 US$m, Q3 10-Q page:11). The inventory valuation adjustment of 445 US$m raised net income without cash and is reversed in cash flow. A last-in first-out liquidation in the first quarter raised pre-tax income by 102 US$m (Q3 10-Q page:12). A foreign exchange loss of 94 US$m was non-cash (Q3 10-Q page:42). Acquisitions in the nine months were TanQuid (194 US$m net of cash), Delta (75 US$m) and other (87 US$m) (Q3 10-Q page:5). Distributions of 880 US$m were paid in the nine months, including 151 US$m to the Class D unitholder and 118 US$m to preferred unitholders (Q3 10-Q page:6). The distribution declared for Q3 2026 is 1.0148 US$ per common unit, payable 2026-11-19, about 139 US$m on common units, 52 US$m on Class D units and 76 US$m to incentive distribution rights (Q3 10-Q page:44). Segment Adjusted EBITDA in Q3 2026 was Fuel Distribution 523, Pipeline Systems 166, Terminals 122 and Refinery 238 US$m (Q3 10-Q page:22); the Refinery segment sells largely to other segments (intersegment revenues 853 US$m of 970 US$m in the quarter, page:22).
