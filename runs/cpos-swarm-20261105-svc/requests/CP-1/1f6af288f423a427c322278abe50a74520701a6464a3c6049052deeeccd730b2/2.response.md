### Financial base view

The financial base is a consolidated REIT whose reported earnings are falling and whose capital structure is being reshaped. Revenue for Q3 2026 was 399,520 against 478,770 for Q3 2025, a decrease the company attributes mainly to sales of certain hotels (Q3 10-Q, page:38); hotels owned fell from 160 to 87 over the year (page:36). Net loss was 87,940 for the quarter and 462,956 for nine months, the latter including 270,883 of impairment and 61,589 of loss on early extinguishment of debt (page:3). Net lease rental income was steady at 101,141 against 101,194. Cash generation is positive but small: operating cash flow of 132,226 for 9M 2026 exceeded the 110,062 of real estate improvements by only 22,164 (derived).

On the derived measures, adjusted EBITDA was 127,761 in Q3 2026 and 495,599 for the trailing twelve months to September 30, 2026. Against total debt principal of 4,652,322 that is 9.39x, or 9.31x net of unrestricted cash; interest expense cover is 1.33x and cover of cash interest paid is 1.47x. These measures are derived by this module, are not company measures, and include in the trailing period the earnings of hotels that were sold before September 30, 2026, while the debt is measured after those sales. The leverage figure is therefore not a pro forma number and should be read as an indicator of direction, not a precise level.

Capital structure has changed materially in 2026: 1,550,000 of senior unsecured notes were redeemed, 745,000 of net lease mortgage notes were issued, and 541,798 of net equity proceeds were raised on April 2, 2026 (Q3 10-Q, pages:47-48). Secured debt is now the larger share of carrying debt (2,821,494 secured against 1,703,944 unsecured), and the nearest hard maturities are the revolver on June 29, 2027 (undrawn) and 580,155 of zero coupon secured notes in 2027. Liquidity is 690,118 and every indenture covenant test is met at September 30, 2026, with the revolver's own financial covenant results undisclosed in the sources supplied. The view is a reported-fact base with stated limits; credit conclusions belong to the later modules.
