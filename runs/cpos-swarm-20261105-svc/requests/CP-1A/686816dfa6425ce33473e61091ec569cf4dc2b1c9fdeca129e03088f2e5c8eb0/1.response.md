### Business and transaction view

Service Properties Trust is an externally managed, listed Maryland REIT with no employees, whose day to day operations are run by The RMR Group LLC (RMR) under two management agreements (Q3 10-Q, page:26) that carry a rolling 20 year term (10-K, page:100). Its asset base is two businesses of unequal stability. The net lease business is 740 properties let to 188 tenants on triple-net terms, with annual minimum rents of 395,492 and a weighted average remaining term of 7.0 years (page:12); one tenant, TravelCenters of America (TA), accounts for 34.2% of historical real estate investment and its leases run to 2033 with a capped BP guarantee. The hotel business is 87 hotels, leased to the company's taxable REIT subsidiaries and run by four operators, of which Sonesta (62 hotels) is related to the company's manager through a shared controlling shareholder and a 34% equity stake. The hotel business supplied most revenue (298,379 of 399,520 in Q3 2026) and the loss in the segment note (page:31); hotels owned fell from 160 to 87 over the year (page:36), and the trailing earnings in the financial base handoff still include hotels sold before September 30, 2026.

The transaction story of the last twelve months is a portfolio and balance-sheet reset: 112 hotels sold in 2025 (10-K, page:7), a restructured Sonesta agreement effective August 1, 2025, a new zero coupon secured note in September 2025, and in 2026 a mortgage-note refinancing of the 2029 unsecured notes, an equity raise, a reverse split, repayment of the variable funding note and a further hotel sale programme. The creditor-relevant effects are lower unsecured debt and a larger secured share, a shrinking and more net lease weighted earnings base, and continued dependence on related-party arrangements whose full terms are not in the pack. The pack contains no sponsor, no sponsor transaction and no rating action, so those categories are Insufficient Information rather than adverse findings.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

Transactions in the current year, taken from the Q3 10-Q unless noted. On March 6, 2026 three subsidiary issuers placed 745,000 of Series 2026-1 net lease mortgage notes in three classes, with a weighted coupon of 5.96%, maturing March 2031, non-recourse and secured on 471 retail net lease properties shared with the older 2023 series, including 158 properties contributed in the transaction (pages:22-23). The proceeds and cash on hand funded the March redemption of all 700,000 of the 8.375% senior guaranteed unsecured notes due 2029 at par plus a make-whole premium of 37,128 (page:48). On April 2, 2026 the company sold 95,833,333 common shares (after the later reverse split) at 6.00 per share, receiving net proceeds of about 541,798, which funded the redemption of all 450,000 of the 5.50% senior guaranteed unsecured notes due 2027 and part of the redemption of all 400,000 of the 4.95% notes due 2027, which were redeemed in January and May (pages:21-22 and 47-48). The manager, RMR, bought 8,333,333 shares in the offering at the public offering price, and the CEO, the CFO and certain trustees bought a further 49,666 shares (page:29). The 45,000 variable funding note was repaid and terminated in September 2026. A 1-for-5 reverse share split took effect after trading on July 6, 2026, and authorized shares were increased from 200,000,000 to 900,000,000 on March 30, 2026 (pages:24-25).

Asset transactions: in 9M 2026 the company sold 32 properties (one hotel in Q1, six hotels and six net lease properties in Q3) for 88,159 and acquired five net lease properties for 15,105 (page:10). In September 2026 the company disposed of a hotel without paying a 11,035 termination fee to Sonesta, which agreed to extend the management agreement for the remaining Sonesta Retained Hotels through June 30, 2043 (page:16). The Omnibus Amendment of Hotel Management Agreements dated September 25, 2026 is listed as filed with the Q3 10-Q but is not in the pack (page:73).

Subsequent events (October 1 to November 2, 2026; not part of September 30 figures): sale of eight hotels with 1,509 keys for 50,600 and one net lease property for 295; acquisition of four net lease properties for 8,314; a conditional agreement to sell one net lease property for 655; and a distribution of 0.05 per share, about 6,491, declared October 8, 2026 (pages:10-11, 26 and 46). New debt issues after the quarter: none disclosed. The revolver, undrawn at 650,000 and maturing June 29, 2027, is under review for extension or refinancing with no assurance of terms (page:47).
