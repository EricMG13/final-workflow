### Financial base view

Financial base view: the pinned filings give a complete reported base for the consolidated issuer at four balance-sheet dates (FY2025, Q1 2026, Q2 2026, Q3 2026) and for the nine months of 2026, with year-on-year comparatives. Leverage in the sense the chart tables ask for is Not supplied because the issuer reports no EBITDA and treats interest expense as an operating cost within net interest income. The funding structure is deposit-led, with securitized borrowings and unsecured notes as the borrowed layers; the unsecured layer grew most in 2026 through the February and July issuances. Capital is reported above the stated minimums and the Company and the Bank are stated as well capitalized, but year-end 2025 capital ratios exist on two bases because of a presentation recast, and one earlier handoff statement is corrected below. Interim cash-paid interest and tax are Not supplied, so no cash interest cover can be built for 2026 periods. Nothing in this base is a credit opinion; the CP-2 synthesis owns that.

### Corrections to the earlier handoff and definition risks
