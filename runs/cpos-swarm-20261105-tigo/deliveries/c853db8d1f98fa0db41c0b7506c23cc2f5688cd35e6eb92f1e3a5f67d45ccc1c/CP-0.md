### Readiness view

The effective source set is the audited 2025 annual report plus three unaudited interim packages that each carry both interim financial statements and a management earnings release, the latest dated on the as-of date itself. That is sufficient to start all four selected consumers on the consolidated Millicom group, so the overall status is READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS rather than READY. The strongest contrary evidence is that governing legal documents, rating-agency material and any instrument-level debt schedule after year-end 2025 are absent, so any claim about ranking, guarantor structure, covenant levels or the revolving credit facility at the latest quarter rests on summary filing disclosures or on year-end 2025 text. A second limit is perimeter: the group now consolidates Colombia Telecomunicaciones (Coltel) and the Ecuador and Uruguay businesses acquired in late 2025, while the Honduras joint venture and Chile associate are equity-accounted, so group debt and group EBITDA sit on a different footing from the full operating footprint. Nothing here makes a consumer conditional or blocked; the next evidence action is to obtain the debt instruments, the revolving credit agreement and its amendments, and the rating-agency publications named in the gaps below. Source sufficiency is not completion of the downstream analysis.
