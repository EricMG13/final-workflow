### Analytical read-through

- Usable coverage: the audited FY2025 statements and three unaudited interim periods (March 31, 2026, June 30, 2026, plus September 30, 2025 for the prior-year comparison) cover discrete quarters, six-month and nine-month year-to-date columns, three segments, issuer-defined Adjusted EBITDA and variable marketing margin, and the single facility's carrying value. The Q2 10-Q reconciles segment profit to pre-tax income (page:21) and net income to Adjusted EBITDA (page:38), so non-GAAP add-backs can be traced to filed GAAP lines.
- Strongest contrary evidence and limitation: the facility terms exist only as summaries. The 10-K states the covenant as "a first lien net leverage ratio of 5.00 to 1.00" tested "only if the loans and certain other obligations under the 2025 Revolving Facility exceed $20.0 million" (page:145), and the 10-Q states only that "The Company was in compliance with all covenants at June 30, 2026" (page:19). Evidence leads to a source risk: the definition of first lien net leverage, its EBITDA add-backs and the credit agreement baskets are not in the pack. The consequence is that a downstream module can describe the test and the compliance statement but cannot verify headroom or restricted-payment capacity.
- Second source risk: the term-loan rate label. The 10-Q says borrowings "bear interest based on the Secured Overnight Financing Rate ("SOFR") of 7.89%" (page:19) while the 10-K pricing is a spread over Term SOFR of 4.50%, or 4.25% after a Moody's rating condition achieved in January 2026 (page:145). The label may be an all-in rate, not the benchmark alone. Downstream modules must quote it as printed and flag the ambiguity.
- Third source risk: management narrative versus filing. The July 29, 2026 release states an outlook for 2026 and the third quarter (page:3); the 10-Q does not. These are attributed management claims, forward-looking and unaudited, and cannot override filed figures.
- Fourth source risk: a large tax-related swing in FY2025 net income. The 10-K statement of operations shows "Income tax benefit (expense) 130,284" against pre-tax income of "21,024" (page:110), and the fourth quarter release says the benefit reduced the full valuation allowance (page:1). Downstream modules must separate net income from operating results.
- What cannot proceed from these sources alone: covenant headroom against the credit agreement definitions, guarantor-level analysis, letter of credit availability under the revolver, rating-agency analysis, market-price or valuation analysis, and anything after July 30, 2026.
- Next evidence action: obtain the August 21, 2025 Credit Agreement (listed as exhibit 10.39, incorporated from the Form 8-K filed August 21, 2025, 10-K page:161), exhibit 21.1, any filing or release dated after July 30, 2026 including a third quarter 2026 10-Q and release, and the 2026 proxy statement.
