### Credit view

Tripadvisor, Inc. is a moderately leveraged credit with ample near-term liquidity and no large maturity before 2031, but its earnings base is shrinking and the case rests on whether Experiences can replace Hotels and Other earnings. The strongest support is liquidity of USD 1,204.5 million (cash of USD 706.8 million plus an undrawn USD 497.7 million revolver), a single term loan of USD 833.9 million with no financial covenant, and about USD 741.0 million of new sale proceeds. The weakest point is falling earnings: LTM Adjusted EBITDA of USD 207.3 million compares with USD 294.7 million in FY2025, and Hotels and Other supplies about 69% of it. What decides the outcome is how quickly that segment erodes and whether the sale proceeds reduce debt.

### Key findings

Tripadvisor's earnings fell sharply in 2026. Adjusted EBITDA was USD 71.1 million in Q3 2026 against USD 107.8 million a year earlier, and USD 163.9 million over nine months against USD 251.3 million, so LTM Adjusted EBITDA is USD 207.3 million against USD 294.7 million for FY2025 on revenue of USD 1,559.1 million over the LTM period [CP-1]. The decline is concentrated in Hotels and Other, whose revenue fell 23% in Q3 2026 and which took a goodwill impairment of USD 369.5 million. Hotels and Other still produced USD 143.6 million of the USD 207.3 million LTM Adjusted EBITDA, at a margin of 23.0%, while Experiences produced USD 63.7 million at a margin of 6.8% [CP-1]. The business therefore leans on the segment management expects to keep being hurt by search changes, which is the most important fact for the credit.

Tripadvisor's debt is simple: one Term Loan B of USD 833.9 million due July 8, 2031, no financial covenant on that loan, and an undrawn USD 500.0 million revolver due June 29, 2028, after the USD 345.0 million convertible notes were repaid from cash on April 1, 2026 [CP-1]. Gross debt to LTM Adjusted EBITDA is 4.02x, the same as at year end 2025 because debt and earnings fell by similar proportions; net of cash it is 0.61x, but net of the operator payables that sit against the cash it is 2.53x, and after deducting stock-based compensation the gross ratio is 6.08x [CP-1]. LTM interest cover on cash interest is 3.50x, down from 4.99x for FY2025 [CP-1]. The revolver carries a maximum total net leverage covenant whose reported ratio is not printed, so headroom cannot be measured [CP-1].

Tripadvisor's liquidity is large but seasonal. Cash of USD 706.8 million and an undrawn revolver of USD 497.7 million give USD 1,204.5 million, or USD 807.7 million after deducting deferred merchant payables of USD 396.8 million owed to experience operators [CP-1]. Operating cash flow was negative USD 122.0 million in Q3 2026, LTM operating cash flow is USD 27.4 million, and LTM free cash flow is negative USD 17.5 million, because experience bookings bring cash in during the first half and reverse in the second half [CP-1, CP-1A]. Scheduled term loan amortisation is only USD 8.5 million a year, with USD 797.8 million due in 2031 [CP-1].

Tripadvisor completed the sale of TheFork to American Express on November 1, 2026 for about USD 741.0 million of net cash against a USD 700.0 million headline price, with an expected fourth-quarter gain of about USD 487.0 million. Management says it currently expects to prioritise reduction of long-term debt, but that is a stated intent, not an executed commitment, and whether the term loan can be prepaid from proceeds depends on a credit agreement that is not supplied [CP-1A]. On an illustrative basis, before seasonal outflow and restructuring cost, cash plus the proceeds would exceed the term loan by USD 613.9 million, which shows the size of the effect rather than a forecast [CP-1].

Tripadvisor has no controlling stockholder since the April 2025 merger with LTRIP and no sponsor, paid no dividends, bought back no shares in 2026 and repaid the notes from cash, which supports a creditor-friendly reading of behaviour; a cooperation agreement dated March 22, 2026 and USD 3.3 million of activism costs show outside pressure on strategy, and a restructuring plan for Hotels and Other began on November 4, 2026 [CP-1A]. No priming, uptiering or collateral release is documented, but the pack cannot show them either way because the credit agreement is missing [CP-1A].

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Tripadvisor has liquidity of USD 1,204.5 million against debt of USD 833.9 million and no financial covenant on the term loan, which removes near-term refinancing and covenant pressure; this is a Documentary Fact [CP-1]. Its capital structure is a single term loan due 2031 with amortisation of USD 8.5 million a year, after the convertible notes were repaid in cash, which keeps debt service predictable apart from the floating rate; this is a Documentary Fact [CP-1, CP-1A]. The TheFork sale added about USD 741.0 million of net cash on November 1, 2026, which creates the option to cut debt; the closing is a Documentary Fact and the debt-reduction intent is Management Language, qualified as not a commitment [CP-1A]. Experiences volumes are still growing, with bookings up about 6% and gross booking value up about 5% over nine months 2026, which supports the replacement thesis even though Experiences revenue fell 3% in Q3 2026; this is a Documentary Fact with an Analyst Interpretation on what it means [CP-1A]. Capital spending of USD 31.8 million over nine months 2026 is light relative to revenue and can be flexed, so free cash flow is driven by operating cash flow; this is an Analyst Interpretation of Documentary Facts [CP-1, CP-1A]. There is no controlling stockholder or sponsor, no dividends, and no buybacks in 2026, which limits value leakage; this is an Analyst Interpretation of documented actions [CP-1A].

Weaknesses. Tripadvisor's earnings are falling and concentrated: LTM Adjusted EBITDA of USD 207.3 million is down from USD 294.7 million for FY2025, with about 69% from Hotels and Other, a segment with a goodwill impairment of USD 369.5 million and a margin that Experiences, at 6.8%, does not yet replace; this is a Documentary Fact [CP-1]. The segment that earns most depends on search traffic from a partner that also competes, and Booking and Expedia together were about 21% of FY2025 consolidated revenue, so a ranking or advertiser change can cut earnings quickly; the facts are Documentary and the mechanism is an Analyst Interpretation [CP-1A]. Cash conversion is weak: LTM free cash flow is negative USD 17.5 million and reported cash is matched by USD 396.8 million of deferred merchant payables, so usable liquidity is lower than the headline; this is a Documentary Fact [CP-1]. Leverage looks lower than it is because Adjusted EBITDA adds back stock-based compensation: gross leverage is 4.02x on Adjusted EBITDA and 6.08x after stock-based compensation, and cash interest cover falls from 3.50x to 2.32x; this is an Analyst Interpretation of CP-1 figures [CP-1]. The term loan rate floats and the 10-K says it is not hedged, so a rate rise reduces cover while earnings fall; this is a Documentary Fact [CP-1]. Governance depends on a board facing activism with no anchor holder, so portfolio actions that affect lenders are plausible and the credit agreement protections are unknown; this is an Analyst Interpretation [CP-1A]. The synthesis cannot test creditor protections, covenant headroom or post-sale net debt from the supplied sources; this is Insufficient Information and is not treated as adverse.
