### Entity and perimeter

The registrant and reporting entity is TPG RE Finance Trust, Inc., consolidated with its subsidiaries and consolidated variable interest entities (10-Q Q3 page:11). It is "organized as a holding company" and operates mainly through TPG RE Finance Trust Holdco, LLC, which it wholly owns (page:11). It reports one operating and reportable segment (page:12). It is externally managed by TPG RE Finance Trust Management, L.P., described as an affiliate of the sponsor TPG (page:74; 10-K page:7); the Management Agreement is listed as an exhibit but its text is not supplied (10-K page:169). The context leaves sponsor blank; that is not evidence of absence.

Perimeter matters for creditor claims. The balance sheet footnote states that consolidated VIE assets and liabilities of $2.3 billion and $1.9 billion at 2026-09-30 can be used only to satisfy the VIEs' obligations, with VIE creditors having recourse only to those assets and not to the registrant (10-Q Q3 page:7). Investment portfolio facilities are partly recourse to Holdco: secured credit agreements carry a 25% recourse guarantee from Holdco (page:47-48, page:50); the BMO asset-specific facility is 25% recourse (page:53). The Term Loan B is guaranteed by "certain subsidiaries" and secured by substantially all assets of the Company and certain subsidiaries (page:56); the guarantors are not named in the pack. The issuer, borrower, guarantor and restricted-entity map for the Term Loan B, the Revolver and the CLO issuers (a Credit Agreement dated 2026-05-14 with Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and other lenders and issuing banks is listed as an exhibit in the Q2 10-Q, page:133, but the filings do not say whether it governs the Term Loan B, the Revolver or both) is therefore an unresolved gap, not a conclusion. The subsidiaries exhibit is listed in the 10-K index but its text is not supplied (10-K page:173).

### Debt, liquidity and covenant evidence present

The Q3 10-Q gives, at 2026-09-30 and in thousands of USD: collateralized loan obligations principal 1,919,500; secured credit agreements 1,522,156; secured revolving credit facility 264,963; asset-specific financing 31,125; mortgage loans payable 60,310; Term Loan B 400,000; Revolver 0 outstanding (page:46, page:55, page:57). It also gives carrying values (page:58), facility-level commitments, maturities and recourse (page:47-54), the Term Loan B terms (Term SOFR plus 2.75%, May 2033 maturity, 0.25% quarterly amortization from December 2026, page:56), the Revolver terms ($100.0 million, Term SOFR plus 2.00%, May 2031 maturity, page:57) and a maturity schedule by year (page:57). Near-term liquidity is tabulated (page:105) and contractual obligations including estimated interest are tabulated (page:108).

The 10-Q prints covenant levels in two packages: investment portfolio financing arrangements (cash liquidity, tangible net worth, debt-to-equity previously, total debt to total assets and interest coverage; page:54-55) and corporate financing (total debt to total assets 83.333%, cash liquidity, interest coverage 1.3 to 1.0; page:57). It states that financial covenants were amended on closing of the Term Loan B and Revolver in the quarter ended June 2026 (page:55). It states compliance with all financial covenants to the extent of outstanding balances (page:55, page:57). It does not print the reported values of the tangible net worth, total debt to total assets or interest coverage tests: the test names appear only on page:55 and page:57, and the other covenant mentions (page:28, page:77, page:97) give the compliance sentence or the cash liquidity amount. The one covenant-linked amount printed is cash held to satisfy the cash liquidity covenant, 52.6 million at 2026-09-30 against 15.0 million at 2025-12-31 (page:28; page:77); the recourse indebtedness against which the 5.0% limb is measured is not printed, so that test's headroom cannot be read from the pack. Page:28 also describes Holdco as guarantor of the Company's recourse indebtedness. The reported debt-to-equity and total leverage ratios of 3.64x and 4.03x are printed (page:104) and are management definitions, not the covenant definitions.

Two availability measures coexist and must not be conflated downstream: "maximum current availability" under the secured credit agreements totals 777,844 thousand (page:47), whereas the company's near-term liquidity counts only 87,317 thousand of secured credit agreement capacity, described as liquidity available without pledging additional collateral (page:77, page:105). The secured revolving credit facility is described as $375.0 million with 264,963 thousand drawn (page:46, page:77), but only 22,000 thousand is counted as available (page:105). Undrawn commitment is therefore not usable liquidity.
