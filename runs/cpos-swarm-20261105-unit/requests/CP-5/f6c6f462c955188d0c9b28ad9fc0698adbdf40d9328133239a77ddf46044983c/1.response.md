### Verdict consistency

The verdicts agree across modules for every shared claim except the maturity table statement. CP-2's confidence of Medium is no higher than the upstream limits allow, and its gap list contains the inherited gaps.

### Contrary evidence and what would change the view

The strongest evidence against the cautious view is liquidity: cash of 608.9 and availability of 838.1 give 1,447.0 against no instrument maturity before 2027-12-01, and the company raised 1,140.7 more in July 2026. Segment contribution margins of about 52%, 50% and 42% suggest earnings power once the fiber build matures, and the 2026 outlook for Adjusted EBITDA of 1,450 to 1,500 compares with trailing 1,492.2, so the outlook range is close to the trailing figure. What would make that case win: capital expenditures falling after the build so that Adjusted EBITDA less capital expenditures covers interest, market access staying open for asset-backed and bond issuance, and the revolvers being extended before 2027-12-30. Downside chain with observable triggers: initiating condition, slower fiber penetration at Kinetic or fewer hyperscaler bookings; first operational break, Adjusted EBITDA below the low end of the outlook, which needs second-half Adjusted EBITDA of at least 651.3 (calculated); financial transmission, interest expense, net of 805 (outlook) then absorbs a larger share of earnings and cash interest cover falls below the trailing 1.94x; liquidity consequence, revolver drawings and letters of credit use the 838.1 of availability while capital expenditures continue; refinancing consequence, the 2027 maturities of the convertible notes and both revolvers meet a market that may be less open, while a failure of the debt service coverage test on the asset-backed notes would trigger rapid amortization of those notes (Q2 10-Q page:30). Observable triggers: quarterly Adjusted EBITDA against the 651.3 second-half requirement, the Windstream Revolver letter of credit balance, asset-backed debt service coverage disclosures, and the outcome of the asset sale offers.
