### Credit view

UWM Holdings Corp is a large, agency-focused mortgage originator whose credit position weakened in the first half of 2026 but was cushioned by new capital on August 5, 2026. The strongest support is the USD 1,650.0 million preferred issue, together with scale in agency-eligible origination and a USD 5,311.5 million servicing-rights asset. The weakest point is the balance sheet: non-funding debt of USD 6,040.4 million is 6.13x total equity of USD 985.3 million, and equity fell USD 615.6 million in the second quarter, mostly from a net loss driven by derivative losses. What decides the outcome is whether the preferred terms leave creditors protected, whether the uncommitted servicing-rights lines stay available into USD 2,375.0 million of 2027 maturities, and whether hedging losses stop.

### Key findings

1. Leverage on the company's own capital measure more than doubled in six months. Non-funding debt (senior notes, servicing-rights lines, borrowings against investment securities and finance leases) rose by USD 1,747.5 million, from USD 4,292.9 million at December 31, 2025 to USD 6,040.4 million at June 30, 2026, while total equity fell by USD 608.3 million, from USD 1,593.6 million to USD 985.3 million, taking non-funding debt to equity from 2.69x to 6.13x [CP-1, Financials and credit metrics]. The increase was USD 1,750.0 million of net draws on the two servicing-rights facilities, which exceeded the USD 1,237.7 million growth in the servicing-rights asset, so part of the borrowing financed operations and distributions rather than the asset. Category: Documentary Fact for the figures; Analyst Interpretation for the use of proceeds.

2. Adjusted EBITDA and net income diverged sharply because of hedging. Adjusted EBITDA for the last twelve months was USD 790.6 million, and non-funding debt was 7.64x that figure, against 6.16x at FY2025; yet the last twelve months produced a net loss of USD 105.0 million. Adjusted EBITDA excludes servicing-rights valuation changes and the result on other interest rate derivatives, which together were a net loss of USD 526.8 million over the last twelve months and USD 538.1 million in the second quarter alone. In the second quarter a derivative loss of USD 603.2 million was only partly offset by a USD 65.1 million servicing-rights valuation gain, so the hedge programme lost money net. The filings do not state the intended hedge ratio. Category: Documentary Fact; Analyst Interpretation on the hedge result.

3. Coverage of interest on non-funding debt is thinner than the headline Adjusted EBITDA suggests. Last-twelve-months Adjusted EBITDA covers interest expense on non-funding debt 2.92x, down from 3.25x at FY2025, and the second-quarter run rate is lower still at 2.14x (Adjusted EBITDA of USD 185.9 million and interest of USD 86.8 million, each annualised). These are accrual proxies because cash interest on non-funding debt is not reported separately. Category: Analyst Interpretation on CP-1 figures.

4. Liquidity is adequate for the near term but depends on lender discretion. Cash of USD 477.4 million (unrestricted) plus USD 800.0 million of undrawn lines gives USD 1,277.4 million, of which USD 300.0 million sits under uncommitted servicing-rights facilities that advance against collateral value and may be curtailed if that value falls [Q2 10-Q `page:54`, `page:55`]. The USD 500.0 million revolver is provided by the controlling holder. Scheduled non-funding repayments are USD 83.7 million in the second half of 2026 and USD 2,375.0 million in 2027 (the 2027 notes of USD 500.0 million and the Conventional servicing-rights facility of USD 1,875.0 million). Without new money, 2027 repayments would be 185.9% of current headroom. Category: Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation.

5. Short-dated warehouse funding is the structural exposure beneath the debt. Warehouse lines of USD 8,600.1 million fund mortgage loans of USD 9,619.1 million, only USD 900.0 million of line capacity is committed, advances are generally 97% to 98% of principal, and lines with USD 5,090.8 million advanced have expiry dates on or before May 14, 2027. Every facility, including the secured and unsecured lines, carries cross-default or cross-acceleration provisions, so a covenant breach or an unmet margin call under one could terminate all of them [Q2 10-Q `page:50`, `page:52`]. The company states compliance with all covenants at June 30, 2026, and no covenant levels are printed. Category: Documentary Fact; Insufficient Information on covenant headroom.

6. Controlling-holder structure cuts both ways. SFS Corp. holds 78.7% of Holdings LLC and about 79% of votes, receives member distributions and Tax Receivable Agreement payments (liability USD 280.0 million, accelerating on a change in control), and also subordinated its USD 500.0 million revolver to the senior notes in September 2025 and took part in the August 2026 preferred issue through a vehicle of the CEO's family [CP-1A, Ownership and control; Governance and sponsor behaviour]. Distributions and dividends paid in the first half of 2026 were USD 321.8 million against a net loss of USD 281.5 million before the dividend was suspended. Behaviour reads as mixed. Category: Analyst Interpretation on documented actions.

7. The August 5, 2026 raise improves the equity position but its effect cannot yet be measured. The USD 1,650.0 million of gross proceeds has not reached any reported statement, its use of proceeds, fees and balance sheet classification are not supplied, and the contingent rights offering of USD 400 million is scheduled to expire after the cutoff. If the proceeds were retained or used to repay secured lines and were classified as equity, non-funding debt to equity would be between 2.29x and 1.67x; if the preferred is classified outside equity and proceeds repay secured lines, it would be 4.46x. These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. Annual dividends at the stated rates would be USD 165.0 million in cash or USD 214.5 million non-cash. Category: Analyst Interpretation; Insufficient Information on terms.

8. Regulatory net worth and liquidity floors are close enough to matter. The most restrictive agency minimums are net worth of USD 743.6 million and liquidity of USD 343.9 million for UWM, against consolidated total equity of USD 985.3 million and unrestricted cash of USD 477.4 million at June 30, 2026 [Q2 10-Q `page:23`]. Consolidated equity includes non-controlling interest and is not UWM's own net worth, which is not printed, so the USD 241.7 million gap is a proxy only. A second-quarter-sized fall in equity (USD 615.6 million) would have exceeded that proxy gap before the August raise. Category: Analyst Interpretation; Insufficient Information on UWM-level figures.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:

- Agency-eligible, high-volume origination. About 93% of loans in the second quarter of 2026 went to Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or Ginnie Mae pools, and originations were USD 84,646.4 million for the first half of 2026, 17.4% above the first half of 2025, so most production has an agency exit and the lines turn over within a month [CP-1A, Company and operating model; CP-1]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Servicing-rights asset and earnings drivers. Servicing income rose to USD 220.5 million in the second quarter from USD 178.8 million a year earlier, and the servicing-rights asset of USD 5,311.5 million is 2.14% of USD 247.6 billion serviced; it supports secured borrowing and can be sold, with USD 801.0 million of net sale proceeds in the first half [CP-1, Earnings, cash flow and servicing drivers]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Fresh junior capital and owner participation. The August 2026 preferred and warrant issue of USD 1,650.0 million included USD 150.0 million from a vehicle of the CEO's family, the dividend was suspended, and the owner's revolver is subordinated to the senior notes [CP-1A, Governance and sponsor behaviour]. Category: Documentary Fact; Analyst Interpretation on creditor benefit.
- Spread maturity profile on senior notes. The four note series mature in 2027, 2029, 2030 and 2031 (USD 500.0 million, 700.0 million, 800.0 million and 1,000.0 million), with none in 2028, and the 2027 notes are callable at par [CP-1, Chart data: debt maturities]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Reported covenant compliance. The company states compliance with indenture and facility covenants at June 30, 2026, and the tests that are printed for note incurrence (Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio of at least 3.0 to 1.0 or Debt-to-Equity Ratio not above 2.0 to 1.0) restrict new non-funding debt [Q2 10-Q `page:54`]. Category: Documentary Fact; the restriction's effect is Analyst Interpretation.

Weaknesses:

- High and rising leverage on thin equity. Non-funding debt to equity of 6.13x and 7.64x LTM Adjusted EBITDA; equity ex non-controlling interest is only USD 133.8 million [CP-1]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Earnings volatility and hedge losses. Net loss of USD 451.9 million in the second quarter after a USD 603.2 million derivative loss; recurring servicing-rights decay of USD 395.1 million in the first half is not added back in Adjusted EBITDA [CP-1, Adjusted EBITDA definition and reconciliation review]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Negative operating cash flow. Operating cash flow was negative USD 1,885.8 million for the first half of 2026, and negative USD 1,084.8 million after adding servicing-rights sale proceeds, against positive USD 1,919.8 million a year earlier on the same basis; secured borrowing of USD 1,750.0 million plugged the gap [CP-1, Calculations]. Category: Documentary Fact; Analyst Interpretation on funding the gap.
- Uncommitted and short-dated funding with cross-default. Only USD 900.0 million of warehouse line capacity is committed, the servicing-rights facilities are uncommitted and collateral-based, and cross-default links all lines [Q2 10-Q `page:15`, `page:52`]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Controlling-holder claims and related-party flows. Tax Receivable Agreement liability of USD 280.0 million, member distributions of USD 261.9 million paid in the first half of 2026, and related-party payments of USD 32.9 million in FY2025 [CP-1A, Governance and sponsor behaviour; Claims outside debt in CP-1]. Category: Documentary Fact.
- Execution and legal risks. The in-house servicing transition (management targets October 2026) and class actions on broker influence, telemarketing and a vendor cyber incident; no accrual or estimate is printed [CP-1A, Timeline; Credit translations]. Category: Management Language for the timeline; Documentary Fact for the proceedings.
- Terms of the August 2026 raise are unknown. Redemption and protective rights of the Series A-1 preferred and the board rights of Oaktree could rank ahead of creditor interests in practice. The filing does not say. Category: Insufficient Information.

### What would change the view

- Improvement: the certificate of designation shows no holder redemption right ahead of debt maturities; use of proceeds repays secured lines; hedge losses stop and equity rebuilds; the 2027 notes and the Conventional facility are extended; uncommitted capacity is replaced by committed lines.
- Deterioration: further derivative losses; servicing-rights fair value falls and lenders curtail advances; a warehouse line is not renewed on or after its expiry; the rights offering fails or the preferred carries a near-term redemption right; distributions to the controlling holder resume while equity is thin.
