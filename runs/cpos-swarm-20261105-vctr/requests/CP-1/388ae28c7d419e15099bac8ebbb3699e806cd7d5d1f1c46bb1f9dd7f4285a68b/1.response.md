### Capital structure and debt terms

- Instrument: term loan under the 2019 Credit Agreement, principal 975,150 at September 30, 2026 (982,538 at December 31, 2025), maturing September 23, 2032, amortizing 1% a year of the 985.0 million dollar original principal in quarterly installments. Q3-10Q page:20 shows the table "Due September 2032$975,150" with an interest rate of 5.82% and an effective rate of 5.98%. Carrying value is 965,911 net of unamortized issuance costs of 3,014 and discount of 6,225 (Q3-10Q page:20).
- Pricing: after the Seventh Amendment of May 18, 2026 the margin is SOFR plus 1.75% (alternate base rate plus 0.75%); the company elects three-month Term SOFR (Q3-10Q page:20). Before it, SOFR plus 2.00% (10-K page:142). Interest rates are floating; the swap was terminated on October 30, 2023, and its deferred gain is being amortized as a credit to interest expense through September 23, 2032 (Q3-10Q page:25), which makes reported interest expense lower than cash interest in each period; the unamortized balance at December 31, 2025 was 11.7 million dollars before tax (10-K page:145).
- Revolver: 100.0 million dollar commitment with a 10.0 million dollar letter-of-credit sublimit, maturing September 23, 2030, undrawn at September 30, 2026 and December 31, 2025 (Q3-10Q page:44). No letter of credit is shown as using it at September 30, 2026: the liquidity table prints an undrawn commitment equal to the full 100,000 (Q3-10Q page:43).
- Ranking and parties: the revolver is described as first lien; the borrower is Victory Capital Holdings, Inc.; the guarantors and collateral package are Not supplied (the filings refer only to "the other loan parties"). No other debt instrument, bond, finance lease or off-balance-sheet debt is printed in the balance sheet or debt notes.
- Total debt per the Q3 earnings release is "approximately $975 million" (R3 page:4), which agrees with the gross principal in the note; the balance sheet shows the net carrying amount.

### Covenants

The 2019 Credit Agreement carries one financial maintenance covenant: a first lien leverage ratio of no greater than 4.00 to 1.00, tested at the end of each fiscal quarter only when revolver borrowings exceed 35.0% of commitments (Q3-10Q page:20). With a 100.0 million dollar commitment the test is not triggered unless more than 35.0 million dollars is drawn (calculated), and the revolver is undrawn. There is no step-down printed. The company states it was in compliance at September 30, 2026. The reported covenant ratio, the covenant definition of EBITDA and first lien debt, and any add-back cap are Not supplied, so headroom is Not calculable. For orientation only, gross term loan principal over the calculated LTM adjusted EBITDA is 1.14x; this is not the covenant metric, because the covenant is defined on first lien debt and a covenant EBITDA that is not in the pack.
