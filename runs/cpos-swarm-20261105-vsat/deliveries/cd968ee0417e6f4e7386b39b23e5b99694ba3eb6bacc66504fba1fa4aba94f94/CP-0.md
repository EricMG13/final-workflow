### Source assessment

#### Entity and perimeter

- The registrant in all four periodic filings is Viasat, Inc., and the statements are consolidated: wholly owned subsidiaries plus the majority-owned subsidiary TrellisWare Technologies, Inc., with a noncontrolling interest in the balance sheet (10-Q June 2026 page:5; balance sheet page:2). The equity-method affiliate JSAT Mobile is outside consolidation (10-Q June 2026 page:43).
- Debt is issued in two groups. Viasat, Inc. is the obligor of the 2022 and 2023 Term Loan Facilities, the Viasat Revolving Credit Facility and the 2027, 2028 and 2031 Notes; the Ex-Im Credit Facility is guaranteed by Viasat and secured by the ViaSat-2 satellite and related assets and a pledge of the borrower's capital stock (page:36). The Inmarsat Term Loan Facility, the Inmarsat Revolving Credit Facility and the Inmarsat 2029 Notes belong to the Inmarsat group, guaranteed by certain material Inmarsat subsidiaries and secured by Inmarsat assets (page:35, page:41). The 2027 Notes paragraph describes the Inmarsat 2029 Notes as secured by liens on assets that are not part of the 2027 Notes collateral (page:39). The note text says none of Viasat's subsidiaries guaranteed the 2022 Term Loan, 2023 Term Loan, Revolving Credit, 2027, 2028 or 2031 Notes as of June 30, 2026 (page:32, page:33, page:37, page:38, page:40, page:43).
- The filing does not give separate summarized financial statements for the Viasat group and the Inmarsat group, no restricted or unrestricted subsidiary split, and no guarantor financial information. Group-level leverage and the cash held by each group are therefore Not supplied from these sources. This is an unresolved perimeter distinction preserved as a gap.
- Amounts in the filings are USD. Statements are in thousands; narrative uses millions or billions; the 10-K text rounds some debt to USD 6.6 billion.

#### Annual report (10-K, FY2026)

- Contains audited balance sheet (page:128-129), statements of operations (page:129-130), cash flows with cash paid for interest and taxes (page:130-131), and the notes: leases (page:172), senior notes and other long-term debt with a fiscal-year maturity table (page:173), segment information (page:203-206), commitments (page:200-201) and contingencies (page:201-202).
- The auditor's opinion covers the financial statements and internal control over financial reporting, with a critical audit matter on communication services revenue recognition (page:125-128).
- Items that make FY2026 not a clean run-rate and that downstream modules must treat deliberately: the cash flow statement carries a gain on sale of an equity interest in an unconsolidated affiliate of 168,062 thousand (page:130; Navarino UK sale in March 2026, page:200), a loss on extinguishment of debt of 11,935 thousand (page:130), and the income statement shows interest income of 205,849 thousand against 83,920 thousand in FY2025 (page:129).

#### Interim reports (10-Q, Q2 FY2026, Q3 FY2026, Q1 FY2027)

- Each carries a balance sheet, statements of operations, cash flows, equity, notes, MD&A, and exhibit lists. Discrete quarters can be read directly from the income statements for Q2 FY2026 (three months, September 30, 2025), Q3 FY2026 (three months, December 31, 2025) and Q1 FY2027 with Q1 FY2026 as comparative. Cash flows are presented six months (September 30, 2025 filing page:3-4) and nine months (December 31, 2025 filing page:3-4) year to date; only Q1 FY2027 and Q1 FY2026 cash flows are three-month figures (June 2026 filing page:3-4).
- Q4 FY2026 is not reported stand-alone; the annual figures and nine-month figures would have to be differenced, and the June 2025 10-Q is not in the pack (Q1 FY2026 appears only as comparative columns in the June 2026 10-Q). A difference is a derivation, not a source fact, and must be labelled so.
- The Q3 FY2026 income statement contains a Ligado-related item that the filing ties to the lump sum payment: the December 2025 10-Q states that 152.5 million of the Ligado lump sum was recognized as interest income and 267.5 million as deferred revenue (page:52), and that the 147.9 million increase in interest income for the quarter was primarily this item (page:74).

#### Results releases (Exhibit 99.1, three files)

- Each file is a short announcement of results and call details, with company boilerplate and contacts. It states that a letter to shareholders and webcast slides are posted on the company's investor site, but these are not in the pack (page:1 of each file). The files contain no reported or adjusted figures, reconciliations, quarter versus year-to-date tables or guidance. They provide dates of the announcements only and cannot be used as quantitative evidence. This is a coverage gap for management narrative and any adjusted metric, and it does not weaken the filed statements.
- The pack lists them as releases standing in for presentations (no deck was retrieved); no slide or speaker-note coverage is claimed.

#### Debt, liquidity and covenants (filed text)

- The 10-Q for June 30, 2026 lists total debt of 6,570,503 thousand by facility against 6,585,115 thousand at March 31, 2026, with current portion of 661,939 thousand against 57,753 thousand (page:32). The 10-K gives the same March 31, 2026 total and a fiscal-year maturity table (page:173).
- Liquidity text: cash and cash equivalents of 1,735,819 thousand (page:2); Viasat revolver 647.5 million with 47.2 million of standby letters of credit and 600.3 million available (page:37); Inmarsat revolver 550.0 million undrawn, maturing March 28, 2027 (page:34). The 10-Q contractual obligations table is on page:75.
- Covenants: the revolvers and the Ex-Im Credit Facility have a maximum total leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio (page:36-37), and the Inmarsat revolver carries total net leverage and interest coverage tests (page:35). The filings state compliance as of June 30, 2026. No threshold, step-down, reported ratio or covenant EBITDA definition is printed, so headroom is Not calculable from these sources.
- Calendar maturity dates for the 2027, 2028, Inmarsat 2029 and 2031 Notes are not printed in the sources reviewed (only the year in the title and fiscal-year buckets in the 10-K table). The term loans, the Inmarsat revolver, the Viasat revolver (with its springing maturity) and the Ex-Im facility have printed dates (page:32-37).

#### Segment, KPI and backlog

- Two reportable segments, communication services and defense and advanced technologies, with business-line revenue, segment expenses and segment operating profit (June 2026 10-Q page:48; 10-K page:205). Segment assets are not presented because the chief operating decision maker is not provided them (page:49). Firm and funded backlog by segment is on page:68 of the June 2026 10-Q. Aircraft in service counts are on page:53.
- Add-backs: depreciation, amortization, stock-based compensation, and acquisition and transaction expenses appear in the cash flow and segment tables, but no company-defined adjusted measure exists in any pack file.

#### Version and relationship notes

- The June 2026 10-Q and the 10-K are different periods of one reporting line, not versions of each other, and all remain active. Figures I cross-checked agree: the March 31, 2026 total debt of 6,585,115 thousand and cash of 1,746,835 thousand appear in both the 10-K and the June 2026 10-Q comparative column.
- One wording difference is a progression, not a conflict: the 10-K says the DCMA had approved incurred costs through fiscal year 2022 (page:202) and the June 2026 10-Q says through fiscal year 2023 (page:9).
- The December 2025 10-Q reported the Ex-Im loan facility of 188.7 million as a subsequent event of January 21, 2026 (page:56); the 10-K and June 2026 10-Q describe the same facility as outstanding. This is consistent. Neither the 10-K notes (Notes 1 to 16) nor the June 2026 10-Q notes (Notes 1 to 10) include a subsequent-events note. The Cooperation Agreement dated May 6, 2026 appears only as an exhibit title (10-K page:124), and its terms are not supplied.
- No draft, unsigned or superseded document is in the pack. Nothing dated after the cutoff of 2026-11-05 is used: the latest source is the 10-Q for June 30, 2026 and the announcement of August 4, 2026.
