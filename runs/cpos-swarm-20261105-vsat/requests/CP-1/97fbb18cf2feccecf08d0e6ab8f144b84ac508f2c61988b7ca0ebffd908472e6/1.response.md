### Financial base view

Viasat has deleveraged and improved cover over the past year, but a refinancing event sits close and the headline numbers flatter the run-rate. On the derived basis stated below, total debt to EBITDA is 4.58x for LTM June 2026 against 5.70x for FY2025 and 4.50x for FY2026, and net debt to EBITDA is 3.37x against 4.43x and 3.31x. LTM is slightly worse than FY2026 because the Q1 FY2027 EBITDA is lower than the Q1 FY2026 quarter it replaces while debt fell by only USD 14.6 million between March and June 2026. Gross debt was USD 6,570.5 million at June 30, 2026, of which USD 661.9 million is current, including the USD 600.0 million 5.625% senior secured notes due 2027. Liquidity is USD 1,735.8 million of cash plus USD 1,150.3 million of undrawn revolver availability, but the Inmarsat USD 550.0 million revolver matures on March 28, 2027 and the Viasat revolver carries a springing maturity tied to other material debt. The strongest support is the cash balance, which covers the whole current debt balance, and the 4.73x LTM coverage of cash interest. The weakest point is quality of the base: FY2026 cash flow from operations includes the USD 420.0 million Ligado lump sum and FY2026 other income includes a USD 168.1 million gain on the Navarino sale, and the company prints no EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA or covenant ratio. What decides the outcome is the 2027 refinancing, the terms of the Viasat revolver springing maturity (conditions not described in the sources), and whether the ViaSat-3 satellites in service lift earnings while capital spending of about USD 1.0 billion a year continues. All metrics below are derived by this analyst from filed GAAP lines and are labelled so; the company's covenant definitions are not supplied.

### Basis of preparation and definitions

- Entity and perimeter: Viasat, Inc. consolidated, which includes wholly owned subsidiaries (the Inmarsat group since May 2023) and the majority-owned TrellisWare Technologies, Inc.; the noncontrolling interest is shown separately (10-Q June 2026 page:5, page:2). Net income attributable to the noncontrolling interest was USD 38,012 thousand in FY2026 (10-K page:129). Consolidated EBITDA below includes 100% of TrellisWare.
- Units: USD thousands in the statements; USD millions in MD&A text. Sign convention: outflows and expenses are shown negative in the statement copies below, as the filings print them in parentheses.
- Derived EBITDA = Income (loss) from operations + Depreciation + Amortization of intangible assets, the last two from the cash flow statement. No unusual item is added back, stock-based compensation is not added back, and equity income, interest income and other income are excluded. The cash flow amortization line (FY2026 USD 315,950 thousand) is consistent with three disclosed components: acquired intangibles USD 260,712 thousand (10-K page:129), capitalized software USD 41.1 million (10-K page:143) and patents, orbital slots and licenses USD 14.1 million (10-K page:141), which sum to USD 315.9 million within rounding.
- Net debt = total debt principal (including finance lease obligations, before unamortized discount and issuance costs) less cash and cash equivalents. Operating leases are outside both debt and net debt; operating lease cost is already deducted in operating income, so derived EBITDA is after lease cost.
- Cash interest = cash paid for interest, net of amounts capitalized, from the cash flow supplemental information.

### Covenants

The filings name the tests but print no thresholds, step-downs or reported ratios. The Viasat Revolving Credit Facility has a maximum total leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio (10-Q June 2026 page:37). The Ex-Im Credit Facility has a maximum total leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio (page:36). The Inmarsat revolver has total net leverage and interest coverage covenants applying to Inmarsat (page:35). The term loans and the notes carry incurrence-style restrictions on debt, liens, asset sales, investments and restricted payments (page:33, page:39-43). The company states compliance with all of them as of June 30, 2026 (page:33-37). Headroom is Not calculable: the covenant levels and the covenant definitions of EBITDA, debt and interest are Not supplied, and the credit agreements are not in the pack. The derived leverage and coverage here must not be read as covenant ratios, and the covenant tests for the Viasat borrower group and the Inmarsat group would each use a different perimeter from the consolidated figures.
