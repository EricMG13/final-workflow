### Business and transaction view

VSE is a single-segment aviation aftermarket business that sells parts (distribution) and repairs components and engines (repair), and in 2026 it bought its way into a larger engine-repair and distribution platform. The strongest support for a creditor is that the largest deal, Precision Aviation Group (PAG), was funded with a large equity raise and tangible equity units alongside a USD 900.0 million term loan B, that PAG already contributes about a third of quarterly revenue, and that the group has raised equity repeatedly since 2024 to fund acquisitions. The weakest points are concentration (one affiliated customer group at about 20% of FY2025 revenue), dependence on OEM licenses and approvals for the highest-value repairs, and an acquisition pace that leaves purchase accounting preliminary for several deals at once. What decides the outcome is integration of PAG and the Q4 2026 cash flow, because debt now rests mostly on floating-rate bank facilities, and because the documents that govern control rights (credit agreement, purchase agreement amendment, exchange and registration agreements) are not in the pack. Management's characterisations (record results, leading position, sole-source repairs) are quoted as Management Language below and are not adopted.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

The transaction that defines the period is the acquisition of Precision Aviation Group (PAG), a portfolio company of GenNx360 Capital Partners, under a stock purchase agreement signed on 2026-01-29 and completed on 2026-05-05. Status: completed. The parties named in the executed agreement listing are VSE Corporation, two VSE acquisition vehicles, GenNx/PAG IntermediateCo Inc. and GenNx360 PAG Buyer, LLC (S1 page:79); a first amendment to the agreement dated 2026-05-04, an exchange and redemption agreement, a registration rights agreement and two lock-up agreements, all dated 2026-05-05 and all with GenNx360 PAG Buyer, LLC, are listed as exhibits (S3 page:33), but none of those documents is in the pack, so their terms are Insufficient Information. The second-quarter 10-Q summarises the deal as total consideration of "approximately $2.02 billion" (S3 page:9). The 10-K and the first-quarter 10-Q describe total upfront consideration of approximately USD 2.025 billion, in the Q1 10-Q "consisting of $1.750 billion in cash" plus an in-kind payment of 1,415,752 shares of newly issued equity of a VSE subsidiary, with up to USD 125.0 million of contingent earn-out on PAG's 2026 adjusted EBITDA (S1 page:73; S2 page:20). The later 10-Qs record total purchase consideration of USD 2,020,007 thousand: cash paid 1,770,370, less cash acquired 22,215, equity at fair value 238,002 and an earn-out at acquisition-date fair value of 33,850 (S3 page:9; S4 page:9 and page:10). These are two bases for the same deal and are logged as a conflict below, not reconciled.

Use of proceeds and sources. The cash price was funded by a February 2026 common stock offering of 4,587,766 shares at USD 188.00 per share (net proceeds USD 829.0 million) and a tangible equity unit offering of 9,200,000 units at a stated USD 50.00 each (net proceeds USD 444.9 million), together about USD 1.3 billion net, plus borrowings of USD 900.0 million under a new term loan B (S4 page:26 and page:29; S2 page:20). Each unit combines a prepaid stock purchase contract, settled in shares on 2029-02-01 within a range of 0.2171 to 0.2660 shares per contract, and a senior unsecured amortizing note paying 5.93% per annum with final installment on 2029-02-01 (S4 page:19, page:26 and page:27). The new debt issues are therefore the term loan B (USD 900.0 million, due 2033-05-05, status: funded 2026-05-05, USD 897.75 million outstanding at 2026-09-30), the revolver upsize from USD 400.0 million to USD 500.0 million (due 2030-05-02, USD 20.0 million drawn at 2026-09-30) and the amortizing notes (USD 61.043 million outstanding at 2026-09-30); the prior term loan was repaid in full, and a debt commitment letter whose bridge and backstop commitments "were reduced to $0" had been signed with the purchase agreement (S4 page:18; S1 page:29). Debt issuance costs of USD 20.8 million were incurred on the amendment (S4 page:18). Liquidity effect and leverage after the deal are in the prior handoff (CP-1): principal debt of USD 978.8 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 296.3 million at 2025-12-31.

Other transactions. Atech Turbine Components, acquired on 2026-08-31 for USD 64.1 million net of cash acquired and funded with cash and revolver borrowings; NorthStar Technologies, acquired on 2026-04-01 for USD 10.1 million; Aero 3, acquired on 2025-12-23 for USD 346.1 million as revised (S2 stated 346.4); each is described as not material or preliminary (S4 page:9, page:12 and page:13). Subsequent event: Test Logic, Inc. was acquired on 2026-10-05, funded with cash on hand, price not disclosed (S4 page:27). Transaction-related status risks: the PAG, Atech, NorthStar and Aero 3 purchase price allocations are preliminary (S4 page:9 to page:15).

Ownership and control. VSE is a public company with no sponsor or controlling shareholder named in the filings or the context; beneficial ownership is Insufficient Information. After closing, the PAG seller holds 1,415,752 shares of a VSE subsidiary, exchangeable at any time and automatically exchanged into Company common stock on a one-for-one basis if not exchanged by 2029-05-05, "subject to a lock-up period" whose length is not stated (S4 page:10). That makes the seller a potential holder of a significant block once exchanged; its percentage of common stock is not stated and is not inferred here.
