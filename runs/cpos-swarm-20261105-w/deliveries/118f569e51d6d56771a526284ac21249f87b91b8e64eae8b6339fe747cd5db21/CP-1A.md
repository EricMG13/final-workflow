### Business and transaction view

Wayfair Inc. is a Delaware-incorporated, Boston-headquartered online home-goods retailer listed on the New York Stock Exchange (10-K page:1, page:9). It has no financial sponsor and the pack contains no merger, acquisition or sponsor transaction. The business sells through six brand storefronts, ships most orders directly from roughly 20 thousand suppliers' warehouses, and is adding its own warehouses, delivery network and a small physical store base (10-K page:9-14). The U.S. segment was 88% of FY2025 net revenue (10-K page:10). Ownership is public but control is concentrated: Class B stock carries ten votes per share, and at year-end 2025 the co-founders and their affiliates held about 18.3% of the economic interest and 67.4% of the voting power (10-K page:62).

For a creditor the transaction history that matters is capital structure change, not a deal. Since October 2024 the company issued four series of fixed-rate senior secured notes through its subsidiary Wayfair LLC totalling 2,600 million, entered into an amended and restated 500 million revolving credit facility, and used cash and the new issues to retire convertible notes that carried lower coupons and whose if-converted value exceeded principal at 2025-12-31 (10-K page:130). The effect, set out in the accepted financial base, is lower total debt and a maturity profile with nothing due in the rest of 2026 or in 2028 and 229 million due in 2027, at the price of higher fixed interest and a secured claim stack that ranks ahead of the remaining unsecured convertible notes. Five qualifications bound the view: the executed indentures and credit agreement are not supplied; two of the four issues have no stated use of proceeds in the pack; the 2026 proxy statement, which carries board and compensation detail, is not supplied; no credit rating is supplied; and the pack gives no management guidance. Nothing after the cutoff is used.

### Transaction summary and new debt issues

There is no merger, acquisition, sponsor buyout or divestiture transaction in the pack, and the context holds no deal terms, so the transaction type is capital structure refinancing, completed, with no pending transaction. Documentary status is that every debt action below is complete as of the Q3 10-Q signed on 2026-11-04. The issuer of each secured series is Wayfair LLC, a subsidiary of Wayfair Inc., under indentures with the guarantors named therein, including Wayfair Inc., and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as trustee and notes collateral agent (10-K page:121-122; Q2 10-Q page:12). The accepted financial base gives the amounts, coupons and maturities, which are not repeated in full here.

New debt issues in the period covered are four. First, 800 million of 7.250% senior secured notes due 2029 were issued on 2024-10-08 (10-K page:121). Second, 700 million of 7.750% notes due 2030 were issued on 2025-03-13 alongside the amended and restated credit agreement that provides the 500 million revolver maturing 2030-03-13 (10-K page:119, page:121). The 10-K states the use: on 2025-03-14 the company repurchased 578 million of 2026 convertible notes in connection with the issuance, and on 2025-05-09 it used the remaining proceeds with cash to repurchase 80 million of 2025 notes and 118 million of 2026 notes (10-K page:128). Third, 700 million of 6.750% notes due 2032 were issued on 2025-11-07 (10-K page:121); the pack does not state a use of proceeds, and the repurchase of 210 million of 2027 notes on 2025-11-12 for 375 million cash (10-K page:128) is close in time but is not linked to the issue by the filing, so no link is asserted. Fourth, 400 million of 7.125% notes due 2034 were issued on 2026-05-18, and the company used the proceeds with cash on hand on 2026-06-29 to settle the remaining 444 million of 2028 convertible notes after a redemption notice of 2026-05-14 (Q3 10-Q page:41, page:14-15). For the 2029 series the pack gives no use of proceeds.

The liquidity effect of the 2026 actions, from the accepted financial base, is that nine-month 2026 net cash on debt principal and extinguishment was 585 while operating cash flow was 506 and cash fell by 438 to 1,038. The effect on leverage is that carrying total debt fell from 3,272 at year-end 2025 to 2,799 and total debt to company-defined Adjusted EBITDA was 3.28x on the last-twelve-month figure of 853. The mechanism for creditors is as follows. Replacing convertible notes with coupons of 3.50% and below by secured notes at 6.750% to 7.750% raises fixed interest; the accepted base puts stated coupons on outstanding debt at about 195 a year against last-twelve-month cash interest of 170. It lengthens maturities, since the earliest remaining maturity is the 229 million 2027 convertible notes. It adds secured claims that share first-priority liens with the revolver, while the remaining convertible notes are unsecured and structurally subordinated. Convertible notes retired for cash above principal convert dilution into a cash cost: cash paid, including accrued interest and fees, was 375 for 210 of principal in 2025 and 247 for 145 in 2026 (Calculations), with extinguishment losses of 165 and 102 equal to the difference to carrying value. Shares were also issued in settlement: 0.9 million on the 2027 notes and 4.5 million on the 2028 notes (Q3 10-Q page:14-15). The 2027 notes themselves became convertible for the quarter ending 2026-12-31 and the filing says conversion may be settled in cash, shares or a combination at the company's election (Q3 10-Q page:13), so the remaining unsecured claim carries a cash call that the company controls through the form of settlement. Recovery prospects appear here as a mechanic only: secured and unsecured claims have different positions against the same asset base, and the pack gives no valuation or recovery analysis.
