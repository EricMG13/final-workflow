### Credit view

Wayfair Inc. is a better credit than it was two years ago, but it is still a thin-margin, discretionary-demand retailer whose leverage looks moderate only on management's own earnings measure. The strongest support is deleveraging: total debt fell to USD 2,799 million and total debt to Adjusted EBITDA to 3.28x from 6.88x in FY2024, with USD 1,098 million of cash and investments, an undrawn revolver and no maturity before September 2027. The weakest point is earnings quality: Adjusted EBITDA adds back USD 293 million of equity compensation, and on operating income plus depreciation and amortization the ratio is 5.10x. What decides the outcome is whether U.S. order growth keeps converting into earnings faster than fixed interest is rising.

### Key findings

First, Wayfair has cut its debt and raised its earnings at the same time. Carrying total debt is USD 2,799 million at 2026-09-30 against USD 3,118 million at 2024-12-31, while last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA is USD 853 million against USD 453 million in FY2024 [CP-1 handoff, leverage and metrics tables]. Total debt to Adjusted EBITDA is 3.28x and net debt to Adjusted EBITDA is 1.99x (Documentary Fact for the inputs, Analyst Interpretation for the improvement being earnings-led, since debt fell 10.2% and Adjusted EBITDA rose 88.3%).

Second, liquidity is ample against the visible maturities. Wayfair holds USD 1,038 million of cash and USD 60 million of short-term investments, has USD 418 million of undrawn revolver after letters of credit, and owes USD 229 million of convertible notes in September 2027 and USD 800 million of secured notes in October 2029 [CP-1 handoff, liquidity and maturity tables]. Cash and investments cover the 2027 principal 4.8 times (Calculations). The remaining unsecured notes are convertible during the quarter ending 2026-12-31 with an if-converted excess over principal of USD 141 million, and the company may settle in cash, shares or both, so the cash call is within its control [CP-1 handoff, debt schedule; CP-1A handoff, transaction summary] (Documentary Fact).

Third, the quality of the earnings measure decides how comfortable the leverage is. Adjusted EBITDA adds back USD 293 million of equity-based compensation and related taxes over the last twelve months, which is 34.3% of Adjusted EBITDA, and the company also paid USD 77 million of tax on net share settlement and USD 60 million of buybacks through financing in nine months 2026 [CP-1 handoff, definition review]. On operating income plus depreciation and amortization of USD 549 million, total debt is 5.10x and net debt 3.10x; operating income of USD 283 million covers net interest expense of USD 156 million 1.81x (Calculations) (Analyst Interpretation).

Fourth, the cost of debt is rising even as the balance falls. Wayfair issued four fixed-rate secured series totalling USD 2,600 million since October 2024 and retired convertible notes with coupons of 3.50% and below, so stated coupons on USD 2,829 million of principal now total about USD 195 million a year against last-twelve-month cash interest of USD 170 million; Adjusted EBITDA covers cash interest 5.02x and run-rate coupons 4.36x [CP-1 handoff, interest cover; CP-1A handoff, transaction summary] (Documentary Fact and Analyst Interpretation).

Fifth, the business is volume-led and U.S.-concentrated. The U.S. was 88% of FY2025 net revenue, Q3 2026 orders delivered rose to 11.0 million from 9.8 million while average order value fell to USD 311 from USD 317, and International Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of USD 54 million in nine months 2026 against U.S. earnings of USD 683 million [CP-1A handoff, Company and operating model; CP-1 handoff, segments] (Documentary Fact). Negative working capital, with accounts payable of USD 1,213 million against inventories of USD 89 million, funds operations and would reverse in a volume decline; nine-month 2026 operating cash flow included a working-capital inflow of USD 64 million against an outflow of USD 76 million a year earlier [CP-1 handoff, cash flow reading].

Sixth, control and creditor protection rest on documents the pack does not contain. The co-founders and affiliates held 67.4% of voting power and 18.3% of the economic interest at year-end 2025, Class B stock is 15.3% of combined shares against a 10% automatic conversion level, no dividend has ever been paid, and no priming, uptiering, drop-down or distribution evidence was found, but the indentures that would show capacity are not supplied [CP-1A handoff, ownership and governance table] (Documentary Fact; absence in the pack is not a finding of absence).

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. The first strength is deleveraging with earnings growth: last-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA of USD 853 million is up from USD 743 million in FY2025 and USD 453 million in FY2024, and total debt to Adjusted EBITDA is 3.28x from 4.40x and 6.88x [CP-1 handoff] (Documentary Fact). The second is liquidity: USD 1,098 million of cash and short-term investments and USD 418 million of undrawn revolver exceed the USD 229 million due before 2029, which cash and investments alone cover 4.8 times (Calculations), and last-twelve-month free cash flow is USD 476 million, 55.8% of Adjusted EBITDA [CP-1 handoff] (Documentary Fact; the multiple is Analyst Interpretation). The third is a longer maturity profile: the 2026 and 2028 convertible notes are gone, the earliest remaining maturity is USD 229 million in September 2027, and the secured notes mature in 2029, 2030, 2032 and 2034 [CP-1 handoff, maturity table] (Documentary Fact). The fourth is repeat demand and growth: repeat customers placed 80.0% of Q3 2026 orders and net revenue rose 9.7% in the quarter, which supports revenue durability [CP-1A handoff; CP-1 handoff] (Documentary Fact; the release KPIs are management-calculated). The fifth is market access: the secured notes carry fair values between 98.9% and 102.9% of principal at 2026-09-30 (CP-1 handoff, debt schedule), consistent with continued access to secured debt markets (Analyst Interpretation).

Weaknesses. The first weakness is thin and adjusted earnings: net income over the last twelve months is a loss of USD 160 million, operating income plus depreciation and amortization is USD 549 million against Adjusted EBITDA of USD 853 million, and the stockholders' deficit is USD 2,804 million [CP-1 handoff] (Documentary Fact). The second is rising fixed interest, as set out in the fourth key finding, which leaves less room if earnings slip [CP-1 handoff; CP-1A handoff] (Analyst Interpretation). The third is demand and concentration risk: a discretionary category, 88% U.S. revenue, a loss-making International segment and a single small-parcel carrier [CP-1A handoff, Company and operating model] (Documentary Fact). The fourth is working-capital and seasonality sensitivity: first-quarter operating cash flow was negative in both years printed, and the 2026 working-capital inflow may not repeat [CP-1 handoff] (Analyst Interpretation). The fifth is that the claim stack is secured-heavy: secured notes are 91.9% of principal and share first-priority liens with the revolver, so the unsecured convertible notes rank behind them and structurally behind subsidiary liabilities, which matters for any loss given default analysis that later modules perform (mechanic only) [CP-1 handoff; CP-1A handoff] (Documentary Fact). The sixth is control and information: founder voting control, no covenant ratio reported, no ratings and no guidance mean that downside protection cannot be measured (Insufficient Information; the missing datapoints are the covenant definition and reported ratio, executed indentures, the proxy statement and ratings, as set out in Gaps).
