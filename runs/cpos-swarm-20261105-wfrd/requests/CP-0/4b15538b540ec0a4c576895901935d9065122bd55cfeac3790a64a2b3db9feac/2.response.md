### Strongest contrary evidence and limitations

- The strongest reason for caution is that all debt terms and covenant levels rest on summaries in the notes, for example Q3 10-Q page:11: "a maximum total net leverage ratio of 3.50 to 1.00". A search of every source for covenant compliance statements, waivers and events of default found only risk-factor language and the "Defaults Upon Senior Securities. None." item in each quarterly report (for example Q3 10-Q page:38), and no ratio is reported against these levels. Mechanism: without the reported covenant ratio or the definitions, covenant headroom cannot be sourced, so CP-1 and CP-2 can only state the levels and say headroom is not supplied. This limits the covenant and liquidity conclusions, not the business or leverage description.
- The latest release defines gross leverage (Q3 release page:15: "Gross Leverage* - Gross Leverage* is a non-GAAP measure") and mentions it on page:1, but the reconciliation retained on pages:15-16 shows only "Net Leverage* (Net Debt*/Adjusted EBITDA*) 0.28 x". Mechanism: a reader could assume a reported gross figure exists; none is in the retained text, so a gross measure must be computed downstream from reported total debt and trailing adjusted EBITDA and labelled as the analyst's own calculation.
- Liquidity beyond cash relies on filed text about a one billion commitment less letters of credit and zero borrowings (Q3 10-Q page:11), while further letters of credit sit under uncommitted bilateral lines (page:11: "$297 million letters of credit under various uncommitted bi-lateral facilities"). Surety bonds are separate (page:34: "surety bonds outstanding of $534 million"). Mechanism: these off-debt claims can be called, so liquidity must be shown net of drawn letters of credit and kept apart from debt.
- Customer concentration and cash access limits are disclosed (page:32: Mexico receivables; page:31: "$164 million" of cash that cannot be immediately repatriated). They are retained evidence, not source gaps, but they affect how cash is used in later modules.
- Conditions outside the filings (Iran Conflict, tariffs) appear as management narrative in the Q3 10-Q (pages:19, 38) and are attributed to management.

### Evidence, source risk, downstream consequence
