### Credit view

Whirlpool is a stretched, non-investment-grade credit with a funded runway to 2028 but little earnings cushion. The strongest support is liquidity and structure: USD 1,103 million of cash and USD 2,193 million of undrawn committed facilities at September 30, 2026, equity raised in February, and only USD 14 million due within twelve months after the June refinancing. The weakest point is earnings: ongoing EBITDA for the latest twelve months is USD 720 million against total debt of USD 6,852 million, or 9.52x, with operating cash flow of negative USD 826 million for nine months and interest expense now running near USD 100 million a quarter. What decides the outcome is whether price increases and cost take-out restore North American margins, and whether year-end working capital release materialises, before the secured facility's borrowing base and its fixed charge test begin to bind.

### Key findings

Leverage has moved sharply the wrong way. Total debt over ongoing EBITDA was 5.43x in FY2024, 6.11x in FY2025 and is 9.52x for the twelve months to September 30, 2026, with net debt over ongoing EBITDA of 7.98x. On the stricter operating-profit basis the latest ratios are 8.46x and 7.10x, so the conclusion does not depend on which EBITDA is used. Total debt rose by USD 332 million over the first nine months of 2026 while ongoing EBITDA fell from USD 1,067 million in FY2025 to USD 720 million for the latest twelve months. The issuer does not print a leverage ratio, so every ratio here is analyst-built.

The refinancing bought time and cost carry. The June 2026 issue of USD 1,000 million of 7.500% notes due 2031 and USD 1,000 million of 7.875% notes due 2034 carries about USD 154 million of annual coupon (derived from the stated rates), replacing euro notes with coupons of 1.25% and 1.10% on about USD 1,290 million of carrying value, so roughly USD 139 million of added annual coupon before any saving on the repaid revolver. Quarterly interest expense reached USD 102 million in Q3 2026 against USD 63 million in Q2, and management guides full-year 2026 interest of about USD 350 million and Q4 interest of about USD 103 million. Ongoing EBITDA over annualised Q3 interest of USD 408 million is 1.76x, and guided full-year 2026 ongoing EBIT of about USD 293 million is 0.84x guided interest. Cash interest is not printed for the interim periods, so these are expense-based.

Liquidity is adequate on paper and uncertain in substance. Cash of USD 1,103 million and undrawn commitments of USD 2,193 million total USD 3,296 million, down from USD 4,101 million at December 31, 2025, and the nominal undrawn amount is mostly an ABL (USD 2,000 million) limited by a borrowing base the filings do not disclose. Operating cash flow was negative USD 826 million for nine months and management's guidance of about USD 700 million for the year implies a fourth-quarter inflow of about USD 1,526 million. That is a seasonal working capital release, not a trend, and it carries execution risk.

Covenant headroom is thin on every proxy available. The ABL tests fixed charge coverage of 1.00x only when Availability falls below the greater of 10% of the Line Cap and USD 135 million. An indicative proxy of ongoing EBITDA less capital expenditures over interest expense is 0.97x, so if availability tightened the test could bind. The proxy is not the covenant definition. The prior facility's interest coverage test of at least 3.0x was relaxed in May 2026 by excluding the first quarter, and expense-based cover has since fallen to 2.21x.

Creditor ranking has shifted. The ABL is first lien and the Secured Notes are second lien on the same collateral pool, secured by substantially all assets of the Company and the guarantors, while about USD 4.8 billion of senior unsecured notes (derived as total debt less the Secured Notes and notes payable) remain. Whether the unsecured note indentures allowed the lien grant or required equal and ratable security is not stated and the documents are not supplied. This is an open legal question, not an adverse finding.

Claims outside reported debt are material in aggregate. Supply chain finance of USD 650 million sits in payables, operating lease liabilities of USD 863 million noncurrent, a USD 575 million cumulative mandatory convertible preferred, Brazilian tax assessments (BEFIEX about USD 545 million, PIS/COFINS about USD 88 million, IPI Amnesty about USD 61 million, and USD 112 million unreserved for DIFAL), and USD 578 million of bank guarantees, letters of credit and surety bonds. None is in total debt.

### Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths. Whirlpool holds scaled, long-established appliance franchises with leading positions in the Americas and a brand portfolio including Whirlpool, KitchenAid and Maytag (Documentary Fact; Management Language for the market-position wording). It has demonstrated market access in 2026, raising USD 524 million of common equity and USD 557 million of mandatory convertible preferred in February (Documentary Fact), and it suspended the common dividend, which retains cash (Documentary Fact). After the June refinancing near-term maturities are cleared: USD 14 million falls due in the next twelve months and USD 585 million in 2028 (Documentary Fact for the 14; Analyst Interpretation for the schedule, which is rebuilt). The asset-based facility of USD 2,000 million and Brazilian facilities of USD 193 million were undrawn at September 30, 2026 (Documentary Fact). Management is executing structural cost take-out and price increases, announced at 7% in North America and up to 10% in Latin America (Management Language), and the SDA Global segment earned segment EBIT of USD 124 million on USD 710 million of nine-month sales (Documentary Fact).

Weaknesses. Leverage of 9.52x total debt over ongoing EBITDA is high and rising (Analyst Interpretation on a Documentary Fact base). Ongoing EBIT for Q3 2026 was USD 97 million against USD 180 million a year earlier and management has cut its 2026 earnings outlook twice since May (Documentary Fact; Management Language for the outlook). North American demand is soft and one retailer is about 15% of sales and about 44% of receivables (Documentary Fact). Operating cash flow is negative on a nine-month basis, and year-end net debt guidance relies on a fourth-quarter release (Analyst Interpretation). The new secured structure subordinates unsecured creditors in the collateral pool and limits flexibility through indenture covenants and a borrowing base (Documentary Fact; legal capacity Insufficient Information). Ratings are below investment grade at all three agencies and moved lower in the first half of 2026, which has reduced commercial paper access (Documentary Fact). Equity-method and other non-core assets are volatile: a USD 124 million impairment of the Whirlpool India stake was booked in Q3 2026 (Documentary Fact). Coverage of interest by ongoing EBITDA fell to 2.21x on an expense basis, and the proxy fixed charge ratio is 0.97x (Analyst Interpretation).

### Assessment and thesis

Assessment (Analyst Interpretation, confidence Medium): the fundamental credit view is weak and weakening, with default risk in the next twelve months contained by liquidity and cleared maturities but with refinancing and covenant risk building as the secured layer grows and earnings stay low. Thesis: Whirlpool can service its debt through 2027 on its liquidity, but its credit quality depends on restoring North American margins and on a year-end working capital release that its own guidance requires.

## Calculations

All inputs are USD millions from the cited pages or the financial base handoff.

1. Leverage and cover are those in the financial base: total debt 6,852 over ongoing EBITDA 720 is 9.52x; net debt 5,749 over 720 is 7.98x; 8.46x and 7.10x on operating-profit EBITDA of 810.
2. Annual coupon on the Secured Notes: 1,000 x 7.500% = 75.00; 1,000 x 7.875% = 78.75; total 153.75, shown as about 154.
3. Coupon on repaid euro notes at carrying value: 587 x 1.25% = 7.34 and 703 x 1.10% = 7.73, about 15 in total; added annual coupon 153.75 - 15.07 = about 139 (approximate; face values in euro and the saving on the repaid revolver are not printed).
4. Run-rate interest: Q3 2026 interest expense 102 x 4 = 408; ongoing EBITDA 720 / 408 = 1.76x. Guided ongoing EBIT 293 / guided interest expense 350 = 0.84x (EBIT basis, management guidance).
5. Fixed charge proxy: (ongoing EBITDA 720 - capital expenditures 405) / interest expense 326 = 315 / 326 = 0.97x. Capital expenditures LTM 405 = 389 - 239 + 255 and interest expense LTM 326 = 341 - 256 + 241 (financial base). Not the covenant.
6. Implied Q4 operating inflow: guided operating cash flow 700 - nine-month outflow (-826) = 1,526. Implied net debt reduction: 5,749 - 4,500 = 1,249 (a ceiling).
7. Liquidity: 1,103 + 2,193 = 3,296; 669 + 3,432 = 4,101 (financial base).
8. Unsecured notes: carrying values at 2025-12-31 of the 0.50% 2028, 4.75% 2029, 6.125% 2030, 2.40% 2031, 4.75% 2032, 5.50% 2033, 6.50% 2033, 5.75% 2034, 5.15% 2043, 4.50% 2046 and 4.60% 2050 notes: 586 + 697 + 600 + 300 + 298 + 300 + 600 + 299 + 249 + 497 + 493 = 4,919, about 4,900. Cross-check: total debt 6,852 - Secured Notes 2,000 - notes payable 14 = 4,838, about 4.8 billion; the 81 difference is not explained in the sources (discounts, "Other, net" of (39) and euro remeasurement are candidates).
9. Back-loading: 5,008 / 6,894 = 72.6%, shown as about 73%.
10. US cash: 3.7% x total assets 16,963 = about 628.
11. Preferred dividend: 575 x 8.50% = 48.9, about 49 a year.
12. Derived maturities against the balance sheet: 14 + 585 + 695 + 592 + 5,008 = 6,894; 6,894 - 6,852 = 42.
