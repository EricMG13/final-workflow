### Strongest contrary evidence and what the sources do not settle

- Perimeter. The 10-K says that "the operations previously reported within the MDA Asia segment are no longer reported as a segment as a result of the deconsolidation of Whirlpool India" (10-K page:4), and the Q3 release flags the 2025 comparative as including the India business (release Q3 page:1). A trend analysis that does not adjust for this would overstate the decline; the release itself shows a like-for-like organic sales measure that removes India sales (release Q3 page:26). The sources do not print a full like-for-like FY2025 income statement.
- Definition difference. Segment-note total EBIT for Q3 2026 is "$ (128)" (10-Q Q3 page:43) and ties to the release's reported EBIT of "$ (128)"; the release's ongoing EBIT of "$ 97" (release Q3 page:11) excludes restructuring, an equity method impairment, M&A costs and a trade customer bankruptcy charge. The two are different measures, both issuer-defined, and downstream modules must label which they use.
- Management claim versus filing. The release says the company has "preserved a clear debt maturity ladder until 2028" (release Q3 page:1). The 10-Q Q3 supports part of this: "We have $14 million of debt maturing in the next twelve months" (10-Q Q3 page:53). The 10-Q does not itself print a maturity table after the refinancing, so the 2028 claim is an attributed management statement that a downstream module cannot verify from a table.
- Net leverage. The releases define "Net debt leverage" and "Ongoing EBITDA" (release Q3 page:9) but the pinned text does not print a computed ratio. The release outlook gives "Net debt below $4.5 billion at year end" (release Q3 page:2), a forward management statement, not a reported ratio.
